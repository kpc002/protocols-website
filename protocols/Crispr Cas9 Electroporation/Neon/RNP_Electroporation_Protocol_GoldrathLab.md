**Cas9 RNP Electroporation Protocol (Seki and Rutt, JEM, 2018)\_v2.4**

Generated by Katia Faliti

Modified by Jinyong Choi

*Re-Modified by Tianda Deng\**

*\*Note: I chose to leave in some components (colored gray) that aren’t necessarily relevant to us now, but could potentially be utilized in the future. So if you’re confused by things that don’t really pertain to you, don’t worry.*

**<span class="underline">Materials</span>**

1.  Spleen + I.Ns C57BL/6 mouse.

2.  **For activated CD4 T cells:**  
    0.5 x 10<sup>6</sup> cells/ml  
    8 ug/ml anti-CD3 (clone l45-2c11, BD)  
    8 ug/ml anti-CD28 (clone 37.51)  
    Pre-activation for 2 days before transfection.

3.  **For resting CD4 T cells:**  
    Preincubated with 2 ng/ ml rhlL-7 for 24h before transfection.

4.  **crRNA and tracrRNA from IDT:  
    **Alt-R CRISPR-Cas9 TracrRNA  
    Alt-R CRISPR-Cas9 TracrRNA-ATTO 550  
    (ATTO 550: a fluorochrome excited by yellow laser, PE channel)  
    Alt-R CRISPR-Cas9 crRNA NTC negative control

5.  **Cas9 Protein**  
    IDT Cas9 v3 (10 mg/ml) or  
    TrueCut Cas9 Protein v2 (5 mg/ml; Invitrogen) or  
    Cas9 (6.4 mg/ml; QB3 Berkley Lab) \*We use this one, it is greatly discounted for UCs.

6.  **Neon Transfection System**

7.  **Neon Transfection System 100uL Kit**

**Catalog \#: MPK10096**

**\*<span class="underline">Crispr Guide Design</span>**

  - I use CHOPCHOP for guide design: <https://chopchop.cbu.uib.no/> because I think it’s simple to use, there are many alternatives (just google “crispr guide generator”)

  - When ordering **crRNA** from IDT, you can check your guides again on their website

  - Make sure to order **crRNA and NOT sgRNA**. To learn the difference on more on RNPs, this is a great resource: <https://www.idtdna.com/pages/education/decoded/article/crispr-guide-rna-format-affects-genome-editing-outcomes>

**(Optional if you start from IVT) <span class="underline">Preparation of In Vitro Transcription</span>**

A 0.3 uM target oligonucleotide mix working solution was prepared by diluting l uL of each target oligonucleotide stock solution (10 uM) in 32 uL nuclease free water.

Use GeneArt Precision gRNA Synthesis kit:

PCR reaction in 25 uL in the following order:

1.  12.5 uL Phusion HF PCR Master Mix 2X

2.  1 uL Tracr Fragment + T7 Primer Mix

3.  1 uL 0.3 uM Target F1/R1 oligonucleotide mix

4.  10.5 uL nuclease-free water

PCR cycles:

1.  Denaturation 98 °C, 10 sec

2.  Denaturation 32 cycles at 98 °C, 5 sec

3.  Annealing at 55 °C, 15 sec

4.  Extension at 72 °C, I min

Purify the PCR products from agarose gel.

16-24 ng for PCR product was used in the IVT reaction (MegaShortScripts T7 Kit), following the instruction.

Incubation at 37 °C for 4 h.

Add 1 uL of Turbo DNAse to the reaction to remove DNA template. Incubate 37 °C for 15 min.

sgRNA was purified using the GeneArt precision kit and eluted in 40 uL RNAse-free water.

**(D0)**

**<span class="underline">Isolation SM CD4 T cells and stimulation</span>**

Activate with anti-CD3 and anti-CD28 (We have an activation protocol on the server)

*\*I personally like using 6 well plates for activation and seed between 1-2x10<sup>6</sup> cells/well.*

**<span class="underline">(D2)</span>**

**<span class="underline">Preparation of crRNA-tracrRNA duplex</span>**

To prepare the duplex, each Alt-R crRNA and Alt-R tracrRNA or Alt-tracrRNA-ATTO550 were reconstituted to **100 uM** with **Nuclease Free Duplex buffer (IDT).**

100 μM = 100 pmol/uL = 10 nmol/100 uL = 2 nmol/20 uL

**\*If you order <span class="underline">10 nmols</span> from IDT, this means resuspend with <span class="underline">100 uL</span> of NF Duplex Buffer (IDT)**

Mix oligos at equimolar concentrations in a sterile PCR tube (e.g. **3 uL** Alt-R crRNA + **3 uL** Alt-R-tracrRNA).

Annealing oligos by heating at 95C for 5 min in a PCR machine, then <span class="underline">cool down at RT at least 1 h.</span>

After duplexing, the concentration of crRNA-tracrRNA duplex in solution is now 50 uM (50 pmol/uL).

**<span class="underline">Precomplexing of Cas9 / RNP</span>**

TrueCut Cas9 Protein v2 concentration: 5 mg/mL (1 ug = 6.1 pmol; 30.5 pmol / uL)

IDT Cas9 Protein v2 concentration: 10 mg/mL (1 ug = 6.1 pmol; 61 pmol / uL)

QB3 Berkley Lab Cas9 Protein: 6.4 mg/mL (1 ug = 6.1 pmol; 39 pmol / uL)

Mix gRNA and Cas9 at \~3:1 molar ratio

In a PCR strip, mix **4.8 uL** crRNA-tracrRNA duplexes (240 pmol) and **2.0 uL** QB3 Cas9 (80 pmol) by gentle pipetting up and down and <span class="underline">incubated RT for at least 20 min</span>. Now total **6.8 uL** mixture generated per reaction.

*\*During this 20 minute incubation is typical when I start prepping the activated cells for Electroporation*

or

In a PCR strip, mix **4.8 uL** crRNA-tracrRNA duplexes (240 pmol) and **1.3 uL** IDT Cas9 v3 (80 pmol) by gentle pipetting up and down and incubated RT for at least 10 min. Now total **6.1 uL** mixture generated per reaction.

Note: Check the amount of crRNA, tracrRNA and Cas9 protein mixture before the reaction \*(meaning make sure you have enough volume).

\~2.4 uL crRNA, \~2.4 uL tracrRNA, and \~2.0 uL Cas9 protein are needed per reaction.

**<span class="underline">For Electroporation with Neon Transfection System</span>**

1.  Harvest activated cells and count cell number

2.  Wash with sterile **PBS twice**: it’s important to remove as much serum as possible since it could interfere with the electroporation

3.  Resuspend cells **@ 2x10<sup>6</sup> cells / 110 uL “R Buffer”** (from Neon Kit)

> *\*I have also resuspended at 1.5x10<sup>6</sup> cells and it worked as well*

4.  Aliquot **6.8 uL of RNP mixture** into sterile Eppendorf tubes

5.  Carefully add **110 uL** **of cells** (now in R buffer) into each Eppendorf: **do not introduce bubbles or you will regret it**

6.  Mix carefully with pipette (NO BUBBLES) then incubate the cells and RNP mixture for **3-5 mins**

7.  Very carefully fill the Neon Pipette with the cell/RNP mixture **without introducing any bubbles\!**

8.  Electroporate with **following settings:**

**Voltage: 1600**

**Width: 10 ms**

**Pulses: 3**

**\**If there are bubbles in the pipette tip during the electroporation, the bubbles will spark and kill off A LOT of cells. Observe the tip as you start the electroporation and if you see a spark, expect dead cells.***

9.  After electroporation, transfer the cells into 2mL of TCM + hIL2 in a 6 well plate and move to 37C incubator.

**<span class="underline">For Electroporation with MaxCyte ATX</span>**

1.  Aliquot **57 uL** of complete media/well in 24 well plate.

2.  Wash cells with electroporation buffer (Hyclone) once and resuspend the cells at 4 x 10<sup>7</sup> cells / **mL** in electroporation buffer.

3.  Aliquot **50 uL** of cells into Eppendorf tubes (2 x 10<sup>6</sup> cells / rxn)

4.  Add **6.8 uL RNP** into the cells and mix well. Now total approx. **57 uL.**

5.  Transfer the cells/RNP mixture to cuvette (OC-100).

6.  Electroporation with **Expended-5 protocol**

7.  After electroporation, transfer the cells into 57 uL media in 24 well, and incubate for 20 min in 37C incubator.

8.  Add 1 mL of R10 + BME + NEAA + 10 ng/mL IL-2.

**<span class="underline">For Electroporation with 4D-Nucleofector Core Unit: Lonza, AAF-1002B</span>**

1.  Aliquot **35 uL** of complete media/well in 24 well plate.

2.  Prepare 25 uL / rxn P4 buffer + Supplement 1 (20.4 uL + 4.6 uL)

3.  1 x 10<sup>6</sup> murine T cells resuspended into **23.5 or 22.2 uL or 21 uL** of P4 buffer (Primary Cell 4D-Nuclcofector X Kit S (32 RCT, V4XP-4032; Lonza)).

4.  Mix T cells with **11.5 or 12.8 uL or 14 uLRNP** in round bottom 96 well and incubate for 2 min at RT. Now total **35 uL**

5.  Transfer the cells+RNP mix to Nucleofection cuvette strips (4D-Nuclcofector X Kit S; Lonza)

6.  Electroporate the cells the 4D-Nucleofector.  
    Pulse for activated murine T cells: **CM137**  
    Pulse for resting murine T cells: **DS 137**

7.  After electroporation, transfer the cells into 35 uL media in 24 well plate, and incubate for 20 min in 37C incubator.

8.  Add 1 mL of R10 + BME + NEAA + 10 ng/mL IL-2.

**<span class="underline">(D3)</span>**

**<span class="underline">Transfection efficiency & Transfer</span>**

  - Check transfection efficiency ATTO 550 fluorescence (PE channel) by flow 1-3 day after transfection

  - Prep cells for *in vivo* transfer
