**Electroporation of primary activated CD8 T cells**

**Coat Plates for activation**

**For goat anti-hamster coated plates**

1.  Prepare Antibody-Coated Plate (Start 2+ hrs before plating)

2.  Preparing a 1:30 dilution of Goat Anti-Hamster IgG antibody in sterile PBS.

3.  Coat wells by adding 550 µL of the diluted GAH-IgG solution to each required well of a 6-well round-bottom plate.

4.  Gently tap the plate to ensure the bottom of each well is fully coated.

5.  Place the plate in a 37°C incubator for at least 2 hours (or overnight at 4°C).

**Spleen/lymph node harvest and homogenization**

1.  Grab spleen and lymph nodes (inguinal, brachial, and axilliary, avoid mLN if you can) and put in a tube with R10

2.  Prepare Single-Cell Suspension by washing spleen/lymph nodes over a 70 uM filter in a petri dish

3.  When spleen in homogenized, place 70 um filter over 50 ml conical

4.  Wash filter by washing the petri dish 2x with 1 ml of RP5

5.  Wash cells 1x at 1600 RPM for 4 min

6.  Dump and add 1 ml ACK lysis and incubate for 4 min

7.  Add 40 ml MACS buffer and spin at 1600 RPM for 4 min

8.  Prepare Biotinylated Antibody Cocktail. The volumes below are *per sample* (adjust as needed based on cell number, typically for up to  total cells).

> *Note: This cocktail targets major non-CD8+ T cell populations. Adjust cocktail based on specific experimental needs or available antibodies.*

**Negative Selection Staining & Bead Incubation**

9.  Resuspend the cell pellet in 500 µL of the freshly prepared Biotinylated Antibody Cocktail (adjust volume based on cell number if necessary, typically \~100 µL per 10^7 cells).

10. Incubate for 15 minutes at 4°C (in the fridge), protected from light.

11. Fill the tube with MACS buffer (\~10-14 mL). Centrifuge at \~400 x g for 4 minutes. Discard the supernatant.

12. Resuspend the pellet in 500 µL MACS Buffer. Add 50 µL of streptavidin magnetic beads.

13. Incubate for 15 minutes at 4°C, protected from light.

14. Fill the tube with MACS buffer. Centrifuge at \~400 x g for 4 minutes. Discard the supernatant. During centrifuge step, wash MACS column (e.g. LS column) in the magnet by rinsing with 3 ml of MACS buffer. Discard flow through. Don’t let filter dry so add more if needed during centrifuge step.

15. Resuspend the pellet in 500 µL MACS buffer.

### 

### **Magnetic Column Separation**

1.  Apply the cell suspension onto the column.

2.  Collect the unlabeled cells that pass through the column. This fraction contains the enriched, untouched CD8+ T cells.

3.  Wash the column three times (3x) with 3 mL of MACS buffer each time. Collect these washes and combine them with the initial flow-through.

4.  Centrifuge the collected flow-through at 1600 RPM for 4 minutes. Discard the supernatant.

5.  Resuspend the purified CD8+ T cell pellet in a small volume (e.g., 500 µL) of R10. Count the cells using a hemocytometer or automated counter. Determine cell viability (e.g., using Trypan Blue).

6.  Stop here and check CD8 T cell purity by flow (pre and post isolation)
    
      - We had 89% purity post isolation of CD8 T cells and 44e6 cells total. Resuspend to 2e6 cells/ml. Plate 1 ml.

7.  Run flow to check purity (can assume 90-95% if in a hurry but always better to flow check)

8.  Stain aliquot of cells with FITC-CD8b (1:100)

9.  Wash 1x with FACS and run on flow.

### 

### **Cell Plating and Activation**

1.  Retrieve the GAH-IgG or anti-CD3 coated plate from the incubator. Carefully aspirate the coating solution from the wells.

2.  Wash each well twice with 1 mL of R10 to remove any residual unbound antibody. Aspirate thoroughly after the final wash.

3.  Dilute *anti-CD3 and anti-CD28 antibodies each 1:500 in R10* for GAH-IgG coated wells*.* Dilute anti-CD28 for the anti-CD3 plate coated wells at 1:250 so there will be 1 ug/ml anti-CD28 in final well.

4.  Add 1 ml of the CD8 T cell suspension (containing cells) to each prepared well.

5.  Add 1 ml of the anti-CD3/CD28 antibody (or just anti-CD28) mixture to each well containing cells (total volume per well = 2 mL). Gently pipette up and down or swirl the plate to mix.
    
      - *Final concentration: 1 x 10^6^ cells/mL*
    
      - *Final antibody dilution: 1:1000 (effective concentration depends on stock)*

6.  Place the plate in a 37°C, 5% CO2 incubator for 18-24 hours.

**CRISPR/Cas9 knock out in primary T cells via electroporation**

1.  To make a Cas9/RNA complex, mix guides plus tracr RNA as below in PCR tubes:
    
      - gRNA thy1 guide only control
    
      - gRNA thy1 guide plus guide of 1<sup>st</sup> target (Dand5)
    
      - gRNA thy1 guide plus guide of 2<sup>nd</sup> target (Sulf2)
    
      - want to do 3:1 molar ratio of guide RNA to Cas9 enzyme.
    
      - Thy1 AC guide at 100uM concentration.
    
      - Experimental guides are at 100uM concentration.

2.  Control (sgThy1 control wells + sgThy1 and expt control wells)
    
      - 3uL thy1 AC crRNA guide from IDT (Box ‘Nicole’ at -20ºC)
    
      - 3uL tracr RNA (Alt-R® CRISPR-Cas9 tracrRNA, ATTO™ 550) (Box ‘Nicole’ at -20ºC)

3.  Experimental (x number of groups needed)
    
      - 1.5uL thy1 AC crRNA guide from IDT
    
      - 1.5uL guide of experimental guide (Alt-R CRISPR-Cas9 crRNA from IDT)
    
      - 3uL tracr RNA (Alt-R® CRISPR-Cas9 tracrRNA, ATTO™ 550)

4.  Incubate in PCR machine at 95C for 5 minutes, then immediately remove at put at RT in the dark for 1 hour for annealing.
    
      - During this time, take 10mL of R10 plus 50U/ml hIL2 (a 1:2000 dilution), and put into a water bath to warm to 37deg.
    
      - Gather materials in hood such as strips, cytokines for supplemented media
    
      - Make cytokine mixes
    
      - Make the P3 + supplement 1 buffer and get to RT. Make a P3 buffer mix below per reaction and keep at room temperature.
    
      - 1x buffer recipe, make enough for each rxn:
        
        1.  <span class="underline">Supplement 1</span>: 3.6uL (made enough for eight samples, 28.8 ul)
        
        2.  <span class="underline">P3</span>: 16.4uL (made enough for eight samples, 131.2 ul)

5.  Add 1.9uL 40uM Cas9 enzyme to each tube of tracrRNA and guide RNA complex (-80’, middle shelf, middle rack). DO NOT MIX. Gently flick the tube to mix sgRNAs with Cas9 and spin down briefly to collect drops from the sides of the tube. Incubate for 20 minutes at room temperature for complex formation.

6.  Do during Cas9 incubation:
    
      - Count cells, then spin down cells in large volume (use 50 mL conical) of STERILE, RT PBS to remove potential RNase contamination from FCS containing media before electroporation (IMPORTANT). Can do 2-10x10^6 cells per reaction. *\*We did 2-6e6 per condition.*
    
      - Turn on Lonza 4D-NucleofectorTM System: select wells, pulse code (see below) and solution (P3 primary).

7.  Immediately after complex formation, transfer cells into Eppendorf tubes in 1 ml of PBS, and spin down on a tabletop centrifuge at 5000 RPM for 5min <span class="underline">room temperature</span>). It is important to prepare everything before resuspending the cells in P3 buffer to minimize the time that cells are exposed to the buffer.

8.  Take supernatant from the cell pellet using pipets (make sure to take off all remaining liquid from cell pellet to not dilute out the small reaction volume of 25 μl). Resuspend <span class="underline">cell pellet in 20μl P3 buffer</span> per reaction.

9.  Divide cells out per sample and then add respective guide RNA complexes. Remember to aliquot a sample for no guide RNA control here (just receives shock and no guide RNA mix- positive control for Thy1 knockout)

10. Transfer 5 μl of CRISPR/Cas9 RNP into the 20 μl P3 buffer with cells and mix gently.

11. Transfer the 25 μl cell/RNP mix to the bottom hole of a well of the Lonza nucleofector strip. Be careful to not create any bubbles. (keep time of cells in P3 buffer as short as possible as it is cytotoxic to the cells). Chip Orientation:
    
      - Open square: Top
    
      - Closed square: Bottom

12. Electroporate cells by putting the nucleofector strip into the Lonza nucleofector and as depicted, start the program
    
      - Naïve CD8 T cells: use DN100 program in P3 buffer
    
      - Activated CD8 T cells: use DS137 program in P3 buffer

13. After electroporation (a few seconds) add 130 μl of 37°C pre-warmed R10 media into the well containing electroporated cells and rest cells for 10 minutes in the incubator (5% CO2, 37°C).

14. In the meantime, prepare a 6-MW plates with R10 and IL-2 at 15 U/mL

15. Dilute 1:3.33 the leftover R10 + IL-2 (50 U/mL): 600 μL of R10+IL-2 with 1400 μL of R10 plain (final 15 U/mL). Fill outer wells with PBS/media.

16. Resuspend the cells in the electroporation well using a pipette, and transfer the cell suspension into a fresh tube or well. To recover any remaining cells, wash the electroporation well with an additional 150 μl R10 media by pipetting, and transfer the media into the same collection tube. Add 700 uL pre-warmed R10 to make 1 ml total. Distribute the control sgThy1 cells across however many wells (can pool 2-3 wells into one cuvette and redistribution after electroporation). For that step, you omit the 700 ul and distribute 300 ul to well if one well -\> one electroporation strip well or distribute 100 ul to each well if three wells -\> one electroporation strip well

17. Count the cells with Trypan Blue as 20-50% of T cells will be lost during the electroporation step.

18. *For in vivo adoptive transfer*, resuspend in PBS at desired concentration and adoptively transfer into recipient mice via intravenous injection. Keep some cells going in culture for future flow and KO validation with flow or Sanger sequencing.

19. *For cells in culture*, split the cells over the original number of cells they were into (e.g., if three wells were originally pooled to do CRISPR on, then split them back into the original 3 wells) for culture

20. Incubate for overnight/24 hrs

21. Count cells prior to replacing media, replenish cytokines every 48 hours
    
      - sgThy1 1: 1.29e6 cells total
    
      - sgThy1 2: 1.62e6 cells total
    
      - sgThy1 + sgDand5: 1.65e6 cells total
    
      - sgThy1 + sgSulf2: 1.35e6 cells total

22. Replace IL2 media with Trm polarization media to some wells or replenish IL2 controls by spinning cells and replacing media as follows:
    
      - IL2 replenishment: (25 U/ml IL2)
    
      - Trm polarization media: 25 U/ml IL2, 10ng/ml IL15, 10 ng/ml TGFb, 10 nM RA)
    
      - IL2 stock: 100,000 U/ml so dilute 1:100 then add 25 ul per ml to get 25 U/ml
    
      - IL15 stock: 10 ug/ml so dilute 1:1000 (1 ul per ml) to get to 10 ng/ml
    
      - TGFb stock: 0.2 mg/ml so dilute 1:20,000 total or 1:200 then add 10 ul per ml to get to 10 ng/ml.
    
      - RA: 0.0015g/ml so dilute 1:1000 then 2 ul per ml to get to 20 nM

23. Incubate for 48 hours. 72 hours post electroporation, transfer cells to Eppendorf tubes.

24. Spin at 2000 RPM for 5 min and collect supernatant by pipetting off sup avoiding cells and save at -80 for ELISA.

25. Collect cells for flow, PCR of genomic DNA for Sanger sequencing validation of knockout, and for RNA extraction -\> cDNA synthesis for qPCR validation of knockout.
