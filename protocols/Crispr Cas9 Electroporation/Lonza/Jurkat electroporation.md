*Jurkat Electroporation with Lonza Electroporator*

1.  Place 1.5 mls of antibiotic free R10 in each well of a 12 well plate (one for each cuvette sample) and equilibrate at 37C for 15-30 min.

2.  Collect Jurkats from 6 T75 flasks and place into 2 50 ml conicals. Take aliquots to count cells.

Cell count: 1.3e6, 80 mls. 104 million cells (2 tubes total), 97% viability

3.  Aliquot number of cells needed (10 million cells per sample (n=7)) 70 million.

4.  Spin at 100xg for 10 min.

5.  During Jurkat centrifugation, take out plasmids from -20C. \*IMPORTANT NOTE: plasmid solution added should not exceed 10% of reaction volume, 100 ul in this case so can only add 10 ul of plasmid max.

Pmaxgfp = positive control, use 2 ug per solution (4 ul)

MF12 (plasmid + BirA + GFP) = 2.05 ug/ul

5 ug = 2.44 ul of MF12

10 ug = 4.88 ul of MF12

20 ug = 9.75 ul of MF12

MF15 (plasmid + BirA + GFP + Fcgr2b) = 2.48 ug/ul

5 ug = 2.01 ul of MF15

10 ug = 4.02 ul of MF15

20 ug = 8.04 ul of MF15

6.  During centrifugation, make nucleofector solution. Add 82 ul of nucleofector solution V and 18 ul of supplement 1 for each sample. \_7\_ samples = (82 \* 7) = 574 ul of nucleofector solution V. \_7\_ samples = (18 \* 7) = 126 ul of supplement 1.

7.  Aspirate all of supernatant (very important to get all of supernat)

8.  Resuspend in \# of samples \* 100 ul (700 ul) room temp nucleofector solution (nucleofector solution V + supplement 1 at a 4.5 to 1 ratio)

9.  Aliquot 100 ul into different tubes and add respective plasmid

10. Add solution to cuvette slowly, making sure there are no air bubbles in cuvette and that the media gets to the bottom of the cuvette. Tap lightly if necessary.

11. Clean off metal panels on cuvette with a chemwipe.

12. Run Jurkat high viability protocol on the electroporator. It is fast, but I swear it does electroporate\!

13. Return to hood and slowly add 500 ul of prewarmed antibiotic free media from 37C

14. Use supplied Pasteur pipette to take all the solution out. Be careful not to pipette up and down. Just slowly draw the solution out of the cuvette and into the 12 well plate.

15. Repeat for each sample \*IMPORTANT NOTE: nucleofector solution is toxic to cells so cells will die as time progresses (no more than 15 min) so batch samples accordingly.

16. Swirl plate gently and incubate at 37C overnight.

*Transfection validation*

1.  Take plate and transfer media to tubes

2.  Spin cells down, dump supernatant.

3.  Add pool left in tube to wells in a 96 well plates

4.  Wash 1x with FACS.

5.  Resuspend cells in 50uL of master mix in FACS wash.

6.  Incubate at 4C for 20 minutes.

7.  Wash cells 1x with FACS wash.

8.  Repeat but wash with PBS instead of FACS.

9.  Make up L/D Blue solution by diluting 1:1000 of the stock to stain with 100uL per sample in PBS

10. 10 samples= 1mL PBS, add 1uL L/D stock.

11. For the comp, use 1 drop Arcs+ beads, 100uL PBS, and 1uL L/D stock. Let stain for 20-30min.

12. Resuspend in 100uL L/D solution.

13. Incubate in dark at 4C for 25min.

14. Add 100uL FACS buffer to cells and spin down and flick plate.

15. Wash 1X with FACS.
