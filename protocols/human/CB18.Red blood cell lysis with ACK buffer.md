**<span class="underline">Red Blood Cell lysis with ACK buffer (CB18)</span>**

**Materials:**

  - ACK buffer 1X

  - sample

  - Sterile physiological saline solution (NaCl 0,9%. Baxter) or 1X PBS /HBSS

  - Falcons (50mL)

**ACK 10X buffer preparation**

  - Make it in H20 (final volume 200 mL)

![](media/image1.emf)

  - Store at 4°C

  - At the moment you need: dilute in H<sub>2</sub>0 to obtain 1X solution and allow it to reach the room temperature. Do not store.

**Protocol**

  - Add 5-10 mL of ACK Lysing Buffer to the cell pellet.

  - Allow the blood sample plus ACK Lysing Buffer to incubate at room temperature for 3 – 5 minutes. Lysis of the red cells should be evident during this incubation.

  - Collect the white blood cells by centrifugation at 1600 rpm for 5 minutes at room temperature.

  - Aspirate the supernatant, leaving approximately 50 uL to avoid disturbing the pellet.

  - Gently mix the cells and the remaining fluid, then add 10 mL sterile physiological saline solution.

  - Collect the cells by centrifugation at 1600 rpm for 5 minutes at room temperature.

  - Aspirate the supernatant. The cells are ready for further analysis.
