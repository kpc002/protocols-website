**WORKPLACE SAFETY:**

Proper precautions on exposure to fluid tissue (blood) specimens, on handling tissue specimens, hazardous chemicals and biohazard as well as infectious waste by using the required Personal Protective Equipment when appropriate:

Eyewear

Mask

Apron

Gloves

Laboratory Gown

Proper hand washing: Hand washing should be done as often as possible especially after removing gloves and before handling food and before touching any clean surface. Hands should be washed with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds.

Injury at work: Any injury must be reported to the appropriate staff or Project investigator within 24 hours to complete and sign an incident report.

5.  > **Fluid Tissue Processing**

<span class="underline">Collection of Peripheral Blood Leukocytes from Venipuncture</span>

1.  Collect blood via venipuncture into sodium heparin blood collection (green top) tubes.

2.  Warm density gradient medium (ie. Lymphoprep), dilution/wash medium (ie. DPBS or HBSS) and centrifuge to room temperature.

3.  Aliquot 4 ml of room temperature density gradient medium into sterile 15 ml conical tubes (plan 2 tubes for each 10 ml tube of blood collected).

4.  Wipe the outside of the blood collection tubes with 70% ethanol.

5.  Carefully remove the caps from the blood collection tubes.

6.  Decant blood into sterile 50 ml conical tube.

7.  Add 8 ml of sterile room temperature wash medium to each blood collection tube.

8.  Using a 10 ml pipette, transfer wash medium from blood collection tubes to 50 ml conical tube containing blood (approximately 1:1 dilution).

9.  Mix diluted blood by inverting 50 ml conical tube 2-3 times.

10. Using a 10 ml pipette, carefully transfer 8-10 ml of diluted blood to each 15 ml conical tube containing density gradient medium. Deliver the diluted blood slowly to the top of the density gradient medium, being careful to not disrupt the medium/blood interface.

11. Centrifuge density gradient medium/blood at 1200 x g for 20 minutes at 20<sup>o</sup>C.

12. Remove tubes from centrifuge taking care to not disrupt the density medium/plasma interface. There will be multiple layers- red blood cells will be at the bottom of the tube below the density gradient medium, the density gradient medium will be above that, a thin layer of leukocytes (buffy coat) will sit atop the density gradient medium, and the diluted plasma will be the top layer.

13. Using a 5 ml pipette, collect the leukocytes from atop the density gradient medium. Transfer to a new sterile 50 ml tube. Combine leukocytes from all tubes from the same sample.

14. Add sterile wash buffer to final volume of 40 ml. Recover cells by centrifugation at 600 x g for 5 minutes at 4<sup>o</sup>C. Repeat 2x. Count cells.

15. Re-suspend cell pellet in ice cold heat-inactivated fetal bovine serum, 500 μl/starting tube of blood. Place on ice.

16. Using a 5 ml pipette and keeping the cells on ice, add freezing medium (ice cold FBS + 20% DMSO) dropwise with constant swirling to 1:1 dilution.

17. Aliquot cells into 3 cryovials. Place in -80<sup>o</sup>C freezer. Cells can be transferred to liquid nitrogen after 24 hours.
