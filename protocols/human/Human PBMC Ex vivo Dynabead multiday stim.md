Ex vivo anti-CD3/28 Dynabead multiday stim of human PBMC to measure cytokine production

Donor Vial info:

PBMC cell count:

If using fresh PBMC:

*PBMC isolation from whole blood in CPT tube*

1.  Invert CPT tube 2-4 times

2.  Centrifuge CPTs at 1800 G for 30 minutes at room temperature, 9-1 acceleration/deceleration (handle with care and keep tubes upright after centrifuging). 

3.  Remove plasma layer being careful not to disrupt cell layer (cloudy layer between CPT gel and plasma).   

4.  Pour remaining plasma and cells from CPT tubes into a 50 mL Falcon tube 

> *NOTE:  No more than 6 CPTs per 50mL Falcon tube, if you have more than 6 divide them evenly into 2 or more Falcon tubes (they can be combined at the lyse step). *

5.  Rinse CPTs 2-3x using ≈ 7mL of RPMI by pipetting up and down.

6.  Dispense each wash from the CPTs into the 50 mL Falcon tube along with the cells in step 4

7.  Bring Falcon tube volume up to 50 mL using RPMI 

8.  Centrifuge at 1500RPM for 5 minutes at RT, aspirate the supernatant, mechanically disrupt pellet by raking

9.  Resuspend cells in 10 ml High Yield Lyse (Thermofisher) and incubate at RT for 10 mins.

10. Bring volume up to 50 mL using RPMI, mixing gently.

If using frozen PBMC:

*Thawing cryopreserved PBMC for immunophenotyping*

Protocol modified from here: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6060642/

2.  Place a cryopreserved PBMC vial in a 37°C water bath. Submerge the cryovial halfway and thaw for 60 seconds.

3.  PBMCs were frozen at 6-7 million cells in 1ml of freezing medium for 1 min.

4.  Add 1 mL of warmed (37°C) complete RPMI slowly dropwise to the thawed PBMCs against the side of the cryovial using a transfer pipette.

5.  Pipette the thawed PBMCs into 5 mL (per vial so 10 ml if thawing two vials) of complete RPMI in a 15ml conical tube that has been warmed to 37°C (no pipetting). Make sure to wipe down the tube

6.  Rinse the cryovials once with 1 mL of warm complete RPMI, pipette the PBMCs into the conical tube with the cell mixture.

7.  Incubate the PBMCs for 5 minutes in the 37°C water bath.

8.  Take aliquot to count cells.

Cell Count Pre-Rest: 1.76e6/ml. 98% viability. 2.5 mls. 4.4e6 cells total.

9.  Centrifuge the PBMCs for 10 minutes at 300xg at room temperature.

10. Pour off the supernatant. This will leave a small amount of complete RPMI behind that aids in pellet resuspension.

11. Add 1 mL of pre-warmed complete RPMI. Mix by flicking or racking the tube (avoid pipetting up and down).

12. Incubate the PBMCs for one hour at 37°C in a CO2 water jacketed incubator (37°C, 5% CO2, 95% humidity). Leave the cap of the conical tube slightly loose so that gas transfer can occur.

13. Centrifuge the PBMCs for 10 minutes at 300xg at room temperature.

14. Resuspend in complete RPMI to 10e6/ml.

15. Take aliquot to count cells

Cell Count Post-Rest:

*Multi-day anti-CD3/28 Dynabead stimulation*

1.  > Prepare CD3/CD28 beads (Invitrogen Dynabeads Human T-Activator CD3/CD28) 

2.  > Vortex 30 seconds 

3.  > Add beads to 1.5 mL conical (15 ul per 1 ml sample in manufacturer protocol)

4.  > Add 1 mL FACS 

5.  > Vortex 30 seconds 

6.  > Place on magnet 1-2 minute 

7.  > Remove supernatant

8.  > Add equal parts R10 to the bead volume used in step 4 (resulting concentration beads to R10 ratio is 1:1)

9.  > Add media to well of 48 well plate, correct up to 1 ml (1 ml – 100 ul cells – 15 ul beads = 885 ul of media

10. > Plate 1-1.2 million cells in 100 ul into R10 in 48 well plate \*Easier on cells to add media first then cells then beads\*

11. > Add washed beads to cells in media in 48 well plate. 15ul of anti-CD3/28 beads per well of 1 ml media.

12. > Add protein, antibody of interest or PBS as a control to respective wells.

13. > Incubate 1-3 days

14. > Four hours prior to harvest, add GolgiStop at 1 ug/ml

*Surface stain*

1.  After 4 hrs, spin and dump supernatant.

2.  Add surface stain master mix to samples. Ab at 1:100

3.  Stain all samples in surface antibody cocktail at room temp for 20 minutes, covered in foil. Make comps during this time. Make FMOs\!

4.  Add 1 ml of FACS, spin, dump supernatant. Repeat.

*Live/Dead staining*

5.  Stain each sample with 1 ml live dead (1 ul L/D Aqua per 1 ml PBS)
    
      - LIVE/DEAD™ Fixable Aqua Dead Cell Stain Kit, for 405 nm excitation Thermofisher, L34965 \*Live/dead stain is on same channel as CD14, CD19 to serve as a dump channel on BV510\*

6.  Incubate at room temp for 10 mins, covered in foil.

7.  Wash 1x with FACS

*Cytokine and transcription factor staining*

1.  Make fixation buffer (1:3 fix to diluent)

2.  Add 250uL/sample. Fix perm= 5mL fix, 15mL diluent

3.  Incubate for 20min at 4C in dark.

4.  At end of fixation incubation, wash 1x with 1X perm buffer (1:10 dilution with diH20)

5.  Stain with fluorochromes for 25-30 min at 4C in dark in perm buffer.

6.  Wash 1X with perm buffer. Wash 1X with FACS.

7.  Resuspend samples in 100uL FACS, transfer to microtubes, and flow.
