**<span class="underline">Percoll separation to remove tumor cells from lung tumor samples. (CB22)</span>**

**Materials:**

  - Percoll 100% stock

  - HBSS-/-1x

  - R10 (RPMI 1640 + 10% FBS + 1% Pen/Strep + 1% Ultra-glutamine)

  - 15 ml Falcons

  - 50 ml Falcons

**PERCOLL preparation**

1.  Use PERCOLL 100% (iso) for all subsequent dilutions: 18 ml PERCOLL stock + 2 ml PBS-/- 10x

2.  PERCOLL 40% (per sample): 4 ml PERCOLL 100% (iso) + 6 ml R10

3.  PERCOLL 70% (per sample): 3.5 ml PERCOLL 100% (iso) + 1.5 ml HBSS-/-1x

**Protocol**

1.  Collect cells by centrifugation (10 min, 400*g*)

2.  Resuspend cell pellet in **8 ml PERCOLL 40%** and transfer to a new 15 ml Falcon tube

3.  Stratify **4 ml PERCOLL 70%** under the 8 ml PERCOLL 40% layer. <span class="underline">NB: put firstly the 8 mL PERCOLL 40% then with 5mL pipette go to the bottom of the falcon and slowly stratify PERCOLL 70%.</span>

4.  Centrifuge at 790*g* for 30 minutes without brake (0)

5.  Remove supernatant (leave 7 ml)

6.  Collect the interface between the 30% and 70% PERCOLL (with a 5 ml pipette). Leave 1 ml of supernatant in the tube.

7.  Transfer to a 50 ml Falcon tube and fill up the tube with HBSS-/- 1x.

8.  Centrifuge at 400*g* for 10 minutes

9.  Remove supernatant completely and resuspend the cells in the desired medium.
