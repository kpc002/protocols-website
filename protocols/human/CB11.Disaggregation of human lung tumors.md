<span class="underline">Disaggregation of human lung tumors</span>

# Modified from *“An optimized disaggregation method for human lung tumors that preserves the phenotype and function of the immune cells*” Quatromoni JG et al, J of Leukocyte Biology 2015 and *“Establishment and identification of human primary lung cancer cell culture in vitro”* Si LL et al, Int J Clin Exp Pathol 2015.

**NOTE:**

After the lung tumor is surgically removed, it is placed in wash media: RPMI 1640 (Lonza) supplemented with 2% FBS (Sigma-Aldrich) and 1% Penicillin-Streptomycin (Euroclone) and brought immediately to the lab. Samples are processed within 60 minutes from removal.

**Time:**

1h and 30 minutes

**Materials:**

  - Petri dish

  - Scissors

  - Gentle MACS tube

  - 70 μm Cell strainer

  - Sterile physiological saline solution (NaCl 0,9%. Baxter)/ 1X PBS /HBSS

  - Black rubber bottom of a 5 mL syringe

  - Falcons (50mL)

  - Trypan Blue 0,04%

  - Labelled Cryovials

  - Cryobox (RT)

  - Freezing Medium (FBS 10% DMSO 4°C)

  - Scales

  - GentleMACS dissociator (Miltenyi Biotech)

**Protocol**

  - Weigh and measure the tumor tissue

  - The tumor tissue is sliced into 1-2mm<sup>3</sup> pieces by sterile microdissection by use of curved scissors

  - Mechanical disaggregation is performed with the Gentle MACS dissociator according to “01\_02\_lung” protocol set from the manufacturer (2 cycles are recommended at least). Addition of sterile physiological saline solution is suggested.

  - The sample is passed through a 70 μm nylon cell strainer. Gentle pressure is applied with the black rubber bottom of a 5 mL syringe to any remaining partially digested tissue on the cell strainer which was then washed with few mL of sterile physiological saline solution. NB: typically, \<5% of the tissue (consisting of mainly acellular-connective tissue) remained on the strainer at the end of this step.

  - The sample is washed with 25 mL of sterile physiological saline solution.

  - The suspension is centrifuged for 15 minutes at 300 g at room temperature, max acceleration (9), max brake (9).

  - The supernatant is aspirated and if lot of erythrocytes are present, lysis is performed using 1X ACK lysis buffer (incubation with 5ml for 5min). The lysis buffer need to be diluted to 1X with sterile di-water.

  - Make up cell suspension to 30 ml with sterile physiological saline solution to inactivate the lysis buffer.

  - The cells are washed in sterile physiological saline solution and then counted in Trypan Blue 0,04%.

  - The cell suspension is centrifuged for 10 minutes at 520 g at room temperature, max acceleration (9), max brake (9).

  - The supernatant is removed and the cells are suspended in FACS buffer for staining or in cold freezing medium (FBS 10% DMSO 4°C) to 1 mL/vial

  - Vials are kept for 24/48h in a cryobox at -80°C, then moved to liquid Nitrogen container.
