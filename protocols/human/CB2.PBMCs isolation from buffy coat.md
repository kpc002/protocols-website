<span class="underline">PBMC Isolation from Buffy Coat plus cell count & freezing</span>

**Materials**

  - Sterile physiological saline solution (NaCl 0,9%. Baxter)/ 1X PBS /HBSS

  - Ficoll solution (RT, use avoiding light exposure)

  - Falcons (50mL)

  - Trypan Blue 0,04%

  - RPMI 10

  - Labelled Cryovials

  - Cryobox (RT)

  - Freezing Medium (FBS 10% DMSO 4°C)

**Protocol per 45ml of blood**

  - Prepare 3 X 50 mL Falcons with 17 mL of Ficoll solution, RT

  - Dilute whole blood 1:2 (e.g. 45 mL blood plus 45 mL physiological saline solution)

  - Stratify diluted blood upon 17ml ficoll layer

  - Centrifuge: 400G, 30’, max acceleration (9), min brake (0), RT

  - Collect PBMC ring within the interphase, transfer it in 3 new 50 mL Falcon and bring to 40 ml with Sterile physiological saline solution (NaCl 0,9%. Baxter)/ 1X PBS /HBS

  - Centrifuge: 1600 rpm, 10’, max acceleration (9), max brake (9), RT

  - Remove supernatant, keeping in Falcon about 5 mL of saline solution

  - Resuspend pellet in 40 mL Sterile physiological saline solution (NaCl 0,9%. Baxter)/ 1X PBS /HBSS

**1<sup>st</sup> Platelets Removal**

  - Centrifuge 900 rpm, 15’, max acceleration (9) max brake (9), RT

  - Remove all supernatant.

**ACK for red blood cell lysis**

  - Add 1 mL of ACK Lysing Buffer to the cell pellet and resuspend the pellet;

  - Pull the 3 falcons in one tube and add additional 2ml of ACK 1X solution (total 5ml of ACK 1X solution), resuspend well the cells;

  - Allow the blood sample plus ACK Lysing Buffer to incubate at room temperature for 5 minutes. Lysis of the red cells should be evident during this incubation.

  - At the end of incubation, top up to 40ml with saline solution and proceed with the 2<sup>nd</sup> Platelets removal step.

**2<sup>nd</sup> Platelets Removal**

  - Centrifuge 900 rpm, 15’, max acceleration (9) max brake (9), RT

  - Remove all supernatant;

  - Resuspend pellet in 50 mL Sterile physiological saline solution (NaCl 0,9%. Baxter)/ 1X PBS /HBSS.
