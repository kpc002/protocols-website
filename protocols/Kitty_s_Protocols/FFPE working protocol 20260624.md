Tissues except SI

1.  Harvest sample into 10% NBF

2.  Incubate at RT with shaking
    
    1.  Kidney- cut 1 kidney in half. Fix for 24h
    
    2.  Liver- cut into lobes and fix for several hours. Cut into
        smaller pieces prior for fixation overnight. Fix for 48h. Change
        10% NBF after 48h
    
    3.  Spleen- put into fixative 40sec-1min after death. If taking
        whole spleen, cut into 2 pieces for embedding

3.  Rinse with PBS for 10 minutes 3X

4.  Arrange tissues if needed. (Combine tissues from different samples,
    orientation)
    
    1.  Embed in 2% LB Agar. (4g in 200ml water, microwave to melt, wait
        until temp between 42’-55’C)
    
    2.  Place tissues into cryomold and fill with agar. Tap to make sure
        bubbles do not form.
    
    3.  Pop agar out of mold and place into cassette

5.  Change to 70% Ethanol. Tissues can sit in alcohol as long as you
    need.
    
    1.  NOTE: If embedding after alcohol, tissue will dry quickly so
        stay alert and keep it moist.

For Small Intestine (fix first as it degrades quick)

1.  Flush small intestine with BD Fix/Perm solution. Remove as much of
    the mesentery to keep intestine straight. Holes in SI will make it
    difficult to flush. (BD fix/perm diluted with 1×PBS at a ratio of
    1:2)

2.  Insert crochet needle into proximal part of small intestine. Wet
    needle prior to insertion.

3.  Place needle with SI into box and cover with 10% NBF

4.  Remove crochet needle and cut longitudinally except the proximal end
    (for placement later).
    
    1.  If SI starts to bunch up, STOP.
    
    2.  Cut intestine longitudinally with scalpel until it can be
        removed easily from needle.

5.  Wash SI by dipping several times into PBS

6.  Swiss roll intestine. Secure with 30G needle.

7.  Fix overnight in 10% NBF at RT in 6 well plate

8.  Rinse with PBS for 10 minutes 3X

9.  Embed in 2% LB Agar. (4g in 200ml water, microwave to melt, wait
    until temp between 42’-55’C)
    
    1.  Slide pin out of swiss roll.
        
        1.  If roll needs to be cut in half: place wet filter paper on
            petri dish cover. This will allow pins to be secure and
            prevent SI from sliding around
        
        2.  Pin both ends of swiss roll with 30g pin
        
        3.  Cut in half with scalpel using sliding motion
    
    2.  Place into large cryomold and fill with agar until it covers the
        SI. Tap to make sure bubbles do not form.
    
    3.  Pop agar out and place into cassette

10. Change to 70% Ethanol. Tissues can sit in alcohol as long as you
    need.
    
    1.  NOTE: If embedding after alcohol, tissue will dry quickly so
        stay alert and keep it moist.
