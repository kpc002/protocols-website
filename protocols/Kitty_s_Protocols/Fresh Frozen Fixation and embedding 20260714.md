Tissues except SI

1.  Fix your samples with PFA 2-4%. RT then 4’C overnight (use capsule
    PFA for freshness)
    
    1.  Kidney- cut 1 kidney in half, fix 24h
    
    2.  Liver- separate into lobes, after a couple hours of fixation,
        cut into smaller pieces for overnight fixation, fix 48h
    
    3.  Spleen- put into fixation 40sec-1min post death

2.  Transfer into 15% sucrose in 1×PBS at 4°C with gentle shaking until
    tissue sinks

3.  Transfer into 30% sucrose in 1×PBS at 4°C with gentle shaking until
    tissue sinks (overnight)

4.  Transfer into 50/50 with 30% sucrose/OCT for 1-4h at RT with gentle
    shaking

5.  Remove excess liquid surround the tissue by absorption with Kim-wipe
    prior to freezing. Otherwise, the liquid will form ice crystal on
    the surface of tissue and prevent tissue attachment to OCT and cause
    a lot of difficulties during sectioning

6.  Transfer into mold with OCT for 1-4h with gentle shaking

7.  <span class="underline">Label the cryomold to mark the orientation
    of the tissue</span>.

8.  Using forceps, lower the cryomold containing embedded tissue into
    the isopentane without fully submerging. Perform in chem hood to
    avoid fumes.
    
    1.  If using shallow mold, put on dry ice to freeze

9.  Once frozen, place the cryomold on a piece of aluminum foil on dry
    ice for 30 min and let isopentane fumes evaporate

For Small Intestine (fix first as it degrades quick)

1.  Flush small intestine with BD Fix/Perm solution. Remove as much of
    the mesentery to keep intestine straight. Holes in SI will make it
    difficult to flush. (BD fix/perm diluted with 1×PBS at a ratio of
    1:2)

2.  Insert crochet needle into proximal part of small intestine. Wet
    needle prior to insertion.

3.  Place needle with SI into box and cover with 2% PFA

4.  Remove SI from crochet needle and cut longitudinally except the
    proximal end (for placement later).
    
    1.  If SI starts to bunch up, STOP.
    
    2.  Cut intestine longitudinally with scalpel until it can be
        removed easily from needle.

5.  Dip into PBS several times to clean out remaining feces

6.  Swiss roll intestine. Secure with 30G needle.

7.  Fix with 4% PFA overnight at 4’C in 6 well plate

8.  Rinse with PBS for 10 minutes 3X

9.  Transfer into 15% sucrose in 1×PBS at 4°C until tissue sinks

10. Transfer into 30% sucrose in 1×PBS at 4°C until tissue
    sinks/overnight.

11. Transfer into 50/50 with 30%sucrose/OCT with GENTLE shaking

12. Transfer into OCT for 1-4hours

13. <span class="underline">Label the cryomold to mark the orientation
    of the tissue</span>.

14. Using forceps, lower the cryomold containing embedded tissue into
    the isopentane without fully submerging. Perform in chem hood to
    avoid fumes.
    
    1.  If using shallow mold, put on dry ice to freeze

15. Once frozen, place the cryomold on a piece of aluminum foil on dry
    ice for 30 min and let isopentane fumes evaporate

For colon/cecum (roll first post fix b/c it will get stiff)

1.  Flush colon with BD Fix/Perm solution. Remove as much of the
    mesentery to keep intestine straight. Holes in SI will make it
    difficult to flush. (BD fix/perm diluted with 1×PBS at a ratio of
    1:2)

2.  Remove hard feces by squeezing out of colon.

3.  Insert crochet needle. Wet needle prior to insertion.

4.  Place needle with SI into box and cover with 4% PFA

5.  Cut open longitudinally and wash with PBS

6.  Swiss roll ASAP.
    
    1.  \*\*Colon is a thicker tissue and becomes stiff fast, making it
        hard to roll post fix
    
    2.  30G needle will not puncture colon, use 27G

7.  Fix overnight at 4’C in 6 well plate

8.  Rinse with PBS for 10 minutes 3X

9.  Transfer into 15% sucrose in 1×PBS at 4°C until tissue sinks
