Western blot

*Sample Prep*

1\. Add 2-ME (under chemical fume hood) to lysates at 1:10 concentration into Laemmli sample buffer at time of sample prep.

2\. Boil samples in thermal cycler at 95C for 5 minutes

3\. Prepare and load 30 ug (10-100ug total protein per well) samples (refer to excel sheet from BCA) into wells and proceed with WB. Dilute as needed with sample buffer to ensure equal protein loading across wells. If you have extra empty wells, fill with buffer + loading buffer to ensure even running of the gel

Protein ladder in Coopersmith freezer- using 5-6 ul is sufficient

*SDS-PAGE*

4\. Run gel at 90V for the first 30 min. After the band has consolidated and made it through the stacking gel section, you can bump up gel to 150V for remaining time (20-40 min). Keep an eye on the gel.

5\. When blue bands reach black line on the gel cassette, stop electrophoresis chamber.

6\. Take out cassette and crack open using green key

7\. Image gel on the imager if interested in protein concentration (similar to Coomassie)

After turning machine on, navigate to Application 🡪 Protein gels 🡪Stain free gel

Exposure: faint bands

Expose gel by hitting camera in bottom right of screen

*Transfer*

8\. While gel is being exposed, prepare the transfer sandwich

9\. Pour transfer buffer in one container and 100% methanol in another

10\. Dip sandwich pads into transfer buffer and place one stack at bottom of transfer sandwich in the Turboblot platform.

11\. Prepare the PVDF membrane next by soaking membrane in 100% methanol until membrane becomes clear (30s-1min) and then dip into transfer buffer before putting membrane onto the transfer stack.

\*Make sure there are no bubbles under the membrane

12\. Place gel on top of membrane and make sure there are no bubbles here either. Use roller to gently roll out any bubbles between membrane and gel.

\*Picture on sheet above Turbo transfer if you have questions about these steps\*

13\. Place top sandwich stack on top of the gel and close the cassette

\*Make sure the iron prongs in the back are present (sometimes they come off)

14\. Run the Turboblot on the mixed MW protocol (7 min) and select the number of gels you are transferring

*Membrane Blocking*

15\. During transfer, prepare 2.5% BSA TBS-T

16\. After transfer, place membrane (check to make sure ladder has transferred to the membrane) in a box to block for 1 hour in 2.5% BSA TBS-T

\*If membrane dries out, soak in methanol and then transfer buffer prior to putting it into the blocking solution

17\. After blocking, wash membrane 3x for 5 min each rocking at RT (one by Yvonne’s bench and chemical fume hood in Ford lab) with TBS-T

*Primary and Secondary Ab Incubations*

18\. Dilute primary abs according to manufacture instructions in 1% BSA-TBST and place on rocker at 4C overnight.

Use 1:500 – 1:1000 p.AKT antibody (CST, 4060S) in 1% BSA

19\. The next morning, wash membrane 3x for 5 min each rocking at RT (one by Yvonne’s bench and chemical fume hood in Ford lab) with TBS-T

20\. Dilute secondary abs according to manufacture instructions in 1% BSA-TBST and place on rocker at RT for 1 hour

Use 1:5000 goat anti-rabbit (abcam, ab205718) in 1% BSA

21\. At end of incubation, wash membrane 3x for 5 min each rocking at RT

*Imaging*

22\. During last wash, bring membranes to Coopersmith lab and use the ECL reagents to image

23\. Make an ECL substrate mix of 1:1 with the clear and brown bottles (to the left of the electrophoresis chamber)

\*Can use Clarity max instead of Clarity if you want brighter bands

24\. Drain membrane well and add ECL reagent and tilt the box back and forth for 1 min

25\. During 1 min, turn on imager

26\. After turning machine on, navigate to Application 🡪 Blots 🡪Chemiluminescence

Optimal exposure (if nothing shows up you can expose for longer amounts of time)

Expose gel by hitting camera in bottom right of screen

27\. Export the membrane image using a flash drive and quantify using Biorad software if desired.
