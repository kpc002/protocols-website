Peptide dose response curve: PMEL/GP100

Spleen processing

1.  Sac one mouse and take spleen. Place in conical tubes with 5mL PBS.

2.  Process spleen through 70um strainers, wash with 5mL PBS, spin down.

3.  Resuspend spleen in 5mL PBS, push through 100um filter and wash with addition 5mL PBS. Take aliquot to count.

4.  Resuspend in 5 ml.

5.  Spin down spleens. Pour off supernatant and resuspend in desired volume for 2 million cells per well.

Peptide stim

6.  Make up R10 and Golgi plug solution

7.  Partition off some of the R10 + golgi plug for unstim controls.

8.  Partition off some for PMA/iono conditions and hgp100 conditions
    
      - PMA/IONO master mix:
        
          - 1:500 dilution of PMA and ionomycin (500x cell stim cocktail from Thermofisher)
    
      - hgp100 reconstituted in H2O (0.25 mM stock)

> 7\. For hgp100 dose response, do 3 replicates for each dose. The doses are: 0.01 uM, 0.1 uM, 1 uM, 10 uM, 100 uM. Take out media the amount that I will add for each. For example, if going to add 6.4 ul to dose 1 of peptide, take out 6.4 ul of media prior to account for that addition to have true calculation.

9.  After making the peptide stocks, plate cells at 2e6 cells/well.

10. Spin, and flick plate.

11. Add 200uL of each master mix containing different peptide doses to appropriate wells. Mix well.

12. Incubate cells at 37 for 4 hours

Flow surface stain

1.  After the incubation, spin cells and wash 1x with FACS

2.  Resuspend in surface stain master mix in FACS

  - CD4- BUV496 (1:200)

  - CD44- APC-Cy7 (1:400)

  - CD8- BUV737 (1:200)

  - Thy1.1- BV711 (1:100)

  - Thy1.2—PerCP (1:100)

  - CD69—BV650 (1:100)

3.  Incubate at 4C for 30 minutes. Make comps during this time\!

4.  Wash cells with 1X with 200uL FACS.

5.  If doing surface staining only, resuspend simple surface stain samples in 100uL, transfer to microtubes and flow.

*Intracellular cytokine stain*

1.  Spin cells down, flick off supernatant.

2.  Wash 1x with FACS.

3.  Resuspend cells in 100uL of FoxP3 fix/perm buffer (if going to combine TF and ICS)

4.  Incubate at 4C for 20 minutes.

5.  Wash cells 2x with 150ul of 1x Perm buffer (dilute from 10x with diH2O).

6.  Resuspend cells in 50uL of intracellular stain 1:100 in perm buffer (same as perm/wash)
    
      - TNF—PE-Cy7
    
      - IFNg—A700
    
      - IL-2—BV421
    
      - Ki67—PE

7.  Incubate at 4C for 30 minutes.

8.  Wash cells 2x, once with 1x Perm buffer, and 1x with FACS wash.

9.  Resuspend cells in 120uL FACS wash, flow.
