**Reporter-gene transduction of P14 into LCMV Armstrong infected mice for tissue embedding**

**CD8 Selection and activation (GFP, tdTomato, mCherry, Ametrine plasmids)**

**Day 1**

1.  Dilute GAH IgG antibody 1:30 in sterile PBS

2.  Aliquot 550 ul/ well in a 6 well plate. Distribute antibody + PBS across the wells

3.  Incubate plate at 37C for 2 hours

4.  Harvest spleen from P14 mice and homogenize over 70 um filter

5.  Centrifuge at 1600 RPM for 4 min and dump

6.  Resuspend pellet in 1-2 ml ACK and incubate at room temp for 5-6 min

7.  Neutralize with 3-4 ml MACS

8.  Count cells
    
    1.  125 million cells

9.  Centrifuge at 1600 RPM for 4 min and aspirate supernatant

10. Add 40 ul MACS buffer for every 10 million cells
    
    1.  500 ul

11. Add 10 ul CD8 antibody biotin cocktail for every 10 million cells
    
    1.  125 ul antibody cocktail

12. Mix well

13. Incubate at 4C for 5 min

14. Add 30 ul MACS buffer for every 10 million cells
    
    1.  375 ul buffer

15. Add 20 ul biotin micro beads for every 10 million cells
    
    1.  250 ul beads

16. Mix well

17. Incubate at 4C for 10 min

18. Rinse LS column by adding 3 mls of MACS buffer when incubation step in step 17 has a few minutes left

19. Apply single cell suspension to column (avoid getting on edge of column)

20. If less than 500 ul, complete to 500 ul before adding to column

21. Collect flow through as this represents the unlabeled CD8 fraction (biotin antibodies label all other cells besides CD8 in this negative selection kit)

22. Wash column with 3 mls of MACS and combine with eluent in step 22

23. Count cells in this fraction- these are your CD8\!
    
    1.  35 million cells

24. Dilute cells to 2e6 cells/ml
    
    1.  17.5 mls

25. Wash GAH-ab coated wells 2x with 1 ml TCM

26. Plate 2e6 cells in 1 ml TCM

27. Dilute anti-CD3 and anti-CD28 antibodies 1:500 in TCM

28. Stock concentration of anti-CD3 and anti-CD28 is 1 mg/ml, final should be 1 ug/ml (adding 2 ug/ml because diluted 2x)

29. Add 1 ml CD3/28 antibody mix to each well, pipette to mix

30. Incubate at 37C for 24 hours

**Day 2**

**Retroviral transduction of P14**

1.  Prewarm centrifuge to 37C

2.  Thaw virus in water bath

3.  Make polybrene mix (70x). For 100 ul of viral solution use:
    
      - 8 ul polybrene
    
      - 10 ul BME
    
      - 82 ul OptiMEM

4.  Combine virus + polybrene mix. For 140 ul virus, add 2 ul polybrene mix (e.g. 2 ml virus, add 28.5 ul polybrene mix)

5.  Transfer media from 6 well into 15 ml conicals, keep warm and separate between P14 backgrounds

6.  Add virus mix to activated T cells

7.  Spin at 37C for 1 hr, 2000RPM

8.  Incubate plate at 37C 1-4 hours

9.  Carefully remove virus mix

10. Spin down old media from step 5 and add same amount of fresh TCM (total volume is 4 mls per well in the end)

11. Incubate for 24 hours at 37C

**Day 3**

1.  Perform sort
    
      - Use the following gating strategy to calculate number of P14 to adoptively transfer

<!-- end list -->

  - Singlets Lymphocytes CD8/VA2 CD8/fluorescent marker
    
      - Can check for transduction efficiency using CD8/GFP/Ametrine for single AT but do not only use transduced cells in your calculation

  - Singlets Lymphocytes CD8 CD8/GFP/Ametrine Thy1.1/Thy1.2 for co-AT

<!-- end list -->

2.  Count cells after sort
    
      - GFP- 1.31e6 cells/ml, 1.5 ml, 86%
    
      - mCherry- 1.02e6 cells/ml, 1.5 ml, 77%
    
      - tdtomato- 8.46e5 cells/ml, 1.5 ml, 67%
    
      - Ametrine- 1.11e6 cells/ml, 1.5 ml, 83%

3.  Dilute cells to 1e6/ml
    
      - GFP- add 450 ul
    
      - mCherry- add 130 ul
    
      - Ametrine- add 160 ul
    
      - Tdtomato- add nothing

4.  Transfer 5e5 cells to mice via IV

5.  Dilute LCMV Armstrong from stock of 4.58e7 PFU/ml by making a 1:45.8 dilution, 2e5 PFU/mouse in 200 ul, keep on ice
    
      - For 15 mice, mix 66 ul stock with 2.934 ml of PBS

6.  Drop off samples in 249

7.  Change into BSL2 clothes

8.  Infect mice with diluted Armstrong with IP injection after mice have been given T cells via retroorbital or tail vein injection (\~1 hr)

9.  Use 5% in isoflurane chamber, keep line parallel in original position

**Day 7 sac and spleen harvest for preparation for FFPE embedding for Xenium**

  

Materials: 

  - 6 well plate, 1.5mL epi, 15 mL conical 

  - Pre-chilled 1x PBS 

  - Ice 

  - Cold 10% NBF ([Sigma](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/ht501128) [Aldrich](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/ht501128) [HT501128](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/ht501128)) 

  - Scissors, forceps 

<!-- end list -->

1.  Set shaker incubator to 4 degrees 

2.  Prep 10 mls of 10% NBF in each tube for spleen

3.  Prep 3 mls of ACK lysis in each tube for blood

4.  Add cold 1x PBS to 6-well plates (one well/spleen) on ice to preserve tissues after harvesting 

5.  Put PBS and 70% EtOH in fridge for Day 2 

6.  Bleed mice under iso and collect blood in ACK lysis buffer
    
      - Surface stain mix
        
        1.  CD8b- BV786
        
        2.  CD45.1- BUV805
        
        3.  CD45.2- PE-Cy7
        
        4.  Keep BV510, FITC, PE, PE-CF594 open for fluorescent dyes

7.  Put spleen in cold PBS in 6 well plate

8.  After spleen is rinsed, add spleen to 10 ml of cold 10% NBF while still in mouse room

9.  Incubate for 24 hours in shaker incubator (set to 4 degrees) 

**Day 1 post fixation/tissue harvest**

Materials: 

  - Pre-chilled 70% ethanol 

  - Pre-chilled PBS 

<!-- end list -->

1.  Remove tissue from NBF and wash with cold PBS twice 

2.  After spleen is rinsed, add spleen to 10 ml of cold 70% EtOH while still in mouse room

3.  Incubate for 24 hours in shaker incubator (set to 4 degrees) 

4.  Can put tissue in cassette in a 50 ml conical or just in a conical with 10 mls of 70% EtOH. Before sending for embedding, make sure sample has been in 70% EtOH for at least 24 hours.
