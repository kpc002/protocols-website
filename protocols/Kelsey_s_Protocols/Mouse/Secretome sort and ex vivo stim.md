**Secretome ex vivo sort and stim**

**Day -4, -2**

**Thaw and culture cancer cell line**

1.  Start cells in a T75 and can cut 1:5-1:8 for two passages before injecting into mice

| **Cell line** | **Media** | **Location Path**           |

**Day -1**

**P14 transfer**

1.  Harvest spleen from P14 mouse. Use two spleens since performing transfer for tumor + Armstrong experiments

2.  Homogenize spleen over 70 um filter

3.  Centrifuge at 1600 RPM for 4 min and dump

4.  Resuspend pellet in 1-2 ml ACK and incubate at room temp for 5-6 min

5.  Neutralize with 3-4 ml of media

6.  Take aliquot for flow

7.  Wash aliquot 1x with FACS

8.  Stain aliquot with surface stain master mix for 20 min at 4C
    
      - CD8b- BV510
    
      - CD45.1- BV786
    
      - CD45.2- FITC
    
      - VA2- PE

9.  While aliquots are staining, count cells
    
      - 165 million cells from two spleens, 10 mls, 78% viability

10. At end of stain, wash cells 2x with FACS

11. Take to flow

12. Take lymphocytes then CD8b/Va2 and calculate what percentage of CD8b+Va2+ of cells
    
      - 31% CD8b+VA2+ of (singlets lymphocytes) gate
    
      - 51.1 million P14, 165 million cells total
    
      - 0.31 \* 165 = 51.1 million P14 total, 10 mls so 5.1e6 P14/ml
    
      - Divide cells for B16 experiments (1e6/200 ul/mouse) and LCMV-Arm (5e4/200 ul/mouse). Use 30e6 P14 for B16 and 1e6 for LCMV. Take 5.88 mls for B16 and resuspend in 6 mls. Take 196 ul for LCMV and resuspend in 4 mls.

13. For LCMV-Armstrong injections, calculate cell counts to reach 2.5e5 cells/ml and inject 50k cells in 200 ul per mouse via RO injection. For cancer cell inoculation, calculate cell counts to reach 5e6 cells/ml and inject 1e6 cells in 200 ul per mouse via RO injection.

14. When injecting, remember to go at a 45-60 degree angle and go to the tear duct and angle behind the eye. You usually start to angle the needle down which does not work- remember to go in at an angle\!

**Day 0**

**Inoculate mice with cancer cells**

1.  Trypsin and count cells

2.  Resuspend cells at 5e6/ml in cold PBS

3.  Filter cells over a 100 um filter

4.  Count cells

5.  Add PBS to get to 3.5e6 cells/ml

6.  Bring cancer cells on ice to mouse room

7.  Shave mice

8.  Inject 500k cells in 100 ul into mice subcutaneously- use other flank if PBS bleeds out

**Day 14 and 21 post P14 transfer**

**Day before takedown**

  - Comps day before in fridge

  - Make phenol-free TCM

  - RP5

  - Make 1x percoll stock, 44% percoll, and 67% percoll
    
      - 1x Percoll stock: 450 ml Percoll, 50 ml 10x DPBS w/o Ca Mg
    
      - 44% Percoll: 44 ml 1x Percoll, 56 ml RPMI 1640. Store at 4C overnight, bring to RT day of
    
      - 67% Percoll: 67 ml 1x Percoll, 33 ml 1x DPBS w/o Ca Mg. Store at 4C overnight, bring to RT day of

  - Collagenase I solution

**Morning of takedown**

  - Add collagenase day of to collagenase I solution

  - Take out buffers to get to RT

  - Filter and then coat tubes with FBS and incubate at 37C

  - Coat GAH plate

  - Turn on shaker and put temp at 37C

  - Get ice

**Coating plate for T cell ex vivo stim**

1.  Dilute GAH IgG antibody 1:30 in sterile PBS (3 mls PBS, 100 ul GAH)

2.  Aliquot 100 ul/ well in a flat bottom 96 well plate. Distribute antibody + PBS across the wells

3.  Do not add GAH to unstim wells

4.  Incubate plate at 37C for 2 hours

**Tissue/organ harvest**

1.  Harvest the spleen, draining lymph node, and tumor from tumor challenged mice and the spleen from LCMV-Armstrong infected mice into RP5

**dLN Processing**

1.  Homogenize lymph nodes over 70 um filter

2.  Centrifuge at 1600 RPM for 4 min and dump

3.  Proceed to enrichment

**Count and enrich T cells (use EasyEights EasySep Magnets in 225)**

1.  Resuspend lymph nodes in 250 ul of RP5

2.  Used 1 ml for spleens, 500 ul for tumor, and 250 ul for dLN samples. Adjust Fc block, antibody cocktail, and beads accordingly.\*\*

3.  Add 20 ul of Fc block per ml of sample in a polystyrene round bottom 5 ml FACS tube

4.  \*\*Instead of using StemCell cocktail, use homemade negative selection kit with biotin-labeled antibodies (anti-NK1.1, MHC II, CD4, B220, Ter119) as the Stemcell kit sometimes negatively selects out the activated CD8 T cells through Gr-1 and/or other negative selection antibodies

5.  Make a master mix of 1:200 concentration of each depleting antibody from the homemade isolation kit in RP5
    
    1.  Don’t add anti-Gr-1 (Ly6c) or CD44 biotin-labeled antibodies because both expressed by activated CD8s

6.  Add 50 ul master mix per ml of sample

7.  Mix and incubate at RT for 10 min rocking

8.  Vortex Rapidspheres for \~30 seconds

9.  Add spheres at 100 ul per ml of sample

10. Mix and incubate at RT for 5 min rocking

11. Top samples off with RP5 to get to 2.5 mls and place tube without lid onto EasyEights magnet

12. Let tube sit on magnet at RT for 5 min

13. Then aspirate media from tube while tube is still adhered to magnet rack to avoid biotin-bead labeled non-CD8s

14. Take 50 ul aliquot of each sample and pool in new 5 ml tube for L/D FMO

15. Spin all tubes at 1600 RPM for 4 min

16. Wash 1x with PBS not FACS

17. Proceed to cell staining

**Stain cells**

1.  Resuspend cells in 0.5 ml of APC-Cy7 L/D stain, resuspended at 1:1000 in PBS
    
      - 12 samples, 6 mls, 6 ul of dye

2.  Stain cells at 4C for 10 min

3.  Wash cells 2x with FACS, pause between 1<sup>st</sup> and 2<sup>nd</sup> wash to take aliquot of each sample for FMOs

4.  Prior to spinning cells down at 2<sup>nd</sup> time, take 50 ul of each aliquot for CD8b and CD45.1 FMOs

5.  Spin all tubes (samples and FMOs)

6.  Resuspend cells in 500 ul FACS + surface stain, at 1:100 with antibodies in 5 ml opaque tubes
    
      - CD8b- BV510 (1:100)
    
      - CD45.1- BV786 (1:100)
    
      - CD45.2- FITC (1:100)

7.  Stain cells at 4C for 20 min

8.  Wash 1x with FACS

9.  Resuspend samples in 500 ul of phenol-free TCM if LCMV Armstrong spleen or B16 spleen, 250 ul if tumor, or draining lymph node

10. Sort Live CD8b+CD45.1+CD45.2+ population into phenol-free TCM

**Remember to bring to sort:**

  - 5 ml opaque collection tubes already coated with FBS with 1 ml of phenol-free TCM

  - FMOs

  - Bring extra phenol-free TCM and sterile tips

  - Cooler with samples

**  
Tumor tissue dissociation**

1.  Dump RP5 from tumor tubes

2.  Use scissors and chop tumor into small pieces inside 50ml falcon turned on its side (should look like mush).

3.  Add 10ml collagenase solution (see recipe below), washing tissue down the side of the tube.

4.  Incubate for 30 min at 37C in shaking incubator.

5.  Pour through cell strainer into a new 50mL conical, mash through 70 um cell strainer, and add 10 ml RP5 to wash cells down.

6.  Put tubes on ice while processing other tumors.

7.  Before spinning, add 2 ml 1x ACK to each tube, and vortex each tube for 30 seconds to further disassociate lymphocytes from tumor cells.

8.  Spin down at 1600RPM for 4 min.

**Percoll**

1.  Make sure you see a pellet, then pour supernatant out.

2.  Resuspend each sample in 5 ml 44% percoll solution, make sure pellet is well resuspended, and transfer to a 15ml round bottom tube (bacteria culturing tube).

3.  Place glass pasteur pipette in conical and add 2 ml 67% percoll solution to top of pipette to make a bottom layer. Don’t disturb after adding\!

4.  Spin at 21C at 2000RPM for 20 min, set acceleration to 3 and brake to 0

5.  Check for an interphase between the two layers and transfer it to 15 mL conical (usually about 2 ml).

6.  Fill to top with RP5 then spin at 1600RPM for 4 min.

**T cell stimulation**

1.  After sort, count cells

2.  After sort, spin tubes down at 1600 RPM for 4 min

3.  Aspirate supernatant

4.  Resuspend cells in 100 ul (50 ul for unstim and 50 ul for experimental well). The tube will already have 50 ul of dead volume so just add 50 ul of TCM

5.  Wash GAH-ab coated wells 2x with 100 ul TCM

6.  Dilute anti-CD3 and anti-CD28 antibodies 1:500 (3 ul in 1.5 mls) in TCM, adding 50 ul per well to 50 ul already in the well

7.  Stock concentration of anti-CD3 and anti-CD28 is 1 mg/ml, final should be 1 ug/ml (adding 2 ug/ml because diluted 2x)

8.  Add 50 ul CD3/28 antibody mix to each well, pipette to mix

9.  Add 50 ul of TCM only to unstimulated controls

10. Incubate at 37C for 4 hours

**T cell supernatant collection**

1.  Transfer cells from flat bottom plate to round bottom plate

2.  Spin at 1500 RPM for 5 min

3.  Aspirate supernatant, leaving cells for flow staining or fixation for bulk RNAseq

4.  Make labels for tubes and remember to go by column and not by row\!

5.  Move supernatant to matrix tubes and store at -80, save in 50 ul aliquots.

**T cell collection for bulk RNA-seq**

1.  After collecting supernatant, check how much volume you have in well. Take 1 ul of cells at a concentration of 500-750 cells/ul and move to PCR tubes

\*Use the same volume for all samples in whatever medium you are using (as long as it is low EDTA)

2.  Add 4.75 ul/ sample of freshly made reaction buffer to the 1 ul of cells (has to be used within two hours of making)
    
      - H20 nuclease and RNAse free – 169 ul
    
      - 10x lysis buffer – 19 ul
    
      - RNAse inhibitor – 2 ul

> Makes 190 ul, enough for 40 samples. Scale up by 2x for more samples (338 ul water, 38 ul lysis buffer, 4 ul inhibitor)

3.  Tap to mix, spin briefly to bring it to the bottom, and snap-freeze on dry ice.

4.  Label tubes

5.  Keep at -80C 

**Recipes**

## 1x Percoll stock

  - 450ml Percoll (GE HEALTHCARE \#17-0891-01)

  - 50ml 10x DPBS w/o Ca Mg (HYCLONE \#SH30378.03)

## 44% Percoll

  - 44ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

  - 56ml RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE \#SH30027.02)

  - store @4°C in fridge overnight

  - DOE: bring to RT

## 67% Percoll

  - 67ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

  - 33ml 1x DPBS w/o Ca Mg (HYCLONE \#SH30028.03)

  - store @4°C in fridge overnight

  - DOE: bring to RT

## Collagenase solution

  - 2ml HG (recipe below)

  - 200ul 1M MgCl2 (AMBION \#AM9530G)

  - 200ul 1M CaCl2 (HYCLONE \#SH30289.01)

  - 10ml BGS

  - QS to 200ml w/RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE \#SH30027.02)

  - store @4°C in fridge overnight

  - DOE: add 20,000 U Type I Collagenase (WORTHINGTON \#4197)

  - DOE: warm to 37°C in water bath

## 100x HG stock solution (sterile)

  - 59.58g HEPES (FISHER \#BP310-500)

  - 14.6g L-glutamine B-grade (CALBIOCHEM \#3521)

  - 500ml (QS to line) RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE \#SH30027.02)

  - use stir bar to mix

  - pH to 7.5 (will take 10-20ml 5N NaOH)

  - filter sterile

  - make 2.5ml aliquots

  - store at -20°C in freezer
