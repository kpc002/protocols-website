**Treg isolation**

**Spleen/LN processing:**

1.  Sac WT B6 mice**.** Process LN and spleen from each mouse through 70 and 40 um.

2.  Count cells.

**Treg MACS isolation**

Magnetic labeling of non-CD4+ cells + labeling of CD25+ cells

\*Work fast and keep all reagents on ice

MACS enrichment of Tregs/Tconvs: primary labeling reagent will leave all non-CD4+ T cells bound on the LD column. Secondary labeling reagent will label CD25+ cells so flow through from LD column = Tconvs on column = Tregs.

7.  Mix well and incubate for an additional 15 min in the fridge

**Magnetic separation: Depletion of non-CD4+ T cells**

8.  Place LD column in the magnet, rinse with 3 ml of MACS buffer

9.  Apply cell suspension onto the column

10. Collect the cells that pass through – THIS IS THE CD4+ T CELL POPULATION

11. Wash the column with 2x1mL of MACS buffer and collect

12. Take off column from magnet and wash 2x with 1 ml to get the non-CD4 T cell population

**Magnetic Labeling of CD25+ T cells**

13. Centrifuge suspension for 5 min at 300xg, aspirate completely

14. Resuspend cell pellet in 90uL of buffer

15. Add 10uL of anti-PE microbeads

16. Mix well and incubate for 15 min in the fridge

17. Bring to 500uL volume in MACS buffer

18. Magnetic Separation: positive selection of CD4+CD25+ Regulatory T cells

19. Place MS column in the magnetic field, rinse with 500 uL of MACS buffer

20. Apply cells to column: FT IS TCONVS –SAVE

21. Wash with 2x 500 uL MACS buffer – SAVE

22. Remove column from separator and add place on a collection tube

23. Add 1mL of buffer to column and immediately flush out beads by firmly pressing on column with plunger (2x)

24. Confirm Treg isolation by flow
