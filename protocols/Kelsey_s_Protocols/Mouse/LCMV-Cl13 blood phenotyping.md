**Phenotyping time course of P14 from blood of LCMV-Cl13 infected mice**

**Day 4, 8, 12, 15, 30 post infection**

*Blood draws*

1.  > Bleed mice with 5mm lances and collect about 50 ul of blood in green microvette blood collection tubes

2.  > Transfer blood immediately into 3mL lysis buffer in FACS tube

3.  > NOTE: if this is not how you typically bleed, that’s fine. Just make sure that all the days are the same and that the same amount of blood is collected from each animal.

4.  > Spin down the blood in lysis buffer at 1500rpm for 5min.

5.  > Dump lysis buffer

6.  > Add additional 3mL lysis buffer, spin down at 1500rpm for 5 min.

7.  > Dump lysis buffer, vortex.

8.  > Transfer samples into labelled 96 well round bottom plate.

9.  > Incubate for 20min.

10. > Wash 2x with FACS buffer

11. > Spin down and resuspend in surface stain (SS) master mix in 50uL.

\*Make an FMO control for CD45.1,PD1, TIM3, TIGIT, CD69, Slamf6, CD39
