*Blood Collection & Serum Prep*

\*Keep everything on ice\*

1.  Perform cardiac puncture or eye blood draw to get at least 250 ul of blood per mouse

2.  Collect blood into Eppendorf tubes to collect blood- keep on ICE

3.  Take 50 ul of blood for blood SS panel, use rest for plaque assay

4.  Use cool tabletop centrifuge (Badell lab) to 4C, spin blood samples in table top centrifuge in Badell lab at 13.3 RPM for 5 minutes

5.  Aspirate supernatant, avoiding the pellet, and place in new Eppendorf tubes (on ice)

6.  If under 300 ul, could add to 96 well plate

7.  Freeze at -80 until plaque assay

*Kidney Collection*

\*Keep everything on ice\*

8.  Day before sac, weigh 14 ml snap cap conicals on scale after adding 1 ml of cold RPMI + 10% FBS

9.  Collect kidney and put into snap cap conicals, keeping everything on ICE

10. Weigh kidney after on the scale and record

11. Freeze at -80 until plaque assay
