**Day -1**

*P14 adoptive transfer*

1.  Harvest spleen/dLN or take from blood

2.  Stain with surface master mix for 30 min at 4C
    
      - CD8- PO
    
      - CD4- PB
    
      - CD45.1- APC
    
      - CD45.2- PE-Cy7
    
      - VA2- FITC
    
      - VB8- PE

3.  Count cells from other 20 ul aliquot during surface stain
    
      - WT P14 Live= 1:2 dilution = \_\_\_
        
        1.  TOTAL cells: \_\_\_
    
      - Fgl2<sup>-/-</sup> P14 Live= 1:2 dilution = \_\_\_
        
        1.  TOTAL cells: \_\_\_

4.  Wash 2x with FACS for flow

5.  Flow for % (gate on 20-30% lymphocytes, then CD8 by CD45.1 to find % double positive, and then from that population gate on Va2 by Vb8). \*\*\*NOTE: P14 are Va2+Vb8+
    
      - P14: 27.6% lymphocyte gate
        
        1.  CD45.1+CD8+ = \_\_\_
        
        2.  Va2+Vb8+= \_\_\_
        
        3.  Final percentage= (.i \* ii/100)= \_\_\_%
        
        4.  Final cells/mL= (iii\* total cell count)= \_\_\_ P14 cells/mL.
        
        5.  Resuspend to get 8000 cells/ml (250 ul of 2000 cells per mouse)

6.  Spin down cells and resuspend to final volume (mL) PBS.

7.  Giving **250 ul** (2,000 cells) per mouse, so need concentration to be: 8,000 cells/ml.

**Day 0**

*LCMV-Clone 13 infection*

1.  **Use double gloves and keep all on ice\! Inject virus within 30-45 minutes**

2.  Thaw vial in waterbath

3.  Add 250 ul of RPMI + 1% FBS to 1 ml of vial (25e6 PFU/vial).

4.  Use one vial of 25e6 PFU per cage because assuming 20% loss

5.  Inject 250 ul IV into each recipient so final dose of virus is 4e6 PFU
