**In vitro OT-I splenocyte stim**

**Day 0**

1.  Sac OT-I Thy1.1 and place spleen in 50mL conicals with 5mL PBS

2.  Process with 70um strainer, wash with 5mL PBS and spin down.

3.  Resuspend cells in 5mL, filter thru 40um strainer, wash with 5mL PBS.

4.  Count cells
    
    1.  WT: 1.08e7, 95% 1.08 ml
    
    2.  WT: 9.24e6, 97% 924 ul
    
    3.  WT:, 9.43e6, 98% 943 ul
    
    4.  KO: 1.3e7, 96% 1.3 ml
    
    5.  KO: 1.05e7, 98% 1.05 ml
    
    6.  KO: 1.01e7, 97% 1.01 ml

5.  Spin cells down, resuspend in R10 to 50x10^6 cells/mL
    
    1.  WT:\_\_\_\_\_mL
    
    2.  KO: \_\_\_\_ mL

6.  Dilute anti-CD28, IL-2, and SIIN
    
    1.  Anti-CD28 (1mg/mL stock, 1ug/mL final)
        
        1.  1:100 working solution
            
            1.  1:100: If need 7 total ml of R10, add 70 ul of CD28
            
            2.  Then from 7 ml of R10, CD28 will be diluted 1:10 into sample
            
            3.  Volume of anti-CD28 added in step 1: 70ul
    
    2.  IL-2- \*if R\&D, need to reconstitute in PBS FBS --(0.2mg/mL =200,000ng/mL stock; 10ng/mL final)
        
        1.  1:2000 working solution
            
            1.  1:200 (5uL into 1 mL R10)
            
            2.  1:10: If need 7 ml of R10, add 700 ul of IL-2
            
            3.  Then from 8 ml of R10, IL-2 will be diluted 1:10 into sample
            
            4.  Volume of IL-2 added in step 2: 700 ul
    
    3.  SIINFEKL (3000uM stock, 0.1uM final) – see Tmem generation protocol
        
        1.  1:3000 working solution
            
            1.  1:3000 (2.33uL into 7 ml R10)
            
            2.  Then from 7 ml of R10, SIINFEKL will be diluted 1:10 into sample
            
            3.  Volume of SIINFEKL added in step 2: 2.33 ul
    
    4.  Take all the volumes of the anti-CD28, SIIN, and IL-2 and add them to final volume 5ml. Correct with R10 to 5ml. (70 ul of CD28, 700 ul IL-2, and 2.33 ul SIIN added=0.772 ml, add 6.228 mls of R10 to master mix to get 7 ml total.

7.  Add 800uL of R10 to each well of 24-well plate

8.  Add 100uL of OT-I cells (5 x 10^6 cells/well), and 100uL of master mix with anti-CD28, IL-2, and SIIN to each well.
    
    1.  Each well is 1 mL total, and all *working* solutions were diluted 1:10

9.  Incubate for 3 days (added 1 ml R10 to surrounding wells for edge effect)

**Day 2**

Fgl2 from LSBio (LS-G12255 / 146848)

1.  Take each well and put into tube

2.  Wash at 1500 RPM for 5 min

3.  Resuspend cells in media according (peptide (10 nM this time), peptide + Fgl2, Fgl2)

4.  Treat wells with Fgl2. (12 wells each, 1.2 mls, need 1.5 mls to be safe of each dilution)
