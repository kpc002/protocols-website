**B16 Cancer cell culture and injection**

**Day -4, -2**

**Thaw and culture cancer cell line**

1.  Start cells in a T75 and can cut 1:5-1:8 for two passages before injecting into mice

**Day 0**

**Inoculate mice with cancer cells**

1.  Trypsin and count cells

2.  Resuspend cells at 5e6/ml in cold PBS

3.  Filter cells over a 100 um filter

4.  Count cells

5.  Add PBS to get to 2.5e6 cells/ml

6.  Bring cancer cells on ice to mouse room

7.  Shave mice

8.  Inject 250k B16-gp33 cells in 100 ul into mice subcutaneously- use other flank if PBS bleeds out.
