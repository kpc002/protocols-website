**Bone marrow chimeras**

You will need \~20 million bone marrow cells to inject for each recipient mouse. Plan out congenic backgrounds so you will be able to distinguish recipient from donor mouse cells.

**Day -1**

1.  Make low pH, antibiotic water for irradiated mice.
    
    1.  Add 2L of tap water to autoclaveable bottle.
    
    2.  Add \~0.48mL HCl and shake.
    
    3.  Dip pH indicator strip into bottle and adjust pH to be between 2.5 and 3.5 (pH \~3.0)
    
    4.  Autoclave on liquid setting for at least 20min.
    
    5.  Add 25mg (1.25mL of bottle in 4°) Polymyxin B and 1g Neomycin to the water after its cooled to touch.
    
    6.  Store water with foil in refrigerator.

**Day 0**

1.  Irradiate bone marrow recipients two times with 4.5 Gy SIX HOURS APART. Each time, this is what the settings should be: 225 kV 17.7 mA config radpls pie, shelf 1, 4.5 Gy, 2 min 46 seconds

\*This dose was optimized to yield 100% mouse survival and 85-95% chimerism.

Bone marrow recipient animals

1.  Pepboy CD45.1+ Thy1.2+

Bone marrow donor animals

2.  Fgl2-/- HSCs, CD45.2+ Thy1.2+

3.  WT HSCs, CD45.2+ Thy1.2+

1.  Euthanize the donor mice: 8 Fgl2-/- mice, 10 B6 mice

2.  Spray the donor mice with 70 % ethanol and cut the abdominal skin, peel down over legs.

3.  Remove the feet and harvest femur and tibia, scraping away muscle.

4.  Transfer the legs to cold PBS.

5.  Remove excess flesh from the leg bone using scissors and forceps and gauze.

6.  Cut off the ends of the bones, and place the bones in PBS

7.  Once all bones are collected, transfer to petri dishes with 10mL media.

8.  Flush the bones using a 23-gauge needle for the tibia and 21 gauge for the femur with a 10 ml syringe. Refilling with petri dish PBS or fresh PBS as needed.

9.  Once finished (bones should look white with no red in them), make a single-cell suspension of all the marrow and filter using a 70um filter. Wash each side of the petri dish 1-2x with PBS. Use another 50 ml conical for the washes. Count both tubes.

10. Pool cells from all mice and count cells.
    
    1.  
11. Spin and filter once more. Resuspend to 40 million cells/ml (20 million cells per 500 ul per mouse adoptive transfer)

12. Inject 500ul of bone marrow cell suspension i.v through penile vein. If using retroorbital, resuspend so there are 20 million cells per 250 ul per mouse adoptive transfer.

Day 0 – Week 8

13. Allow mice to recover in sterile cages in sterile room with 150 mls antibiotic water

14. Replace antibiotic water 2x a week.

15. Six weeks post transplantation, recipient mice can be analyzed for the presence of donor-derived cells via flow cytometry.

**Week 6 & 8**

1.  Bleed mice with 5mm lances, fill heparinized capillaries and dump in 5mL FACS tube with 4mL lysis buffer.

2.  Spin tubes down, aspirate supernatant and add 4mL lysis buffer and resuspend.

3.  Spin tubes down again, aspirate supernatant and transfer all volume to 96-well round bottom plate.

4.  Add to 300uL with lysis buffer, and transfer 40uL to isotype well (combine so one isotype well per cage).

5.  Spin plate down, flick.

6.  Resuspend in 50uL antibody master mix.

  - CD4- PB (1:100)

  - CD8- PO (1:100)

  - CD3- BV786 (1:200)

  - CD45.1- FITC (1:100)

  - CD45.2- PE-Cy7 (1:100)

  - CD19- PerCP (1:100)

  - Fgl2- PE (1:300)

  - CD11b- APC (1:200)

7.  Incubate at 4C for 30 minutes.

8.  Wash 1x with 200uL FACS buffer.

9.  Resuspend in 75uL FACS buffer, transfer to microtubes, flow.

NOTE: All BM transferred (including CD8 T cells) should be CD45.2; none should be CD45.1, in both WT and KO recipients.
