**Isolating P14 from blood of donor mice for adoptive transfer**

*P14 adoptive transfer*

1.  Use 5-6 week old mice and collect blood from P14 mice.

2.  Add 1 mL of Na citrate (4%) in 5 mL Snap-cap tube

3.  Bleed the mice around 5-10 drops of blood

4.  After bleeding, add 1 mL of RPMI 1% FBS in the tube containing the blood

5.  After that add 1 mL of Histopaque 1083 (Mouse) via Pasteur pipette (Try not to disturb the tube)

6.  Centrifuge at 2000 RPM for 20 mins (NO Brakes)

7.  Isolate the PBMCs and transfer them to a new tube containing 1 mL RPMI 1% FBS

8.  Centrifuge at 1850 RPM for 5 mins

9.  After centrifugation, discard the supernatant, and resuspend the pellet in 1 mL RPMI 1% FBS

10. Count the cells

11. Take a 20 ul aliquot for flow and a 20 ul aliquot for counting

12. Add 1 ml of FACS to aliquot for flow and spin

13. Stain with surface master mix for 30 min at 4C
    
      - CD8- PO
    
      - CD4- PB
    
      - CD45.1- APC
    
      - CD45.2- PE-Cy7
    
      - VA2- FITC
    
      - VB8- PE

14. Count cells from other 20 ul aliquot during surface stain

        1.  TOTAL cells: \_\_\_
    
      - Fgl2<sup>-/-</sup> P14 Live= 1:2 dilution = \_\_\_
        
        1.  TOTAL cells: \_\_\_

15. Wash 2x with FACS for flow

16. Flow for % (gate on 20-30% lymphocytes, then CD8 by CD45.1 to find % double positive, and then from that population gate on Va2 by Vb8). \*\*\*NOTE: P14 are Va2+Vb8+
    
      - P14: 27.6% lymphocyte gate
        
        1.  CD45.1+CD8+ = \_\_\_
        
        2.  Va2+Vb8+= \_\_\_
        
        3.  Final percentage= (.i \* ii/100)= \_\_\_%
        
        4.  Final cells/mL= (iii\* total cell count)= \_\_\_ P14 cells/mL.
        
        5.  Resuspend to get 8000 cells/ml (250 ul of 2000 cells per mouse)

17. Spin down cells and resuspend to final volume (mL) PBS.

18. Giving **250 ul** (2,000 cells) per mouse, so need concentration to be: 8,000 cells/ml.

**Day 0**

*LCMV-Clone 13 infection*

1.  **Use double gloves and keep all on ice\! Inject virus within 30-45 minutes**

2.  Thaw vial in waterbath

3.  Add 250 ul of RPMI + 1% FBS to 1 ml of vial (25e6 PFU/vial).

4.  Use one vial of 25e6 PFU per cage because assuming 20% loss

5.  Inject 250 ul IV into each recipient so final dose of virus is 4e6 pfu

**Day 15**

*Reconstitute peptide*

Dissolve gp33-41 in DMSO at a concentration

1 mg in 0.4 ug/ml

If reconstitute in 250 ul. 4 mg/ml, 4 ug/ul

Dilute 1:10 in media 0.4 ug/ul.

Add 1 ul (at 0.4 ug/ul) for every 1 ml of media. (i.e. 5 mls at 0.4 ug/ml. Need 5 ul)

*Sac*

*Blood Collection & Serum Prep*

\*Keep everything on ice\*

1.  Perform cardiac puncture or eye blood draw to get at least 250 ul of blood per mouse

2.  Collect blood into Eppendorf tubes to collect blood- keep on ICE

3.  Take 50 ul of blood for blood SS panel, use rest for plaque assay

4.  Use cool tabletop centrifuge (Badell lab) to 4C, spin blood samples in table top centrifuge in Badell lab at 13.3 RPM for 5 minutes

5.  Aspirate supernatant, avoiding the pellet, and place in new Eppendorf tubes (on ice)

6.  If under 300 ul, could add to 96 well plate

7.  Freeze at -80 until plaque assay

*Kidney Collection*

\*Keep everything on ice\*

8.  Day before sac, weigh 14 ml snap cap conicals on scale after adding 1 ml of cold RPMI + 10% FBS

9.  Collect kidney and put into snap cap conicals, keeping everything on ICE

10. Weigh kidney after on the scale and record

11. Freeze at -80 until plaque assay

*Lymph node and spleen collection*

12. Take all lymph nodes and spleen

13. Process through 70 and 40 um filters

14. Flow stain

\* Used 250 ul of spleen from 5 mls reconstituted for each staining well.
