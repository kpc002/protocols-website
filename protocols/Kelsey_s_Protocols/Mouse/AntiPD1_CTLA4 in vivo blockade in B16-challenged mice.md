**Anti-PD1/CTLA4 in vivo blockade in B16-challenged mice**

**Day -4, -2**

**Thaw and culture cancer cell line**

1.  Start cells in a T75 and can cut 1:5-1:8 for two passages before injecting into mice

|               |           |                             |
| ------------- | --------- | --------------------------- |
| **Cell line** | **Media** | **Location Path**           |
| B16-gp33      | DMEM/F12  | LAB220/LN2-5/CR-50/BX-01622 |

**Day 0**

**Inoculate mice with cancer cells**

1.  Trypsin and count cells

2.  Resuspend cells at 5e6/ml in cold PBS

3.  Filter cells over a 100 um filter

4.  Count cells

5.  Add PBS to get to 3.5e6 cells/ml

6.  Bring cancer cells on ice to mouse room

7.  Shave mice

8.  Inject 500k cells in 100 ul into mice subcutaneously- use other flank if PBS bleeds out

**Day 7**

**P14 transfer**

1.  Harvest four spleens from P14 mice.

2.  Homogenize spleen over 70 um filter

3.  Centrifuge at 1600 RPM for 4 min and dump

4.  Resuspend pellet in 1-2 ml ACK and incubate at room temp for 5-6 min

5.  Neutralize with 3-4 ml of media

6.  Take aliquot for flow

7.  Wash aliquot 1x with FACS

8.  Stain aliquot with surface stain master mix for 20 min at 4C
    
      - CD8b- BV510
    
      - CD45.1- BV786
    
      - CD45.2- FITC
    
      - VA2- PE

9.  While aliquots are staining, count cells
    
      - 7.74e6/ml, 40 ml, 75% viability
    
      - 309 million cells from four spleens, 75% viability

10. At end of stain, wash cells 2x with FACS

11. Take to flow

12. Take lymphocytes 🡪 then frequency of Va2 in CD8b/CD45.1+ gate and calculate what percentage of CD8b+Va2+ of cells
    
      - 0.95 (Va2)\* 35% CD8b+CD45.1+ of (singlets 🡪lymphocytes) gate
    
      - 0.33 \* 309e6 = 101e6 P14

13. For cancer cell inoculation, calculate cell counts to reach 5e6 cells/ml and inject 1e6 cells in 200 ul per mouse via RO injection.

14. When injecting, remember to go at a 45-60 degree angle and go to the tear duct and angle behind the eye. You usually start to angle the needle down which does not work- remember to go in at an angle\!

15. Measure tumors

**Day 9, 13, 17, 21**

2.  Treat mice with 100 ug of the following antibody groups:
    
      - Anti-PD1
    
      - Anti-PD1 isotype (Rat IgG2a)
    
      - Anti-CTLA4
    
      - Anti-CTLA4 isotype (Syrian Hamster IgG)
    
      - Anti-PD1 + Anti-CTLA4
    
      - Dual isotype

3.  Antibodies are resuspended in -80 at 1 mg/ml, so take 2 vials of each since I need to treat about 7 mice per group and each antibody is needed 2x.

4.  Inject intraperitoneally 100 ul of 1 mg/ml (100 ug)

5.  Measure tumor and record on IACUC sheet
