**Human TIL stimulation**

***Cell preparation***

1.  If using fresh TIL, see tumor digestion protocol

2.  If using frozen TIL, see frozen PBMC protocol

3.  After these steps, continue to protocol here

 

***Anti-CD3/28 Stimulation***

1.  Prepare CD3/CD28 beads (Invitrogen Dynabeads Human T-Activator CD3/CD28) 
    
      - Vortex 30 seconds 
    
      - 200 μL beads into 1.5 mL conical 
    
      - Add 1 mL FACS to wash
    
      - Vortex 30 seconds 
    
      - Place on magnet 1-2 minute 
    
      - Remove supernatant 
    
      - Add 200 μL of R10 to 200 μL beads (this can be done at varying amounts; resulting concentration beads:FACS is 1:1) 

2.  Plate 1e6 cells per well in a 24 well plate

3.  Supplement to 1 ml of R10 in each well

4.  Add 25 ul of equilibrated CD3/CD28 beads to each well per 1 ml of R10. Mix thoroughly. \*Make sure to include an unstimulated (no CD3/28 beads) control\*

5.  Incubate in 37°C incubator

6.  At desired harvest time (48 hours later in this expt) remove cells from incubator 

7.  Add Golgi Plug to each well of sample at 1 ul per ml of media. Add 5 ul of CD107a-APC antibody here as well because CD107a is internalized and needs to be added during stim for optimal staining. Pipette well up and down a few times and place back into the incubator

8.  Incubate again at 37C for 4 hours

9.  At end of 4 hours, collect cells from well by gently pipetting. Wash well 2x with 1 ml of R10 and add to cells.

10. Place cells + R10 on magnet 1-2 min to remove beads 

11. Aspirate supernatant (including cells; leaving beads behind) and transfer to clean flow tubes 

12. Wash flow tubes still containing beads 2x with 500 μL R10 each time, letting sit for 1-2 min 

13. Transfer R10 used to wash beads to new flow tubes containing cells

14. Discard tubes containing CD3/CD28 beads

15. Spin flow tubes with cells at 1500 RPM for 5 min 

16. Wash 1x with FACS

17. Transfer cells from flow tubes to 96 well plate

***Surface Stain***

1.  Make surface antibody master mix in FACS. Make FMOs at this time.

2.  Resuspend cells in 100 ul of surface stain master mix

3.  Mix cells + master mix by light vortex.

4.  Incubate cells for 20 min at room temp in the dark.

5.  Wash 1x with FACS

***L/D Stain***

1.  Wash 1x with PBS

2.  Resuspend samples in 1 ml of PBS + L/D stain (made from -20 stock at a 1:1000 dilution)

3.  Incubate cells for 10 min at room temp in the dark

4.  Wash 2x with FACS

***Cell Fixation and Intracellular TF and cytokine staining***

1.  Fix cells using 100 ul fixation buffer diluted 1:4 (i.e. 5 mls concentrate, 15 mls diluent) from eBioscience Foxp3 Transcription Factor Staining kit and resuspend thoroughly.

2.  Incubate in dark at 4C for 20 minutes.

3.  Wash with 100 ul 1x perm wash (from 10x stock).

4.  Make intracellular antibody master mix in perm wash, using 1:100 for all antibodies, except Ki67, use 10 ul per sample of 1e6 cells (i.e. add 60 ul if doing three samples). Make FMOs at this time for: Granzyme B, IL2, TNF, IFNg, and Ki67.

> *\*Hopefully unstimulated control can be used for these markers in the future, but unstimulated TILs may have residual staining for these markers, making the gate hard to make. If we see no differences between FMOs and unstim controls in these markers, we can definitely just use unstim control going forward\!\**

5.  Resuspend cells in 50 μl intracellular stain and stain at RT or 30 minutes covered in foil.

6.  Wash with 100 ul 1x P/W buffer, spin.

7.  Rewash with 100 ul FACS, resuspend and run flow
