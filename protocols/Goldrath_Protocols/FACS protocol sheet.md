# FACS protocol sheet

<span class="underline"></span>

1.  You will need about 10<sup>5</sup> – 5x10<sup>6</sup> cells for FACS staining (optimal = 10<sup>6</sup>) per sample

2.  Spin the cells down
    
    1.  96 well plates or 15 ml conical tubes – 1800 rpm for 5 minutes
    
    2.  Eppendorf tubes – 30 seconds at max

3.  You must first FC block (If you are staining for FC, proceed to next step)
    
    1.  Make master mix of 50 μl FACS buffer/sample plus 0.25 μl FC block/sample (e.g., if you have 8 samples, mix 400 μl FACS buffer + 2 μl FC block)
    
    2.  Resuspend your cell pellet in 50 μl master mix and vortex
    
    3.  Incubate on ice for about 5 minutes

4.  Biotin labeling (If you are NOT biotin labeling, proceed to next step)
    
    1.  Add 0.5 μl biotin antibody per sample
    
    2.  Incubate on ice for 15 minutes
    
    3.  Wash with \~1.5 ml FACS buffer and vortex
    
    4.  Spin
    
    5.  Aspirate and discard supernatant

5.  Add antibodies to your sample in 50 μl

6.  Wash
    
    1.  96 well plates – 2 times in 250 μl FACS buffer
    
    2.  Eppendorf or FACS tubes – 1 time in 1.5 to 3 ml FACS buffer

7.  Fix
    
    1.  Add 50-200 μl Fix buffer
    
    2.  Store at 4°C
