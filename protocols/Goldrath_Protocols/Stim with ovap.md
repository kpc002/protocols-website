# Stimulation of cells with Ova peptide in 12 well plate

<span class="underline"></span>

1.  Cells should be in 1 ml of media per well on a 12 well plate

2.  Use 100 nM Ova peptide for stimulation (stock is 1 mM)

3.  So….need a 1:10,000 dilution, but it is needed at 2X since 1 ml Ova peptide will be added to 1 ml of cells….therefore a 1:5000 is really needed.

4.  Do a serial dilution

> 1<sup>st</sup> do a 1:500  10 μl Ovap into 5 ml RPMI
> 
> 2<sup>nd</sup> do a 1:10  400 μl Ovap dilution into 3.6 ml RPMI (for a total of 4 ml)

5.  Add 1 ml of the 2<sup>nd</sup> dilution into each of your wells so that there is now a total of 2 ml volume in each well
