RNA Isolation:

Before Beginning:

  - Remember to wipe down the bench with a wet paper towel to remove some of the dust

  - Make sure filter tips are being used

  - Pour eppi tubes that will be used onto a paper towel.

  - Get a new aliquot of chloroform.

Homogenization and isolation:

1.  > Spin down sample and aspirate off any remaining buffer.

2.  > Flick tube to loosen pellet.

3.  > Homogenize samples in 0.5 mL of TRIzol Reagent (1 mL per 50-100mg of sample). Mix well until there is no longer any clumps.

4.  > Incubate the homogenized samples for 5 min. at room temperature.

5.  > Add 1uL of glycoBlue as a carrier.

6.  > Add 100 uL of chloroform for every 0.5 mL of TRIzol and shake vigorously by hand for 15 seconds.

7.  > Incubate them at room temp. for 2-3 minutes

8.  > Centrifuge the samples at 11,000 rpm (12,000 x g) for 15 minutes at 2 to 8 °C.

9.  > Transfer the aqueous layer (the clear one on top) into a new tube. Be careful not to disturb the lower phenol-chloroform layer (red) or the interphase (white).

RNA Precipitation:

1.  Precipitate RNA by adding 250 uL of isopropyl alcohol and mixing well.

2.  Incubate samples at room temp. for 10 minutes.

3.  Centrifuge samples at 11,000 rpm (12,000 x g) for 10 minutes at 2 to 8 °C

4.  There should be a blue pellet visible on the bottom of the tube.

RNA Wash and Resuspension:

1.  Aspirate off supernatent.

2.  Wash pellet by adding 1 mL of cold 70% ethanol.

3.  Mix sample by vortexing and centrifuge at 9,000 rpm for 5 minutes at 2-8 °C.

4.  Aspirate all but about 100 uL of the ethanol, and then spin again as above for 2 minutes.

5.  Use a pipet to remove the rest of the EtOH.

6.  Turn tube on its side and allow to dry on a kimwipe.

7.  Resuspend the pellet in 10 uL of water and put onto ice or in the freezer.
