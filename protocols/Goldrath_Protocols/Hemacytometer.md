Hemacytometer

  - Spin cell down at 300g for \~5 min

  - Resuspend in appropriate volume of media to count

  - Mix: 2ul cell + 18 ul of Tryphan blue (dilution factor: 10)

  - Wait \~ 5 min to stain

  - Inject 10ul to hemacytometer

  - Count
