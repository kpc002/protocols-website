# In vitro IFN-gamma:

1)  > Plate 4x10<sup>6</sup> cells in 24 well dish, 100μL of 4x10<sup>7</sup> cells/mL.

2)  > Add 1 mL of RP10 or OVAp at 10nM (10<sup>-8</sup>M) in RP10.

> Peptide stock at 1mM, do 1:1000, 10μL in 10mLs = 10<sup>-6</sup>M
> 
> Then do 1:100, 120μL in 12mLs = 10<sup>-8</sup>M
> 
> Keep at stock of the 10<sup>-6</sup>M in freezer diluted in PBS.

3)  > Incubate at 37 for 3 hours, add 1μL golgi stop and incubate for 3 more hours.

4)  > Harvest into eppendorf tubes, spin down, block with 5mL of Fc block for 5 minutes, add primary antibodies in 25μL/sample.

5)  > Incubate 20 minutes on ice.

6)  > Wash 1x 1.5 mLs.

7)  > Add 250μL fix/perm, incubate 30 mintues.

8)  > Wash twice in 1 mL of cytowash/perm (diluted in H2O as per Pharmingen).

9)  > Resuspend in 25μL/sample diluted Fc block (5μL/sample Fc and 20μL/sample cytowash/perm, 5 minutes room temp.

10) > Add anti-IFNgamm PE in 25μL/sample cytowash/perm. 30 minutes at 4C, wash 1x 1.5mLs cytowash/perm.

11) > Resuspend in 500μL/sample Fixative.
