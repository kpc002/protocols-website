**RNA Isolation**: ***DATE***:­­­­­­\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

<span class="underline">Before Beginning</span>:

  - wipe down bench w/ WET paper towel to remove dust

  - make sure FILTERED tips are being used

  - Pour eppi tubes that will be used onto paper towel, NEVER USE FINGERS to reach in and grab\!

  - Get a new Aliquot of Chloroform into Eppi tube.

<span class="underline">Isolation</span>:

  - add 1ul of glycoBlue as a carrier

  - add 100ul of Cholorform for every 0.5mL of Trizol and shake (not vortex) vigorously for 15 seconds.

  - Incubate them at RT for 2-3 minutes.

  - Spin @ 11,000rpm for 15’ at 2-8<sup>o</sup>C

  - Transfer aqueous layer (clear top layer) into a new tube.

<span class="underline">RNA Precipitation</span>:

  - add 250ul isopropyl alcohol and mix well

  - incubate samples at RT for 10’

  - spin at 11,000 rpm for 10’ at 2-8<sup>o</sup>C

  - there should be a blue pellet visible at bottom

<span class="underline">RNA wash and Resuspension</span>:

  - aspirate off supernatant

  - wash pellet w/ 1ml cold 70% Ethanol

  - vortex and spin at 9,000rpm for 5’ at 2-8<sup>o</sup>C

  - aspirate all but 100ul of ethanol and then repeat steps until ALL ETOH is removed.

  - Lay on Eppi tubes on side to allow further drying.

  - Resuspend the pellet in 10ul of DepC H<sub>2</sub>O and put on Ice for immediate use or -20<sup>o</sup>C freezer.

**DNase Treatment of RNA**:

RNA \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ 10 ul

10X DNase Buffer \_\_\_\_\_\_ 2.5 ul

DNase \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_0.5 ul

DepC H<sub>2</sub>O \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ \_\_\_ 12 ul

  - incubate at 37<sup>o</sup>C for 20’

  - vortex DNase inactivation reagent

  - add 5ul of DNase inactivation reagent/ tube

  - incubate at RT for 2’

  - centrifuge 1’ at 10,000 rpm

  - pipette up supernatant, do not disturb beads.
