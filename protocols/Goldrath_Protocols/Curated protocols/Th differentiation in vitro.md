*<span class="underline">Th cell differentiation in vitro protocol</span>*

<span class="underline">*Antibodies and reagents*</span>

Media is RPMI 1640 (for Th1 and Th2) or IMDM (for Th17) supplemented with 10% FBS, 0.5% penicillin/streptomycin, 2 mM L-glutamine, 1 mM sodium pyruvate, 10 mM Hepes, non-essential amino acids and 50 μM β-Mercaptoethanol.

Mouse specific anti-CD3 (2C11), anti-CD28 (PV1) and anti-IFNγ (R4/GA2) antibodies were from Bio Express (West Lebanon, NH, USA). Human IL-2 was from NCI Biological Resources Branch (Frederick, MD, USA). Mouse IL-4, IL-12 and IL23 and mouse-specific anti-IL-4 (11B11) antibody were from eBioscience. Mouse IL-6 and human TGF-β1 were from R\&D Systems.

<span class="underline">*Helper T cell (Th) differentiation*</span>

Coat a 24-well plate O/N @ 4 °C with 1 μg/ml anti-CD3 and 2 μg/ml anti-CD28 antibodies. This is Day -1\!

Prior to use wash once with sterile PBS.

Naïve T cells were isolated from mouse spleens and lymph nodes using the CD4<sup>+</sup>CD62L<sup>+</sup> MACS kit from Myltenyi Biotec (Bergisch Gladbach, Germany) and seeded into plates anti-CD3/anti-CD28 coated plates @ 10<sup>6</sup>/ml in 2ml/well. This is Day 0\!

Helper T cell differentiation was induced by the following reagents:

\- for Th1 in complete RPMI:

IL-2 (100 u/ml),

IL-12 (10 ng/ml) and

anti-IL-4 antibody (10 μg/ml);

\- for Th2 in complete RPMI:

IL-2 (100 u/ml),

IL-4 (10 ng/ml) and

anti-IFNγ antibody (10 μg/ml).

On Day 3, expand each well of Th2 into a well of a 6-well plate containing 2 more ml of fresh media with IL-2 (same as above). Pool 2 wells of Th1 into a well of a 6-well plate containing 2 more ml of fresh media with IL-2 (same as above).

Th1 and Th2 cells were analyzed on Day 5 or Day 7.

Th17 were grown in complete IMDM with IL-6 (20 ng/ml), IL-23 (10 ng/ml), TGF-β1 (0.3 ng/ml), anti-IL-4 antibody (10 μg/ml) and anti-IFNγ antibody (10 μg/ml).

Th17 cells were analyzed on Day 4 (no pooling or change of media necessary\!).

*<span class="underline">Th cell analysis</span>*

Cells can be analyzed before re-stimulation (“naïve” Th1, 2 or 17 cells) or after re-stim – the re-stim can be done with either anti-CD3 (can be coated on a plate (preffered) or added in solution; no anti-CD28 is necessary at this step\!) or with PMA (50 ng/ml) plus Ionomycin (1 μM), depending on which function is analyzed\!

Typically, PMA/I stim is used if cells are analyzed for intracellular cytokines for FACS (5h @ 37 °C stim – add Brefeldin A for entire duration of stim\! Follow intracellular cytokine FACS protocol afterwards\!), and anti-CD3 stim is used for cytokine analysis by ELISA (24 h @ °C stim).
