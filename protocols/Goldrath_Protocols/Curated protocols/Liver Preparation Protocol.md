Liver Preparation Protocol

1\. Chop livers into small pieces with a razor blade. Push liver pieces through a 70um filter into a 50ml conical tube using plenty of 5% BGS + PBS to wash through. Push through using the bottom of a syringe.

2\. Wash all liver cells twice in 40 mL PBS+5% BGS. Spin at 1600rpm for 8 minutes each time (centrifuge should be at room temp). The second washing step is actually worthwhile as it leads to better separation.

3\. Spin cells in 25 mL 37% Percoll at 1750rpm for 25 minutes. To make a Percoll working solution, add 8.5ml Percoll to 16.5ml PBS (25ml total).

4\. At this point the lymphocytes will be at the bottom of the conical with a layer of hepatocytes near the top of the gradient. You can aspirate off the hepatocytes at this point and proceed with the lymphocytes as normal.

5\. Ack the lymphocytes. I normally do this for 2-3 minutes, as the erythrocytes are hard to get rid of in the liver. I resuspend in 250ul 5% BGS in order to count the cells.

6\. Stain as normal.
