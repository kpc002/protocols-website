Stimulation of Peripheral T cells with anti-CD3 and CD28

COAT THE PLATE

  - Coat 24 well plate with goat anti-hamster Ig (IgG H+L Cat\#6061-01 Southern Biotech goat anti hamster))
    
      - Dilute 1:100 in carbonate buffer
    
      - Add 250ul/well (6 well plate use 1 ml)
    
      - Incubate at 37’ for 1 hour

  - Wash wells 1 time with 1ml HBSS

  - Add anti-CD3 (Clone\#145.2C11)
    
      - For suboptimal stimulation use 20-50ng/250ul in HBSS per well
    
      - For maximal stimulation use 200ng/250ul
    
      - Incubate 1 hour at 37’C

  - Wash wells 1 time with 1m RP10 (RPMI 10% FCS)

HARVEST CELLS

  - Harvest cells from lymph node and spleen in HBSS

  - Lyse red blood cells with 5 ml ACK \~2 min RT

  - Add 5 ml PBS

  - Spin

  - Wash with 5 ml PBS

  - Purify if necessary, otherwise resuspend in RP10 and count

ADD CELLS TO PLATE

  - Plate cells in wells 0.5-5 x 10<sup>6</sup>

  - Add 0.5 ml soluable anti-CD28 (Clone\#37.51) diluted in RP10 at 1:1000 final concentration

  - Incubate plate at 37’ C for desired amount of time

Carbonate Buffer/Coating buffer

0.1 M sodium carbonate, pH 9.5. 8.40 g NaHCO3, 3.56 g Na2CO3,

to 1.0 L; pH to 9.5. Freshly prepare or use within 7 d of preparation, stored at

2–8°C.
