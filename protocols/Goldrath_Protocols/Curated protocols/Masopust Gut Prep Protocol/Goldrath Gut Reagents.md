<span class="underline">10X HEPES BICARBONATE STOCK (STERILE):</span> makes 500ml

\-WILL NEED 100ml PER LITER OF CMF

\-10.5g Na-bicarbonate

\-11.9g HEPES

\-500ml ddH20

\-\>pH to 7.2 using pH meter (do not usually need to add anything

\-\>filter sterilize in hood using 0.2um pore bottle top filter-\>store @RT in dark; do not use after 30 days

<span class="underline">CMF SOLUTION:</span> makes 1L

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 150ml PER MOUSE

\-100ml **10x** HBSS w/o Na-bicarb. Ca Mg

\-100ml **10x** HEPES-bicarbonate (recipe above)

\-20ml Fetal Bovine Serum

\-780ml ddH20

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>DOE: transfer to ice bucket

<span class="underline">DTE SOLUTION:</span> makes 500ml

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 60ml PER MOUSE

\-460ml CMF (recipe above)

\-40ml Fetal Bovine Serum

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>DOE: add 77mg Dithioerythritol (Sigma 233152)

\-\>DOE: warm to 37 <sup>o</sup>C in water bath

<span class="underline">**100x** HG STOCK SOLUTION (STERILE):</span> makes 500ml

\-59.58g HEPES

\-14.6g L-glutamine B-grade

\-500ml (QS to line) RPMI 1640 1x

\-\>use stir bar to mix

\-\>pH to 7.5 (will take 10-20ml 5N NaOH)

\-\>make 10ml aliquots

\-\>store @20<sup>o</sup>C in freezer long-term

<span class="underline">COLLAGENASE SOLUTION:</span> makes 200ml

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 35ml PER MOUSE

\-2ml HG (recipe above)

\-200ul 1M MgCl<sub>2</sub>

\-200ul 1M CaCl<sub>2</sub>

\-10ml Fetal Bovine Serum

\-QS to 200ml w/RPMI 1640 1x

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>Day of experiment (DOE): add 20,000 U Type I Collagenase (WORTHINGTON)

\-\>DOE: warm to 37 <sup>o</sup>C in water bath

<span class="underline">1x PERCOLL STOCK (STERILE):</span> makes 500ml

\-450ml Percoll (Sigma P1644)

\-50ml **10x** DPBS w/o Ca Mg

\-\>keep sterile

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge long-term

\-\>if removed 50ml from original 500ml percoll bottle to make room for

DPBS, bring this to 1X too, do not discard

<span class="underline">44% PERCOLL:</span> makes 100ml

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 5ml PER TISSUE (IEL+LP) PER MOUSE

\-44ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

\-56ml RPMI 1640 1x

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>DOE: bring to RT

<span class="underline">67% PERCOLL:</span> makes 100ml

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 2ml PER TISSUE (IEL+LP) PER MOUSE

\-67ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

\-33ml 1x DPBS w/o Ca Mg

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>DOE: bring to RT
