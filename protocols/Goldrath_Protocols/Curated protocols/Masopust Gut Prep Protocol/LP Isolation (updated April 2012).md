 1. Add Collagenase to solution

 2. Bring reagent to appropriate temperature

\-Collagenase to 37<sup>o</sup>C (water bath)

\-Percolls to RT (bench)

 3. Add 50ml RPMI 1640; Invert to mix

 4. Cfg 15 sec @1600rpm + pour off RPMI (discard)

 5. Add 35ml Collagenase sol.; shake for 45-60 min @37<sup>o</sup>C

 6. Take out and let pieces settle

 7. Pour off supe. into new 50ml conicle (can use strainer)

\-THESE ARE YOUR LP LYMPHS\!

 8. Cfg supe. @4 <sup>o</sup>C for 5 min @1600rpm

 9. Discard supe. + resus pellet in 5 ml 44% Percoll; tfr to 15 ml

round-bottom tube

10. Underlay with 3ml of 67% Percoll

11. Cfg @RT for 20 min @2000rpm (NO BRAKE\!)

12. Remove lymphocyte interface into 15ml conicle

13. Qs to 15ml w/ RPMI/5%FBS or PBS

14. Cfg @4 <sup>o</sup>C for 10min @2000rpm (brake)

15. Pour off supe. (discard); resus pellet in RPMI/5%FBS

\-THESE ARE YOUR PURIFIED LP LYMPHS\!
