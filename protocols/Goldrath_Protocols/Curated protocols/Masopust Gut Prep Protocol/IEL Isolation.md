 1. Turn on shaker and add DTE to DTE solution

 2. Bring reagents to appropriate temperature

\-CMF on ice (ice bucket)

\-DTE sol to 37<sup>o</sup>C (water bath)

\-Percolls to RT (bench)

 3. Resect small intestine from mouse

 4. Cut out peyers patches

 5. Push out fecal content

 6. Cut longitudinally to flatten intestine

 7. Cut intestine into smaller pieces (laterally)

 8. Place pieces into 50ml conicle w/30ml CMF

 9. Let pieces settle; pour off supe. (discard)

10. Wash with another 30ml CMF; pour off supe. (discard)

11. Add 30ml DTE; shake for 30 min @37<sup>o</sup>C

12. Vortex 15 sec (funnel to bottom)

13. Let pieces settle; pour off supe. into clean 50ml conicle

\-THESE ARE YOUR IELs\!

14. Wash int. pieces with additional 30ml DTE sol

15. Vortex 15 sec and decant supe. into IEL tube

> \*if you are isolating cells from LP start this prep now by
> 
> adding EDTA

16. Spin down IEL; discard supe.

17. Resus. In 5ml 44% Percoll + tfr to 15 ml round bottom tube

18. Underlay 3ml of 67% Percoll w/ 5ml pipet

19. Cfg @RT for 20 min @2000rpm (NO BRAKE\!)

20. Remove lymphocyte interface into 15ml conicle

21. Qs to 15ml with RPMI/5%FBS or PBS

22. Cfg @4 <sup>o</sup>C for 10min @2000rpm (brake)

23. Pour off supe.; resus pellet in RPMI/5%FBS

\-THESE ARE YOUR PURIFIED IEL\!
