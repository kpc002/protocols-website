# cryosectioning

             Microscopy Core Gladstone Institutes Freezing Protocol
                        histology@gladstone.ucsf.edu

            Fix tissue for 8-24 hours. Perfusion gives a much better result
            than drop-fixing. Use 20x the volume of fixative to tissue
                            Brains - Use longer fixation



                            Place in 10% sucrose for 1 hr
Sucrose gradient allows
easier section cutting by
cryoprotecting tissue
                            Place in 20% sucrose for 1 hr
against ice crystal
formation

                              Place in 30% sucrose O/N

                                             Next Day
          Prepare freezing equipment at least 20 minutes before you need it.
      Place a beaker of isopentane (2-methyl butane) and surround with dry ice.
          Place dry ice into the beaker until 1.5cm from top and slowly pour
                isopentane over the dry ice in the beaker. (see photo)
                    Wait until boiling stops and top off if necessary



            Fill an appropriately sized cryomold with a decent amount of
                                 OCT and orient tissue
            (remember the initial cutting surface is the base of the mold)



                 Leave for 15mins . This helps prevent tissue curling
                      away from the OCT in section when cut



                             Freeze tissue by floating OCT
                            filled mold in the isopentation
                                   Do not submerge!


                       Store samples in a labeled plastic bag at
                               -80 until ready to section
