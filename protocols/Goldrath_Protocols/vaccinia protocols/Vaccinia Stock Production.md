Vaccinia Stock Production

1.  > Begin with 10 – 175 cm<sup>2</sup> flasks of confluent TK<sup>-</sup> cells. (They must be totally confluent)

2.  > Aspirate the media (DMEM) and infect each flask with 10 ml of balanced salt solution (BSS) with 0.1% BSA and 0.5-1.0x10<sup>7</sup> PFU of vaccinia virus. (Stock for the 10 ml is generally made by adding 0.5-1.0x10<sup>8</sup> PFU to 100 ml of BSS + 0.1% BSA.

3.  > Incubate for 2 hours at 37C. Rock flasks every 20 minutes.

4.  > Aspirate off BSS/0.1% BSA and virus.

5.  > Add 25-30 ml of DMEM with 7.5% fetal bovine serum.

6.  > Incubate for 48 hours. All cells should demonstrate marked CPE. If not, incubate until they do.

7.  > Collect supernatants from flasks and trypsinize (trypsin versene) any cells that still adhere. Pool cells and supernatants in two 250 ml centrifuge tubes. One wash of flasks with BSS/BSA may be needed. Pellet cells and discard the supernatant. Resuspend in 10mls of BSS/0.1% BSA.

8.  > Freeze thaw three times. Sonicate in cup horn sonicator at a setting of 5 for 2 minutes. Vortex well. Total virus yield will usually be about 1-5X10<sup>10</sup> pfu.

9.  > Dilute in BSS/0.1% BSA to 130 ml, aliquot in 1 ml samples and freeze at –85C.

Note: When making new recombinants, take 0.5 ml of final plaque purification and add 1-1.5 ml BSS/BSA. Infect a confluent 50 cm<sup>2</sup> flask of TK- cells for 2 hours. Incubate for 48 hours until CPE is demonstrated. Harvest and pellet virus. Resuspend in 5-6 ml of BSS/BSA. Infect one 175 cm2 flask of TK<sup>-</sup> with 5 ml of above. From the virus collected from one 175 cm<sup>2</sup> flask you can continue with stock production (10 flasks).
