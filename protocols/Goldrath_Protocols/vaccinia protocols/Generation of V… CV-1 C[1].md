Generation of Vaccinia Recombinants in CV-1 Cells

1.  Start with a confluent T-25 flask of CV-1 cells. Figure about 5 x 106. These cells are grown in DMEM with FCS, Splitting requires trypsin (1X concentration) with no special considerations. The cells grow somewhat slowly, so during heavy usage, 1:5 splits are appropriate. However, the cells can be reseeded quite sparsely for maintenance.

2.  Wash the cells 1X with DPBS. Infect the cells with normal WR vaccinia virus (CR19) at 0.05 pfu/cell or 2.5x105 pfu total in BSS/BSA. Incubate at 37C/9% CO2 for 2 hours rocking every 15-20 min. Note: I use 1ul of CR19, with titer \~1x108pfu/ml.

3.  30 min. before the end of infection, start transfection of DNA. We used to use Cellphect kit by Pharmacia, but I find that Lipofectamine 2000 or Fugene works about 5-10 X better. For LF2000, we use about 5ul miniprep DNA, 6ul LF2000. Note: Centrifuge the (nonsterile) DNA minipreps at high speed for 1 min. to pellet any infectious contaminants prior to pipetting.

4.  Aspirate off virus and add 3.5ml DMEM +7.5% FCS plus 3.5ul gentamycin (1:1000) (optional)

5.  Add the DNA/lipofectamine and incubate for 48 hours.

6.  Harvest the cells by trypsinization with versene. Pellet cells by centrifugation. Resusp. in 0.5 ml BSS/BSA and freeze thaw (dry ice/37C water bath) three times. Sonicate for 2 min. in a cup horn sonicator at maximal intensity. Store at –80C. Follow with three plaque purifications.
