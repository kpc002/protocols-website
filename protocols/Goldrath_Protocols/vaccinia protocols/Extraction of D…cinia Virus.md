Extraction of DNA from Purified Vaccinia Virus

1\. Measure OD260 of purified virus. (Separate protocol) 1 OD260=64ug/ml virus. DNA is equivalent to 4% of the virus mass. Therefore, 1 OD260 equals about 2.5ug DNA.

2\. Suspend virus at 50-200ug DNA/ml in 50mM Tris pH 7.8 in a volume of 1.2ml.

3\. Add to virus suspension:

0.1ml 1M Tris pH 7.8

0.1ml 10% SDS

0.2ml 60% sucrose (wt/vol)

0.4ml proteinase K (10mg/ml)

Incubate for 4 hours at 37C

4\. Carefully extract the DNA. The DNA is very sensitive to shearing.

a. 2X phenol (equilibrated in 50mM Tris pH 7.8)

b. 1x phenol:chloroform (1:1)

c. 1x chloroform

Mix gently by rolling or rocking tube. DO NOT SHAKE. Spin 10 min. at 3000 rpm between extractions. Remove aqueous phase by sucking up gently using pipet tip that has its end cut off.

5\. Add Sodium Acetate (pH 7.0 to 0.1M and then 2.5 volumes of ethanol. Mix gently and leave for several hours at -20C.

6\. Recover DNA by centrifugation. Wash twice in 95% ethanol and dry. Then dissolve in sterile double distilled water. Measure OD at 260 and 280.

Note: from crude stock use Rnase.

\-------

The above is from our lab protocol notebook. My own changes are as follows:

Don't bother measuring Ods. Do step 2 at 1/10 scale, for 200ul final volume (do duplicates if you're worried this won't give you enough DNA). Use sucrose and proteinase K powder, so that you can substitute virus stock for the volume of water they would otherwise take up. Protease K treatment is better at 50C. Extract once with 200ul phenol, once with 500ul chloroform is plenty. I add 16.5ul 3.5M NaAcetate pH 5.2. After thoroughly mixing ethanol, spin at room temperature, freezing is unnecessary. Washing once with 100% ethanol is plenty. Resuspend final DNA in 50ul TE (10mM tris, 1mM EDTA, pH 7). RNAse is not necessary. PCR with 5ul DNA in 50ul reaction volume.
