**Cell culture: TH1/ TH2 / TH17**

\-Prepare 24 well plate with anti-CD3: 1hr incubation with anti-hamster IgG followed by 1hr incubation with anti-CD3.

> Coat 24 well plate with goat anti-hamster Ig (IgG H+L Cat\#6061-01 Southern Biotech goat anti hamster))

  - Dilute 1:100 in carbonate buffer

  - Add 250ul/well (6 well plate use 1 ml)

  - Incubate at 37’ for 1 hour

> Add anti-CD3 (Clone\#145.2C11)

  - For suboptimal stimulation use 20-50ng/250ul in HBSS per well

  - For maximal stimulation use 200ng/250ul

  - Incubate 1 hour at 37’C

\-Harvest spleens and ACK cells. MACS enrich for CD4+ T cells. Count cells before and after MACS enrichment.

\-Add CD4<sup>+</sup> cells 0.5 x 10<sup>6</sup>/ml per well in a 24 well plate. Cells should be in 1ml T cell media for culturing (RPMI 10%).

\-Add anti-CD28 (1 ug/ml)----- use 1:1000 dilution from the stock we have

\-Add hIL2 (10 U/ml) but only to TH1 or TH2 cells NOT TH17 cells.

\-Add:

For TH1 cells:

IL-12 (5 ng/ml, 1:2000)

Anti-IL-4 (10 ug/ml, 1:100)

For TH2 cells:

> IL-4 (10 ug/ml, 1:100)

Anti-IFNγ (10 ug/ml, 1:100)

For TH17 cells:

TGFb (1ng/ml, 1:2000)

IL-6 (10 ng/ml, 1:10000)

> Anti-IL-4 (10 ug/ml, 1:100)
> 
> Anti-IFNγ (10 ug/ml, 1:100)

\- Culture for 5 days (TH1 or TH2 cells) or 3 days for TH17 cells.

\-Wash/ Purify cells

Harvest cells, treating them very gently wash them in T cell media, count them and re-plate them in 24 well plate in equal numbers per condition.

\-Restimulate cells

Using PMA and ionomycin, restimulate the cells before intracellular staining. Also Golgi stop must be added to culture (1:1000) to prevent cytokines being transported out of the cells.

PMA: stock is 1mg/ml from Hedrick lab.

Use at 50ng/ml.

Need to dilute 20,000x

First dilute 1:200 by adding 2ul to 398ul media

Then dilute 1:100 by adding 10ul to 990ul media in which T cells have been placed.

Ionomycin: stock is 1000x from Hedrick lab.

Add 1ul to 1000ul media in which T cells have been placed.

\-Assay: assay after 4-5 hours stim with PMA and ionomycin.

Intracellular staining

IFN-γ (Th1) \[got about 11% (1<sup>st</sup> experiment)\]

IL-4 (Th2) \[got about 30% (1<sup>st</sup> experiment)\]

IL-17 (TH17)

ELISA: take 48hr supernatants (do not add Golgi stop if you want to do this)

IFN-γ (Th1)

IL-4 (Th2)
