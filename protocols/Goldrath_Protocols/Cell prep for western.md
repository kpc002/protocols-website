# Cell prep for Western

<span class="underline"></span>

1.  Harvest tissue into RPMI

2.  Grind tissue through cell strainer in RPMI buffer in a petri dish

3.  Transfer to a 15 ml conical

4.  Spin at 1800 rpm for 5 minutes

5.  Aspirate and discard supernatant

6.  Resuspend pellet in 1 ml Ack and incubate at RT 1 minute

7.  Add \~13 ml RPMI

8.  Spin at 1800 rpm for 5 minutes

9.  Aspirate and discard supernatant

10. Resuspend pellet in desired volume of RPMI

11. Count cells

> Minimum requirement of cells for
> 
> Western 2 x 10<sup>6</sup>
> 
> RNA 2 x 10<sup>5</sup>
> 
> Flow 2 x 10<sup>5</sup>

12. Cells for Western – spin in eppendorf tube at max for 30 seconds – aspirate and discard supernatant – save pellet at -80°C

13. Cells for RNA – spin at max for 30 seconds – aspirate and discard most of the supernatant – resuspend pellet in remaining supernatant – add 500 μl Trizol – store at -20°C

14. Cells for Flow – leave cells in RPMI and store at 4°C
