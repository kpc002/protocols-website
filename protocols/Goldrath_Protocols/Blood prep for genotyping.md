# Preparation for blood - genotyping

<span class="underline"></span>

1.  Bleed animal into 50 μl EDTA in a microfuge tube

2.  If the blood is fresh, spin at max for 30 seconds. If you are not going to use it that day, put it at 4°C for one day.

3.  If the blood is a day old, there should already be a pellet, so you don’t need to spin it down.

4.  Aspirate and discard supernatant

5.  Add 0.5 ml Ack and vortex

6.  Incubate at room temp for 5 minutes

7.  Spin at max for 30 seconds

8.  Aspirate and discard supernatant

9.  Wash with \~1 ml FACS buffer and vortex

10. Spin at max for 30 seconds

11. Aspirate and discard supernatant

If you are not FC blocking go to step 15.

If you are not FC blocking and are not biotin labeling, go to step 20.

12. Make master mix of 50 μl FACS buffer/sample plus 0.25 μl FC block/sample (e.g., if you have 8 samples, mix 400 μl FACS buffer + 2 μl FC block)

13. Add 50 μl of your master mix to each sample to resuspend the pellet and vortex

14. Incubate on ice for at least 5 minutes

If you are NOT biotin labeling your samples, proceed to step 20.

15. Add 0.5 μl of your biotin antibody per sample

16. Incubate on ice for 15 minutes

17. Wash with \~1.5 ml FACS buffer and vortex

18. Spin at max for 30 seconds

19. Aspirate and discard supernatant

20. Make master mix of 50 μl FACS buffer/sample

21. Add 50 μl of your master mix to each sample to resuspend the pellet and vortex

22. Incubate on ice for 10 minutes in the dark

23. Wash in \~1.5 ml FACS buffer and vortex

24. Spin at max for 30 seconds

25. Aspirate and discard supernatant

26. Add 50 μl FACS fix buffer and vortex

27. Put sample on ice or at 4°C until you run flow cytometry
