**Lymphocyte Tissue Preparation from subcutaneous tumors**

Miguel Reina-Campos 10/2019

1.  Inject 3ug of iv antibody (ie 15ul CD8a-APC-Cy7 + 185ul PBS). 3-4 min after, sack the mice (cd).

2.  Open skin by incision in the lower part of the back and use hands to expose tumor

3.  Resect tumor and place in P24 well on ice, if tumor is large enough can be split into two:
    
    1.  Dry well for histology (frozen – OCT) is needed
    
    2.  RP5 media for flow

4.  Collect draining lymph node by exposing it through the fat and remove it with tweezers

5.  Collect spleen

6.  Weight tumors before starting tissue prep

Start with tumor processing:

1.  Mince tissue with scissors in a 50 ml falcon. Alternatively chop down with a razor blade in a P60 plate on ice.

2.  Add Collagenase-I digestion in 5ml in the 50 ml falcon and incubate @37C for 30 min

Collagenase-I recipe – Prepare under the hood

\-2ml HG (aliquoted in the -20 fridge) – Do not refreeze aliquot

\-200ul 1M MgCl2 (J’s bench)

\-200ul 1M CaCl2 (J’s bench)

\-10ml Fetal Bovine Serum

\-QS to 200ml w/RPMI 1640 1x

100x HG STOCK SOLUTION (STERILE): makes 500ml

59.58g HEPES

14.6g L-glutamine B-grade

500ml (QS to line) RPMI 1640 1x

use stir bar to mix

pH to 7.5 (will take 10-20ml 5N NaOH)

make 10ml aliquots

store @20oC in freezer long-term

While tumors are dissociating, process spleens and lymph nodes and leave them on ice

3.  Filter digested tumors using a 70 um filter (reuse filter for same samples), rinse it with cold PBS. Use syringe plunger to dissociate tumor chunks through the filter.

4.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm 4 min

5.  After centrifugation and filtration of tumors, resuspend pellet in 5ml 44% Percoll using a 10 ml pipette.

<span class="underline">1x PERCOLL STOCK (STERILE):</span> makes 500ml

450ml Percoll (Sigma P1644)

50ml **10x** DPBS w/o Ca Mg

keep sterile

store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge long-term

if removed 50ml from original 500ml percoll bottle to make room for DPBS, bring this to 1X too, do not discard.

<span class="underline">44% PERCOLL:</span> makes 100ml

WILL NEED AT LEAST 5ml PER TISSUE (IEL+LP) PER MOUSE

44ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

56ml RPMI 1640 1x

store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

DOE: bring to RT

<span class="underline">67% PERCOLL:</span> makes 100ml

WILL NEED AT LEAST 2ml PER TISSUE (IEL+LP) PER MOUSE

67ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

33ml 1x DPBS w/o Ca Mg

store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

DOE: bring to RT

6.  Add the 44% percoll-cell suspension into a 15ml Falcon tube

7.  Insert a glass Pasteur pipette and deliver 67% Percoll to the bottom of the tube.

8.  Spin 20 min at 2,000 rpm (NO brake) and collect interphase with Pasteur pipette into 15ml falcon tubes, fill up with RP5 and spin down at 2,000 rpm for 8min.

9.  Re-suspend pellet in desired counting volume and continue with your staining.
