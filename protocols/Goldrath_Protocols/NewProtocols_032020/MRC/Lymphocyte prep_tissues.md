**Harvesting Kidney, Lung, Liver for Lymphoycte isolation**

\- Inject 3ug of iv antibody (ie 15ul CD8Ae780 + 185ul PBS). 3-4 min after, sack the mice and harvest organs of interest

\- Harvest kidneys and Lungs and place in PBS 5% FBS or complete media and on ice

**Kidneys:**

\- Make one cut with scissors to increase their surface area and cut 2x with tissue chopper (tissue shouldn’t be dry under chopper\!)

\- Transfer cut kidneys into pyrex conical containing a magnetic stirr bar and add 25ml of enzyme media (complete RPMI+0.5mg/ml Collagenase D)

\- Stirr for 45min at 37C

\- Filter through 70um cell strainer and mash with syringe plunger. Spin down, remove supernatant and re-suspend pellet in 5ml 44% Percoll\*\* (transfer into 15ml round-bottom Falcon conical). 

\- With a pasteur pipette go all the way to the bottom of the tube and slowly add 2.5ml of 67% Percoll

\- Carefully place inside centrifuge and spin down at 2000rpm for 20min at 25C (NO brake\!). 

\- Collect interphase and place in 15ml conical. Fill up tube up to 15ml with RPMI5% FBS and spin down for 8min at 2000rpm.

\- Remove supernatant and re-suspend pellet in suitable volume (1ml of complete RPMI) for counting.

\- Proceed with counting/staining

\*\* To make 100% Percoll: mix 9 parts of Percoll stock with 1 part of 10X PBS.

   - To make 44% or 67% Percoll, use 100% Percoll as explained above and dilute in PBS+5%FBS

**Lungs:**

\- Cut x2 w tissue chopper and place in 25ml of enzyme media (complete RPMI+0.5mg/ml Collagenase D) in pyrex conical + stirr bar

\- Stirr for 45min at 37C

\- Filter through 70um cell strainer and mash with syringe plunger. Spin down, remove supernatant and re-suspend in 2ml ACK lysis buffer. Incubate at RT for 5min.

\- Wash and spin down

\- Re-suspend in counting volume (2ml). Count and continue with staining. 

**Liver:**

\- Cut liver with tissue chopper

\- Press through 70um cell strainer

\- Re-suspend in 20ml cold PBS/3% FBS

\- Spin for 7min at 1500 rpm, remove SNT, re-suspend in 20ml (wash x3 times)

\- Spin again, remove SNT, re-suspend

\- Spin again (last time)

\- Re-suspend in Isotonic Percoll (34% final). 

    Per liver use 25ml of isotonic percoll.

    To make 100ml (4 livers) —\>     62.5ml PBS1x

                                                      3.75ml PBS 10x

                                                      33.75ml Percoll

    \*Make at RT. Use 25ml per liver

\- Spin at 1750rpm for 12min at RT. Brake ON. 

\- Harvest pellet (RBC + lymphocytes), aspirate fat and everything else. With vaccum\! Pellet is very loose

\- Wash in 10ml Facs buffer

\- Re-suspend in ACK, leave for 4-5min

\- Filter through mesh

\- Count and stain
