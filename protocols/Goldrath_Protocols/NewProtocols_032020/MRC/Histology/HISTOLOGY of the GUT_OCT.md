**Frozen HISTOLOGY of the GUT**

**01/2020 - LLB**

Procedure for OCT sections:

1\) Take down one mouse at a time (it’s a time-sensitive thing\! Gut will lose its architecture super fast). 

2\) Pull out gut (I typically grab the intestine by the stomach, cut it from the stomach and pull it vertically). Cut the 3-5cm piece you are interested in (I typically do ileum, but you can do duodenum, jejunum, ileum, colon). Put tissue on a paper tissue, squirt some PBS1x with pasteur pipette and remove feces by gently pushing with scissors. Cut/open the gut longitudinally, mucus facing up and muscle down. Keep it relatively moist with pasteur pipette+PBS1x. \*\*Don't worry about removing fat, it's part of the natural structure of the gut and won't hurt for OCT sectioning.

     \*Don’t do any washing steps. Unnecessary and they will damage structure\!

3\) Have a cassette filled with OCT ready.

4\) Make swiss roll with wooden stick (I use cotton swab sticks): place stick below (on the muscle side). Now roll gut put muscle touching stick, mucus is in the outside. 

     \*When rolling don’t force tissue into roll. Move stick forward instead of force-rolling the tissue around it (tissue is super soft)

5\) Once rolled place in cassette filled with OCT by putting stick vertical on cassette and pushing tissue down with forceps. Try to embed it flat. Add more OCT on top. 

\- Procedure for Paraffin sections:

1\) Take down one mouse at a time (it’s a time-sensitive thing\! Gut will lose its architecture super fast). 

2\) Pull out gut, remove feces by gently pushing with scissors, put on tissue and cut/open longitudinally, mucus facing up and muscle down. Keep it relatively moist with pasteur pipette+PBS.

     \*Don’t do any washing steps. Unnecessary and they will damage structure\!

3\) Cut in 2-3cm pieces (one distal, medial, proximal). Have a cassette ready with 2 sponges with holes made by the cut plastic stick. 

4\) Make swiss roll with wooden stick: place stick below (on the muscle side). Now roll gut put muscle touching stick, mucus is in the outside. 

     \*When rolling don’t force tissue into roll. Move stick forward instead of force-rolling the tissue around it (tissue is super soft)

5\) Once rolled place in cassette in between sponges (to flatten it). Remove stick, close cassette and dump in fixative (this has to be done within 10min after death). Fix in 10% Formalin solution (=4% Paraformaldehyde) +1mM CaCl2 in PBS1x for 24h at RT **(24h preserves epitopes pretty well. **Some people fix for 48h, but you risk losing epitopes).

6\) After fixation, dump PFA in appropriate biohazard container, and wash with deoinized water twice (dump fixative, fill bottle with water - let sit for 5min for every wash).  

7\) Place in 70% EtOH (70% EtOH preserves tissue for longer than just PBS).

8\) Bring to the core for embedding/sectioning in 70% Ethanol. (Tissues can remain in ethanol for days/weeks before being sent in to the core)
