**Lara Labarta-Bajo (Zuniga Lab) - Protocol is a combination of the Surh lab and Goldrath lab protocols**

<span class="underline">Materials:</span>

\- Hot-plate stirrer (Scilogex)

\- Tissue chopper - McIlwain tissue chopper

\- 50ml pyrex conicals

\- Stirring magnets

\- Stripping buffer:

PBS1x + 3% FBS + 10mM EDTA + 20mM HEPES + Pen/Strep (100u/ml pen, 100ug/ml strep), 1mM Na-pyruvate

\- Enzyme media:

RPMI + 3% FBS, 20mM HEPES, Pen/Strep (100u/ml pen, 100ug/ml strep), 1mM Na-pyruvate +\*on day of harvest add

0.4mg/ml Collagenase D (Roche) and DNAse I 10ug/ml (Roche)

\- Percoll 100% (9 parts of Percoll from commercial bottle + 1 part of PBS 10x)

d-1 Prepare:

\- Rinse 50ml conicals with ddw

\- Prepare stripping buffer (30ml/sample per IEL).

\- Prepare RPMI 5% FBS (as re-suspension media)

\- Prepare PBS 5% FBS (to wash)

\- Prepare enzyme media w/o enzymes (20ml per sample)

d0 Harvest

\- Turn on stirrer and set at 37ºC

\- Put stripping buffer at 37ºC

\- Enzyme media at 37ºC

\- Set centrifuge at RT. Pull out Percoll and keep at RT

\- Set up big collection container (waste)

\- Thaw collagenase and DNase at RT

\- Collect SI and colon, remove fat (cotton swabs are useful), remove PP’s, empty fecal contents by gently pressing it down with your scissors. Cut it longitudinally, fold and cut in 1cm pieces. 

\*Keep tissues in PBS- they should never be dry

\- Wash x3 with PBS5% directly into 50ml Pyrex conical

\- Add 30ml Stripping buffer and stir for 20min at 37ºC

\- Shake a bit and collect soup (these are your IEL\!) into 50ml falcon. Add RPMBI5% to Pyrex conical and collect some more. Add RPMI5% up to 50ml in conical and keep on ice until Percoll.

\- Wash remaining tissue in PBS5% once and strain through tea strainer

\- Pick gut pieces and Chop 2x with tissue chopper and add to 30ml enzyme media (in pyrex conical)

\- Stir for 45min at 37ºC

\- Pipette up and down 3 times with automatic pipette (10ml glass pipette) and

\- Filter through cell strainer & spin down

\- Re-suspend in 5ml 44% Percoll then add up to 2ml of 67% Percoll below

\- Spin 20min 2,000 rpm (NO brake) and collect interphase with Pasteur pipette

\- Collect interphase in 15ml falcon tubes, fill up with 5%RPMI and spin down at 2,000rpm for 8min.

\- Re-suspend pellet in desired counting volume and continue with your staining.
