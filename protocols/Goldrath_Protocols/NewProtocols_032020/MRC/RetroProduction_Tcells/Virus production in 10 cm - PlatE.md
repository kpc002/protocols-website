**Virus production in 10 cm-dish**

<span class="underline">Day 0</span>: plate 2.5x10<sup>6</sup> PlatE (or PhoenixE) cells in a 10 cm dish (total volume 8ml) in DMEM complete media without P/S

<span class="underline">Day 1:</span> transfection with MIRUS TRANS-LT1 (MIR 2300)

Just before transfection, change the medium adding 12 ml of fresh new media and move the cells back to the incubator

In a 14-ml tube, add in this order

  - DNA: 10 ug transfer vector + 5ug pCL-Eco

  - OPTIMEM serum-dree medium to .4 ml total volume, mix well by pipetting and swirl the tube;

  - Add MIRUS reagent to DNA (3 to 6 ug of MIRUS/ug of DNA). Dispense MIRUS directly into the liquid, dropwise.

  - Swirl the tube, do not pipette or vortex

  - Let it stand at RT for 20 to 30 min

  - Gently add the DNA/OPTIMEM/Mirus complex to the cells, dropwise, in a circular motion.

  - Put the plate back in the incubator (5% CO2)

  - 16hs after transfection, gently remove the medium and replace with 10 ml fresh medium for virus collection.

  - Collect the virus at 24 and 48h, store virus in the fridge and pool the fractions at the end; at the end, filter virus through a 0.45 um filter (do not use 0.2).

  - Store in the fridge and use virus within 24h

  - Store at -80C long term. Avoid freeze-thawing

Prewarm at RT optimum and MIRUS prior to using
