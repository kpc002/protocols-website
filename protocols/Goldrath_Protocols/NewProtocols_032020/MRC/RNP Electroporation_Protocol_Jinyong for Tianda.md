**Cas9 RNP Electroporation Protocol (Seki and Rutt, JEM, 2018) for Tianda**

Generated by Katia Faliti

Modified by Jinyong Choi

**<span class="underline">Materials</span>**

1.  Spleen + I.Ns C57BL/6 mouse.

2.  CD4 T cells isolation kit (Stemcell Technologies)

3.  Culture T cells in RPMI 10% FCS Hyclone  
    \+ 2mM Glutamax, 0.1 mM NEAA, 50 uM BME and 100 U/ml PenStrep

4.  **For activated CD4 T cells:**  
    0.5 x 10<sup>6</sup> cells/ml  
    8 ug/ml anti-CD3 (clone l45-2c11, BD)  
    8 ug/ml anti-CD28 (clone 37.51)  
    Pre-activation for 2 days before transfection.

5.  **For resting CD4 T cells:**  
    Preincubated with 2 ng/ ml rhlL-7 for 24h before transfection.

6.  **crRNA and tracrRNA from IDT:  
    **Alt-R CRISPR-Cas9 TracrRNA  
    Alt-R CRISPR-Cas9 TracrRNA-ATTO 550  
    (ATTO 550: a fluorochrome excited by yellow laser, PE channel)  
    Alt-R CRISPR-Cas9 crRNA NTC negative control

7.  **Cas9 Protein**  
    IDT Cas9 v3 (10 mg/ml) or  
    TrueCut Cas9 Protein v2 (5 mg/ml; Invitrogen) or  
    Cas9 (6.4 mg/ml; QB3 Berkley Lab)

**(D-7)**

**<span class="underline">Isolation SM CD4 T cells and stimulation</span>**

Coat the 24-well plate with 8 ug/mL of anti-CD3 and anti-CD28

Culture the cells at 0.5 x 10<sup>6</sup> cells/well in R10 + BME (50 uM) + NEAA (1:500) + 2 ng/mL rhIL-7.

**(D-5) RNP Electroporation**

**<span class="underline">Preparation of crRNA-tracrRNA duplex</span>**

To prepare the duplex, each Alt-R crRNA and Alt-R tracrRNA or Alt-tracrRNA-ATTO550 were reconstituted to **100 uM** with NF Duplex buffer (IDT).

100 μM = 100 pmol/uL = 10 nmol/100 uL = 2 nmol/20 uL

Mix oligos at equimolar concentrations in a sterile PCR tube (e.g. 10 uL Alt-R crRNA + 10 uL Alt-R-tracrRNA).

Annealing oligos by **heating at 95C for 5 min** in a PCR machine, then cool down at **RT at least 1 h.**

After duplexing, the concentration of crRNA-tracrRNA duplex in solution is now 50 uM (50 pmol/uL).

**<span class="underline">Precomplexing of Cas9 / RNP</span>**

TrueCut Cas9 Protein v2 concentration: 5 mg/mL (1 ug = 6.1 pmol; 30.5 pmol / uL)

IDT Cas9 Protein v2 concentration: 10 mg/mL (1 ug = 6.1 pmol; 61 pmol / uL)

QB3 Berkley Lab Cas9 Protein: 6.4 mg/mL (1 ug = 6.1 pmol; 39 pmol / uL)

Mix gRNA and Cas9 at \~3:1 molar ratio

In a PCR strip, mix **4.8 uL** crRNA-tracrRNA duplexes (240 pmol) and **2.0 uL** QB3 Cas9 (80 pmol) by gentle pipetting up and down and incubated RT for at least 10 min. Now total **6.8 uL** mixture generated per reaction.

or

In a PCR strip, mix **4.8 uL** crRNA-tracrRNA duplexes (240 pmol) and **1.3 uL** IDT Cas9 v3 (80 pmol) by gentle pipetting up and down and incubated RT for at least 10 min. Now total **6.1 uL** mixture generated per reaction.

Note: Check the amount of crRNA, tracrRNA and Cas9 protein before the reaction.

\~2.4 uL crRNA, \~2.4 uL tracrRNA, and \~2.0 uL Cas9 protein are needed per reaction.

**<span class="underline">For Electroporation with MaxCyte ATX</span>**

1.  Aliquot **57 uL** of complete media/well in 24 well plate.

2.  Wash cells with electroporation buffer (Hyclone) once and resuspend the cells at 4 x 10<sup>7</sup> cells / **mL** in electroporation buffer.

3.  Aliquot **50 uL** of cells into Eppendorf tubes (2 x 10<sup>6</sup> cells / rxn)

4.  Add **6.8 uL RNP** into the cells and mix well. Now total approx. **57 uL.**

5.  Transfer the cells/RNP mixture to cuvette (OC-100).

6.  Electroporation with **Expended-5 protocol**

7.  After electroporation, transfer the cells into 57 uL media in 24 well, and incubate for 20 min in 37C incubator.

8.  Add 1 mL of R10 + BME + NEAA + 10 ng/mL IL-2.

**(D-4)**

**<span class="underline">Transfection efficiency check and replace the media with rhIL-7</span>**

Check transfection efficiency ATTO 550 fluorescence by flow 1 day after transfection:

Take 80 uL of cells from culture (gently pipetting up and down the cells before taking)

Stain the cells with flow Ab mixture (50 uL per well):

CD4-BV510, L/D-eF780, crRNA-ATTO 550 (PE channel of LSRII-2 and Fortessa), and Ab for target protein

Prepare R10 + BME (50 uM) + NEAA (1:500) + 2 ng/mL rhIL-7.

Spindown the cells at 1,600 rpm for 4 min at 4C.

Remove the supernatant and resuspend the cells with prepared R10 + BME + NEAA + rhIL-7

Culture the cells for o/n.

**(D-3)**

**<span class="underline">Sorting and Adoptive Transfer</span>**

Next day, spindown the cells at 1,600 rpm for 4 min at 4C.

Remove the supernatant and wash the cells with PBS.

Stain the cells with L/D-eF780 for 10 min at room temperature.

Wash the cells with MACS buffer twice and resuspend the cells with MACS buffer (200 uL).

Sort the live cells (eF780 negative), count cells by Accuri, and transfer the cells to recipient mice.

5x10<sup>3</sup> cells per mouse for LCMV infection.

50x10<sup>3</sup> cells per mouse for protein immunization.

Preserve the leftover cells to check knockdown efficiency.

Wash the cells with PBS once.

Centrifuge the cells at 5,000 x g for 10 min at 4C.

Remove supernatant (do not touch cell pellet).

Store the cell pellet at -80C
