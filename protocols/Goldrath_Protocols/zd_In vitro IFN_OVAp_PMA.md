# In vitro IFN-gamma:

1)  > Harvest Spleens into RP10 --\> grind cells through strainer --\> transfer into 15 ml conical tube

2)  > Spin at 1800 for 5min --\> resuspend in 1ml Ack for 1min at RT

3)  > Count cells and resuspend at 4x10<sup>7</sup> cells/ml

4)  > Plate 4x10<sup>6</sup> cells in 24 well dish, 100μL of 4x10<sup>7</sup> cells/mL (2 wells per sample).

5)  > Add 1 mL of RP10 to unstimulated cells.

6)  > Add to Stimulated cells the following: either OVAp or PMA
    
    1.  > Add 1 ml RP10 plus 10 μl / 10<sup>-6</sup>OVAp to stim cells.
    
    2.  > Add 500μl PMA (5ng/ml) plus 500μl Ionomycin (500ng/ml)

7)  > Incubate at 37<sup>o</sup>C for 2-3 hours.

8)  > Mix 7.3 μl golgi stop with 100 μl RP10 and add 10 μl to each well.

9)  > Incubate at 37 <sup>o</sup>C for another 3 hours.

10) > Harvest into eppendorf tubes and spin at max for 1 minute.

11) > Mix 0.25 μl FC block in 25μl FSB per sample and add 25μl to each sample.

12) > Incubate at RT for 5 minutes.

13) > Add \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ biotin antibody to each sample.

14) > Incubate on ice for 10-15 minutes.

15) > Wash in 1.5 ml FSB.

16) > Add 25 μl of stain to each sample and incubate on ice for 10-15 minutes.

17) > Wash 1x in 1.5 ml FSB.

18) > Add 250μL fix/perm, incubate 30 mintues at RT in dark.

19) > Wash twice in 1 mL of cytowash/perm (diluted in H2O as per Pharmingen).

20) > Resuspend in 25μL/sample diluted Fc block (0.25μL/sample Fc and 25μL/sample cytowash/perm, 5 minutes room temp.

21) > Add anti-IFNgamm-PE in 25μL/sample cytowash/perm.

22) > Incubate for 20-30 minutes at 4 <sup>o</sup>C, wash 1x 1.5mLs cytowash/perm.

23) > Resuspend in 500μL/sample Fixative.
