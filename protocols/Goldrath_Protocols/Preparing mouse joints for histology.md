**Preparing mouse joints for histology**

3.7% Formalin solution in PBS:

  - Dilute 37% Formalin 1 to 10 in PBS

14% EDTA solution:

  - Add 140 g <span class="underline">free acid</span> EDTA to 700 ml distilled H<sub>2</sub>O

  - On stir plate in the fume hood, add ammonium hydroxide 30 ml at a time until solution clears (about 90 ml total).

  - Add H<sub>2</sub>O to almost 1L. Check pH and adjust with ammonium hydroxide dropwise up to pH 7.2, then adjust final volume to 1L.

Sac mice, remove as much skin from legs as possible.

Harvest paws and knees; immediately fix in 3.7% formalin solution.

Fix for 1 day on shaker at RT.

Rinse with 14% EDTA solution.

Incubate in 14% EDTA solution on shaker at RT for 5 more days. Replace with new 5% EDTA solution daily.

Test if decalcified: bones should feel elastic. A test cut should not have resistance and not feel like cutting sand.

For paraffin embedding, place in 30%, 50%, and 70% ETOH for at least 30 min each prior to submitting to core lab.
