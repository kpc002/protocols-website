1.  Dialyze antibodies overnight against 0.1 M NaHCO<sub>3</sub> adjusted to pH 8.3 (8.2 – 8.6)

2.  Adjust protein concentration to 1mg/mL

3.  Warm biotin succidimide ester to room temperature

4.  Rapidly weight out and dissolve NHS-d- biotin into DMSO at a concentration of 1mg/mL and dd 120 uL per mg of Ig and mix immediately.

> **used 0.006 g biotin in 6 mL DMSO** – too hard to weight out any less

5.  Leave the mixture at room temperature for 2 hours shaking on the belly dancer

6.  Dialyze overnight against PBS + 0.1% NaN<sub>3</sub>
