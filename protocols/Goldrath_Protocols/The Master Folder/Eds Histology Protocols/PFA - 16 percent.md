Edward Yang – 11/1/08

**<span class="underline">16% PFA – for 500 mL</span>**

<span class="underline"></span>

<span class="underline">Materials</span>

350mL DI H20

80 grams of dry PFA

50 mL **10x** PBS

Very large beaker (1 liter flat bottom)

500mL beaker (round)

Magnetic stir/ heat plate

Magnetic stir bar

Thermometer

pH meter

10-12N HCL

10-12N NaOH

<span class="underline">Method – ***EVERYTHING* is done in the chemical hood**</span>

1.  Fill 500mL beaker with 350mL DI H20 and place into very large beaker. Fill large beaker with H20 until covering \~2/3 of 500mL beaker. Place thermometer in outer water bath.
    
    1.  Heat to between 60-70 degrees C. **Do not exceed 70 degrees C as that is the flash point of PFA.**

2.  Weigh out 80g of dry PFA **in the hood**. Place magnetic stir bar (and start stirring) into 500mL beaker and then pour in dry PFA slowly.

3.  Use plastic pipet to pipet in 1-2 pipet-full’s worth of NaOH. Watch temperature to make sure it’s at \~65 degrees C.
    
    1.  As PFA begins to go into solution, pipet in NaOH dropwise to improve solubility, conservatively.

4.  Add NaOH dropwise until PFA **completely** goes into solution. The color of the solution should be completely clear/transparent.
    
    1.  Once in solution, pour in 50mL of 10x PBS, fill the rest up to the 500mL line with DI H20, and stir until mixed.

5.  Turn off heat, pour out outer water bath, and fill with cold/RT water. Cool PFA solution until room temperature.

6.  pH the solution to about 7.2-7.4 pH using HCL.

7.  Sterile filter PFA using \~0.1 micron filter. Label with date and 16%.
