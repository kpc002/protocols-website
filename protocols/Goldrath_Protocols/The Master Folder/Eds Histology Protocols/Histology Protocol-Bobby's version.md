**Epic Histology Protocol – for Spleen and Lymph Nodes**

**<span class="underline">Tissue Processing</span>**

**Materials**

  - 4% Formaldehyde/Paraformaldehyde(PFA) – see PFA protocol
    
      - Make fresh from high concentration stock each time
    
      - Use 15 conicals – enough to cover spleen/LN

  - 5% Sucrose/ 4% formaldehyde (4mL 30% sucrose, 6mL 16% formaldehyde bring up to 24mL using 1X PBS)

  - 10% Sucrose (make dilution from 30% stock)

  - 30% Sucrose (30g of sucrose in 100ml of 1XPBS)

  - OCT, Cryo-molds cassette, and dry ice, 2-Methyl Butane

  - Fisherbrand Superfrost-Plus microscope slides

  - 1x PBS

**Method**

<span class="underline">Fixation and Dehydration (keep timings of each step consistent for all the experiment related tissues taken at different days)</span>

1.  Sacrifice mouse and harvest spleen/ lymph nodes/ lungs (inflated with 1:1 of 1%PFA +OCT, and tied at trachea using floss).

2.  Drop organs into 4% formaldehyde/5% sucrose (6/12 well plate) **<span class="underline">IMMEDIATELY</span>** upon removal and place on ice.
    
    1.  Attach tubes to a rocker or periodically tap to make sure fixative penetrates all directions.
    
    2.  Leave organs in fixative (4% formaldehyde/5% sucrose) anywhere from 2 to 4 hours at 4°C (keep time consistent between tissues related to one experiment, but taken on different days)

3.  Aspirate out the fixative and pour in 2 mL of 10% Sucrose solution (rinse PFA out with 10% sucrose once) and Incubate for 2-3 hrs at 4°C.

4.  Replace 10% Sucrose with 30% Sucrose and incubate at 4 degree C° (from 2hr up to O/N).

<span class="underline">Embedding</span>

5.  Put processed organs into respective small Petri dishes with fresh OCT and use to:
    
    1.  Wash residual sucrose off by rolling around in OCT (make sure not to damage the tissues)

6.  Fill up a **Cry-mold cassette** completely with OCT
    
    1.  Place the tissue at the bottom of the cassette, making sure that there are no bubbles, incubate for 5-10min (prepare freezing setup as following while incubating)

> 7\. Place a glass petri dish (found in the fume hood) in a shallow ice bucket and fill the surrounding with crushed dry ice, pour enough 2-MethylButane (also in fume hood) into the glass petri dish,

8.  Place cassettes with tissues/OCT into the petri dish until OCT solidifies (keep frozen ones on dry ice until all the blocks are done)

9.  Store all blocks in labeled boxes at -80°C.

**<span class="underline">Fixation (if samples were not fixed before embedding) and Staining</span>**

**Materials**

  - Fixing reagent of choice: **4%** **Paraformaldehyde** (dilute from 16 % PFA using 1xPBS)
    
      - See protocol for making 16% PFA (stored in -20 outside the cold room)

  - 1X PBS (ridiculous amounts) – use 10X PBS for dilutions rather than 1X stocks

  - Coplin jars or slide holder chambers (for many samples) for washes

  - Glass slide holder (compatible with slide holder chamber)

  - Pap pen (creates liquid repelling barrier around sample)

  - Large Kimwipes

  - **Universal Blocking Buffer: (primarily used, serums of secondary Ab species added to reduce unspecific bindings to secondary Ab)**
    
      -        1%BSA (blocking & stabilizer)
    
      -        0.1% cold fish skin gelatin (blocking)
    
      -        0.5% Triton X-100 (penetration enhancer)
    
      -        0.05% sodium azide (preservative)
    
      -        0.01M PBS, pH 7.2-7.4
    
      -        Mix well and store at 4 ºC.
    
      -        Note: this blocking buffer can also used as un-conjugated primary antibody dilution buffer. Do not use it to dilute HRP conjugated antibody as the sodium azide is inhibitor of HRP. Do not use it to dilute Alkaline Phosphatase (AP) conjugated antibody as the phosphate in PBS is an inhibitor of AP.  

  - Fc Block (FACS room 4 degree fridge, Ab bin)

  - Vector Labs Avidin/Biotin blocking kit (if using biotinylated antibodies or tetramer staining is done)

  - Hydration chamber (basically some sort of plastic sealable container with **wet** paper towels on the bottom – incubate slides so liquid does not evaporate)

<span class="underline">Pap Penning and Preparation for Blocking/Staining</span>

1.  After taking slides out of -20, let them equilibrate to room temp (skip step 2 if your samples already fixed)

2.  Fix for 10 min using 4% PFA followed by 3 PBS (1X) wash

3.  Do 2 x PBS (1x) wash and dry excess PBS, avoid touching the sections

4.  Circle each individual section a few times with pap pen, give it enough time for pap pen but not your sections to dry

5.  Dip sections into PBS again to retain moisture if needed

6.  Make master mix of 100uL of universal Block + 5uL Fc block per sample (for Biotinylated Ab or tetramer go to step 7; otherwise go to step 8)
    
    1.  Place slides in humidity chamber and pipet 100 uL/ section of Blocking buffer onto pap pen circled section, making sure to cover section incubate for 30 min RT (dilute your Ab in Uni Block at this step)

7.  If using biotinylated Ab or Tetrameres – perform Avidin/Biotin block **in this order**:
    
    1.  Tap slides on paper towel to remove blocking buffer
    
    2.  Add 2drops of avidin block into 500uL of universal + FC block (prepared in step6) onto each section – incubate 30 minutes
        
        1.  Rinse slides with PBS Briefly
    
    3.  Apply 100uL/section of biotin block in universal Blocking buffer (2drop Biotin in 500uL Uni Block) – incubate 15 minutes
    
    4.  Rinse slides briefly in PBS (move to step 9)

8.  Remove blocking solution **carefully** using edges of Kimwipe (preferred for ear sections and lung as they fall off easily) or aspirator (for spleen or well adhered tissues). DO NOT TOUCH SECTION\!

9.  Apply 100 uL /section of Ab to the slides

10. Incubate over night at 4 degree (inside the humidity chamber) (make sure the lid is closed otherwise you sections will dry and won’t work)

11. Wash w/ PBS (1X), 2-3 times (5’each) to wash the unbound Ab (if you do not need a secondary Ab staining, move to step 14) (prepare your secondary Ab at this step using Uni Block)

12. Apply 100 uL/ section of secondary Ab and incubate for 1hr at RT

13. Remove the Ab and wash 2-3 times (10’ each) with PBS

14. Stain the nucleus using 100 uL of 600nM DAPI in PBS with 0.1% triton (can be found in Bobby’s bin inside FACS room 4 degree fridge, in the Styrofoam tube holder)

15. Wash with PBS (10’) once

16. Mount using Invitrogen Pro-Long Gold Antifade Reagent (place 50-100 uL of AF on each section and hold one edge of the cover slip against the slide, below the drops, while holding the slide up, slowly reduce the gap between top of the coverslip and the slide so that all the air gets pushed out as the antifade fills the gap between the slide and cover slip) (avoid letting coverslip move around after its completely laid on the slide)

17. Remove excess antifade by gently pushing on the cover slip and aspirating the excess antifade getting pushed out the edges of the slide (again prevent coverslip from moving around)

18. Seal using nail polish, let it dry in the humidity chamber or dark spot like a inside a door at the bench
