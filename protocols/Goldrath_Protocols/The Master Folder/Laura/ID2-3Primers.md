Primers

There are **4** PCR reactions used to distinguish the mice in this colony:

The FLP’d and un-FLP’d alleles can be distinguished by:

FLP\_3\_F + FLP\_4\_R will yield a band for the FLP’d allele at

**Flp\_deleted\_3\_F** – ATCCTGCCTTTTATCCTCTTT

**FLP\_4\_R** - CCCTGGACCTATGAAGGCG

Gives a band of \~780 bp if the rearranged allele is present and no band otherwise.

**FLP\_4\_F** - CACTCACCATTTATTTAGTGGCAAAAG

**FLP\_4\_R** – CCCTGGACCTATGAAGGCG

Gives a band of \~390 bp if either the FLP’d OR Un-FLP’d allele is present. There is also a non-specific band at \~600 bp you can ignore.

The Cre-rearranged alleles can be identified by:

**ID2KI\_CRE\_F\_3** - ACTTTATACGAAGTTATATGTGAAATCGCT

**ID2KI\_CRE\_R\_3 –** GAAGTTATGATCTAAGGCACCCG

This reaction gives a band of \~620 bp. There are also a few nonspecific bands that show up occasionally.

The WT allele can be identified by:

**Id2/3\_Geno\_Wt\_F\_2** - CCTTTTATCCTCTTTCTCCCCAGG

**Id2/3\_Geno\_R\_2 –** TAAGGCTCGGGGTAGCC

Gives a band of \~490 bp. This reaction does not amplify the KI allele.

All these reactions run on the program called “52” on the PCR machine and they all have the following recipe for a 25 ul reaction volume:

1 ul genomic DNA

5 ul 5x buffer

2 ul MgCl2

0.5 ul dNTPs

0.5 ul F primer

0.5 ul R primer

0.5 ul Taq

15 ul ddH20
