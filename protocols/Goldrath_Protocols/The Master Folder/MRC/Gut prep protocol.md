**IEL and Lamina Propria prep**

1.  3 minutes prior to sacrifice, inject mice retro-orbitrally w/ 200 uL antibody mix (15 uL CD8a-APCcy7 + 185 uL PBS)

2.  Sacrifice mice, remove small intestine and spleen, place in ice cold RP5 or CMF, and trim off fat

3.  Place paper towels on desk and wet w/ CMF

4.  Wet gut w/ CMF, cut out Peyer’s patches and clean out the poop

5.  Cut the small intestine in half lengthwise and then cut intestine into small (\~1 cm pieces)

6.  Place in flask and wash 2X w/ CMF, pour out contents carefully so no gut comes out

7.  Dump out CMF carefully and replace w/ \~50 mL DTE, place on 37°C stir plate for 30 min

8.  Pour contents into 50 mL conical with cell strainer

9.  Wash out flask 1X w/ DTE and add to 50 mL conical

<span class="underline">If preparing lamina propria:</span>

1.  Transfer contents of cell strainer back into flask and add \~30 mL of collagenase solution

2.  Incubate for 45 min at 37° while stirring

3.  Pour contents into 50 mL conical with cell strainer

4.  Wash out flask 1X w/ DTE and add to 50 mL conical

<span class="underline">Proceed as follows below for both IEL and lamina propria</span>

10. Spin at 1600 rpm for 4 min

11. Make sure you see a pellet, then pour supernatant out and wipe top of conical

12. Resuspend each sample in 5 mL 44% percoll solution, make sure pellet is well resuspended

13. Place glass pasteur pipette in conical and add 2 mL 67% percol solution to top of pipette to make a bottom layer. Don’t disturb after adding\!

14. Spin at 21°C at 2000 rpm for 20 min, set acceleration to 4 and brake to 0

15. Check for an interphase between the two layers and transfer it to 15 mL conical (usually about 3 mL)

16. Fill to top with PBS then spin at 2000 rpm for 5 min
    
    1.  If interphase remains, take out the top layer, add PBS, and spin again

17. Resuspend pellet in 250 uL of RP5

18. Count and stain IEL

19. Keep on ice and analyze by flow the next day

**Gut Prep Reagents**

<span class="underline">10X HEPES BICARBONATE STOCK (STERILE):</span> makes 500ml

\-WILL NEED 100ml PER LITER OF CMF

\-10.5g Na-bicarbonate (FISHER \#BP328-1

\-11.9g HEPES (FISHER \#BP310-500)

\-500ml ddH20 (Millipore)

\-\>pH to 7.2 using pH meter (do not usually need to add anything

\-\>filter sterilize in hood using 0.2um pore Nalgene \#291-3320 bottle top -\>store @RT in dark; do not use after 30 days

<span class="underline">CMF SOLUTION:</span> makes 1L

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 150ml PER MOUSE

\-100ml **10x** HBSS w/o Na-bicarb. Ca Mg (MEDIATECH \#20-021-CV)

\-100ml **10x** HEPES-bicarbonate (recipe above)

\-20ml Fetal Bovine Serum (HYCLONE \#SV30014.03)

\-780ml ddH20 (Millipore)

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>DOE: transfer to ice bucket

<span class="underline">DTE SOLUTION:</span> makes 500ml

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 60ml PER MOUSE

\-460ml CMF (recipe above)

\-40ml Fetal Bovine Serum (HYCLONE \#SV30014.03)

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>DOE: add 77mg Dithioerythritol (CALBIOCHEM \#233152)

\-\>DOE: warm to 37 <sup>o</sup>C in water bath

<span class="underline">1x PERCOLL STOCK (STERILE):</span> makes 500ml

\-450ml Percoll (GE HEALTHCARE \#17-0891-01)

\-50ml **10x** DPBS w/o Ca Mg (HYCLONE \#SH30378.03)

\-\>keep sterile

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge long-term

\-\>if removed 50ml from original 500ml percoll bottle to make room for

DPBS, bring this to 1X too, do not discard

<span class="underline">44% PERCOLL:</span> makes 100ml

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 5ml PER TISSUE (IEL+LP) PER MOUSE

\-44ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

\-56ml RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE \#SH30027.02)

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>DOE: bring to RT

<span class="underline">67% PERCOLL:</span> makes 100ml

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 2.5ml PER TISSUE (IEL+LP) PER MOUSE

\-67ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

\-33ml 1x DPBS w/o Ca Mg (HYCLONE \#SH30028.03)

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>DOE: bring to RT

<span class="underline">**100x** HG STOCK SOLUTION (STERILE):</span> makes 500ml

\-59.58g HEPES (FISHER \#BP310-500)

\-14.6g L-glutamine B-grade (CALBIOCHEM \#3521)

\-500ml (QS to line) RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE \#SH30027.02)

\-\>use stir bar to mix

\-\>pH to 7.5 (will take 10-20ml 5N NaOH)

\-\>make 10ml aliquots

\-\>store @20<sup>o</sup>C in freezer long-term

<span class="underline">COLLAGENASE SOLUTION:</span> makes 200ml

\-WILL NEED AT LEAST 35ml PER MOUSE

\-2ml HG (recipe above)

\-200ul 1M MgCl<sub>2</sub> (AMBION \#AM9530G)

\-200ul 1M CaCl<sub>2</sub> (HYCLONE \#SH30289.01)

\-10ml Fetal Bovine Serum (HYCLONE \#SV30014.03)

\-QS to 200ml w/RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE \#SH30027.02)

\-\>store @4<sup>o</sup>C in fridge overnight

\-\>DOE: add 20,000 U Type I Collagenase (WORTHINGTON \#4197)

\-\>DOE: warm to 37 <sup>o</sup>C in water bath
