**RNA Isolation from Sorted Cells**

**and**

**cDNA preparation**

1.  Sort \~50K cells of interest into 500 ul of Trizol. Careful not to dilute Trizol too much – Sort into media, spin down and resuspend in Trizol if necessary.

2.  Extracted cells in Trizol can be kept at -80C.

3.  Thaw Trizol:cells @ RT

4.  Add 100 ul chloroform.

5.  Vortex 15 seconds.

6.  Incubate 2-3 minutes at RT.

7.  Centrifuge 15 min at 12000 g at 4C – Work on ice from now on.

8.  Transfer aqueous phase to new clean tube \~250 ul (if 500 ul were used).

9.  Add 1ul glycoblue.

10. Add 300 ul isopropanol.

11. Incubate at -80C for at least 1h.

12. Centrifuge 20 min at highest speed 4C.

13. Remove supernatant.

14. Wash with 500 ul 75% ethanol (cold).

15. Centrifuge 15 min at 7400g 4C.

16. Remove ethanol and air dry (10 min).

17. Resuspend in 20 ul water.

**Remove DNA contamination**

1.  Add 2ul of 10X TURBO DNase Buffer and 1ul TURBO DNase to 20 ul of RNA, and mix gently

2.  Incubate at 37C for 20-30 min

3.  Add resuspended Inactivation Reagent (2ul to 23 ul mix), and mix well

4.  Incubate 5 min at RT, mixing occasionally

5.  Centrifuge at 10000g for 1.5 min and transfer the RNA to a fresh tube

**cDNA preparation – Assuming RNA comes from same amount of cells**

**Quantify with Qubit/Nanodrop**

18. Use kit (pink box @ -20C): Mix 8 ul RNA, 1ul dNTP, 1ul random hexamer (50 ng/ul).

19. Incubate at 65C for 5min.

20. Prepare 2X reaction mix in order – Make MasterMix
    
    1.  10x RT buffer – 2 ul
    
    2.  25 mM MgCl2 – 4 ul
    
    3.  0.1 M DTT – 2 ul
    
    4.  RNase out – 1ul

21. Add 2X reaction to RNA.

22. Incubate at RT for 2 minutes.

23. Add 1ul superscript II to each tube.

24. PCR program:
    
    1.  RT 10 min
    
    2.  42C 50 min
    
    3.  70 15 min

25. Chill on ice.

26. Add 1 ul RNAse H and incubate 37C 20 min.
