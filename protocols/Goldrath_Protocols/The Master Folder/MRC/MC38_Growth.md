Growth Conditions:
   • Cells are adherent
   • Maintain cells at 80% confluency
   • Cells are normally split on Tuesdays and Fridays
   • Split cells 1:10 for use in 2-3 days
   • Split 1:40 for routine Tues/Fri maintenance
   • (trypsin 1X for 2-3 min is sufficient)


Medium:
Dulbecco's modified MEM with 10% fetal bovine serum, 2mM glutamine, 0.1 mM nonessential amino
acids, 1 mM sodium pyruvate, 10 mM Hepes, 50ug/ml gentamycin sulfate, pen/strep.

       Formulation:
       500 mL DMEM (4.5g/l glucose)
       Remove 78.5 ml, discard

       Add:
       50 mL FBS (for 10%) Use BGS
       5 mL non-essential amino acids (10mM or 100x stock)
       5 mL sodium pyruvate (100 mM stock)
       5 mL Glutamine (200 mM stock)
       0.5 mL Gentamicin (50 mg/mL stock)
       5 mL Hepes (1 M stock)
       5 mL Pen/Strep (100x stock)
