# CD8+ T Cell Enrichment from Spleen + Lymph Nodes

Prepared by Hannah Tsunemoto

Updated February 15, 2016

**Background:**

This protocol is for the enrichment of CD8+ T cells collected from mouse spleen and lymph node tissues for cell cultures in 96-well plates.

**Caution:**

  - CAUTION: Comply with all biological and chemical safety guidelines, including disinfection of all materials contacting cultures by autoclaving or by contact (15 minutes) with 70% ethanol or 10% household bleach.

  - All steps should be performed to preserve sterility in the Tissue Culture (TC) room in the biological safety cabinet using aseptic techniques

  - CD3 and CD28 Antibodies are kept in 4<sup>o</sup>C box in 4<sup>o</sup>C fridge in TC room

  - Biotin-Abs are kept in 4<sup>o</sup>C fridge in main lab area

**What you need:**

  - Biotin-Abs (see **Table 1**)

  - Goat Anti-Hamster IgG Abs (GAH-IgG)

  - CD3 Abs

  - CD28 Abs

  - Phosphate buffered saline (PBS)

  - MACS buffer

  - T-Cell Media (TCM)

  - Ammonium-Chloride-Potassium Lysing Buffer (ACK)

  - Streptavidin beads

  - MACS column

  - Magnet (located in Hedrick Lab)

  - Round-bottom 96-well plate

  - 15 mL Falcon tubes

**Procedure:**

1.  **2Hrs before plating:** prepare 96-well plate with GAH-IgG
    
    1.  Dilute GAH-IgG Ab 1:50 in sterile PBS
    
    2.  Aliquot 50 uL/well into a round-bottom 96-well plate; tap plate to distribute liquid equally across bottom of wells
    
    3.  Incubate at 37<sup>o</sup>C for 2 hrs

2.  Process spleen and/or lymph node tissues collected from mouse
    
    1.  Homogenize tissue(s) in PBS with frosted glass slides and place solution in 15 mL tube
    
    2.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant
    
    3.  Resuspend pellet in 1-2 mL ACK and incubate at room temperature for 5-6 min
    
    4.  Neutralize with 3-4 mL MACS
    
    5.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant

3.  Treatment of cells with Biotin-Ab stain (see **Table 1**)
    
    1.  Resuspend pellet in 500 uL Biotin-Ab stain
    
    2.  Incubate at 4<sup>o</sup>C (fridge) for 15 min protected from light
    
    3.  Fill tube with MACS buffer
    
    4.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant
    
    5.  Resuspend pellet with 50 uL streptavidin-beads and 500 uL MACS Buffer
    
    6.  Incubate at 4<sup>o</sup>C for 15 min protected from light
    
    7.  Fill tube with MACS buffer
    
    8.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant
    
    9.  Resuspend pellet in 500 uL MACS buffer

4.  Column purification of streptavidin-bound cells
    
    1.  Attach MACS column to magnet
    
    2.  Pre-treat MACS column with 3 mL MACS buffer and discard flow-through
    
    3.  Run cell solution through column, **collecting flow-through**
    
    4.  Wash column three times (3X) with 3 mL MACS buffer
    
    5.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant
    
    6.  Resuspend pellet in 500 uL TCM
    
    7.  Count cells (1:1000 dilution) using Beckman Coulter Cell Counter
    
    8.  Dilute cells to wanted concentration of 2x10<sup>6</sup> cells/mL

5.  Plating cells in pre-treated 96-well plate (see **step 1**)
    
    1.  Wash GAH-Ab with 100-200 uL TCM twice (discard supernatant into cell bucket)
    
    2.  Plate 100 uL of 2x10<sup>6</sup> cell/mL solution \[final conc. 2x10<sup>5</sup> cells/well\]
    
    3.  Dilute CD3 and CD28 Abs 1:500 in TCM
    
    4.  Add 100 uL CD3/CD28 Ab mixture to each well, pipette to mix
    
    5.  Incubate at 37<sup>o</sup>C until further processing
