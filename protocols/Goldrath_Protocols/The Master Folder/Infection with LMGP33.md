Infection with LM-gp33

1.  Swipe glycerol stock with sterile pipette tip. Grow overnight in 5ml BHI (brain heart infusion) media in shaker at 37’C.

2.  In the morning, add 50ul of overnight stock to 5ml BHI media. Take 1ml and measure the OD. Should be pretty low \~.02-.03 OD600.

3.  Place new mixture in shaker. It should reach around 0.1 OD in 2.5-3 hours.

4.  Make calculations based on OD600 of 0.1=2x10^8 cfu/ml.

5.  Infect i.v at 3x10^4 pfu/mouse.

Note:

LM-gp33 is much less virulent than LM-OVA. It needs to be grown overnight for it to reach optimal growth point.

Adjust infection concentration up for rechallenge.

Rechallenge? 5x10^5 pfu/mouse i.v
