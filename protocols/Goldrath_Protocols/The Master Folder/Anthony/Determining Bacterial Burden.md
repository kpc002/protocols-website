Bacterial infections.

Recombinant L. monocytogenes strains LM-OVA (expressing secreted ovalbumin protein) and wild-type strain 10403s were provided by H. Shen (University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania) and J.T. Harty (University of Iowa, Iowa City, Iowa)34, 43. Bacteria were grown in tryptic soy broth (TSB) with 50 mug/ml of streptomycin to an absorbance at 600 nm of about 0.1. For challenge experiments about 0.61 times 105 to 1.0 times 105 CFU were injected intravenously. The actual number of bacteria injected was confirmed by dilution and growth on tryptic soy agar plates containing streptomycin.

Determination of CFU.

On various days after infection, spleens and livers were removed and placed in a 0.2% IGEPAL (Sigma) solution. Organs were homogenized and serial dilutions were plated onto TSB plates containing 50 mug/ml of streptomycin. Bacterial colonies were counted after plate incubation for about 24 h at 37 °C. Dashed lines on graphs (Figs. 1, 5 and 6) indicate the limit of detection (approximately 100 organisms).
