<span class="underline">Bodipy Staining Protocol</span>

1.  Fc Block

2.  Surface Stain

3.  Wash with PBS

4.  Stain with 500ng/ml Bodipy in PBS for 10min at RT

5.  Wash twice with FSB and run.

> Note: Run all samples at the same time and do not wait too long before running
> 
> Bodipy Stock 5mg/ml. Dilute 1:100 for 50ug/ml stock. Add 10ul of diluted stock to 1ml PBS for final concentration of 500ng/ml Bodipy.

<span class="underline">Mitotracker Staining Protocol</span>

1.  Wash cells with PBS

2.  Add 25nM mitotracker stain in PBS. Incubate at RT for 15 min.

3.  Wash with Facs buffer.

4.  Fc Block

5.  Surface stain

Note: Mitotracker is fixable

Mitracker stock is 1mM. Dilute 1:1000 for 1uM. Add 15ul of diluted stock to 600ul PBS for a final of 25nM.
