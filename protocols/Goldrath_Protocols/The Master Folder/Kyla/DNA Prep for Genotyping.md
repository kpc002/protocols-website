DNA Prep for Genotyping

1.  > Cut a small piece of mouse tail (0.5-1 mm)

2.  > Add 400ul tail juice. Shake at 55C for a minimum of 45 min or until tail dissolved.

3.  > Add 150ul 5M NaCl to each tube.

4.  > Vortex and spin at max speed for 5 min

5.  > Take off \~400ul supernatant and place in a clean new epp. Discard pellet.

6.  > Add 250ul isopropanol to each tube of supernatant. Vortex and spin max speed for 5 min. DNA pellet should be visible (maybe small).

7.  > Remove supernatant from pellets and discard.

8.  > Add 500ul 70% EtOH to each pellet. Spin 5 min max speed.

9.  > Remove 70% EtOH and allow pellets to dry for 30 min to overnight.

10. > Resuspend in 250ul water.
