# AD081409 Andrews CD3 CD28 protocol

   in vitro stim of T cells by plate bound anti­CD3 and soluble anti­CD28 antibodies
Goldrath Laboratory
Andrew Doedens

Materials: (all located in TC 4 degree...Ab in box, binding buffer is in a filter flask)
­Bicarbonate Binding buffer, pH9.6
­Goat anti hamster antibody, Vector Laboratories, Cat# AI‐9100
Follow instructions for resuspension (add 500ul H20, NOT PBS) This makes a 3mg/ml
solution. It contains azide. Comes in a small brown bottle with a grey rubber lid.
­anti­CD3 (clone 2C11) we have a large amount from UCSF Ab core @ 1.8mg/ml
(CONTAINS NO AZIDE...KEEP STERILE!)
­anti­CD28 (clone 37.51) we have a large amount from UCSF Ab core @ 1.0mg/ml
(CONTAINS NO AZIDE...KEEP STERILE!)

Procedure:
Coat wells with 1/100 goat anti hamster antibody in binding buffer. Place in the 37
degree TC incubator for 1 hour. Suggested volumes sufficient to cover the bottom of
                    the well yet not waste antibody are listed in the table below.
          Coating
          Volumes     After the 1 hour at 37 degrees, aspirate and rinse in PBS (use
                      about ~2X the volume you used to coat...the bicarbonate buffer
 96well   50ul        contains sodium azide, which you want to wash out)
 48well   100ul       Aspirate PBS (be careful not to let plate dry after aspiration)
 24well   250ul       Add anti‐CD3 (2C11) diluted in PBS and incubate 1 hour at 37
 12well   500ul       degrees

 6well    1ml         Aspirate and rinse in PBS (with ~2X the volume you used to
                      coat)

Dilute cells in media (1 million per ml is a good density) and add 1ug/ml soluble
CD28 Ab if costimulation is desired. Add cells+media to coated wells. Of course have
an uncoated well for a negative control.
T cell density in your culture will affect proliferation. So unpurified splenocytes
plated at 1 million per ml will proliferate less than purified T cells at the same
density. However, too dense and your culture will go acidic (yellow) before the end
of your assay (typically 3 days) which of course will negatively affect proliferation.
For simplicity, I plate unpurified or purified cells @ 1million/ml., in spite of the
issues listed above.

In a trial experiment, the CD8+ gate of unpurified splenocytes at 1 million per ml
using the above protocol proliferated as follows:

So based on this, for maximal stimulation, I would suggest a anti­CD3 coating
dose of 20ug/ml (or a 1/90 dilution of our 1.8mg/ml stock). An anti­CD3 low
dose could be 1 ug/ml, or 1/1800 of our anti­CD3 stock in PBS.
1ug/ml soluble CD28 is sufficient for maximal co‐stimulation (see below). Diluting
goat anti‐hamster below 1/100 reduces proliferation for all but the highest CD3
doses (@20ug/ml...not shown). For co­stimulation, add 1/1000 anti­CD28 to the
cells+media just before or after plating.
