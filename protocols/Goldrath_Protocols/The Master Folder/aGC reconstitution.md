1\. Dilute your 1mg in 1ml DMSO  
2\. Heat to 80C for 20 min  
3\. You should now have your stock solution. Aliquot in 10ul volumes in 1.5ml tubes.  
4\. Add 990ul to a 10ml aliquot to inject into mice. This will be a 10ug/ml working stock.  
5\. Inject 100ul into a mouse: 1ug in 100ul
