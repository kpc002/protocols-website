Purification:

1.  All left over cells resuspend in 200μl FSB (remain in 50ml conical tube)

2.  Add 2μl CD45.1 Biotin for 15min on ICE

3.  Add 10ml FBS

4.  Spin at 1800 for 5min

5.  Resuspend pellet in 150μl SA beads
    
    1.  Transfer into FACs tubes (falcon)

6.  Incubate for 30min for 4<sup>o</sup>C

7.  Add 900μl FSB

8.  Place on magnet for 5min

9.  Aspirate liquid into another tube (depleted fraction)

10. Repeat 3X

11. Add1ml FSB
    
    1.  remove 20μl for staining

12. Transfer rest into eppendorf tube

13. Count cells
    
    1.  Remove 5x10<sup>5</sup> cells for RNA
    
    2.  Spin 30seconds at 30K
    
    3.  Resuspend in 500μl Trizol. Freeze at -20 <sup>o</sup>C.

14. Remaining cells spin down and remove as much liquid as possible.
    
    1.  Freeze at –80<sup>o</sup>C

Stain in 50μl FSB

Fitc - CD45.1 \_\_\_\_\_\_\_ 1.0μl

PE – B220 \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_1.0μl

SA PerCP \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ 0.1μl

APC CD8 \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ 1.0μl

Stain for 10min at 4<sup>o</sup>C
