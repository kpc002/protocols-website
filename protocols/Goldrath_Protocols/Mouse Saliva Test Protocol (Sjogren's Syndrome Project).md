**Mouse Saliva Test Protocol**

Using Schirmer’s test and Saliva Weight test

1.  Preparing Pilocarpine solution:

> \*Note: Powdered Pilocarpine found in chemical stock
> 
> \*Note: Can prepare/store at 4°C stock solution at 5 mg Pilocarpine/mL PBS.

1.  For injections, dilute the 4°C stock solution 1:100 in ddH20 or PBS.

2.  For a 5 mL solution, do the following:
    
    1.  Add 4950 ul ddH20
    
    2.  Add 50 ul Pilocarpine (of 5 mg/mL PBS solution)

3.  Injections:
    
    1.  Use **10 ul/g body weight** of mouse.

<!-- end list -->

2.  Inject Pilocarpine solution s.c. under sterile conditions.
    
    1.  Wait at least **<span class="underline">5 minutes</span>** after pilocarpine injection before tests.

3.  Schirmer’s test (zone quick test)
    
    1.  Wipe excess saliva from mouth of mouse prior to test.
    
    2.  Using forceps, place hook of phenol thread behind the teeth and on the tongue.
    
    3.  Place mice on its side on dry surface. Time for <span class="underline">**2** **minutes **</span>duration.
    
    4.  Measure thread (in mm) according to complete red color change.

4.  Saliva Weight test (epi-kimwipe)
    
    1.  Have pre-weighed 1.5 mL eppendorf tubes filled with rolled up quarter sized kimwipes (roughly 2’’ x 2’’) ready.
    
    2.  Wipe excess saliva from mouth of mouse prior to test.
    
    3.  Using forceps, place kimwipe roll behind teeth. Place mice facedown onto dry surface. Time for **<span class="underline">5 minutes</span>** duration.
    
    4.  Post-weigh eppendorf-kimwipe tubes to calculate saliva (in mg).
