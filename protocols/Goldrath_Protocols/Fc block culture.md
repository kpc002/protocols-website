Fc blocking antibody production using 2.4G2 cell line from ATCC

Culture method: <https://www.atcc.org/Products/All/HB-197.aspx>

Media

RPMI

10%BGS

PSG+L-glutamine

Protocol

1.  Thaw the hybridoma.

2.  Resuspend in \~10 ml media to dilute DMSO.

3.  Spin down (125g, 5 min) and discard media.

4.  Resuspend in 4 ml and add to a well in a 6-well plate

5.  When the media is yellow, add more media.

6.  Grow the cell to bigger flasks (200 ml total should be enough).

\* If stock cell line is running low. Save a flask for cryopreservation.

7.  Let the cell produce anti-body till they are all dead (media should be yellow)

\*Start from 100,000 cells/ml in big flask, media should be ready to harvest after 10 days.

8.  Harvest the sup and test blocking ability with flow cytometry.
