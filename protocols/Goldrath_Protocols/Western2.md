# Western using Invitrogen System

<span class="underline"></span>

## SDS-PAGE

1.  Thaw cell pellets on ice

2.  Prepare RIPA buffer

> Add 1:100 phosphatase inhibitor set I
> 
> 1:100 phosphatase inhibitor set II
> 
> 1:500 protease inhibitor set III
> 
> (I usually take 500 µl RIPA and add 5 µl phos inhib I, 5 µl phos inhib II, and 1 µl prot inhib III)

3.  Add 20-30 μl RIPA buffer + inhibitors to each pellet (typically, for 5x10<sup>6</sup> cells, I add 25 μl)

4.  Incubate on ice 10-30 minutes

5.  Freeze/Thaw (*optional step*)

> MeOH/dry ice 3 min
> 
> 37°C water bath 3 min
> 
> Repeat 3 times

6.  Put samples in shaker (on max) at 4°C for 10 min (*optional step*)

7.  Spin at 10,000 x g for 10 minutes at 4°C

8.  Transfer supernatant to new tube

9.  Add 1 µl NuPage reducing agent and NuPage sample buffer to a final concentration of 1X (stock is 4X)

10. Heat sample to 70°C for 10 minutes in heat block

11. Load samples and molecular weight markers onto SDS-PAGE gel

12. Add running buffer to inner and outer chambers

> 50 ml 20X NuPage MES or MOPS SDS buffer
> 
> 950 ml Deionized water
> 
> (this buffer can be reused 2-3 times)

13. Run gel at 200V for 30-50 minutes

## TRANSFER

1.  Make up transfer buffer

> 50 ml 20X NuPage transfer buffer
> 
> 100 ml Methanol
> 
> 850 ml Deionized water
> 
> (this buffer can be reused 2-3 times)

2.  Cut 4 pieces of filter paper and 1 piece of PVDF membrane per gel to be transferred

3.  Soak sponges and filter paper in transfer buffer

4.  Soak PVDF membrane in

> Methanol 30 seconds
> 
> Water 1 minute
> 
> Transfer buffer 1 minute+

5.  Soak gel in transfer buffer

6.  Assemble the gel/membrane sandwich in the blot module in the following way

> Blotting pad
> 
> Blotting pad
> 
> Blotting pad
> 
> Filter paper
> 
> PVDF membrane
> 
> Gel
> 
> Filter paper
> 
> Blotting pad
> 
> Blotting pad
> 
> Cathode Core (-)

7.  Make sure to add enough blotting pads to rise 0.5 cm over the rim of the cathode core

8.  Place the anode core (+) on top of the pads and place the entire blot module in the running chamber

9.  Fill the inner chamber with transfer buffer until the gel/membrane sandwich is covered

10. Fill outer chamber with deionized water (\~650 ml)

11. Transfer at 30 V for 1½ hours

12. Wash at RT in PBST on shaker for 5’, 5’, and 10’ (change the wash buffer in between)

13. Block in 5% milk (2.5 g nonfat dry milk in 50 ml PBST) RT on shaker for 1 hour or at 4°C overnight.

14. Wash at RT in PBST on shaker for 5’, 5’, and 10’ (change the wash buffer in between)

15. Primary antibody (dilute antibody in block solution\*) – Incubate O.N. at 4°C on shaker or for 1 hour at RT on shaker

16. Wash at RT in PBST on shaker for 5’, 5’, and 10’ (change the wash buffer in between)

17. Secondary antibody (dilute antibody in block solution\*) – Incubate 1 hour at RT on shaker

18. Wash at RT in PBST on shaker for 5’, 5’, 5’, 5’, and 10’ (change the wash buffer in between)

## DETECTION

19. ECL (Amersham – ECL Plus, cat. \# RPN2132) – Mix detection solutions A and B in a ratio of 40:1 (e.g., 1 ml A + 25 μl B) on a piece of saran wrap

20. Drain excess wash buffer from membrane and place it protein side down onto the ECL mixture

21. Incubate at RT for \~4 minutes

22. Drain off excess ECL mixture and wrap blot in saran wrap

23. Expose blot to film and develop

> \*Antibodies diluted in milk can be saved and stored at -20°C for reuse.

*STRIPPING A BLOT*

Strip buffer: 80 ml 10% SDS

25 ml 1 M Tris HCl, pH 6.7

2.9 ml Beta mercaptoethanol

Bring up to 400 ml with water

> Heat buffer to 55°C - 60°C with slow stirring (I usually do this in a 600 ml beaker).
> 
> When the buffer gets to temperature, soak membrane (PVDF) in MeOH for \~30 sec and then in PBST for \~30 sec.
> 
> Leave blot in buffer (with slow stirring) for 25-30 minutes.
> 
> Wash in PBST
> 
> Continue with blocking, primary antibody, and secondary antibody.

***PBST:***

46 g Na<sub>2</sub>HPO<sub>4</sub>

11.84 g NaH<sub>2</sub>PO<sub>4</sub>

23.36 g NaCl

4 mL Tween 20

Up to 4 L with water
