Maintenance for the -80:

  - Must be defrosted at <span class="underline">minimum</span> once a year

  - The middle of the freezer must always be full

  - If freezer is not completely full, fill it with empty shelves

  - Clean the filter in the front every month
    
      - Slide out the metal guard
    
      - Slide out the filter
    
      - Wash with warm water and dry overnight
    
      - Replace filter and guard next morning

  - Clean the filter on the bottom every month
    
      - Slide out the 2inch filter under the front side of the freezer
    
      - Wash with warm water and dry
    
      - Replace the filter that same day

  - Shave off ice once a month

  - Clean the compressors and system on the side of the -80 annually
    
      - AGR will do this as part of their service

  - If it is not maintaining temperature or losing temperature quickly, call AGR Mechanical
    
      - They come out the same day or next day and will monitor the machine and give a diagnostics report

  - Service the machine <span class="underline">regularly</span>
    
      - AGR has a service where they come out and clean the machine, check the system, monitor it for an hour, and regulate the compressors, etc
    
      - $135/service or $290/4 services in one year

\*Note: Apparently our system, is the “older” version of this machine and has lower watts than the newer models, which is why it takes a little longer to get back to the correct temperature, but as told by the technician, this is expected with this model.
