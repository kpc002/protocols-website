Tail prep

1.  Cut \~1 cm mouse tail.

2.  Thaw tail juice at 37C and turn on shaker calibrated at 55C.

3.  Add 400 ul tail juice into tail sample.

4.  Shake at 55C for at least 1 hour.

5.  Add 150 ul of 5M NaCl. Spin max speed, 5 min.

6.  Transfer sup to new tube.

7.  Add 250 ul isopropanol. Invert.

8.  Pellet: max speed, 5 mins. Discard sup.

9.  Wash pellet with 500 ul 70% EtOH.

10. Spin max speed, 5 mins.

11. Discard sup and air dry pellet.

12. Resuspend in 200 ul water.
