# Cell Prep from Lymph nodes and/or Spleen

1.  Spleen and/or lymph nodes should be in \~5 ml RPMI buffer.

2.  Put a cell strainer in a 6 cm petri dish containing \~5 ml RPMI.

3.  Pour tissue into cell strainer

4.  Using the bottom of a syringe plunger, grind tissue through cell strainer

5.  Remove cell strainer and transfer RPMI + cells to a 15 ml conical

6.  Spin at 1800 rpm for 5 minutes

7.  Aspirate and discard supernatant

8.  Resuspend pellet in 1 ml Ack buffer and incubate at room temp for 1 minute

9.  Add \~13 ml RPMI and mix

10. Spin at 1800 rpm for 5 minutes

11. Aspirate and discard supernatant

12. Resuspend pellet in whatever volume RPMI and pour into a 50 ml conical through a cell strainer

13. Take an aliquot and count cells – multiply the number of cells counted in a 16 square area by 10<sup>4</sup> multiplied by your dilution factor multiplied by your total volume to give you the total number of cells

14. Spin the resuspension at 1800 rpm for 5 minutes

15. Aspirate and discard supernatant

16. Resuspend final pellet in desired media at your desired concentration
