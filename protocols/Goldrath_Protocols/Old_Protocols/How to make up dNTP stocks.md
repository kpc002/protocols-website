How to make up dNTP stocks:

1.  Take 100uL ea of dATP, dTTP, dCTP, dGTP in an eppi ( =400uL total)

2.  To dilute to 1X: add 600uL UltraPure H2O ( =1mL total volume)

3.  Aliquot out 50uL 1X dNTPs (and place in dNTP box with correct labeling on the tubes)
