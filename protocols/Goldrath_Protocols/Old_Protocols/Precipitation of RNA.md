Precipitation of RNA/DNA:

1.  Add 1 μL/100 μmL of glycogen (at 2.5 mg/mL) or 1 μmL/100 μmL of tRNA when precipitating \<10 μg/mL of product.

2.  Add 0.1 volume NaOAc 3M pH 5.3 or NH4OAc 10M, vortex.

3.  Add 2.5-3.5 volume 100% EtOH.

4.  Freeze thaw 3x at –80ºC.

5.  Spin 20 minutes maximum speed in 4ºC tabletop.

6.  Remove supernant and add 750 μL of cold 70% EtOH.

7.  Aspirate supernant carefully avoiding pellet.

8.  Dry briefly.
