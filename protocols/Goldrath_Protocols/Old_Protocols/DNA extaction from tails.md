# Extraction of DNA from mouse tails

1.  Place small piece of tail or ear in 400 μl of tail juice and incubate overnight at 55° C. Vortex and proceed to step 3.

2\. If frozen, thaw sample in incubator.

3.  Add phenol-chloroform IMA (25:24:1) to tail juice mixture at approximately 1:1 ratio (\~400μl) in fume hood, mix well.

4.  Spin samples in centrifuge for 5 min. at 12,000 rpm (room temperature).

5.  Add 100μl TE (pH 8.0) to one well for every sample in a clean 96 well plate.

> 6\. Obtain a new set of eppendorf tubes and pipette in \~400 μl of ethanol.

7.  Remove tubes from centrifuge and pipette top layer (aqueous-phase DNA) into the tubes with ethanol, avoiding <span class="underline">any</span> phenol layer or interface (sludge).

8.  Mix tubes with DNA + ethanol until white clumps of DNA are visible.

9.  Use pipette tip to remove each clump of DNA into a well containing the TE. If difficult to remove DNA, centrifuge in order to force the DNA to pellet.

10. Cover wells and seal sides with tape until further use. 4° C for near term or -20° C for longer storage.

11. Dispose of phenol-chloroform in container in fume hood, return to refrigerator.
