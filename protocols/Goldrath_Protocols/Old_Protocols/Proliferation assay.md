Proliferation assay: T cell activation and CFSE labeling

We usually do this assay before ordering bulk FBS so as to pick the best lot number.

1.  > Get naive spleen(s) from young mouse, process, and Ack .

2.  > Coat 96-well plate with goat anti-hamster and anti-CD3

<!-- end list -->

  - Goat anti-hamster: dilute the ab in PBS at 1:50 (100 ul per well)

> Pipet 100 ul to the well and swirl
> 
> Incubate at 37C for at least 1 hr
> 
> Flick off the liquid and wash with PBS twice

\- anti-CD3: repeat the same thing but use anti-CD (1:1000)

leave in 4C for prolonged storage

3.  > Enrich for T cells

<!-- end list -->

  - follow MACS Streptavidin Microbeads protocol online

  - for biotinylated ab cocktail, use: NK1.1, F4/80, MHCII, B220, CD11b, and TER-119 at 1:200 and 500ul per spleen

<!-- end list -->

4.  > Labeling T cells with CFSE

> \- Save some cells for unstained control and compensation set up

  - follow “Labeling T cells with CFSE” protocol on Goldrath server (skip step 5)

<!-- end list -->

5.  > Plate the labeled cells in coated plate

100 ul of stained cells in testing media (1 mil cell/ml)

\+ 100ul of testing media with 2X anti-CD28 (1:1000)

6.  > Record CFSE signal for 0hr, 24 hr, 48hr, 72hr, and 96hr on FITC channel. Suggested stain set

> FITC\* CFSE (for detection only)
> 
> eF450 CD8a
> 
> PE Cy7 CD4
> 
> APC Cy7 CD44

\*Cell at 0 hr is very bright in FITC adjust PMT so that FITC peak is at maximum.

After 24 hr, FITC brightness will reduce but you should still have a single peak. This peak should represent your undivided T cells.
