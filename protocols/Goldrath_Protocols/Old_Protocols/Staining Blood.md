Staining Blood:

1.  Spin blood tubes at 5000 RPM for 5 minutes

2.  Suck off PBS (clear supernatant)

3.  Resuspend blood pellet in 1x Ack; Incubate \~10 minutes at room temperature

4.  Spin at 5000 RPM for 5 minutes (should see a white pellet)

5.  Suck off supernatant leaving a white pellet behind

6.  Resuspend pellet in 150 microliters of FACs buffer and trasfer to a “v”-bottom plate

7.  Spin the plate at 2000 RPM for 1 minute; flick the plate to get rid of supernatant

8.  Add 50 microliters of stain to each well and mix with pipette

9.  Incubate the plate for about 20 minutes on ice

10. Add 150 microliters of FACs buffer to the well; spin at 2000 RPM for 1 minute and flick the plate

11. Resuspend pellet in 150 microliters of FACs buffer; spin 2000 RPM for 1 minute and flick the plate

12. Resuspend the pellet in 150 microliters FACs buffer and transfer to the FACs tubes

13. Analyze the samples on the FACs machine
