# Phenotyping:

1.  Take 2 x 10<sup>6</sup> cells (50 μl) from the 4 x 10<sup>7</sup> cell suspension from the IFNγ cell prep and plate on 96 well plate (based on your map)

2.  Spin 2000 rpm for 3 minutes

3.  Flick solution out to just leave the pellet

4.  FC block
    
    1.  Add 25 μl FSB to samples that are NOT to be FC blocked
    
    2.  Make FC block and add 25 μl to all other wells
        
        1.  25 μl FSB per sample
        
        2.  0.25 μl FC block per sample

5.  Incubate at 4°C for 5 minutes

6.  Biotin staining
    
    1.  Add 25 μl FSB to all single color controls EXCEPT SA PerCP
    
    2.  Make Biotin stain and add 25 μl to all other wells
        
        1.  25 μl FSB per sample
        
        2.  \~0.25 μl CD45.1 biotin per sample

7.  Incubate at 4°C for 15 minutes

8.  Wash
    
    1.  Add 150 μl FSB to each well
    
    2.  Spin 2000 rpm for 3 minutes
    
    3.  Flick out solution
    
    4.  Resuspend pellet in 250 μl FSB
    
    5.  Spin 2000 rpm for 3 minutes
    
    6.  Flick out solution

9.  Staining
    
    1.  Add 50 μl FSB to each single color control along with \~0.25 μl Ab
    
    2.  Add 50 μl master mix to appropriate wells (according to map)

10. Incubate at 4°C for 15 minutes

11. Wash
    
    1.  Add 150 μl FSB to each well
    
    2.  Spin 2000 rpm for 3 minutes
    
    3.  Flick out solution
    
    4.  Resuspend pellet in 250 μl FSB
    
    5.  Spin 2000 rpm for 3 minutes
    
    6.  Flick out solution

12. Resuspend final pellet in 200 μl FACS fix buffer

13. Store at 4°C

MASTER MIXES:

|     |            |         |       |     |
| --- | ---------- | ------- | ----- | --- |
|     | FITC       | PE      | PerCP | APC |
| 1   | CD44       | CD62L   | SA    | CD8 |
| 2   | Ly6C       | CD122   | SA    | CD8 |
| 3   | CD44       | CD127   | SA    | CD8 |
| 4   | Vα2        | CD5     | SA    | CD8 |
| 5   | CD62L      | CD27    | SA    | CD8 |
| 6\* | B220/CD45R | CD16/32 | SA    | CD8 |

\*DO NOT FC BLOCK SAMPLES THAT WILL BE STAINED WITH MM \#6\!\!\!\!\!\!\!
