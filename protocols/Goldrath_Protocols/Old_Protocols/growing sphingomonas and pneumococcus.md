*S. pneumoniae* URF918 cultured in Todd-Hewitt broth (BD) at 37 °C in an incubator at 5% CO<sub>2</sub> were collected at a mid-log phase and then washed twice in PBS. For induction of pulmonary infection, mice were anesthetized with isoflurane and restrained on a small board. Mice were inoculated with *S. pneumoniae* (1 × 10<sup>6</sup> to 1 × 10<sup>7</sup> colony-forming units in a volume of 50 μl per mouse) by insertion of a 24-gauge catheter into the trachea. For induction of systemic infection, *S. pneumoniae* (1 × 10<sup>7</sup> colony-forming units) were injected intravenously into mice. At 6 h (spleen) spleen cells were collected and CD19<sup>−</sup> cells positive for the α-GalCer-loaded CD1d tetramer were analyzed immediately after isolation for intracellular cytokines without restimulation or pretreatment with brefeldin. Bacteria is plated at 100 μl on 5% (vol/vol) sheep blood Mueller-Hinton agar plates and cultured for 18 h, followed by colony counting.

## Now I used 50ml media grown for 6-7 hrs inoculated with 50ml of an overnight 12 hr growth from single colony. I suggest you do two 50 ml cultures since a lot of bacteria is lost in the wash steps. I spin 3600 rpm on table top centrifuge for 20 minutes at 4C. Then I use a BD BBL™ CrystalSpec™ Nephelometer

*N. aromaticivorans* (ATCC 700278), and *E. coli* (DH5α and ATCC 25922) or other Ecoli strain as control were grown overnight in Mueller Hinton broth, diluted in fresh medium, grown for 8 hr at 37°C to an OD of 0.5 at 600 nm, washed, and diluted in PBS. Bacterial suspension (100 μl) containing 5 × 10<sup>7</sup>*N. aromaticivorans*, 5 × 10<sup>7</sup>*E. col*i was injected intravenously into 4- to 20-week-old mice.

You can look at 6 hrs here as well. Most of my experiments were at 12 hrs for these. You will probably need 10 fold more S. yanoikuyae or S. paucimobilis to get good IFNgamma. There were never any other cytokines but good upregulation of CD25 and CD69. Always use CD44 to get rid of non specific staining together with any stain.
