Plate \#2 cont.

All samples 2 tubes, \#1 primary biotinylated ab. \#2 directly conjugated abs plus SA-PETR

1\) place 2x10<sup>6</sup> cells = 50 μL of 4x106 cells/μL in each well.

2\) add 5μL/sample Fc block each well, mix and let sit on ice or room temp.

3\) add 25μL of biotinylated antibody in FACs buffer

4\) incubate for 20 minutes on ice, add 150μL of FACs buffer, spin 3-4 minutes at 2000rpm, flick , wash again in 200μL.

5\) add 50μL/sample of secondary mix containing directly conjugated abs plus SA-PETR, pipet up and down in each well. **Incubate at ROOM TEMP for 30 minutes.**

6\) wash 2x as before, fix in 250μL/sample fix.
