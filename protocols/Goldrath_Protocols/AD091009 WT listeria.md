Goldrath Lab, September 10, 2009
Andrew D.
We have stocks of WT listeria. Obtained from Hedrick lab in one of Warrens old boxes through Fang.
The tube of listeria is shown below. I grew up this stock in TSB with no antibiotics and froze it down
in filter sterilzed 40% final glycerol in eppi tubes with green lines on cap. These 100ul aliquots are
can be found in the green box, top shelf of -80, as shown below. Note this strain is erythromycin sensitive,
as can be seen in the photo below of o/n cultures with no bacteria, or with bacteria - or + 4ul of our erythromycin stock.
