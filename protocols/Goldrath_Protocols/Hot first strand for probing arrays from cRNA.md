Hot first strand for probing arrays from cRNA:

2 μg of amplified cRNA in 2 μL

3 μL of random hexamers 150 ng total

70°C for 5 minutes

cool on ice, leave at room temp for 10'

\= 5 μL

Premix:

6.0 μL 5X first strand buffer

3.0 μL DTT 0.1M

1.5 μL dCTG 10mM

  0.6 μL 240μM dATP

12.0 μL αdATP-<sup>33</sup>P

2.0 μL Superscript II

<span class="underline"> 0.5 μL</span> RNAsin

 25.6 μL + 5 μL=30.6 μL, close enough for government work

42°C for 1 hour

Purify probe over column, count 1 μL with 1 mL of scintillation fluid.

Boil probes for 5 minutes, cool on ice, and add approximately equivalent amounts by cpm of each probe to filters which have been prehybing at 65°C for at least an hour. Hyb overnight.

Hyb: <span class="underline">for 50 mL use</span>

0.25 M NaPhosphate pH7.2 12.5 mL of 1 M

250 mM NaCl 2.5 mL of 5 M

5% SDS 12.5 mL of 20%

1 mM EDTA 0.1 mL of 0.5 M

1% BSA 0.5 g

dissolve at 37°C, sterile filter, store at 4°C

just before use add ssDNA which has been boiled for 5 minutes, on ice until added

100 mg/mL ssDNA 0.5 mL of 10mg/mL

Wash 3X 5 minutes 2X SSC 1% SDS. 25 or so mL each room temp(500mL, 25mL of 20% SDS and 50 mL of 20XSSC)

Wash 2X 20 minutes 0.2XSSC 1% SDS 100 mLs each at 60-65°C (500mL, 25mL of 20% SDS and 5 mL of 20XSSC)

Rinse several times in 5X SSC to remove all traces of SDS ie bubbles
