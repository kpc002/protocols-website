Southern Protocol

1.  **Make template DNA for Probe**
    
    1.  5’Probe \#3:

> \-PCR amplification:

|                       |         |
| --------------------- | ------- |
| H<sub>2</sub>O        | 13.5 ul |
| 5X buffer             | 5 ul    |
| 25mM MgCl<sub>2</sub> | 3 ul    |
| 10um dNTPs            | .5 ul   |
| 10 uM F oligo         | 1.25 ul |
| 10 uM R oligo         | 1.25 ul |
| DNA (100ng)           | .2 ul   |
| Taq                   | .25 ul  |

> Use program probe (94C 3min, 94C 30sec, 58C 30sec, 72C 1min, 72C 5min, 4C forever)
> 
> Run out on gel, remove band
> 
> \-Restriction digest from pTOPO vector:
> 
> use Alison’s CsCl tube from –20C

|                |       |
| -------------- | ----- |
| H<sub>2</sub>O | 4 ul  |
| BSA            | 4 ul  |
| 10X Buffer     | 4 ul  |
| EcoRI          | 3 ul  |
| vector         | 25 ul |

> Incubate 1 hr at 37C, run out on gel, cut out smaller band

2.  5’P2 (3’ probe)

> \-PCR amplification:

|                       |         |
| --------------------- | ------- |
| H<sub>2</sub>O        | 13.5 ul |
| 5X buffer             | 5 ul    |
| 25mM MgCl<sub>2</sub> | 3 ul    |
| 10um dNTPs            | .5 ul   |
| 10 uM F oligo         | 1.25 ul |
| 10 uM R oligo         | 1.25 ul |
| DNA (100ng)           | .2 ul   |
| Taq                   | .25 ul  |

> Use program probe (94C 3min, 94C 30sec, 58C 30sec, 72C 1min, 72C 5min, 4C forever)
> 
> Run out on gel, remove band

\-test concentration using UV spec

2.  **Restriction Digest of sample**
    
    1.  Use HindIII for 3’ southern and NcoI for 5’ southern

|                     |                |                     |        |
| ------------------- | -------------- | ------------------- | ------ |
| HindIII digestion   | NcoI digestion |                     |        |
| 10X buffer (NE \#2) | 3.5 ul         | 10X buffer (NE \#3) | 3.5 ul |
| Enzyme              | 1.5 ul         | Enzyme              | 1.5 ul |
| H<sub>2</sub>O      | 25 ul          | H<sub>2</sub>O      | 25 ul  |
| DNA (10ug)          | 5 ul           | DNA (10ug)          | 5 ul   |

> Incubate 8 hrs at 37C (no more to prevent NcoI star activity)

3.  **Run out gel**

> \-Pour 0.8% gel (measure out 400ml of TAE and add 20ul EtBr) in a large mold with one row of wells at the top
> 
> \-run at 40V overnight

4.  **Southern Blot Transfer**

> \-take a picture of the gel to make sure restriction digest worked (should see a smear of DNA, not bands)
> 
> \-cut out relevant part of gel, measure size, mark top left corner
> 
> \-rotate for 5 min in 0.2N HCl (no longer\!), must be made fresh\!
> 
> \-wash with DI water
> 
> \-rotate for 20 min with 0.4M NaOH
> 
> \-set up blot:

1.  4 squares chromatography paper (1 cm bigger than gel) dry

2.  2 squares chromatography paper (1 cm bigger) wet in NaOH

3.  Hybond-xl membrane (exact size of gel) wet in NaOH (corner marked same as gel)

4.  Gel (don’t need to invert)

> \-surround gel with parafilm to prevent short circuiting

5.  3 squares chromatography paper (exact size) wet in NaOH

6.  two long wicks, with one end in dish holding NaOH, and tapered ends that rest on top of the gel

7.  weight

> \-allow transfer to occur for 1.5-2 hours (ok to go over, membrane is charged to hold DNA-\> also means no need to crosslink)

5.  **Probe Preparation**
    
    1.  denature probe:

> \-heat the tailshaker up to 98C
> 
> 50ng probe template DNA
> 
> 0.5ng lamda HindIII digestion DNA (for ladder labeling)
> 
> 10ul random nonmers
> 
> add H<sub>2</sub>O to 34ul
> 
> \-incubate 10 min at 98C
> 
> \-don’t forget to also denature sheared salmon sperm DNA (25ul/blot)
> 
> \-(while waiting heat up hyb ovens to desired Temp: 68C for 5’P2 and 65C for 5’ probe \#3)
> 
> \-cool on ice 1 min

2.  Radiolabel probe

> \-Add: 10ul 5X dCTP buffer
> 
> 1 ul Klenow
> 
> 5ul P32 dCTP
> 
> \-incubate 10 minutes at 37C
> 
> \-add 2ul Stop mix
> 
> \-clean on G50 spin colums:
> 
> \-vortex tubes before use
> 
> \-break off botton and loosen cap
> 
> \-spin at 3K rpm for 1 min into collection tube to get rid of liquid
> 
> and form column
> 
> \-toss flow through and collection tube, replace with labeled eppie
> 
> with 2.5ul 2M NaoOH
> 
> \-add probe mix directly to column
> 
> \-spin 3Krpm for 2 min
> 
> \-toss column and keep probe in eppie (can leave at RT until
> 
> membrane is ready)

6.  **Hybridization of probe to membrane**

> \-after the blotting is finished remove chromatography paper and gel
> 
> \-add 10 ml quikhyb solution hyb tube (incubate in oven while waiting for blot to finish to prewarm)
> 
> \-add 25ul denatures salmon sperm DNA and coat sides of tube
> 
> \-roll membrane and put in tube (DNA side facing in), try and keep edges from overlapping
> 
> \-prehyb for 30min in hyb oven
> 
> \-add radiolabeled probe to hyb tube (wash with 1mL quikhyb from tube)
> 
> \-incubate rotating for 1 hr
> 
> \-put plastic shields in front of ovens

7.  **Washing the membrane**

> \-pour off quikhyb buffer (blotting the rim to prevent spills) and add \~10 mL 2X SSC 0.1% SDS, rotate 10 min
> 
> \-repeat wash with 2X SSC 0.1% SDS, twice with 0.2X SSC 0.1% SDS and twice with 0.1 SSC 0.1% SDS
> 
> \-during the last wash blank the phosphoimager screen
> 
> \-pour off remaining wash solution, remove membrane and place DNA side down on a piece of saran wrap, cover the back of the membrane completely, folding all sides in to prevent evaporation and contamination
> 
> \-tape blot to the phosphoimager screen (on the side with the grid) and add the blank side
> 
> \-store for 72-98hrs for good exposure

8.  **Reading Blots**

> \-Use imager in the Aria room
> 
> \-open typhoon program (change acquisition mode to storage:phospho)
> 
> \-run and save (remember which squares your blots are on)

9.  **PRAY\!\!\!**
