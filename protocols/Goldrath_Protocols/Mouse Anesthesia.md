**Mouse Anesthesia**

Using Ketamine-Xylazine

  - Prepare anesthetic solution under sterile conditions:

> draw 200ul of ketamine solution (100mg/ml) and
> 
> 100ul of xylazine solution (20mg/ml) using an insulin syringe.
> 
> Add 1700ul of dd H2O (-\> 10ug/ul Ketamine, 1ug/ul Xylazine)
> 
> Aliquot in two Eppi tubes, store at 4C

  - For shorter duration of anesthesia dilute anesthetic solution with another 500ul of ddH2O (-\> 8ug/ul Ketamine, 0.8ug/ul Xylazine)

  - Prior to anesthesia weigh mouse. Prepare 10ul of anesthetic solution for each g of body weight.

  - Inject i.p. or s.c.

  - Monitor respiration and fill out Post-Anesthetic recovery record. Stay with mouse until it fully recovers (takes about 2h after injection\!)

Ketamine: used 80-100mg/kg

Xylazine: used 5-10mg/kg
