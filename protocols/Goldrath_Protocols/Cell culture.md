# Cell Culture

> First you need to find out what media your cells need to grow in and whether your cells are adherent or not.

## SPLITTING (PASSING) CELLS

1.  Aspirate and discard media (only if your cells are adherent to the bottom)

2.  Wash cells with PBS

3.  Add minimum amount of trypsin (just enough to lightly cover the bottom of the flask or plate), shake, and put back at 37°C/6% CO<sub>2</sub> incubator

4.  After about 5 minutes, once all the cells have been released from the bottom, add media and transfer cell suspension to a conical tube

5.  Spin 1800 rpm for 5 minutes

6.  Aspirate and discard supernatant

7.  Resuspend cells in some volume of media

8.  Count (if required)

9.  Plate cells at desired concentration

10. Put in 37°C/6% CO<sub>2</sub> incubator

## FREEZING CELLS

1.  Freezing media = 20% serum and 10% DMSO in media (store in aliquots at -20°C)

2.  Count cells and resuspend cells in freezing media such that they are at 4 x 10<sup>6</sup> cells/ml

3.  Aliquot into cryotubes up to \~1.5 ml

4.  Put at -80°C overnight

5.  Put tubes in liquid nitrogen storage
