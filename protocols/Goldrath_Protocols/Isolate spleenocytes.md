Isolate spleenocytes

1.  Harvest mouse spleen in eppie tube containing cell media.

2.  Fill petri dish with 5 ml T cell media (TCM).

3.  Transfer spleen into petri dish.

4.  Use rough sides of 2 microscope slides to grind the spleen.

\*tips: keep the 2 slides at 90 angle to avoid too much capillary action

5.  Rinse slides with 1 ml TCM before discarding.

6.  Pipet up and down to dissociate cells

7.  Transfer to 15 ml conical tube.

8.  Rinse petri dish with 1 ml TCM and transfer to conical. Discard dish.

9.  After a few seconds, debris will settle.

10. Transfer sup to a new conical.

11. Filter through nitrex (70 um)

12. Spin down at 1600 rpm, 4 min. Discard sup.

13. Ack pellet using 2 ml Ack for 7 mins.

14. Fill up the tube with TCM and spin down (same settings). White pellet should be visible.

15. Resuspend in 1 ml TCM.

16. Mix 10 ul of cells in 10 ml sheath for counting.
