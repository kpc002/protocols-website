![](media/image1.wmf)

<span class="underline">Injection of 10<sup>5</sup> PFU of VSV-ova yields good CD8+ T cell response</span>. Mice were adoptively transferred with 40,000 OT-1 T cells. Approximately 6 weeks later, mice were infected intravenously with the indicated amount of VSV-ova. PBL was collected at the times indicated, and donor OT-1 cells were identified by flow cytometric analysis of CD8 and CD45.1 expression.
