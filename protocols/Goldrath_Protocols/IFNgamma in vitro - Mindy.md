# In vitro IFN-gamma:

1)  Count cells and resuspend at 4x10<sup>7</sup> cells/ml

2)  Plate 4x10<sup>6</sup> cells in 24 well dish, 100μL of 4x10<sup>7</sup> cells/mL (2 wells per sample).

3)  Add 1 mL of RP10 to unstimulated cells.

4)  Add 1 ml RP10 plus 10 nM OVAp (10<sup>-8</sup>M) to stimulated cells.

> Peptide stock at 1mM, do 1:1000, 10μL in 10mLs = 10<sup>-6</sup>M
> 
> Then do 1:100, 120μL in 12mLs = 10<sup>-8</sup>M
> 
> or
> 
> Keep at stock of the 10<sup>-6</sup>M in freezer diluted in PBS.
> 
> Add 100 μl of OVA stock to 1 ml RP10 per sample.

5)  > Incubate at 37 for 2-3 hours.

6)  > Mix 7.3 μl golgi stop with 100 μl RP10 and add 10 μl to each well.

7)  > Incubate at 37 for another 3 hours.

8)  > Harvest into eppendorf tubes and spin at max for 1 minute.

9)  > Mix 0.25 μl FC block in 25μl FSB per sample and add 25 μl to each sample.

10) > Incubate at RT for 5 minutes.

11) > Add 0.5 μl \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ biotin antibody to each sample.

12) > Incubate on ice for 10-15 minutes.

13) > Wash in 1.5 ml FSB.

| \# of samples: | FITC | PE | PerCP | APC | FSB |
| μl/sample      |      |    |       |     |     |

14) > Add 25 μl of stain to each sample and incubate on ice for 10-15 minutes.

15) > Wash 1x in 1.5 ml FSB.

16) > Add 250μL fix/perm, incubate 30 mintues at RT in dark.

17) > Wash twice in 1 mL of cytowash/perm (diluted in H2O as per Pharmingen).

18) > Resuspend in 25μL/sample diluted Fc block (0.25μL/sample Fc and 25μL/sample cytowash/perm, 5 minutes room temp.

19) > Add 2μL/sample anti-IFNgamm PE in 25μL/sample cytowash/perm.

20) > Incubate for 20-30 minutes at 4C, wash 1x 1.5mLs cytowash/perm.

21) > Resuspend in 500μL/sample Fixative.
