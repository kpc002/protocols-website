**Preparation of Il-15 complexes for injection**

Sterile 0.1% BSA (w/v in PBS) solution

Recombinant Human IL-15 (R\&D 247-IL-005/CF), make 100ng/ul aliquots. Store at -80C

Recombinant Human IL-15 R alpha Fc Chimera (R\&D 147-IR-100), dilute to 0.5ug/ul. Store at 4C.

For 1 dose add 5ul of IL-15 (100ng/ul) to 4.7ul sIL-15Ra-Fc (0.5ug/ul) and incubate at 37C for 30 minutes.

Add 190ul of sterile PBS containing 0.1% BSA and inject i.p. or i.v.
