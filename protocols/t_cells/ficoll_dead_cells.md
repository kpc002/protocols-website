---
title: Ficoll protocol for removing dead cells
order: 18
---

Usually used when culturing cells in vitro & prior to transfer in vitro

1.  Condense *in vitro* cell culture into 5ml media by spinning cells down and removing excess media (do not condense more than 50ml to 5ml, or you will overload your gradient) and transfer into a 15ml conical tube. After removing media, make sure cells are well resuspended in remaining 5ml media.
2.  Gently layer 2ml Lymphocyte Separation Media (aka Ficoll -- in Hedrick TC fridge) into the bottom of the 15ml conical, being careful to keep a distinct interface between the media and Lymphocyte Separation Media by pipetting slowly and preventing air bubbles. Can also use glass pipette like we use for percoll to layer in ficoll.
3.  Spin cells at 2000 RPM for 15 minutes in chilled centrifuge.
4.  After the spin, the live cells can be found at the interface between the media and Lymphocyte Separation Media, while the dead cells will be found at the bottom of the tube. Carefully pipette live cells into a fresh tube, wash cells with additional media, and spin down again.
