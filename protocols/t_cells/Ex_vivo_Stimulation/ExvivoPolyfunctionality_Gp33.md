**<span class="underline">*Ex vivo* peptide re-stimulation to assess cytokine production by antigen-specific CD8 T cells</span>**

LLB 2020

1.  Whole splenocytes (whole blood or tissue) - maximum concentration of 1x10^7 cells/ml (I plate 2x10^6 cells in 200ul). Doing a higher concentration won't work.

2.  Re-suspend cells in:
    
    1.  **TCM + 1ug/ml of GP33-41 peptide + GolgiPlug (1:1000) + 1:100 of anti-CD107a** (conjugated to your preferred fluorophore) and incubate at 37C for 5h.
    
    2.  Make the negative control (no peptide) by pooling a little bit of cells from all samples: **TCM + 1ug/ml Brefeldin A + 1:100 of anti-CD107a**

3.  After 5h;
    
    1.  Spin down flick to remove supernatant
    
    2.  Stain with live/dead stain + anti-CD8 (and congenic markers etc) in PBS1x for 10-15min at RT.
    
    3.  Wash and re-suspend in Cytofix/Cytoperm (BD kit) 20 min @4C (in the fridge).

4.  Wash off fix, and resuspend in FACS buffer. \*Cells can be left in the fridge overnight (or for 2-3 days until acquisition).

5.  On the day of acquisition, spin down and remove PBS/facs buffer.

6.  Re-suspend cells in Perm Buffer 1X (eBioscience kit/FoxP3 kit) containing:
    
    1.  anti-IFNg (1/100)
    
    2.  anti-TNFa (1/100)
    
    3.  anti-IL-2 (1/100) – PE (\*Important: IL-2 only works in PE)

7.  Stain for 30min at 4C

8.  Wash off stain, and re-suspend in volume for acquisition
