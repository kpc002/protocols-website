**Altered peptide ligand in vitro stim of OT-I**

**Day 0**

**Harvest and baseline flow**

1.  Harvest splenocytes from OT-I mice

2.  Stain and flow the cells for baseline, zero time.

3.  Stain the cells with Live/Dead dye at 1:1000 dilution, for 15 min at RT. Wash 2x. Prepare a tube for compensation: overheating the cells (\~0.5 million) at 95°C for 3 min, then stain with Live/Dead dye.

4.  Stain all cells with surface antibodies mixture for 30 min at 4C. Wash 2x

> Surface stain master mix:

  - CD4 – BUV496 (1:100)

  - CD8 – BUV805 (1:100)

  - L/D/CD14/19 – BV510 (CD14/19-1:200)

  - CD3 – BUV737 (1:100)

  - CD44 – APC-Cy7 (1:400)

  - CD62L – PE-Cy7 (1:100)

  - CD69 – BV711 (1:100)

  - CD25 – FITC (1:100)

  - FCGRIIB – APC-Strep (1:200)

  - TIGIT – PE (1:100)

<!-- end list -->

5.  Add the counting beads, 10,000/50ul/sample.

**In vitro stimulation (N4, APL, unstim)**

1.  Use OT-I splenocytes at a concentration of 10X10<sup>6</sup> /ml, and plate 3X10<sup>6</sup> /300ul in 24-well plate in R10. 37°C, 5%CO2, for 4 days.

> The cells will be stimulated with the OVA peptides, SII**N**FEKL and APLs (altered peptide ligands): Q4, T4 and V4 (1nM) or unstimulated. The same setting is for each of WT OT-I, KO OT-I splenocytes. You need around total 60 million cells for one 24-well plate.

**Days 1, 2, 3, 4**

1.  Harvest the cells at the days 1, 2, 3, and 4 and stain the cells with 4 panels, split the cells from one well to 4 parts and method as the above. All tetramers are PE color, so we must have 4 panels.

2.  Stain the cells with Live/Dead dye at 1:1000 dilution, for 20 min at RT. Wash 2x. Prepare a tube for compensation: overheating the cells (\~0.5 million) at 95°C for 3 min, then stain with Live/Dead dye.

3.  Stain all cells with surface antibodies mixture for 30 min at 4C. Wash 2x

> Surface stain master mix:

  - CD4 – BUV496 (1:100)

  - CD8 – BUV805 (1:100)

  - L/D/CD14/19 – BV510 (CD14/19-1:200)

  - CD3 – BUV737 (1:100)

  - CD44 – APC-Cy7 (1:400)

  - CD62L – PE-Cy7 (1:100)

  - CD69 – BV711 (1:100)

  - CD25 – FITC (1:100)

  - FCGRIIB – APC-Strep (1:200)

  - Tetramers – PE (N4, Q4, T4, V4)

<!-- end list -->

4.  Add the counting beads, 10,000/50ul/sample.
