**Intracellular cytokine stain/peptide restim**

*Reconstitute peptide*

1.  Dissolve gp33-41 in DMSO. If 1 mg of peptide, reconstitute peptide in 250 ul media to get 4 mg/ml

*Lymph node and spleen collection*

1.  Take all lymph nodes and spleen

2.  Process through 70 and 40 um filters

3.  Flow stain or start peptide stim

4.  Count cells

5.  To start peptide stim, plate 5% of spleen (250 ul from 5 ml of spleen resuspended in media) into well in 96 well u-bottom plate

6.  If some samples are a little lower or higher, this is okay. Use a standard volume across samples (use cell count to relate back to spleen)

7.  Spin plate at 1500 RPM for 3 min

*Make stim media (gp33 + Golgi Plug)*

1.  Calculate the amount of media you will need by multiplying your number of samples by 200 ul. Account for unstim here. You can make an unstim sample for each mouse (just golgi plug in media no gp33) or a pooled unstim sample per tissue.

2.  Add Golgi Plug to media at 1:500 (500x cocktail)

3.  Partion off the amount you will need for stim vs unstim into two separate tubes

4.  To the stim tube, add peptide. To the unstim tube, add nothing.

*Prepare peptide for stim*

1.  When time to use the peptide, make an intermediate dilution of 1:10 with media to get to 0.4 mg/ml (0.4 ug/ul

2.  Then, add 1 ul (at 0.4 mg/ml) for every 1 ml of media to bring final concentration to 0.4 ug/ml

3.  Flick plate and resuspend in media supplemented with gp33 + golgi plug (stim samples) or golgi plug (unstim samples)

4.  Incubate at 37C for 4-6 hours
