**Ex vivo peptide restim: OVA/OT-I**

**Plate cells from dLN, tumor, spleen**

1.  Plate cells. 2 million cells per sample is a good starting point. Add spleen (\~5% which is 250 of 5 ml), lymph nodes (all), tumors (all). Plate however many plates you need according to map. Remember to include unstim wells as these will be the best control for cytokines.

2.  Spin plate at 1500 RPM for 3 min and flick. Resuspend in R10 +/- peptide detailed below.

3.  Proceed with ICCS plate first and setting up ex vivo stim and then while ICCS plate is incubating, proceed with surface staining

**Ex vivo stimulation **

Reagents needed:

  - Golgi plug (Golgi plug has BFA and Golgi stop has monensin)

  - OVA 257-264 peptide

  - R10 media

<!-- end list -->

1.  > Make up dilution of R10 +stim condition (no stim, OVA257-264 peptide)

2.  > Make enough for 200 ul per well (R10 + golgi plug for unstim controls, R10 + golgi plug + peptide for experimental wells, or R10 + golgi plug + PMA for positive control).

3.  > Add in a 1:1000 dilution of Golgi Plug into media before distributing to three groups. I have 10 mls for each condition, so 30 mls total (e.g. add 30 ul of GP into media and then divide into each condition)

<!-- end list -->

  - Unstim: do not add peptide but should have golgi plug

  - For OVA 257-264: From -80 stock make an intermediate 1:1000 dilution in R10 to go from 3 mM to 3 uM (e.g. 3 ul into 3 mls). Then add into media as a 1:300 solution (e.g. add 33.3 ul of 1:1000 solution into 10 mls of R10) 

<!-- end list -->

4.  > Add 200uL of each master mix to appropriate wells. Mix well. 

5.  > Incubate cells at 37 for 4 hours. 

6.  > During 4-hour incubation, continue protocol with other panels. After 4-hour incubation, proceed with protocol with surface staining + ICCS plate.
