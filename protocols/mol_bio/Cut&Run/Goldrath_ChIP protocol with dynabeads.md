Goldrath Lab ChIP Protocol

**Fixation:**

\-Harvest cells (between 5-10 million the more the better) and place them in 10mL media or PBS

\-Add 270ul 37% formaldehyde (to make 1% formaldehyde) and incubate (better rotate) for 10 minutes at RT

\-Stop the reaction with 500ul 2.5M glycine (to make .125M glycine) and incubate (better rotate) for 5 minutes at RT

\-Spin the cells down and wash once with ice cold 10 mL PBS. You can flash freeze the cells at this point if you wish to sonicate at a later time.

\-Spin again and resuspend in 300ul Lysis Buffer. Add 3ul each of fresh protease inhibitors (PIC cocktail \[100x\] and benzamidine\[0.1M\]) to the solution. Incubate on ice for 5 minutes.

**Sonication:**

\-Sonicate 20 cycles 30s on/off by Bioruptor-plus \[10 cycles 2x with a brief jostle in between\]

\-Spin down sonicated lysate (5min at 13K rpm 4°C) to get rid of debris. Put the supernatant (the lysate) in a new eppendorf tube. The lysate can be stored at -80°C or used immediately.

\-To test the sonication efficiency take 30ul of lysate. Add 100ul of NFW and incubate overnight at 65°C to reverse the crosslinks (shake for the first hour).

\-Add 1 ul RNase A (stock at 100mg/ml) and incubate at 37C for 30 minutes. Add 2ul proteinase K (stock at 20mg/ml) and incubate at 55C for 1 hour.

\-Extract DNA using Zymo DNA Clean & Concentrate kit. (DNA fragment)

\-Measure DNA with nanodrop

\-run 10ul of DNA on a 1% agarose gel to test sonication (should see a smear around 300-500 bp).

\-Can use the remaining DNA as input

**Binding Ab to dynabeads for CHIP (4 hours or overnight)**

\-Make Blocking solution: 0.5%w/v BSA in PBS

\-Suspend the dynabeads well

\-Transfer 30uL of suspension (per ChIP) into a 1.5mL EP tube containing 1mL blocking solution

\-Rotate to wash the beads with the blocking soluton for 5 minutes

\-Place the tube on the magnet then remove the supernatant by pipette

\-Repeat wash

\-suspend the beads with 200uL Blocking solution

\-Add Ab and rotate at 4 degree for more than 4 hours or O/N

\-Wash the beads twice with blocking buffer and then WBI buffer

**Immunoprecipitation:**

\-use 5-10ug of DNA/Immunoprecipitation (using more can decrease the efficiency of the pulldown). (Around 2-2.5x10<sup>6</sup> cells)

\-Dilute with 9 volumes of Dilution Buffer. Add protease inhibitors (PIC cocktail and benzamidine) to the solution.

\-Add it to washed dynabeads and rotate overnight at 4°C. Remember to save an input sample (leave at 4C until elution step).

\-Spin down complexes for 30 seconds at 8000rpm and put on magnet and discard the supernatant. Resuspend the beads in 1mL WBI, rotate at 4C for 5minutes and spin as before.

\-Repeat wash with WBII, WBIII and two times with 1X TE buffer.

\-Elute with 200ul Elution Buffer and shake for 30 minutes at 65C then reverse the crosslinks overnight at 65C. Remember to reverse crosslink your input sample as well (add 30ul of input to 200ul of Elution Buffer).

**DNA extraction:**

\-Add 1ul of RNase A (stock at 100mg/ml) and incubate at 37C for 30minutes. Add 2ul of proteinase K (stock at 20mg/ml) and incubate at 55C for 1 hour.

\-Spin down sample as before and transfer the supernatant to a new tube (this is the eluted DNA/protein complexes. Discard the beads).

\-Extract DNA with Zymo DNA Clean & Concentrate kit. Follow the instruction of the kit and Elute in 55ul elution buffer.

**Reagents:**

\-Protease Inhibitor Cocktail Set V, EDTA-free (Calbiochem 539137-10VL), use at 1X

\-Benzamidine (Sigma B6506-5G), use at 1mM

\-Formaldehyde solution (Sigma F8775)

\-Dynabeads Protein G (Invitrogen 10003D)
