**End Repair:**

End-It DNA end repair kit (Epicenter ER0720)

 

25uL total:

17uL DNA (No EDTA in elution buffer)

2.5uL 10x End-repair buffer

2.5uL 2.5mM dNTPs

2.5uL 10mM ATP

0.5uL End-it Enzyme mix

 

Incubate 45 minutes, 25degC in a PCR tube in the PCR machine.

Zymo DNA clean/concentrate, elute in 2x8uL 10mM Tris pH8.0, heated to 55degC, incubate on column at least 3 minutes per elution, 16uL total eluate. Alternatively add 3x volume SPRI or Ampure bead, wash twice with fresh 80% EtOH, elute in A-tail mix + H2O to 25uL.

 

**A addition**

25uL total:

16uL End-repaired DNA

2.5uL 10x NEBuffer 2

5uL 1mM dATP

1.5uL Klenow (3’-5’ exo(-), 5U/uL)

 

Incubate 30-45 minutes, 37degC in PCR machine

Zymo DNA clean/concentrate, elute twice with same 10uL pre-heated 10mM tris pH 8.0, 11uL total. Alternatively, add 3x volume SPRI buffer, wash twice with fresh 80% EtOH, elute in ligation mix + H2O to 13.5 uL

 

**Adaptor ligation**

Check Kappa Hyperprep kit for adaptor concentration to use

DO NOT ADD ADAPTER TO MASTER MIX – it will ligate and form dimers in the tube. Add individually to each reaction.

15uL total

6uL DNA

7.5uL 2x Quick ligase buffer

1.5 uL adaptor oligo

0.5 uL Quick DNA ligase (1U/uL); or high concentration T4 ligase (Fermentas/Epicenter)

 

 

Incubate 45 minutes, 20degC, PCR machine

\+2uL USER enzyme (works in ligation buffer) - adapter has a hairpin, this enzyme cut a uracyl in the hairpin to linearize the strands

Incubate 37degC 15 minutes

Bring volume to 50uL with 10mM tris pH 8.0

Add 1.75X Ampure/SPRI beads

Mix, incubate 5’

2x fresh 80% EtOH rinse (don’t resuspend the beads, just run the EtOH down the side)

Remove ALL of the EtOH, allow beads to air-dry briefly

Resuspend beads in 15uL 10mM tris pH 8.0, incubate 10 with occasional vortexting

Gather beads with magnet and keep supernatant for PCR
