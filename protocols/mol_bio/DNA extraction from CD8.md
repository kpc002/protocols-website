**DNA extraction from CD8**

**Qiagen DNA blood extraction kit**

1.  Add ethanol to wash buffer concentrates if this is a new kit. If an old kit,look for evidence of ethanol being added to concentrate by prior user.

2.  Heat up thermal mixer to 56C

3.  Reconstitute protease if in lyophilized form. Otherwise, reconstituted solution will be in 4C

4.  After collecting the cells, spin and pipette off the supernatant. Important here to get all of the sup without disturbing the pellet

5.  Resuspend cell pellet in 200 ul of PBS

6.  Add 20 ul of protease/sample and mix by pipetting

7.  Add 200 ul buffer AL. Mix by vortexing

8.  Incubate at 56C for 10 min. Centrifuge briefly to remove drops from lid

9.  Add 200 ul of 98-100% ethanol. Vortex. Centrifuge briefly to remove drops from lid

10. Pipette mixture onto QIAamp mini spin columns + 2 ml collection tube and centrifuge at 6000xg (8000 RPM) for 1 min. Discard flow through and collection tube

11. Place spin column in new 2 ml collection tube and add 500 ul buffer AW1. Centrifuge at 6000xg (8000 RPM) for 1 min. Discard flow through and collection tube

12. Place spin column in new 2 ml collection tube and add 500 ul buffer AW2. Centrifuge at full speed for 3 min. Discard flow through and collection tube

13. Put spin column into new collection tube and spin at full speed for 1 min. This eliminates possible AW2 buffer carryover to next step.

14. Place spin column in new 1.5 ml centrifuge tube (not collection tube) and add 200 ul of distilled water and incubate at RT for 1 min

15. Centrifuge at 6000xg (8000 RPM) for 1 min to elute DNA

16. Measure DNA concentration and 260/280 on nanodrop. Continue to PCR amplification or freeze at -80

**Sanger Sequencing Prep**

Preparation of PCR product:

  - FWD primer = 2.5 ul (of 1:10 dilution)

  - RVS primer = 2.5 ul (of 1:10 dilution)

  - Q5 high fidelity polymerase master mix 2x = 25 ul

  - Genomic DNA = 150-250 ng (usually do around 200 ng)

  - H20 = fill up to 50 ul

Run PCR product

  - Middle thermal cycler Q5 program is 1 hour and 45 min

Running gel

  - Follow the protocol in the lab gel area

  - Load 16.67 ul of PCR product and 3.33 ul of 6x loading dye

  - Load ladder in first well, followed by samples

  - Run gel for 100V for 30 min

Submitting to Eton:

  - 8 ul/sample for PCR products (8 ul for each reaction)

  - Primer are prepared at 5 ul in 5 uM concentration (5 ul of each fwd and rvs for every reaction, keep primers separate)

  - Go up to 50 ul with H20 after taking out genomic DNA amount and master mix amount (30 ul)
