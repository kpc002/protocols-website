# Trizol RNA Isolation

- Sort ~50K cells of interest into 500 ul of Trizol. Careful not to dilute Trizol too much – Sort into media, spin down and resuspend in Trizol if necessary. 
- Extracted cells in Trizol can be kept at -80C. 
- Thaw Trizol:cells @ RT 
- Add 100 ul chloroform. 
- Vortex 15 seconds. 
- Incubate 2-3 minutes at RT. 
- Centrifuge 15 min at 12000 g at 4C – Work on ice from now on. 
- Transfer aqueous phase to new clean tube ~250 ul (if 500 ul were used). 
- Add 1ul glycoblue. 
- Add 300 ul isopropanol. 
- Incubate at -80C for at least 1h. 
- Centrifuge 20 min at highest speed 4C. 
- Remove supernatant. 
- Wash with 500 ul 75% ethanol (cold). 
- Centrifuge 15 min at 7400g 4C. 
- Remove ethanol and air dry (10 min).  
{{< hint info>}}
Make sure to take off all ethanol (with P10) 
{{</ hint>}}
- Resuspend in 10 ul water.
