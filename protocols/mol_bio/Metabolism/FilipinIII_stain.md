**Filipin III Fluorescence Staining of Free Cholesterol in Immune Cells for Flow**

Filipin Complex: Sigma (F-9765)

Stock: 25 mg/ml in DMSO

Working Solution: 0.05 mg/ml in Perm/Wash Buffer

1)  Perform surface stain in FACS buffer

2)  Wash cells with FACS buffer 1X

3)  Thoroughly resuspend cells in 100 ul of BD Cytofix/Cytoperm solution per well and incubate for 20 min at 4C in the dark. Avoid cell aggregation by vortexing prior to adding the Cytofix/Cytoperm solution

4)  Wash cells 2X in a buffer that contains saponin (BD Perm/Wash buffer)

5)  Stain cells with 100 ul of filipin working solution for 2h at room temperature (protected from light)

6)  Rinse 2X with PBS

7)  Keep protected from light and at 4C
