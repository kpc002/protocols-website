**RNA extraction with Trizol (adapted from Kyla Omilusik)**

1.  > Thaw Trizol sample at RT.

2.  > Add 2ul glycoblue

3.  > Add chloroform
    
      - For 500ul Trizol, use 100ul chloroform
    
      - For 1000ul Trizol, use 200ul chloroform

4.  > Shake vigorously for approx 15s

5.  > Incubate 2-3 min at RT

6.  > Centrifuge 15 min at 12000g, 4C

7.  > Carefully transfer aqueous phase to a fresh tube (work on ice from this point)
    
      - Pipet in circular motion, avoid interphase by leaving generous amount of aqueous phase
    
      - For 500ul Trizol, transfer \~250ul
    
      - For 1ml Trizol, transfer \~500ul

8.  > Add isopropanol (flick, do not pipette)
    
      - For 500ul Trizol, add 250ul isopropanol
    
      - For 1ml Trizol, add 500ul isopropanol

9.  > Centrifuge 20 min at 12,000g, 4C
    
      - Then remove supernatant with pipette tip

10. > Wash with 1ml cold 75% EtOH (make fresh using DNAse/RNAse-free water)
    
      - Flick to loose pellet

11. > Centrifuge 15 min at 7,400g, 4C
    
      - Pipette of as much EtOH as possible. Use 10ul micropipettor for small volume
    
      - Dry 10-15 min

12. > Resuspend RNA pellet in 10ul nuclease-free water

13. > Incubate 10-15 min at 55-60C.

**Optional: DNAse I digestion (from Turbo DNA-free procedure)**

1.  > Add 1ul 10X Turbo Dnase buffer and 1ul Turbo Dnase to sample. Mix gently.

2.  > Incubate 20-30 min at 37C

3.  > Add 2ul of resuspended DNA Inactivation Reagent and mix well.

4.  > Incubate 5 min, RT. Mix occasionally.

5.  > Centrifuge 1.5 min at 10,000g

6.  > Transfer the supernatant to a fresh tube. Avoid pellet.
