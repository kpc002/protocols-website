  Isolate RNA from blood or sorted cells

-   Sort \~50K cells of interest into 500 μl of Trizol. Be careful not to dilute Trizol too much
    -   Sort into media, spin down and resuspend in Trizol if necessary.
-   Extracted cells in Trizol can be kept at -80°C.
-   Thaw Trizol:cells \@ RT
-   Add 100 μl chloroform.
-   Vortex 15 seconds.
-   Incubate 2-3 minutes at RT.
-   Centrifuge 15 min at 12000 g at 4°C -
-   **Work on ice from now on.**
-   Transfer aqueous phase to new clean tube \~250 μl (if 500 μl were used).
-   Add 1μl glycoblue.
-   Add 300 μl isopropanol.
-   Incubate at -80°C for at least 1h.
-   Centrifuge 20 min at highest speed 4°C.
-   Remove supernatant.
-   Wash with 500 μl 75% ethanol (cold).
-   Centrifuge 15 min at 7400g 4°C.
-   Remove ethanol and air dry (10 min).
-   Resuspend in 10 μl water.

::: callout-tip
Make sure to take off all ethanol (with P10)
:::
