**<span class="underline">Protocol for Real Time qPCR</span>**

**Materials**

  - RNAse free Eppendorf, 1.5 ml

  - cDNA samples (obtained by RT-PCR, protocol MB5)

  - RNAse free water (Qiagen, supplied with RNeasy kit)

  - TaqMan® Universal PCR Master Mix, no AmpErase® UNG (ThermoFisher Scientific; cat\# 4324018)

  - TaqMan® Probes

  - MicroAmp® Fast Optical 96-Well Reaction Plate with Barcode, 0.1 mL (ThermoFisher Scientific; cat\# 4346906)

  - Microseal® 'B' PCR Plate Sealing Film, adhesive, optical (Bio-Rad; cat\# MSB1001)

**Note**

  - Use 15 ng of cDNA per well (or 10 ng/well when the target gene is highly expressed)

  - Preferably run the samples in triplicates

  - Keep everything on ice

  - Vortex the probe thoroughly and spin down before use

**Protocol**

|             | **µl per well** | **X (number of wells+3)** |
| ----------- | --------------- | ------------------------- |
| **SYBR 2X** | 5               | \=5\*(X+3)                |
| **Primers** | 1               | \=1\*(X+3)                |

1.  Make the correct amount of PCR mix (prepare for 3 extra wells):

2.  Vortex the PCR mix and spin down

3.  Add the PCR mix to each well (6 µl/well) of the PCR plate (Note: don’t put the pipet tip too far into the solution, otherwise too much of the mix will attach to the outside of the tip and you will not have enough)

4.  Prepare the cDNA sample in RNAse free water to get 15 ng/well, you need 4 µl/well
    
      - Calculation:

> … (number wells/sample) x 15 (ng/well) / … (ng/µl cDNA ‘stock’) = … µl cDNA ‘stock’
> 
> … (number wells/sample) x 4 µl (total volume/well) - … µl (cDNA ‘stock’) = … µl RNAse free water

5.  Add 4 µl of the diluted cDNA samples to the appropriate wells

6.  Seal the plate and make sure the edges are sealed properly

7.  Spin down the plate (make sure there are no bubbles)

8.  Keep the plate at 4°C until the run (protected from light)

9.  Run on the Real time PCR machine:

      - Open Giovanni’s folder and run protocol
    
      - 10 µL volume
