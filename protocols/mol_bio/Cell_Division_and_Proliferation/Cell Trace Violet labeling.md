Cell Trace Violet labeling

1.  Prepare CTV stock by adding 20ul DMSO to one vial of reagent.

2.  Warm cells in PBS (w/o Ca++ and Mg++) for 10min at 37’C

3.  Dilute cells to 10x10^6cells/ml

4.  Add 1ul of CTV in DMSO to each ml of cell suspension in PBS.

5.  Vortex gently to ensure equal exposure to dye

6.  Incubate cells for 20min at 37’C in the dark

7.  Add 5X of staining volume of culture medium containing at least 1% protein to cells. Incubate for 5 min.

8.  Pellet cells by centrifugation and wash 2X

9.  Resuspend and plate out.
