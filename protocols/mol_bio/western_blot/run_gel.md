## Prepare sample

-   total volume depends on the gel usually between 15 and 20ul can be loaded
-   for 20µl
    -   5µl 4x loading dye
    -   2µl 10x reducing agent
    -   13µl sample
        -   keep µg protein between the samples consistent, fill with IPN150
-   heat at 70°C for 10min
-   spin down shortly
-   keep on ice

## Load and Run gel

-   Gel used: Bis-Tris NuPage 4-12%
-   prepare MES running buffer by diluting the 20x stock
-   fill chamber with running buffer
-   load 8µl PageRuler™ Plus Prestained Protein Ladder
-   load samples
-   add 500µl Antioxidant to the inner chamber
-   run at 120V until desired location

## Transfer

-   soak membrane in methanol for 30s
-   soak membrane in transfer buffer \> 2min
-   prepare transfer cassette (see image)

![Transfer sandwich](transfer.svg)

-   fill with transfer buffer
-   add ice pack into chamber
-   run at 70V for 90min at 4°C

## Blocking

-   Block membrane in TBS-T + 5% BSA (or milk powder) for 1h at room temperature
-   wash 3x with TBS-T for 5 min each.

## Staining

-   stain with primary AB in TBS-T + 5% BSA + 0.01% NaN3 over night at 4°C
-   wash three times
-   add secondary antibody TBS-T + 5% BSA, incubate for 1 hour at room temperature
-   wash at least three times

## Developing

I used Amersham™ ECL™ Prime Western BlottingDetection Reagent

-   Mix solutions A and B at 1:1
-   Drain excess wash buffer from the washed membrane and place it protein side up in a suitable box or on a sheet of plastic wrap or other suitable clean surface. Add detection reagent onto the membrane and make sure it completely covers the membrane.
-   Incubate for 5 minutes at room temperature.
-   Drain off excess detection reagent by holding the membrane edge gently against a tissue.
