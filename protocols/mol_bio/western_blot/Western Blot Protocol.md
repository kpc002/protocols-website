**Western Blot Protocol**

**Quantify Protein (BCA Protein Assay)**

1.  Dilute Standard in 96 well flat bottom plate
    
    1.  Add 184 ul of H<sub>2</sub>O to the first well of the column then 100ul to the rest of the wells in the column. Add nothing to the last well
    
    2.  Add 16ul of BSA (2mg/ml) to the first well and then do 1:2 dilutions down the column (transfer 100ul).
    
    3.  Standard curve 16ug, 8ug, 2ug, 1ug, 0.5ug

2.  Dilute samples in 96 well flat bottom plate
    
    1.  Add 2ul of sample to 98ul of H<sub>2</sub>O

3.  Mix reagent A to B in a 50:1 mixture (5mL A to 100mL B) and add 100ul to samples. Add to lowest \[BSA\] up to highest \[BSA\] then add to samples.

4.  Incubate at 37°C for 15min (or until samples turn purple)

5.  Read in plate reader @ 560nm

6.  Determine protein concentration using standard curve. Calculate volume of sample needed for 15ug.

**Sample Preparation:**

| Sample Volume           | Sample Buffer (4x 1x) | Reducing Agent (10x1x) | H<sub>2</sub>O | Total Volume |
| ----------------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------- | ------------ |
| Volume determined above | 5ul                   | 2ul                    | Make up volume | 20ul         |

1.  Heat @ 70°C for 10mins

2.  Spin down for about 1 min@ max speed

3.  Store at -20°C
    
    1.  If stored at -20°C, thaw samples then heat for a few minutes @ 70°C and spin down @ max speed

**Electrophoresis**

1.  Make 1L of running buffer (20X1X)
    
    1.  50mL running buffer, fill to 1L in graduated cylinder with water
    
    2.  Take 200mL and place in a second graduated. Add 500ul of antioxidant (do this immediately before you are going to run you gel).

2.  Set up gel apparatus
    
    1.  Wash gel wells with running buffer and take the tape off
    
    2.  Place 200ml of running buffer with antioxidant solution from 1b. in the middle part of the gel box and check for leaks
    
    3.  Pour running buffer from 1a. on the outside

3.  Load 7ul of ladder and 20ul of sample (all of sample that you prepared above)

4.  Run @ 100V for 5 minutes, check, then turn up

**Transfer**

1.  Set up transfer apparatus

2.  Make up transfer buffer
    
    1.  Add 100mL 10X transfer buffer into a graduated cylinder. Fill to 500mL with H<sub>2</sub>O then add 200mL methanol. Fill to 1L with H<sub>2</sub>O (if you add methanol directly to undiluted 10X transfer buffer, you will cause precipitation)

3.  Cut membrane to size slightly smaller than filter paper. Do not touch the membrane (the membrane will get dirty from protein from your hands).

4.  Set up 3 small plastic containers and fill with methanol, dH<sub>2</sub>O, or transfer buffer respectively
    
    1.  Place membrane in methanol for 30sec to 1 min, then rinse in dH<sub>2</sub>O, then transfer buffer, allowing it to sit transfer buffer while you proceed

5.  Fill large glass dish with transfer buffer and put in transfer holder black side down
    
    1.  Crack open gel and cut off wells at the bottom
    
    2.  In holder place foam, 2 filter papers, gel, membrane, 2 filter papers, and then foam. Roll out the bubbles and close holder under transfer buffer

6.  Place holder in apparatus (black to black), then place in cold room on stir plate. Pour transfer buffer from bowl into the apparatus

7.  Run at 70V (around 200mA) for 80min.
    
    1.  Check after 5min that the timer is working
    
    2.  May check periodically to make sure that the transfer is running and no errors have occurred.

**Blocking**

1.  Make up blocking buffer in 50mL conical
    
    1.  BSA (5%) or milk in TBS-0.1% tween-20 (ex. 2.5g BSA per 50ml)

2.  Take blot out and place in container. Pour blocking buffer (10-20mL) to cover blot. Do not pour directly on the blot.

3.  Place on shaker 1-2 hrs @ RT or @4°C overnight

**Primary**

1.  Dilute primary Ab in blocking buffer from above.

2.  If you need to work with a small volume of Ab, Place blot in a bag that is sealed on 3 sides, then add \~ 2ml of diluted Ab and seal the top. Label the blot then place face down on the shaker. Shake 1-2hr @ RT or @4°C overnight (this will depend on the primary antibody you are using).

3.  If you can work in a larger volume of Ab. Add 10ml of diluted Ab to container that you blocked in. Shake 1-2hr @ RT or @4°C overnight (this will depend on the primary antibody you are using).

4.  Cut open bag and place blot in container (or pour of primary Ab from container) and rinse the blot 3x quickly with TBS-0.1% Tween-20. Depending on your primary Ab, you may be able to reuse it several times so you can store it at 4°C until next use otherwise discard down sink).

5.  Wash 3x in TBS-0.1% Tween-20 for at 15-20 mins each time

**Secondary**

1.  Dilute secondary Ab (1:10,000) in blocking buffer from above. Add secondary Ab for 45mins shaking @RT.

2.  Rinse blot in TBS-0.1% Tween-20 3x for 15- 20mins each time

**Developing**

1.  Remove all liquid from container that blot was washing in.

2.  More sensitive ECL kit
    
    1.  Make 4mL of a 1:1 mixture of the reagents and add to the blot
    
    2.  Leave on for 5 mins then aspirate off

3.  Less sensitive ECL kit
    
    1.  Make 4mL of a 1:1 mixture of reagents and add to the blot
    
    2.  Leave on for 1 min then aspirate off

4.  Pick up blot with forceps (make sure you pick up by edge of blot) and let drip onto a kimwipe to remove last bits of developing reagent. Place blot into a plastic divider inside cassette and gently remove bubbles with fingers.

5.  Develop in dark room as soon as possible
    
    1.  Draw ladder before removing membrane from cassette

**Stripping**

1.  Add 174ul **β-Mercaptoethanol** to 25ml of stripping buffer – work in fumehood as this will stink.

2.  Place blot in hybridization tube and add stripping buffer (in hood). Place in heated spinner @ 70°C for 30 min with counter balance tube.

3.  Discard buffer in fume hood with water running.

4.  Rinse tube with dH2O water until there are no bubbles and you can’t smell the stripping buffer anymore.

5.  Place blot in container with TBS-0.1% Tween-20 and shake for 2-3x 15-20mins @ RT.

6.  Start at blocking step

**Recipes:**

**<span class="underline">10x Transfer Buffer</span>**

30.3g Tris

144.1g glycine

up to 1L dH20

**<span class="underline">10X TBS</span>**

24g Tris

88g NaCl

up to 1L of dH20

pH 7.6

**<span class="underline">1X TBS-0.1% Tween-20</span>**

100ml 10x TBS

900ml dH20

1ml Tween-20

**<span class="underline">Stripping Buffer</span>**

62.5mM Tris-HCl pH6.8

2% SDS

100mM mercaptoethaol (stock at 14N – add before each use)
