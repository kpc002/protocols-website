# Western Blot Buffers

## Western Lysis Buffer

### IPN150

- 50mM Tris/HCl pH 7.6
- 150mM NaCl
- 5mM MgCl2
- 0.1% NP40
  
add fresh:
- 1x protease inhibitor ([Sigma](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/roche/coedtafro?context=product))
- 1mM DTT
- Benzamidine (1mM)

If analyzing phosphorylation:
- 1:100 Phosphatase Inhibitor Cocktail 2 ([Sigma](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/SIGMA/P5726))
- 1:100 Phosphatase Inhibitor Cocktail 3 ([Sigma](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/p0044))



## 10x Transfer buffer

For 1000ml
- 30g Tris base
- 144g Glycin
- H20

## 1x Transfer buffer

- 100ml 10x transfer buffer
- 700ml H20
- 200ml Methanol

## 10x TBS

- 24.3g Tris base
- 88g Nacl
- 1000ml H20
- adjust pH to 7.6

## 1x TBS-T

- 100ml 10x TBS
- 900ml H20
- 1ml Tween 20
