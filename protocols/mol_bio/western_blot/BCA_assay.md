# Protein quantification with BCA assay

## Prepare protein standard

- Use a 96-well flat bottom plate
- Add 168 ul H20 to the first well of the first two columns.
- Add 100 ul H20 to the remaining wells of the first two columns
- Add 32 ul BSA (2 mg/ml) to the first well of each column and then do a 1:2 dilution by transferring 100ul down the column,
- Standard curve: 32ug, 16ug, 8ug, 4ug, 2ug, 1ug, 0.5ug, 0.25ug

## Prepare samples

- If expecting high protein concentration: Add 2 ul sample to 98ul H20.
- If expecting lower protein concentration: Add 5 ul sample to 95ul H20.

## BCA Assay

- Mix reagent A to B in 50:1 mixture.
- Add 100ul to each well.
- Incubate at 37°C for 10-15 min
- Read plate at 560nm
