## Prepare protein standard

-   Use a 96-well flat bottom plate
-   Add 168 µl H~2~0 to the first well of the first two columns.
-   Add 100 µl H~2~0 to the remaining wells of the first two columns
-   Add 32 µl BSA (2 mg/ml) to the first well of each column and then do a 1:2 dilution by transferring 100µl down the column,
-   Standard curve: 32µg, 16µg, 8µg, 4µg, 2µg, 1µg, 0.5µg, 0.25µg

## Prepare samples

-   If expecting high protein concentration: Add 2 µl sample to 98 µl H~2~0.
-   If expecting lower protein concentration: Add 5 µl sample to 95 µl H~2~0.

## BCA Assay

-   Mix reagent A to B in 50:1 mixture.
-   Add 100µl to each well.
-   Incubate at 37°C for 10-15 min
-   Read plate at 560nm
