# Protein Lysis

- Prepare lysis buffer (see [Buffers](/docs/mol_bio/western_blot/buffers/#ipn150))
- Pellet cells and resuspend in Lysis Buffer
- Keep on ice for 30 minutes
- Spin at 16000 g for 5 min at 4°C
- Transfer supernatant into new tube
- freeze at -80°C
