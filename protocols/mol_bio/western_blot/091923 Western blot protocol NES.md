**<span class="underline">Western blot buffers</span>**

**Western Lysis Buffer**

**IPN150**

  - 50mM Tris/HCl pH 7.6

  - 150mM NaCl

  - 5mM MgCl2

  - 0.1% NP40

add fresh:

  - 1x protease inhibitor ([<span class="underline">Sigma</span>](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/roche/coedtafro?context=product))

  - 1mM DTT

  - Benzamidine (1mM)

If analyzing phosphorylation:

  - 1:100 Phosphatase Inhibitor Cocktail 2 ([<span class="underline">Sigma</span>](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/SIGMA/P5726))

  - 1:100 Phosphatase Inhibitor Cocktail 3 ([<span class="underline">Sigma</span>](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/p0044))

**10x Transfer buffer**

For 1000ml

  - 30g Tris base

  - 144g Glycin

  - H20

**1x Transfer buffer**

  - 100ml 10x transfer buffer

  - 700ml H20

  - 200ml Methanol

**10x TBS**

  - 24.3g Tris base

  - 88g Nacl

  - 1000ml H20

  - adjust pH to 7.6

**1x TBS-T**

  - 100ml 10x TBS

  - 900ml H20

  - 1ml Tween 20

# **<span class="underline">Protein Lysis</span>** 

  - Prepare lysis buffer (see [Buffers](https://protocols.beetletun.de/docs/mol_bio/western_blot/buffers/#ipn150))

  - Pellet cells and resuspend in Lysis Buffer

  - Keep on ice for 30 minutes

  - Spin at 16000 g for 10 min at 4°C

  - Transfer supernatant into new tube

  - freeze at -80°C

# **<span class="underline">Protein quantification with BCA assay</span>** 

## Prepare protein standard 

  - Use a 96-well flat bottom plate

  - Add 184 ul H20 to the first well of the first two columns.

  - Add 100 ul H20 to the remaining wells of the first two columns

  - Add 16 ul BSA (2 mg/ml) to the first well of each column and then do a 1:2 dilution by transferring 100ul down the column,

  - Standard curve: 16ug, 8ug, 4ug, 2ug, 1ug, 0.5ug, 0.25ug, and 0ug

## Prepare samples 

  - If expecting high protein concentration: Add 2 ul sample to 98ul H20.

  - If expecting lower protein concentration: Add 5 ul sample to 95ul H20.

## BCA Assay 

  - Mix reagent A to B in 50:1 mixture.

  - Add 100ul to each well.

  - Incubate at 37°C for 10-15 min

  - Read plate at 560nm

# **<span class="underline">Run Western Blot</span>** 

## Prepare sample 

  - total volume depends on the gel usually between 15 and 20ul can be loaded

  - for 20ul
    
      - 5ul 4x loading dye
    
      - 2ul 10x reducing agent
    
      - 13ul sample
        
          - keep ug protein between the samples consistent, fill with lysis buffer

  - heat at 70°C for 10min

  - spin down shortly

  - keep on ice

## Load and Run gel 

  - Gel used: Bis-Tris NuPage 4-12%

  - prepare MES running buffer by diluting the 20x stock

  - fill chamber with running buffer

  - load 8ul PageRuler™ Plus Prestained Protein Ladder

  - load samples

  - add 500ul Antioxidant to the inner chamber

  - run at 120V until desired location

## Transfer 

  - soak membrane in methanol for 30s

  - soak membrane in transfer buffer \> 2min

  - prepare transfer cassette (see image![A close-up of a computer screen Description automatically generated](091923%20Western%20blot%20protocol%20NES_media/media/image1.png)

<!-- end list -->

  - make sure all bubbles are removed between gel and membrane

  - fill with transfer buffer

  - add ice pack into chamber

  - run at 70V for 90min at 4°C

## Blocking 

  - Block membrane in TBS-T + 5% BSA (or milk powder) for 1h at room temperature

  - wash 3x with TBS-T for 5 min each.

## Staining 

  - stain with primary Ab in TBS-T + 5% BSA over night at 4°C

  - wash three times

  - add secondary antibody TBS-T + 5% BSA, incubate for 1 hour at room temperature

  - wash at least three times

## Developing 

I used Amersham™ ECL™ Prime Western Blotting Detection Reagent

  - Mix solutions A and B at 1:1, if primary ab is strong, can add PBS to dilute

  - Drain excess wash buffer from the washed membrane and place it protein side up in a suitable box or on a sheet of plastic sheet protecter or other suitable clean surface. Add detection reagent onto the membrane and make sure it completely covers the membrane

  - Incubate for 45sec at room temperature

  - Drain off excess detection reagent by holding the membrane edge gently against a tissue
