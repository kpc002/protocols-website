**<span class="underline">Hi Seahorse Assay on sorted cells with the Seahorse HS Mini instrument</span>**

> Day before – hydrate sensor cartridge by filling all wells of calibration plate with 200 uL water. *Don’t forget to include the surrounding wells (which need 400 uL each)*.

Incubate at 37C in a <span class="underline">non-CO<sub>2</sub></span> incubator overnight.

> Day before – Prepare XFp PDL or HS PDL Miniplates – obtain the XFp PDL Miniplates and remove the foil seal(s) from the plate(s) that will be used. Place the plates in a non-humidified, non-CO<sub>2</sub> 37 °C incubator overnight.

Once you have sorted cells of interest\!

1.  Spin cells at 500g for 5 minutes without topping up the tubes – aspirate gently with pipette

2.  Resuspend cells in 100 µL Seahorse XF media and count

3.  Adjust volume to concentration of **200,000 cells in 50 µL** (125,000 cells minimum requirement)

4.  Add sterile water to the moat around the cell culture wells, 400 μL per chamber.

5.  Plate cells in wells B-C-D for WT and E-F-G for KO (in 50 uL of XF media)

6.  Plate 50 µL of XF media in wells A and H as background

7.  Centrifuge the plate at 500g for 5 minutes – verify cell adherence and monolayer

8.  ~~*Gently* lift the silicone inserts from the plate using the provided tool (do not remove entirely)~~

9.  ~~Spin the plate again for 1 minute~~

10. ~~Remove the silicone inserts entirely.~~

11. *Gently* add 130uL of XF media (to avoid detaching adhered cells)

12. Incubate at 37C in a <span class="underline">non-CO<sub>2</sub></span> incubator for 1hr

13. Replace the water in the sensor cartridge with XF calibrant (200ul/well),
    
    1.  Incubate at 37C in a <span class="underline">non-CO<sub>2</sub></span> incubator for 30 minutes

14. Resuspend Mitochondrial Stress test reagents in desired assay media.

15. After cartridge is incubated for 30 minutes – add reagents to injection ports

16. Run sensor cartridge with calibration plate

17. Add plated cells to the HS Mini + run Mitochondrial Stress Test

**<span class="underline">Reagents to Make</span>**

<span class="underline">Seahorse XF Media</span>

RPMI (9.7 mL) + 1mM pyruvate (100 µL) + 2mM L-glutamine (100 µL) + 10mM glucose (100 µL)

pH – 7.4 – incubate the final XF Assay Medium at 37 °C until ready for use

<span class="underline">Component Concentrations</span>

\*\*\* See page 14 of the Mito Stress Test user guide\*\*\*

Resuspend using Seahorse XF Media

Port A - 1.5 µM Oligomycin

Port B - 1.0 µM FCCP

Port C - 1.0 µM FCCP

Port D - 0.5 µM Rotenone + Antimycin
