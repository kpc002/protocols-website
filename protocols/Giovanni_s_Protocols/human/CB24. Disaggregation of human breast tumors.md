<span class="underline">Disaggregation of human breast tumors (CB 24)</span>

**NOTE:**

After the breast tumor is surgically removed, it is placed in wash media: RPMI 1640 (Lonza) supplemented with 2% FBS (Sigma-Aldrich) and 1% Penicillin-Streptomycin (Euroclone) and brought immediately to the lab. Samples are processed within 60 minutes from removal.

**Time:**

1h and 30 minutes

**Materials:**

  - Petri dish

  - Scissors

  - 100 μm Cell strainer

  - Sterile physiological saline solution (NaCl 0,9%. Baxter)/ 1X PBS /HBSS

  - Black rubber bottom of a 5 mL syringe

  - Falcons (50mL)

  - Trypan Blue 0,04%

  - Labelled Cryovials

  - Cryobox (RT)

  - Freezing Medium (FBS 10% DMSO 4°C)

  - Liberase TL Research Grade

  - DMEM

  - Scales

**Protocol**

  - Weigh and measure the tumor tissue

  - The tumor tissue is sliced into 1-2mm<sup>3</sup> pieces by sterile microdissection by use of curved scissors

  - Enzimatic digestion is performed with Liberase TL Research Grade. Use DMEM 10 mL/gr tissue and add 0.25 U/mL Liberase

  - Incubate at 37°C for 30 minutes

  - The sample is passed through a 100 μm nylon cell strainer. Gentle pressure is applied with the black rubber bottom of a 5 mL syringe to any remaining partially digested tissue on the cell strainer which was then washed with few mL of sterile physiological saline solution. NB: typically, \<5% of the tissue (consisting of mainly acellular-connective tissue) remained on the strainer at the end of this step.

  - The sample is washed with 25 mL of sterile physiological saline solution.

  - The suspension is centrifuged for 15 minutes at 300 g at room temperature, max acceleration (9), max brake (9).

  - The cells are washed in sterile physiological saline solution (5 ml) and then counted in Trypan Blue 0,04%.

  - The cell suspension is centrifuged for 10 minutes at 520 g at room temperature, max acceleration (9), max brake (9).

  - The supernatant is removed and the cells are suspended in FACS buffer for staining or in cold freezing medium (FBS 10% DMSO 4°C) to 1 mL/vial

  - Vials are kept for 24/48h in a cryobox at -80°C, then moved to liquid Nitrogen container.
