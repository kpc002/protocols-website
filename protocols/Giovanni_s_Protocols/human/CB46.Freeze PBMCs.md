**<span class="underline">How to freeze PBMCs (CB46)</span>**

**Materials:**

Fetal Bovine Serum (FBS)

dimethylsulfoxide, DMSO

Labeled cryogenic vials

PBS-/-

**Freeze Procedure:**

# Pre-freeze

1)  Check for bacterial, yeast, or fungal contamination under a microscope.

2)  Determine cell count. Calculate total number of cells and determine amount of freeze medium needed. (Cells should be resuspended in freeze medium at 0.5\*10^6 – 100\*10^6/ml).

## Freeze

3)  ## Centrifuge cells in 50 mL Falcon tube at 520g for 10 minutes.

4)  ## While cells are spinning, make freeze medium (e.g., 90% FBS, 10% DMSO, it should be used ICE COLD). Label cryogenic vials with date, cell type, and user’s initials.

5)  Eliminate supernatant from centrifuged cells and add freeze medium. Resuspend cells carefully and rapidly.

6)  Quickly aliquot 1 mL of freeze stock per cryogenic vial. Screw each vial closed.

7)  Put vials into cryo-box and place box, into –80°C freezer.

8)  After 24 hours, transfer the cryovials into appropriate rack in liquid N<sub>2</sub> tank.
