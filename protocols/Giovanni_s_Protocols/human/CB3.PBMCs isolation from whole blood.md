<span class="underline">PBMC isolation from whole blood and leukapheresis</span>

**Materials**

  - Sterile physiological saline solution (NaCl 0,9%. Baxter9)

  - Ficoll solution (RT, use avoiding light exposure)

  - Falcons (50mL)

  - Trypan blue 0,04%

  - Labelled cryovials

  - Cryobox (RT)

  - Freezing medium (FBS 10% DMSO 4°C)

**Protocol**

  - Divide blood in 3 Falcons (50mL) and make up to a final volume of 30 mL with physiological solution, resuspend properly. *If you are working with leukapheresis (1-2ml) top up to 30ml with saline solution*;

  - Stratify on the layer of 17 mL of Ficoll solution

  - Centrifuge: 400 G, 30’, max acceleration (9), min brake (1), RT

  - Collect PBMC’s ring and make up to a volume of 50 mL of physiological solution

  - Centrifuge: 520 G, 10’, max acceleration (9), max brake (9), RT

  - Remove supernatant, resuspend pellet with the appropriate amount of physiological solution

  - Cell count: average dilution 1:5 in trypan blue 0,04% (80uL trypan plus 20uL cells)

  - Make up to a final volume of 40 mL of physiological solution

  - Centrifuge: 520 G, 15’, max acceleration (9), max brake (9), RT

  - Remove supernatant, resuspend in appropriate amount of freezing medium (FBS 10% DMSO, 1mL/vial)

  - Keep vials 24/48h at -80°C, then move to liquid Nitrogen container
