**Genotyping Procedure**

<span class="underline">Tail Prep</span>

1.  > Cut \~1 cm of mouse tail (or smaller) and place in labelled epi tube (colorful tubes)

2.  > Thaw tail juice in 37ºC water and turn on shaker calibrated at 55ºC at 1000rpm

3.  > Add 400μl of tail juice into tail sample

4.  > Shake tails at 55ºC at 1000rpm for 1 hour (or even o/n until digested)

5.  > Add 150μl of 5M NaCl to each sample and <span class="underline">vortex</span>

6.  > Spin samples at max speed for 5 minutes

<!-- end list -->

  - > While samples are spinning, label new (clear) tubes

<!-- end list -->

7.  > Transfer 400μl of supernatant to a new tube (clear tube)

8.  > Add 250μl of isopropanol to each sample and <span class="underline">vortex</span>

9.  > Spin samples at max speed for 5 minutes

10. > Discard the supernatant by dumping into a waste beaker

11. > Wash pellet with 500μl of 70% EtOH and <span class="underline">vortex</span>

12. > Spin samples at max speed for 5 minutes

13. > Discard supernatant by dumping over waste beaker and air dry pellet overnight

<!-- end list -->

  - > Fold paper towels between drying rack rows and leave tubes to dry upside down

<!-- end list -->

14. > The next day, resuspend pellets in 200μl of Ultrapure water

<span class="underline">PCR</span>

1.  > Use PCR Calculator to calculate amount of ingredients needed to make master mix and write down in lab notebook

| **Master Mix Ingredients** | **Amount (μl)** |
| -------------------------- | --------------- |
| 5X Buffer                  |                 |
| MgCl<sub>2</sub>           |                 |
| Primer 1                   |                 |
| Primer 2                   |                 |
| Primer 3                   |                 |
| dNTP                       |                 |
| Taq                        |                 |
| Ultrapure H<sub>2</sub>O   |                 |

2.  > Thaw DNA samples, master mix ingredients (above), and positive/negative controls

<!-- end list -->

  - > Keep all ingredients on ice

<!-- end list -->

3.  > Label PCR strip tubes corresponding to DNA samples, positive control, negative control, and water

4.  > Label epi tube with PCR type and “master mix”

5.  > Add PCR ingredients to labelled epi, starting with Ultrapure water (largest volume). Vortex to mix ingredients and spin down for 5 seconds (short).

6.  > Add 22μl of master mix to each PCR tube

7.  > Add 3μl of each DNA sample to its corresponding PCR tube (25μl total in PCR tube)

8.  > Spin down PCR tube strip

9.  > Run samples in PCR machine - select correct program for the type of PCR (ER-Cre, ID2, etc)

<span class="underline">Gel electrophoresis</span>

| 2.5% Gel         | Small   | Big       |  | 1.5% Gel         | Small   | Big            |
| ---------------- | ------- | --------- |  | ---------------- | ------- | -------------- |
| Agarose Powder   | 2.5 g   | 5.0 g     |  | Agarose Powder   | 1.5 g   |                |
| 1X TAE Buffer    | 100 ml  | 200 ml    |  | 1X TAE Buffer    | 100 ml  |                |
| Microwave Time   | \~2 min | \~3.5 min |  | Microwave Time   | \~2 min | \~3 min 30 sec |
| Ethidium Bromide | 4 μl    | 8 μl      |  | Ethidium Bromide | 4 μl    | 8 μl           |

\*amount depends on agarose % or size of gel

1.  > Clean bottle by clearing away excess gel and dispose in designated waste bucket

2.  > Place the bottle on scale and tarr it. Add 4g\* of agarose powder to bottle

3.  > Add 100ml\* of 1X TAE buffer

4.  > Microwave bottle for \~2min\*

<!-- end list -->

  - > Do not leave the microwave unattended. Solution should not bubble over the top.

<!-- end list -->

5.  > Using silicone oven mitts, swirl bottle until solution runs clear

6.  > Cool down the solution.

7.  > Add 4g\* of ethidium bromide and swirl to mix.

8.  > Assemble mold and place desired comb into mold. Pour solution <span class="underline">slowly</span> over mold to minimize bubbles.

<!-- end list -->

  - > Use a pipette tip to move bubbles away from the comb and to the side.

<!-- end list -->

9.  > Allow gel to cool on bench or in cold room for 10-15 minutes

10. > Carefully remove comb from gel

11. > Place gel mold into gel electrophoresis box

<!-- end list -->

  - > Buffer should be changed once a day or more as needed

<!-- end list -->

12. > Add 10μl of DNA ladder (ER-Cre = 100bp) to first well

13. > Load 20μl of each PCR sample into a well

14. > Attach electrodes with black electrode in the back (DNA side) and red in front. Set up for 100V for 1 hour and run the gel.

<!-- end list -->

  - > Check at about 30-45 minutes to make sure samples don’t run off the gel
