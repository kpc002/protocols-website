**<span class="underline">Protocol for surface staining for flow cytometry</span>**

**NB:** many chemokine receptors recycle continuously through early endosomes , so if you freeze the endocytic pathway you can only address the fraction that is on the surface. If you stain at RT (or even 37°C) you can hit a much larger fraction, and get better signal. It is important to titrate your antibodies under the conditions you will be staining during experiments.

**Materials**

  - Live/Dead Fixable Viability Kit

  - ~~ArC Amine Reactive Compensation Beads (Life Technology)~~

  - ~~ArC Amine Reactive Negative Beads (Life Technology)~~

  - ~~Compbeads Anti-mouse Ig K (BD) or~~ UltraComp eBeads (eBioscience)

  - Sterile FACS washing buffer (1X PBS -/- supplemented with 2% FBS or 1X HBSS -/- supplemented with 2% FBS )

  - Fix solution: formaline (FA) 1% in HBSS-/- or in PBS 1X-/-

  - Antibodies

  - Sample

**Protocol**

  - wash the sample with 2mL (20 X V<sub>staining</sub>) of HBSS-/- o 1X PBS-/- (in order to eliminate FBS or BSA that can bind Live/Dead)

  - centrifuge 4 minutes at 1600 rpm at RT

  - aspirate the supernatant

  - make Live/Dead mix <span class="underline">for 4 samples</span> of around 10 million cells each (add 1 uL Live/Dead in 19 uL H2O; then add 380 uL PBS-/-)

  - add 50 uL of Live/Dead mix to each sample and 50 µL of Fc Block (ready to use solution) and incubate for 15 minutes RT in the dark

  - wash the sample with 2 mL of FACS buffer

  - centrifuge 4 minutes at 1600 rpm at RT

  - aspirate the supernatant

  - make the Ab mix in FACS buffer

  - add 100 uL of Ab mix to each sample and incubate for 20 minutes 4°C in the dark.

  - wash the sample with 2 mL of FACS buffer

  - centrifuge 4 minutes at 1600 rpm at RT

  - aspirate the supernatant

  - fix the sample with 150 uL of Fixation Buffer (BioLegend – brown bottle)

  - incubate 10 minutes at 4°C

  - wash the sample with 2 mL of FACS buffer

  - centrifuge 4 minutes at 1600 rpm at RT

  - aspirate the supernatant

  - resuspend the sample with 200 uL of FACS washing buffer

  - keep the sample at 4°C until the acquisition

**Fluorescence compensation**

Compensation controls are required for each fluorochrome and should contain both a positive and a negative population. Be sure to use specific beads: for mouse anti-Human antibodies use Compbeads Anti-mouse Ig K (BD), for rat anti-Human antibodies use UltraComp eBeads (eBioscience). For some antibodies it is recommended to use cells for compensation.

  - Gently vortex beads

  - Put 30 uL of beads and add the 1 µL of the antibody in a FACS tube. For the negative control: put beads alone.

  - ~~For Live/Dead positive: 2 drops of ArC Amine Reactive Compensation Beads + 2,5 uL of Live/Dead. For Live/Dead negative: 2 drops of ArC Amine Reactive Negative Beads. NB: <span class="underline">at the end of incubation, do not fix neither wash the Live/Dead comps\!</span>~~

  - Incubation for 20 minutes at RT in the dark

  - wash the tube with 2 mL of FACS buffer

  - fix the sample with 150 uL of Fixation Buffer (BioLegend – brown bottle)

  - incubate 10 minutes at 4°C

  - wash the sample with 2 mL of FACS buffer

  - centrifuge 4 minutes at 1600 rpm at RT

  - aspirate the supernatant

  - resuspend the sample with 200 uL of FACS washing buffer

  - keep the sample at 4°C until the acquisition
