## Achieve Better Resolution With Higher Sensitivity Detectors

-  Cytek® Systems use detectors called Avalanche Photodiodes (APDs), whereas many other cytometers use Photomultiplier Tubes (PMTs)
-  Quantum efficiency (QE) is the ability to convert photons to electrons
-  APDs have higher QE which translates to better resolution, especially with fluorophores that emit at longer wavelengths
