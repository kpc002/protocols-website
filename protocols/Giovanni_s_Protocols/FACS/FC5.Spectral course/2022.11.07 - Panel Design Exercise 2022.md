# Exercise 5: Design a Panel

Source: `2022.11.07 - Panel Design Exercise 2022.pptx`

## Slide 1 — Exercise #5: Design a Panel

![Exercise 5 title and aims](2022.11.07%20-%20Panel%20Design%20Exercise%202022_files/slide-1.png)

### Aims

Design an 11-color panel:

- Classify the antigens.
- Consider co-expression.
- Characterize the spread of available fluorochromes.
- Assign fluorochromes.

## Slide 2 — Steps to Assigning Fluors in a Panel

![Steps to assigning fluorochromes](2022.11.07%20-%20Panel%20Design%20Exercise%202022_files/slide-2.png)

1. Classify the antigens.
   - Define a gating strategy.
   - Consider co-expression.
   - Assess reagent availability.
2. Characterize the uniqueness, brightness, and spread of available dyes.
3. Assign fluorochromes.
   - **Tertiary antigens:** Use very bright or bright dyes that receive low spread.
   - **Secondary antigens:** Assign based on co-expression.
     - If there is no co-expression, use any bright dye still available.
     - If there is co-expression, use bright dyes that do not spread into tertiary antigens.
   - **Primary antigens:** Use dim fluorochromes that contribute low spread.

## Slide 6 — Fluorochrome Spread

![Stain Index Reduction matrix showing fluorochrome spread](2022.11.07%20-%20Panel%20Design%20Exercise%202022_files/slide-6.png)

The Stain Index Reduction (SIR) matrix is read as: **the dye in a row impacts the dye in a column**. The color scale runs from **no impact** to **high impact**.
