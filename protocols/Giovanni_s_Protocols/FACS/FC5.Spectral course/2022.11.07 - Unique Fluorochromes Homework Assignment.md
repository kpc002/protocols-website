**Unique Fluorochromes Homework Assignment**

*Directions – Using the [5L Fluorochrome Selection Guide](https://f.hubspotusercontent00.net/hubfs/6765000/Website%20Downloadable%20Content/Data%20Sheets/Fluorochrome%20Guides/N9-20017%20Rev%20B_5L%20UVVBYGR%20Fluorochrome%20Guideline.pdf) and the [Spectrum Viewer](https://spectrum.cytekbio.com/) **with 5L configuration** setting, answer the following questions.*

| Fluorochrome Pairs                           | Are the signatures unique? | What is their similarity index? |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| ViaDye® Red & APC-Fire<sup>TM</sup> 750      | YES                        | 0.75                            |
| Super Bright 645 & BV650                     | NOPE                       | 0.98                            |
| cFluor® V420 & cFluor® V450                  | YES                        | 0.91                            |
| APC-Fire<sup>TM</sup> 750 & APC-eFluor® 780  | NOPE                       | 1                               |
| DsRed & tdTomato                             | NOPE                       | 0.99                            |
| cFluor<sup>TM</sup> YG584 & PE               | YES                        | 0.88                            |
| Spark NIR<sup>TM</sup> 685 & Alexa Fluor 647 | YES                        | 0.92                            |
| cFluor® BYG610 & PE-Fire<sup>TM</sup> 640    | YES                        | 0.45                            |

## Bonus assignment:

Using the spectrum viewer and the configuration that matches your Aurora, load the fluors from the first panel you hope to run on your Aurora or Northern Lights and have a look at the distribution of the fluors in the spectrum viewer, as well as the similarity index and complexity index.

How does your spectrum and similarity matrix look?
