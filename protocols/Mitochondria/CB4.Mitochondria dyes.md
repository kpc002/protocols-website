**MitoTracker Green FM probe (FITC)**

What for: stains total mitochondria, it measures mitochondrial mass / cell content

MW 671.88

Stock Solution 1 mM

DMSO volume 74.42 µL

Working Solution 25 nM

D.F. 1:40,000 (dilute 1:40 in warm PBS and then 1:1,000 into final sample tube)

Use working concentrations of 25–500 nM

**MitoTracker Deep Red FM probe (APC)**

What for: labels ‘healthy’ mitochondria with robust proton gradient, it measures respiration

MW 543.58

Stock Solution 1 mM

DMSO volume 91.98 µL

Working Solution 25 nM

D.F. 1:40,000 (dilute 1:40 in warm PBS and then 1:1,000 into final sample tube)

Use working concentrations of 25–500 nM

**MitoSOX Red (BV605 or PE)**

What for: measures superoxide accumulation

Make a 5 mM stock solution of MitoSOX reagent by dissolving the contents of the vial in 13 µL of anhydrous DMSO

Working Solution 5 µM

D.F. 1:1,000 (into final sample tube)

Optimize the staining solution concentration between 100 nM to 5 µM to maximize signal-to-noise ratio and minimize cellular toxicity.

**Additional Notes**

Include unstained controls & FMOs

Work on congenically mixed cells to avoid technical biases

**Steps**

Goal is to have 1-5 million cells/tube

Centrifugation steps carried out at 400g for 3 minutes

  - > Equilibrate one vial of dye at room temp, then resuspend it

  - > Count the cells from each condition and place them into individual FACS tubes

  - > Wash cells in warm PBS and resuspend them in 1 mL of warm PBS

  - > Add 1 µL of dye to each FACS tube with the cell suspension (dilution 1:1,000)

  - > Stain for 20 minutes in the incubator at 37 ºC

  - > Quickly wash cells in warm PBS

  - > Stain for 10 minutes at RT dark in PBS
    
      - > Congenic staining + reporter genes

  - > Wash cells in warm PBS

  - > Acquire samples

Mito**SOX** Red (PE-Texas Red)

**Materials**

  - MitoSOX Red (\#M36008): 5 mM stock (dissolve 50 μg vial in 13 μL anhydrous DMSO; aliquot and store at -20°C protected from light; stable for months).

  - HBSS **with** Ca/Mg (pre-warmed)

  - FACS buffer: PBS + 2% FBS (ice-cold).

  - Centrifuge at **4°C**

**Step-by-Step Protocol**

Resuspend cells in ice-cold FACS buffer at 1–2 × 10<sup>6</sup> cells/mL.

Aliquot 100–500 μL (0.5–2 × 10<sup>6</sup> cells) per tube for samples/controls.

Prepare fresh 5 μM working solution: Dilute 5 mM stock 1:1000 (5 µM) in pre-warmed (37°C) HBSS (**with** Ca/Mg; no serum).

Centrifuge cells; aspirate supernatant.

Resuspend pellet in 500 μL of 5 μM MitoSOX solution per tube.

Vortex gently to mix.

Incubate 20 min at 37°C in dark (water bath or incubator; gentle agitation optional).

Stop reaction: resuspend cells in 3 mL ice-cold FACS buffer; centrifuge (300–400 × g, 5 min, 4°C).

Aspirate supernatant from MitoSOX-washed cells.

Resuspend in 50 μL Fc block solution; incubate 5–10 min on ice.

Without washing, add live/dead + surface antibody cocktail (in FACS buffer; total final volume 100 μL per tube, i.e., 50 μL Fc + 50 µL antibody mix).

Vortex gently.

Incubate 20 min on ice (or 4°C), dark (longer for dim markers like PD-1).

Add 2 mL ice-cold FACS buffer; centrifuge (300–400 × g, 5 min, 4°C).

Resuspend in 200 μL ice-cold FACS buffer.

Filter through 40 μm strainer into FACS tubes to remove clumps.

Keep on ice/dark until acquisition (analyze within 1–2 hours; MitoSOX signal can drift).
