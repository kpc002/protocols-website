## FRIDAY, 4/10/2026

- Transfer tissues from the -80 to -20
- Make sure the cryostat is on and is around -16 degrees.
- pop sample that you want to section out of the holder.
- Grab one of the spiral things, put OCT on it, and let sample bind to it.
- Use the little handle on the mounting area to lock the spiral into place.
- Trim section to the depth you want.
- Put the glass rectangle down
- Then change to 10 um and cut section, drop slide on it to get section onto slide and check under brightfield.
- Need to use one of the good slides for the final cut.
- Store slide in a slide storage rack in the microtome until finished.
- lock the cycler into place for clean.
- Empty the bottom shavings for cleanup into biohaz.
