## FFPE Antibody Staining

## Introduction

How to stain FFPE tissue with antibodies.

## Materials

## › Materials

- › The white cups for holding slides in Kitty's cabinet, or the colored boxes, but only clean ones.
- › Prepare Xylene and ethanol mixes beforehand
- › Antibodies
- › Cas block
- › Dako dilution buffer
- › Slide holding bins
- › Vectashield
- › Pressure cooker
- › Pap pens

›

## Procedure

## Day 1

1. 60 C bake all slides for 30 min
2. Let the slides cool for 7 min.
3. Deparaffinize FFPE slides and antigen retrieval

|   Table1 | Table1       | Table1                          |
|----------|--------------|---------------------------------|
|          | A            | B                               |
|        1 | Solution     | Incubation time                 |
|        2 | Xylene       | 10 min                          |
|        3 | Xylene       | 10 min                          |
|        4 | 100% Ethanol | 3 min                           |
|        5 | 100% Ethanol | 3 min                           |
|        6 | 95% Ethanol  | 3 min                           |
|        7 | 70% Ethanol  | 3 min                           |
|        8 | Tap Water    | 3 min                           |
|        9 | PBS/H2O      | Until pressure cooker is ready. |

4. Make target retrieval solution - 50 ml. You probably need 100 ml or 200 ml.
5. Place in jar containing Target Retrieval solution in pressure cooker. Steam &gt; low pressure &gt; start
6. When finished, put the slides that just went through the Xylene and ethanol course in, and start the pessure cooker again.
7. Steam at 20min under low pressure
8. Remove entire jar from water bath and cool at room temperature for 20 min.
9. Rinse 3x in PBS transfering from well to well back and forth every 5 min.
10. Put into the slide rack and dry off some of the PBS so that you can circle with the pen.
11. Use pen to draw circle around each sample
12. FOR PARAFFIN sections block: use Cas block for 60 min at room temperature, place in a humidified chamber by ripping paper towels and wetting them and putting them in Kitty's slide box

|   Table2 | Table2                               |         |
|----------|--------------------------------------|---------|
|          | Reagent                              | Amount  |
|        1 | 100X 9 ph antigen retrieval solution | 500 ul  |
|        2 | Sterile H2O                          | 49.5 ml |

13. Add primary antibody diluted in Dako antibody diluent. Place at 4°C overnight
14. Wash 3x 10 min with PBS
15. Add Secondary antibody (i.e. invitrogen goat anti-mouse Alexa 488 #A11029 use at 1:1K) for 30-60min room temperature
16. Wash 3x 10 min with PBS
17. Add Vectashield vibrance mounting medium and place glass coverslips on top. Afterwards store at 4°C or -20°C.
18. Visualize in confocal microscope.
