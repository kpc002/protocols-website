# Transfection

Creating retrovirus for a plasmid.

Procedure

Procedure

**Day 1: Plating Cells**

1\. **Use medium without PenStrep** (improves transfection efficiency)

2\. **Plate cells as follows:**

**10 cm dish**:

2.5 × 106 cells

10 mL medium

**6-well plate**:

0.5 × 106 cells per well

2 mL medium per well

**Day 2: Transfection**

3\. **Remove media**, replace with **fresh media from Plate E cells**

4\. Prepare **transfection mix (per well):**

**Tube 1:**

5 μg ( = 50 μL) pCL-Eco plasmid

550 μL OptiMEM

Aliquot 600 μL into 15 mL Falcon tubes Add 10 μg of transfer plasmid

**Tube 2:**

45 μL Mirus transfection reagent

655 μL OptiMEM

**Combine tubes:**

> Page 1 of 2

Add 700 μL of Tube 2 mix into each Falcon tube

Incubate at **room temperature for 20 min**

5\. **Add dropwise to cells**

**Day 3**

6\. Remove old media

7\. Add **fresh TCM media**

**Day 4**

8\. Harvest **first round of virus (supernatant)**

9\. Store in fridge

10\. Add **fresh TCM media** to cells

**Day 5**

11\. Harvest **remaining virus**, combine with Day 4 supernatant

12\. Spin at **1600 rpm for 4 min**

13\. Aliquot:

**2 mL per 5 mL Eppi** or use directly

14\. **Freeze at -80°C**

15\. If using **fresh virus**, **filter through 0.45 μm filter**
