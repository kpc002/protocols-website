## Procedure

1. Low percentage agarose for large fragments

Use 0.5 - 1% for 6 kb of fragment for better separation, or 2% for 100 - 800 bp.

2. If doing 0.7%, get 0.7g of agarose into one of the gel flasks, put into weighing scale.
3. Add 100 mL of TAE buffer to the flask.
4. Put in microwave 2 min. Shake to dissolve crystals.
5. Pour the gel cage into the box.
6. Then put the boiling glass into water to cool until its safe enough to touch.
7. Pipette 4 µL EB into the cooled glass (10 mg/ml EtBR).
8. Add the comb to the top of the cage wide teeth down.
9. Add the solution to the cage. Let dry for about 30-45 min.
10. Put the dried gel into the gel running box with the holes facing up.
11. Fill with TAE buffer until the gel is fully submerged.
12. Add 10 ul of Generuler 1kb plus ladder.
13. Add a 1:6 dilution of 6x orange to the DNA you want to run in the gel. For instance 30 ul DNA, add 5 ul of 6x orange.
14. Put the DNA into one of the wells on the gel and keep track of which one it is.

15. Connect black wire to the top of the box (negative) and red to the bottom of the box (positive).
16. 95 V, 30 A, 300 W. Time 30:00 min - Run
