## Procedure

## Processing G blocks from IDT

1. Put the g-blocks in the small centifuge for 1 min at 3000 rpm.
2. Pre-heat the thermo-mixer to 50C. And put a thermometer in.
3. Add 20 ul of sterile water to each g block.
4. Breifly vortex and spin on the table top centrifuge (a few seconds at each).
5. Put in the thermo-mixer at 50C for 20 min. Make sure the thermometer is at 50C
6. Vortex and then tabletop centrifuge for a few sec each.
