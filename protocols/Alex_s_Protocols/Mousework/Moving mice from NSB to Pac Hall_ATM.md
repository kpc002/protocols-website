## Procedure

## Procedure

1. Go to the NSB vivarium.
2. Turn the air and lights on in the bio hood.
3. Locate the mice that you want to transport and bring them to the bio hood.
4. Grab a clear plastic cage with the white bedding from next to the fume hood. You will need one for every cage that you have.
5. Bring or take a picture of the cage cards on the mice cages.
6. Put the mice into the clear plastic cages.
7. Close the clear plastic cages and put them into one of those big opaque garbage bags.
8. Go to Pac hall, put on PPE, and go to the Goldrath mouse room.
9. Grab waters, feeders, and cage card holders.
10. Put food in the feeders, and put it in the cage.
11. Put the water in the cage and put the sticker on the top of the water.
12. Add cage cards to the cages.
13. Put the cages on the rack. Make sure it clicks.
14. Change into new PPE and leave Pac hall.
