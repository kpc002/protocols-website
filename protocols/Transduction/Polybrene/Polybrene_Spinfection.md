**Polybrene/ Spin infection:**

Plate 10<sup>6</sup> Scid cells per well of 24 well plate.

Spin 2 minutes at 1200 rpm

Remove media

Add <span class="underline">Polybrene</span> (2μg/μl stock) to 1 ml viral supernatant and add immediately to cells.

8μg/ml final for Scid cells

**<span class="underline">OR</span>**

<span class="underline">Lipofectamine, Invitrogen</span>,

10μg/ml final for primary neurons.

20μg/ml final for primary thymocytes

<span class="underline"> </span>

Centrifuge for 45 minutes at 2000rpm, at 30<sup>o</sup>C

Remove the viral supernatant and add appropriate medium to cells scaling up volume as necessary.

Polybrene: Hexadimethrine Bromide, Sigma cat \# H-9268
