## Day 1: Activate T cells

See [CD8 Selection and Activation](/protocols/t_cells/cd8_selection.html)

## Day 2: Transduction

-   Prewarm centrifuge to 37°C

-   Thaw virus (2ml) in water bath\
    [How to make retrovirus](/protocols/infections/retrovirus)

-   Make Polybrene mix (70x)\
    For 100ul

    -   8 µl Polybrene
    -   10 µl BME (Mercaptoethanol)
    -   82 µl Optimem

-   Combine Virus and Polybrene Mix

    -   For 140 µl virus, add 2 µl mix
    -   e.g. 2ml virus --\> 28.5 µl mix

-   Transfer media from 6 well into 15ml falcon, keep warm

    ::: callout-tip
    Keep media for the different congenics separately
    :::

-   Add virus mix to activated T cells

-   Spin at 37°C for 1h, 2000 rpm

-   Take plate out and incubate at 37°C for 1-4h

-   Carefully remove virus mix

-   Spin down old media, add back and add same amount of fresh TCM (total volume 4ml per well in the end)

## For mixed transfer

### Day 3:

Transfer a total of 500.000 P14. Gated on Ametrine+ T cells, there should be a 1:1 ratio for the congenics.

::: callout-tip
Interactive calculator at [lab.beetletun.de](https://lab.beetletun.de)
:::

## For culturing

### Day 3:

-   Transfer in T75 flask
-   Wash wells with 2ml TCM, add to flask
-   Add 6 more ml TCM with IL-2 for final concentration of 1:2000

### Day 4:

Add another 12 ml TCM with Il-2 (f.c. 1:2000)

### Day 5:

Do whatever you want with the cells. E.g. FACS sorting for RNA Isolation
