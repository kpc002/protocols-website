## CMF solution
- 100ml 10x HBSS w/o Na-bicarb. Ca Mg (MEDIATECH #20-021-CV) 
- 100ml 10x HEPES-bicarbonate (recipe above) 
- 20ml BGS
- 780ml ddH20 (Millipore) 
- store @4oC in fridge overnight 

## DTE solution
- 460ml CMF (recipe above) 
- 40ml BGS
- store @4oC in fridge overnight 
- DOE: add 77mg Dithioerythritol (CALBIOCHEM #233152) 
- DOE: warm to 37 oC in water bath 

 
## 1x Percoll stock
- 450ml Percoll (GE HEALTHCARE #17-0891-01) 
- 50ml 10x DPBS w/o Ca Mg (HYCLONE #SH30378.03) 

## 44% Percoll
- 44ml 1x Percoll (recipe above) 
- 56ml RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE #SH30027.02) 
- store @4oC in fridge overnight 
- DOE: bring to RT 


## 67% Percoll
- 67ml 1x Percoll (recipe above) 
- 33ml 1x DPBS w/o Ca Mg (HYCLONE #SH30028.03) 
- store @4oC in fridge overnight 
- DOE: bring to RT 

 
## Collagenase solution

- 2ml HG (recipe above) 
- 200ul 1M MgCl2 (AMBION #AM9530G) 
- 200ul 1M CaCl2 (HYCLONE #SH30289.01) 
- 10ml BGS
- QS to 200ml w/RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE #SH30027.02) 
- store @4oC in fridge overnight 
- DOE: add 20,000 U Type I Collagenase (WORTHINGTON #4197) 
- DOE: warm to 37°C in water bath 

# Stock solutions

These can be stored for a longer time.

## 10X HEPES bicarbonate stock
- 10.5g Na-bicarbonate (FISHER #BP328-1 
- 11.9g HEPES (FISHER #BP310-500) 
- 500ml ddH20 (Millipore)   
- pH to 7.2 using pH meter (do not usually need to add anything)
- filter sterilize in hood using 0.2um pore Nalgene #291-3320 bottle top 
- store @RT in dark; do not use after 30 days 

 
## 100x HG stock solution (sterile)
- 59.58g HEPES (FISHER #BP310-500) 
- 14.6g L-glutamine B-grade (CALBIOCHEM #3521) 
- 500ml (QS to line) RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE #SH30027.02) 
- use stir bar to mix
- pH to 7.5 (will take 10-20ml 5N NaOH)
- filter sterile
- make 2.5ml aliquots
- store at -20°C in freezer
