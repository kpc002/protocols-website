# Cell culture media

::: callout-tip
Make sure to sterile filter all media before use.
:::

## TCM

-   500mL RPMI (1X)
-   50mL FBS
-   5mL PSG (100X)
-   500μl of BME (55mM)
-   5mL Hepes (1M)

## Plat E Media

-   500mL DMEM (1X, with Glucose)
-   50mL FBS
-   5mL PSG (100X)
-   500μl of BME (55mM)
-   5mL Hepes (1M)
-   1 µg/mL puromycin
-   10 µg/mL blasticidin

## KMR

-   DMEM, 10% FBS, 1% PSG

## EL-4

-   DMEM, 10% FBS

## Freeze media
- 45ml FBS
- 5ml DMSO

## B16, Yumm, Yummer, MC38 
- 500ml DMEM/F12 (11330032 Life Tech)
- 50ml FBS
- 5ml Pen Strep (15140-122 Life Tech)
- 5ml L-glutamine (25030-081 Life Tech)
- 5ml non-essential amino acids (#07600 Stem cell Tech)
- 1ml geneticin for B16gp33 only (10131035 Gibco) final concentration 100ug/ml 

## 6419c5 and 2838c3 PDAC cell lines 
- 500ml DMEM (11965092 Life Tech)
- 50ml FBS
- 5ml Pen Strep (15140-122 Life Tech)
- 5ml non-essential amino acids (#07600 Stem cell Tech)
- 5ml L-glutamine (25030-081 Life Tech)
