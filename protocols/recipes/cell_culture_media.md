# Cell culture media

{{< hint info >}}
Make sure to sterile filter all media before use.
{{< /hint >}}

## TCM
- 500mL RPMI (1X) 
- 50mL FBS
- 5mL PSG (100X)
- 500ul of BME (55mM)
- 5mL Hepes (1M) 

## B16 Media
- 500mL DMEM (1X, with Glucose) 
- 50mL BGS
- 5mL PSG (100X)
- 500ul of BME (55mM)
- 5mL Hepes (1M) 

## Plat E Media
- 500mL DMEM (1X, with Glucose)
- 50mL BGS
- 5mL PSG (100X)
- 500ul of BME (55mM)
- 5mL Hepes (1M)
- 1 µg/mL puromycin
- 10 µg/mL blasticidin

## MC38 Media
- 500mL DMEM (1X, with Glucose)
- 50mL BGS
- 5mL PSG (100X)
- 500ul of BME (55mM)
- 5mL Hepes (1M) 
- 5mL Na Pyr (100mM)
- 5mL MEMNEAA (100X) 

## KMR
- DMEM, 10% FBS, 1% PSG 

## EL-4
- DMEM, 10% BGS
