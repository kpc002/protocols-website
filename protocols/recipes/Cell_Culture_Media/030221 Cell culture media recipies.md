**D10 media** (for cell lines, human cells)

100 mL FBS

10 mL 100x Penicillin/Streptomycin/Glutamine mix (ThermoFisher 10378016)

10 mL 100x NEAA (ThermoFisher 11140050)

10 mL 100mM Sodium pyruvate (ThermoFisher 11360070)

5 mL 1M HEPES (ThermoFisher 15630130)

1 mL 55mM (1000x) βME (ThermoFisher 21985023)

950 mL DMEM (remove 50 mL to a conical tube)

**R10 media** (for primary cells)

100 mL FBS

10 mL 100x Penicillin/Streptomycin/Glutamine mix (ThermoFisher 10378016)

10 mL 100x NEAA (ThermoFisher 11140050)

5 mL 1M HEPES (ThermoFisher 15630130)

1 mL 55mM (1000x) βME (ThermoFisher 21985023)

950 mL RPMI1640 (remove 50 mL to a conical tube)
