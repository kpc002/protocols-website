Mouse iterative single-cell dissociation protocol, version JH_7.5, for flow cytometry.

## Reagents

1. DMEM with GlutaMAX, D-glucose, 1 mM NaPyr, penicillin/streptomycin, and 25 mM HEPES.
2. The same medium supplemented with 10% FBS.

### 10× PDAC dissociation buffer

Prepare 40 mL (sufficient for approximately 18–20 samples; scale as needed).

| Reagent | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Collagenase XI | 400 mg |
| Soybean trypsin inhibitor | 400 mg |
| Dispase II (5 U/mL = 10 mg/mL) | 50 mg / 5 mL |
| DNase (10 mg/mL) | 20 mg / 2 mL |

Use DMEM with GlutaMAX, D-glucose, penicillin/streptomycin, 1 mM NaPyr, and 25 mM HEPES. Do not add FBS.

## Procedure

1. Euthanize by CO2 asphyxiation followed by cervical dislocation; remove the pancreas rapidly (about 1–2 minutes).
2. Place pancreata in 5 mL medium without FBS and keep on ice until all samples are collected.
3. Mince finely for 2 minutes. Return to a 15 mL conical and add 7.6 mL medium without FBS, for a total volume of approximately 12.6 mL.
4. Add 1.4 mL 10× dissociation buffer per pancreas.
5. Warm quickly to 37°C in a 37°C water bath for 10 minutes.
6. Transfer to a 37°C rocking incubator.
7. Incubate for 20 minutes at 37°C, rocking and triturating gently every 10 minutes.
8. Let tissue pieces settle. Transfer supernatant to a new 15 mL tube and centrifuge 5 minutes at 4°C.
9. Resuspend in 5 mL medium with FBS and keep on ice.
10. Add fresh dissociation buffer to the remaining tissue. Incubate 20 minutes at 37°C, rocking and triturating gently every 10 minutes with LoBind tips. Add DNase as needed and repeat for three total collections.
11. Prepare 10 mL medium with FBS in a new 50 mL conical with a nylon strainer on top.
12. Pour harvested tubes through the strainer, mash through a 100 µm strainer with a plunger, and wash the strainer with 10 mL medium with FBS.
13. Centrifuge at 300 × g for 5 minutes at 4°C.
14. Resuspend in 5 mL medium with FBS.
15. Perform a viable cell count using AO/PI or Trypan Blue and save dissociation images.
