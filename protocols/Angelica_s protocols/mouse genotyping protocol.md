## Prepare 96-well plate and tubes

1. Thaw tail juice from −20°C before going downstairs.
2. Label a 96-well plate with cage numbers and N/L/R for each mouse.
3. Take the blood tube from above Anh's desk and add 100 µL to each well; prepare extra wells as needed.
4. Label microcentrifuge tubes with cage numbers and N/L/R for each mouse.

## Tail and bleed mice

1. Scruff mice and ear punch them with N/L/R for each mouse.
2. Hold the mouse by the scruff and the base of the tail with your ring finger.
3. Snip the tail into a tube.
4. While holding the tail base, squeeze down the tail to release blood into the well.
5. Dry the tail on a paper towel.

## Process tails

1. Add 400 µL tail juice to each tail tube.
2. Shake at 55°C and 1,000 rpm for 1 hour.
3. Add 150 µL 5 M NaCl to each sample and vortex.
4. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 5 minutes.
5. Transfer 400 µL supernatant to a new tube.
6. Add 250 µL isopropanol to each sample and vortex.
7. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 5 minutes.
8. Pour off the supernatant.
9. Wash the pellet with 500 µL 70% ethanol and vortex.
10. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 5 minutes.
11. Pour off the supernatant.
12. Dry tubes upside down over paper towels in a chemical hood for 1 hour.
13. Resuspend in 200 µL ultrapure water and store at −20°C until use.

## Process blood

1. Add 100 µL 1× ACK to each well and incubate 5 minutes at room temperature.
2. Centrifuge at 2,000 rpm for 1 minute and discard supernatant.
3. Add 50 µL Fc block to each well and incubate 10 minutes at 4°C.
4. Add 150 µL FACS buffer to each well, centrifuge as above, and discard supernatant.
5. Add 100 µL antibody cocktail per sample and incubate 15 minutes at 4°C in the dark:
   - CD8a PE-Cy7 (1:200)
   - CD45.1 eFluor 450 (1:200)
   - CD45.2 FITC (1:200)
   - APC Vα2 (1:200)
6. Add 150 µL FACS buffer to each well, centrifuge, and discard supernatant.
7. Prepare compensation controls: use half a drop of vortexed beads with 1 µL antibody per color; incubate 15 minutes at room temperature in the dark; add 200 µL FACS buffer to bullet tubes and refrigerate until running.
8. If fixing: add 100 µL fixation buffer and incubate 15 minutes at 4°C; add 150 µL FACS buffer, centrifuge, discard supernatant, then add 100 µL FACS buffer and transfer to bullet tubes.
9. If not fixing: add 100 µL FACS buffer and transfer to bullet tubes.

## Run PCR

1. Resuspend 10 µL primer stock in 90 µL ultrapure water.
2. Make master mix according to the appropriate sheet.
3. Combine 24 µL master mix with 1 µL tail DNA.
4. Run PCR:
   - AR: use `TOUCHDOW` on the rightmost machine, or an equivalent touchdown protocol.
   - UBC-Cre: use `GENO_TD` on the middle machine.
5. The run takes approximately 90 minutes.

## Run gel

While PCR is running, make the gel.

1. Set out the gel tray, gel basin, and required combs.
2. For a small 2.5% gel, combine 2.5 g agarose with 100 mL 1× TAE in a 250 mL flask.
3. For a large 2.5% gel, combine 5 g agarose with 200 mL 1× TAE in a 500 mL flask.
4. Microwave for 1 minute; swirl. Microwave for 30 seconds; swirl. Microwave for 20 seconds; swirl until flash boiling stops. Confirm agarose is fully melted, then cool under running cold water while swirling until the flask is safe to handle.
5. Add 4 µL EtBr for a small gel or 8 µL for a large gel and swirl.
6. Pour into the gel basin. If it leaks, place ice around the sides to stop the leak.
7. Let harden for about 20 minutes. Meanwhile, fill the main running basin to the line with TAE.
8. Remove combs once hardened. Place gel in the running apparatus and add enough TAE to cover it.
9. Load 7 µL ladder and 10 µL samples.
10. Run at 100 V and image. Orient red to red and black to black: the red lead is at the lower end of the gel and the black lead is toward the ladder.
    - AR gel: 45 minutes.
    - UBC-Cre gel: 45 minutes.
