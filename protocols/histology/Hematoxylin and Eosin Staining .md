**Hematoxylin and Eosin Staining on paraffin sections**

**(adapted from Xenium 10X protocol)**

<span class="underline">Deparaffinization</span>

Deparaffinization steps should be performed in a fume hood due to the
hazardous nature of xylene.

1.  Section FFPE tissues at 5um.

2.  Let dry overnight at RT.

3.  Place slides in a section dryer oven and incubate uncovered at 60°C
    for 30 min-1h

4.  Remove from the oven and cool down to room temperature for 7 min.
    Move the slides to each successive jar.

5.  Xylene Jar 1 and incubate for 10 min.

6.  Xylene Jar 2 and incubate for 10 min.

7.  100% Ethanol Jar 1 for 3 min.

8.  100% Ethanol Jar 2 for 3 min.

9.  96% Ethanol Jar 1 for 3 min.

10. 96% Ethanol Jar 2 for 3 min.

11. 70% Ethanol Jar for 3 min.

12. Nuclease-free water Jar for 20 sec.

<span class="underline">H & E Stain</span>

1.  Milli-Q Water Jar 1 for 2 min

2.  Hematoxylin Solution Jar for 20 min at room temperature.

3.  Milli-Q Water Jar 2 for 1 min at room temperature.

4.  Milli-Q Water Jar 3 for 1 min at room temperature

5.  Milli-Q Water Jar 4 for 1 min at room temperature. (gently shake off
    as much water as possible prior to bluing)

6.  Bluing Solution Jar for 1 min at room temperature.

7.  Milli-Q Water Jar 5 for 1 min at room temperature.

8.  70% Ethanol Jar for 3 min at room temperature.

9.  95% Ethanol Jar 1 for 3 min at room temperature.

10. Eosin Solution Jar for 10-20 sec at room temperature. (Adjust timing
    based on how dark it seems)

11. 95% Ethanol Jar 2 for 30 sec at room temperature.

12. 95% Ethanol Jar 3 for 30 sec at room temperature.

13. 100% Ethanol Jar 1 for 30 sec at room temperature.

14. 100% Ethanol Jar 2 for 30 sec at room temperature.

15. Xylene Jar 1 for 3 min at room temperature.

16. Xylene Jar 2 for 3 min at room temperature.

17. Apply cytoseal mounting media and coverslip
