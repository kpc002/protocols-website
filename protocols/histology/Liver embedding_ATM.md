## Procedure

## Day 1

1. Put the 5 liver lobes in 25 ml NBF on ice in the mouse room.
2. Added another 25 ml NBF upstairs.
3. Put on the shaker thing next to the water bath. cover with foil, shake for a few hours.
4. Cut the livers into smaller pieces and put back onto the shaker in 50 ml NBF

## Day 2

5. Pour out the old NBF and put in 50 ml new NBF then back on shaker

## Day 3

6. Make 4% agar.
2. 4 grams LB agar miller (above histology bench) in 200 ml H2O.
7. Uncapped - microwave 1.5 min. Watch so it doesn't boil over.
8. Once fully dissolved, pour into the little bowl on the hot plate.
9. Make sure agar is cooled to 55C before using. Use hotplate if its working.
10. Pour NBF into waste and put livers in PBS during manipulation.
11. Get Xenium practice slide

12. Get cassette
13. Position tissues in cassette, couple drops of PBS to hydrate
14. Remove couple drops of PBS when finished positioning
15. Add agar at 55C
16. Once hardened, take picture
17. Pop into cassette
18. Put into 70% ethanol container
