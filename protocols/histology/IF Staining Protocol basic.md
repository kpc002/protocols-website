**Basic Immunofluorescent Staining Protocol for OCT sections**

1.  Section frozen sections at 10um.

2.  Dry at RT for 1 hour

3.  Pap pen around tissue

4.  Wash 3x PBS for 10min

5.  Permeabilize if needed (nuclear or intracellular markers) with
    <span class="underline">0.1%</span> Triton-X in PBS at RT in
    humidified chamber.
    
    1.  If surface markers only, skip to step 6.
    
    2.  All incubation steps after this will be in humidified chamber.

6.  Block with DAKO block 1h

7.  Wash 3x PBS for 10min

8.  Primary Ab overnight at 4’C in humified chamber. Dilute antibody
    with DAKO antibody dilution buffer.

Next Day

1.  Wash 3x PBS for 10min

2.  If secondary is needed: Dilute secondary antibody with DAKO antibody
    dilution buffer (1:500-1:1000). Incubate at RT for 1-2h.

3.  Wash 2x PBS for 10min.

4.  DAPI (dilute 10ul in 1ml). Incubate 1min

5.  Dump DAPI off

6.  Dry slide as much as possible.

7.  Add Vectashield vibrance mounting media and cover with glass
    coverslip

8.  Let cure for at least 1-2h in the dark at RT (best O/N)

> Note: 0.1% Tween 20 in PBS can be substituted for PBS during wash
> steps
