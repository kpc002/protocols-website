**Post-Xenium IF & HE Protocol**

Miguel Reina-Campos

11/17/23

Updated 1/26/24 by Vida Luna

Slides are kept hydrated in PBS-T (0.05% tween-20 in 1x PBS) after run completion

Retrieve slides and keep at 4ºC. If storing, refresh PBS-T every 3-4 days to avoid bacterial buildup

There is no need to remove the slides from the cassette during IF stain

Reagents:

  - PBS-T (0.05% tween-20 in 1x PBS)

  - CAS-block

  - Xenium cassette plastic covers (2 per slide)

  - DAKO antibody diluent

  - Anti-CD8a (unconjugated, Abcam)

  - 1x PBS

  - Humid chamber (slide chamber with wet paper towels)

  - Anti-Rabbit AF594

  - WGA-FITC stock (1 mg/ml)--stored in -20

  - DAPI stain

  - Vectashield Antifade Hardset Mounting Reagent

  - Xylene-based mounting media - Cytoseal XYL

<span class="underline">When ready to perform IF</span>:

Wash once with PBS-T (1 mL) 1 min at RT

1.  **<span class="underline">Block:</span>**
    
    1.  Remove PBS-T
    
    2.  add 500 ul of CAS-block
    
    3.  add a plastic cover
    
    4.  Incubate for 2h at RT

2.  **<span class="underline">Primary:</span>**
    
    1.  Remove CAS-block
    
    2.  Add 200 ul of primary Ab diluted in DAKO Antibody diluent per slide
    
    3.  anti-CD8a (Unconjugated, Abcam) 1:200
        
        1.  \~50 ul per drop
    
    4.  add a cassette lid
    
    5.  Incubate overnight at 4C in a humid chamber

3.  **<span class="underline">Wash:</span>**
    
    1.  3x wash with 1 mL of regular 1X PBS, 5 min each
    
    2.  In the dark

4.  **<span class="underline">Secondary:</span>**
    
    1.  Add 200 ul of conjugated secondary antibody per slide
        
        1.  Anti-Rabbit AF594 1:200 in DAKO Antibody diluent
    
    2.  1 hour at room temp, add cassette lid

5.  **<span class="underline">Wash:</span>**
    
    1.  3x wash with 1 mL of regular 1X PBS, 5 min each

6.  **<span class="underline">WGA stain + DAPI</span>**
    
    1.  From the 1 mg/mL stock solution of WGA-FITC (kept at -20), thaw the stock and prepare 1 mL of a 10 ug/mL solution in PBS (can refreeze stock after thawing)
        
        1.  1mg/ml 10ug/ml is 1:100 dilution
        
        2.  For 1 ml of PBS, add 10 ul of WGA-FITC stock solution
    
    2.  Add 500 ul of WGA antibody solution per slide
    
    3.  Incubate at RT 15 min protected from the light
    
    4.  Wash 3x with 1mL of regular PBS
    
    5.  Add 500 ul of a 1:1000 DAPI solution in PBS to each slide
    
    6.  Incubate 5 min
    
    7.  Wash 2x with 1 mL of regular PBS
    
    8.  Dismount slide from cassette
    
    9.  Dry carefully and mount coverslip using Vectashield Antifade Hardset Mounting reagent.
    
    10. Let dry at RT for 20 min
    
    11. Proceed to slide scanning at 20X using DAPI, FITC (green, WGA), and TRITC (red, CD8a) channels.

7.  **<span class="underline">Coverslip removal</span>**
    
    1.  Soak slides in PBS at 4C overnight
    
    2.  Remove the coverslip by sliding it outside of the slide, DO NOT LIFT. Lifting the coverslip will break/dislodge the tissue
    
    3.  Wash 3x in regular PBS in a plastic container 5 min each
    
    4.  Wash 2x in ddH2O in a plastic contained 5 min each
    
    5.  Stain with Hematoxylin for 15 min
    
    6.  Wash 4x with tap water in a plastic container, 30 seconds each, until water comes out colorless
    
    7.  Dip 4 times in Blueing reagent
    
    8.  Wash 4x with tap water in a plastic container, 30 seconds each
    
    9.  Submerge in Eosin for 2 minutes
    
    10. Transfer to a 95% etOH container for 3 min
    
    11. Transfer to a new clean 95% eTOH for 3 min
    
    12. Transfer to a new clean 100% etOH for 5 min
    
    13. Transfer to a new clean 100% etOH for 5 min
    
    14. Transfer to a new clean Xylene for 5 min
    
    15. Transfer to a new clean Xylene for 5 min
    
    16. Mount coverslip on the slides using H\&E mounting media
        
        1.  Xylene based mounting media – Cytoseal XYL
    
    17. Let it air dry in the fume hood for 1h
    
    18. Proceed to scan H\&E 20X on the slide scanner
