# Cell Trace Violet

This composite protocol combines:

- [CellTrace Violet protocol](../t_cells/ctv.md) (`protocols/t_cells/ctv.md`)
- [Cell Trace Violet labeling protocol](../Goldrath_Protocols/The%20Master%20Folder/Anthony/Cell%20Trace%20Violet%20labeling.md) (`protocols/Goldrath Protocols/The Master Folder/Anthony/Cell Trace Violet labeling.md`)

## Composite protocol

1. **Prepare the dye stock.** Immediately before use, add the appropriate volume of DMSO to one vial of CellTrace reagent and mix well; frozen stock may also be used. The Goldrath protocol specifies adding **20 µL DMSO** to one vial. [Source: `protocols/Goldrath Protocols/The Master Folder/Anthony/Cell Trace Violet labeling.md`]
2. **Prepare the cells.** Pellet cells by centrifugation and remove the supernatant. The Goldrath protocol additionally warms cells in calcium- and magnesium-free PBS for 10 minutes at 37°C, then dilutes them to **10 × 10^6 cells/mL**. [Source: `protocols/Goldrath Protocols/The Master Folder/Anthony/Cell Trace Violet labeling.md`]
3. **Prepare the staining solution.** Dilute the CellTrace DMSO stock in pre-warmed (37°C) PBS or another protein-free buffer to a working concentration of **1–10 µM**. The t_cells protocol notes that 5 µM is used as a 1:1,000 dilution of stock; the Goldrath protocol specifies **1 µL CTV stock per mL** of cell suspension. [Source: concentration guidance from `protocols/t_cells/ctv.md`; volume guidance from `protocols/Goldrath Protocols/The Master Folder/Anthony/Cell Trace Violet labeling.md`]
4. **Label the cells.** Gently resuspend cells in the dye solution. Vortex gently to ensure equal dye exposure, as specified in the Goldrath protocol. [Source: `protocols/Goldrath Protocols/The Master Folder/Anthony/Cell Trace Violet labeling.md`]
5. **Incubate.** Incubate for **20 minutes at 37°C**, protected from light. The t_cells protocol also permits room-temperature incubation. Gentle agitation during staining can improve uniform labeling. [Source: room-temperature option and agitation note from `protocols/t_cells/ctv.md`]
6. **Quench free dye.** Add at least **5× the original staining volume** of culture medium containing at least 1% protein and incubate for **5 minutes**.
7. **Wash and resuspend.** Pellet cells by centrifugation, then resuspend in fresh, pre-warmed complete culture medium. The Goldrath protocol specifies washing cells twice. [Source: twice-wash instruction from `protocols/Goldrath Protocols/The Master Folder/Anthony/Cell Trace Violet labeling.md`]
8. **Recover and use.** Incubate for at least 10 minutes before analysis to allow acetate hydrolysis, then proceed with stimulation, incubation, or analysis. The Goldrath protocol instead concludes by resuspending and plating the cells. [Source: recovery/analysis instruction from `protocols/t_cells/ctv.md`; plating instruction from `protocols/Goldrath Protocols/The Master Folder/Anthony/Cell Trace Violet labeling.md`]

## Differences between the source protocols

| Topic | `protocols/t_cells/ctv.md` | `protocols/Goldrath Protocols/The Master Folder/Anthony/Cell Trace Violet labeling.md` |
|---|---|---|
| Dye stock preparation | Appropriate DMSO volume; frozen stock is acceptable. | Specifies 20 µL DMSO per vial. |
| Cell preparation | Pellet and remove supernatant. | Warm cells in Ca/Mg-free PBS for 10 minutes at 37°C and use 10 × 10^6 cells/mL. |
| Dye concentration | Recommends 1–10 µM; notes 5 µM as a 1:1,000 stock dilution. | Uses 1 µL CTV stock per mL of cell suspension; does not state final µM. |
| Mixing | Gentle resuspension; gentle agitation is suggested. | Specifies gentle vortexing. |
| Staining temperature | Room temperature or 37°C. | 37°C only. |
| Post-stain handling | Quench, pellet, resuspend in fresh medium, and allow at least 10 minutes for hydrolysis before analysis. | Quench, pellet, wash twice, resuspend, and plate. |
| Guidance for tracking generations | 5–10 µM for five or more generations; 1–2 µM for fewer than five. | No generation-based concentration guidance. |
