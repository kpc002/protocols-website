This composite brings together Plat-E transfection, retrovirus collection, and primary CD8 T-cell transduction workflows. Source protocols use different plate formats, reagent amounts, media, and downstream handling. Choose a single compatible source-specific route when the alternatives differ; do not combine quantities across routes without confirming the current lab-approved conditions.

## 1. Prepare Plat-E cells

All four sources use Plat-E packaging cells and specify medium without Pen/Strep during the transfection setup.

### 10 cm or 6-well production route

**Derived from: Alex Monell and Maximilian Heeg.**

1. Plate Plat-E cells the day before transfection in medium without Pen/Strep.
2. For a 10 cm dish, use 2.5 × 10<sup>6</sup> cells in 10 mL medium. For a 6-well plate, use 0.5 × 10<sup>6</sup> cells per well in 2 mL medium.
3. On the following day, replace the medium with fresh Plat-E medium before adding the transfection mixture.

### 96-well screening route

**Derived from: Goldrath Lab.**

1. Plate 2–4 × 10<sup>4</sup> Plat-E cells per well in a 96-well flat-bottom plate, with a final volume of 200 µL per well.
2. Use the middle 60 wells for transfection and fill the outer 36 wells with 100 µL medium.
3. Use Plat-E medium without Pen/Strep. The source defines this medium as DMEM with L-glutamine, 10% FBS, 50 µM BME, and 10 mM HEPES.
4. At least one hour before transfection, remove 150 µL medium and replace it with 50 µL fresh, warm Plat-E medium without Pen/Strep.

### Expanded 10 cm production route

**Derived from: Nicole Sharping.**

1. For a full transduction timeline, thaw Platinum-E cells five days before transduction, wash, and expand them on a 15 cm plate in D10.
2. Two days before transduction, trypsinize, count, and plate 2 × 10<sup>6</sup> cells per 10 cm plate.

## 2. Transfect Plat-E cells

All sources use pCL-Eco helper plasmid, a plasmid of interest, OptiMEM or serum-free DMEM, Mirus/TransIT-LT1 reagent, a room-temperature incubation, and dropwise addition to cells. Use one complete format below.

### Alex Monell 10 cm route

**Derived from: Alex Monell.**

1. Prepare Tube 1 with 5 µg pCL-Eco in 550 µL OptiMEM. Aliquot 600 µL into a 15 mL tube and add 10 µg transfer plasmid.
2. Prepare Tube 2 with 45 µL Mirus reagent and 655 µL OptiMEM.
3. Add 700 µL of Tube 2 mixture to each Tube 1 aliquot, incubate at room temperature for 20 minutes, then add dropwise to cells.

### Goldrath Lab 96-well route

**Derived from: Goldrath Lab.**

1. Per transfection well, add 1 µL of 100 ng/µL pCL-Eco and 9 µL OptiMEM to Tube 1, then add 200 ng shRNA plasmid.
2. Per well, prepare Tube 2 with 0.6 µL Mirus reagent and 9.4 µL OptiMEM.
3. Add Tube 2 to Tube 1 last, mix, incubate for 20 minutes at room temperature, and add the mixture dropwise to cells.
4. The source notes that a 6-well format typically uses approximately 0.8–1.2 × 10<sup>6</sup> cells in 2 mL per well and scales the reagents approximately tenfold.

### Nicole Sharping single- or double-plasmid route

**Derived from: Nicole Sharping.**

1. Warm 700 µL serum-free DMEM to room temperature.
2. For a single transduction, add 6 µg plasmid of interest and 2 µg pCL-Eco. For a double transduction, distribute 6 µg total plasmid of interest among the plasmids and add 2 µg pCL-Eco.
3. Add 30 µL room-temperature TransIT-LT1, mix, incubate at room temperature for 20–30 minutes, then drip the mixture onto cells.
4. Incubate overnight and replace Plat-E medium with fresh R10 the next morning; retain subsequent medium for virus collection.

### Maximilian Heeg 10 cm or 6-well route

**Derived from: Maximilian Heeg.**

1. For a 10 cm dish, mix 5 µg pCL-Eco in 550 µL OptiMEM, add 10 µg plasmid, then add a mixture of 45 µL Mirus and 655 µL OptiMEM.
2. For a 6-well plate, mix 0.5 µg pCL-Eco in 125 µL OptiMEM, add 2 µg plasmid, then add a mixture of 7.5 µL Mirus and 117.5 µL OptiMEM.
3. Incubate for 20 minutes at room temperature and add dropwise to cells.

## 3. Collect or use retrovirus

### Harvest-and-store route

**Derived from: Alex Monell and Maximilian Heeg.**

1. The day after transfection, replace old medium with fresh T-cell medium (TCM).
2. On the next day, harvest the first virus-containing supernatant, store it refrigerated, and add fresh TCM.
3. On the following day, harvest the remaining supernatant and combine it with the first collection.
4. Spin at 1600 RPM for 4 minutes, aliquot or use immediately, and freeze stored virus at −80°C. For fresh virus, the sources specify filtration through a 0.45 µm filter.

### Direct-use or small-volume harvest route

**Derived from: Nicole Sharping and Goldrath Lab.**

- Nicole Sharping directs changing to R10 the morning after transfection and retaining that medium for transduction; before loading virus onto Retronectin, spin the viral medium to pellet floating Plat-E cells.
- Goldrath Lab directs replacing 70 µL with warm T-cell medium on Day 3, harvesting 70 µL supernatant on Day 4, and harvesting/pooling the remaining 170 µL per well on Day 5. The source activates CD8 T cells on Day 4 for next-day transduction.

## 4. Activate and transduce primary CD8 T cells

**Derived from: Nicole Sharping.** This is the only supplied source that specifies primary CD8 T-cell activation and Retronectin-based transduction.

1. On the day before transduction, coat a flat-bottom 6-well plate with anti-CD3 in sterile PBS (5 µg/mL for P14 or 10 µg/mL for wild-type CD8 T cells) for 3 hours at 37°C.
2. Isolate CD8 T cells, then activate approximately 5 × 10<sup>6</sup> cells per well in 2 mL R10 with 2 µg/mL anti-CD28 and 50 U/mL hIL-2 for at least 24 hours.
3. Coat non-tissue-culture-treated 6-well plates overnight at 4°C with 1 mL per viral group of 15 µg/mL Retronectin in sterile PBS.
4. Remove Retronectin, block with 4 mL R10 per well for 30 minutes at 37°C, and warm the centrifuge to 37°C.
5. Remove the block, load 1 mL virus per well for 30 minutes at 37°C, replace with a second 1 mL virus aliquot, and incubate for another 30 minutes.
6. Pellet activated cells, resuspend them in the remaining viral medium with 50 U/mL IL-2, transfer them to the virus-coated wells, and spin at 1600 RPM and 37°C for 45 minutes.
7. Culture overnight, replace medium with fresh R10 plus IL-2 the next morning, and maintain cells at 1 × 10<sup>6</sup>/mL. The source reduces IL-2 to 25 U/mL at 5–6 days after activation.
