This combined reference preserves the approaches from the two original protocols. Confirm the appropriate tissue, media, centrifuge setting, and ACK lysis conditions with the current lab-approved procedure before starting.

## Original protocols

- [Cell Prep from Lymph nodes and/or Spleen](<../mice/spleen&lymph node cell prep.md>)
- [Spleen processing](../mice/spleen.md)

## Combined procedure

### 1. Collect and prepare the tissue

1. Collect spleen and/or lymph nodes into media.
2. Use the starting medium and volume appropriate to the selected original protocol:
   - Lymph node and/or spleen approach: approximately 5 mL RPMI.
   - Spleen-only approach: a plate containing media (RP5), then approximately 3 mL RP5 in a small petri dish per spleen.

### 2. Disrupt and filter the tissue

Choose the method that matches the original protocol being followed.

- **Cell-strainer method:** Place a cell strainer in a 6 cm petri dish containing approximately 5 mL RPMI. Pour the tissue into the strainer and use the bottom of a syringe plunger to grind it through. Remove the strainer and transfer the cells and RPMI to a 15 mL conical.
- **Microscope-slide/Nitex method:** Grind a spleen in RP5 with the rough surfaces of two microscope slides. Rinse the slides with RP5 and collect the cells in the petri dish. Pass the suspension through a Nitex filter into a 15 mL conical, then rinse the dish with RP5 and filter the rinse to recover remaining cells.

### 3. Pellet the cells

Use the centrifugation setting from the source protocol selected above.

- Lymph node and/or spleen approach: 1800 rpm for 5 minutes.
- Spleen-only approach: 1600 RPM for 4 minutes.

Discard the supernatant while retaining the pellet.

### 4. Lyse red blood cells with ACK

1. Resuspend the pellet in 1 mL ACK buffer.
2. Use the ACK exposure specified by the selected source protocol:
   - Lymph node and/or spleen approach: incubate at room temperature for 1 minute, then add approximately 13 mL RPMI and mix.
   - Spleen-only approach: use 1 mL of 1X ACK for approximately 5 minutes, or until the mixture appears clear and lysed. Dilute ACK first if it is not 1X.
3. Centrifuge again using the selected protocol's setting, then discard the supernatant and retain the pellet. The spleen-only source notes that the pellet should be white at this stage.

### 5. Finish the cell preparation

- For the lymph node and/or spleen approach, resuspend in RPMI, pass through a cell strainer into a 50 mL conical, take an aliquot to count cells, then pellet again at 1800 rpm for 5 minutes before resuspending in the desired medium at the desired concentration.
- For the spleen-only approach, resuspend the final pellet in 1 mL RP5, TCM, or MACS for the next step.
