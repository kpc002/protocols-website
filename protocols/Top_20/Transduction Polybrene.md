## Source

This page is a focused composite of the Polybrene-related steps from Maximilian Heeg’s source protocol. Among the four supplied source files, it is the only file containing a Polybrene workflow.

## 1. Activate T cells

**Derived from: Maximilian Heeg.**

Activate T cells using [CD8 Selection and Activation](/protocols/t_cells/cd8_selection.html) before transduction.

## 2. Prepare virus and Polybrene mixture

**Derived from: Maximilian Heeg.**

1. Prewarm the centrifuge to 37°C.
2. Thaw 2 mL retrovirus in a water bath. See [Make Retrovirus](/protocols/infections/retrovirus) for virus production.
3. Make a 70× Polybrene mixture for each 100 µL needed:
   - 8 µL Polybrene
   - 10 µL BME (mercaptoethanol)
   - 82 µL Optimem
4. Combine virus and Polybrene mixture at 2 µL mixture per 140 µL virus; for example, 2 mL virus requires 28.5 µL mixture.

## 3. Transduce cells

**Derived from: Maximilian Heeg.**

1. Transfer medium from the 6-well plate into a 15 mL Falcon tube and keep it warm. Keep media for different congenic groups separate.
2. Add virus/Polybrene mixture to activated T cells.
3. Spin at 37°C for 1 hour at 2000 RPM.
4. Incubate cells at 37°C for an additional 1–4 hours.
5. Carefully remove the virus mixture. Pellet the retained original medium, return it to the cells, and add an equal volume of fresh TCM to reach 4 mL per well.

## 4. Use or culture cells

**Derived from: Maximilian Heeg.**

- For mixed transfer on Day 3, transfer 500,000 total P14 cells and target a 1:1 congenic ratio among Ametrine-positive T cells.
- For culture, transfer cells to a T75 flask on Day 3, wash wells with 2 mL TCM into the flask, then add 6 mL TCM plus IL-2 at a final 1:2000 concentration. Add 12 mL more TCM plus IL-2 on Day 4. Day 5 can be used for downstream work such as FACS sorting for RNA isolation.
