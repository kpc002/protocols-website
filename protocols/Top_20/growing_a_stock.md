## Procedure

1.  Inject 3000-4000 CFU into hosts [(I just start with the vial in \~3-4mL Brain-heart infusion (BHI) media, with no antibiotic and move to step 9)]{style="background: #fcaeae"}
2.  Sacrifice mice between 48 and 60 hrs post-infection
3.  Harvest and tease apart spleen in 5ml PBS+0.1% NP40
4.  Plate 200ml of spleen solution onto BHI+erythromycin plate \[spread out sequentially to get single colonies\]
5.  Incubate for 48 hours\
    There should be little to no growth after 24 hours -- the presence of large colonies at this time point suggests contamination.\
    Do not grow past 48 hours -- overgrowth may occur
6.  Harvest a single colony into 200ml of BHI+ erythromycin (in mc tube)\
    Take several medium-sized colonies (growth will be heterogeneous)
7.  Grow for 4 hr with shaking 37°C (set speed higher to ensure agitation in mc tubes)
8.  Inoculate 4ml of BHI (no antibiotic at this step) with LM-OVA from previous step
9.  Expand with shaking until broth is mildly cloudy\
10. Add 4ml of bacteria to 26ml of BHI broth in a 50ml Falcon tube (or 200ml flask) [(depending on growth kinetics, you could set this up as an overnight)]{style="background: #fcaeae"}
11. Expand with shaking for several hours until broth is cloudy, add erythromycin for the last 45-60min of culturing (once the OD is at about 0.6) [(I don't bother with erythromycin since these are stable/do everything sterile)]{style="background: #fcaeae"}
12. Read OD: should be at 0.6 -- 0.8 before moving on to step 13 [(OD can be pushed a little higher; if overgrowth occurs, just add BHI and allow to re-enter log-phase of growth)]{style="background: #fcaeae"}
13. Add 15ml of the 40% glycerol stock [(40% glycerol stock is 20mL glycerol in 30mL BHI; I sterile filter mine and use fresh)]{style="background: #fcaeae"} to the 30ml of cloudy bacteria culture
14. mix gently on ice and aliquot 1ml in screw top cryotubes (keep on wet ice while aliquoting) [(I like my LM to be on wet ice to facilitate glycerol uptake for a bit before moving them into the -80°C freezer).]{style="background: #fcaeae"}
15. Freeze -80°C
