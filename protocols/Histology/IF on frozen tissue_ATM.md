## SATURDAY, 4/11/2026

Protocol I performed 4/10/2026 and 4/11/2026 .

- Mark around the tissue OCT square with a Pap pen
- Add dako block for 1 hr at RT
- Dump off
- Wash 3x with PBS for 5 min each
- Add primary Ab on.
- I did 1:100 ECAD AF594, 1:100 CD45.1 AF488, 1:400 HA rabbit from CST.
- Put overnight in cold room at 4C

## Next day

- Wash 3x PBS 5 min each
- Add secondary AB
- I added donkey anti rabbit 1:500 AF647
- Leave at RT for 1-3hr
- Wash 3x PBS
- 20 ul DAPI in 1 ml PBS
- Add DAPI mix for 1 min at RT.
- Dry off slide a bit
- Add vectashield vibrance on top of tissue, just enough to cover. put tissue slide in the slide book.
- Add cover slip atop the slide, make sure cover slip doesnt touch the white part.
- Wait 20 min RT.
- Put slide book in the cold room.
