title: "NES 042924 CD8 T cell transduction and double transduction"
description: "NES protocol for retroviral transduction and sequential double transduction of activated primary CD8 T cells."

author: "Nicole Sharping"

---

Purpose: To introduce genetic modifications to *in vitro* cultured mouse T cells.

**<span class="underline">Timeline</span>**

**Day -5**

1.  Break out Platinum-E Retroviral Packaging Cells for Ecotropic virus (Plat-E cells), stored in the liquid nitrogen.
    
    1.  Thaw cells until no longer frozen on skirt of the hood (2-3 minutes)
    
    2.  Move cells to 15 ml conical, add fresh D10 media to wash.
    
    3.  Spin cells down at 1600 RPM for 4 minutes, then plate onto 15cm plate with 15-20 ml D10. Culture at 37 degrees.

**Day -2**

2.  Trypsinize cells from plate (5ml trypsin \[Trypsin-EDTA, Mediatech\] for 3 minutes, stop with 5ml D10), count cells, and plate 2x10<sup>6</sup> onto 10cm plates (for as many transductions as needed).

**Day -1**

3.  Two options
    
    1.  Transfect Plat-E cells for single transduction (**single plasmid**)
        
        1.  Put 700uL serum-free DMEM (SF-DMEM) into a 1.5ml epiendorf, warm to room temperature
        
        2.  Add 6ug plasmid of interest and 2ug pCL-ECO helper plasmid to SF-DMEM. Pipette to mix well
        
        3.  Add 30uL room-temperature Mirus Bio TransIT-LT1 Transfection Reagent (Fisher) to SF-DMEM. Pipette to mix well.
        
        4.  Let sit at room temperature for 20-30 minutes to allow micelles to form.
        
        5.  Drip SF-DMEM onto previously-plated Plat-E cells
        
        6.  Let sit overnight, change plat-E media to fresh **R10** first thing the next morning. Do not change media again until transduction, as you are now collecting retrovirus.
    
    2.  Transfect Plat-E cells for double transduction (**2+ plasmids**)
        
        1.  Put 700uL serum-free DMEM (SF-DMEM) into a 1.5ml epiendorf, warm to room temperature
        
        2.  Add **total** 6ug plasmid of interest (e.g. if doing a double transduction, add 3ug of each) and 2ug pCL-ECO helper plasmid to SF-DMEM. Pipette to mix well
        
        3.  Add 30uL room-temperature Mirus Bio TransIT-LT1 Transfection Reagent (Fisher) to SF-DMEM. Pipette to mix well.
        
        4.  Let sit at room temperature for 20-30 minutes to allow micelles to form.
        
        5.  Drip SF-DMEM onto previously-plated Plat-E cells
        
        6.  Let sit overnight, change plat-E media to fresh **R10** first thing the next morning. Do not change media again until transduction, as you are now collecting retrovirus.

**Day 0**

4.  Activate T cells
    
    1.  Coat a flat-bottom, 6-well plate with 5ug/ml anti-CD3 (for P14) OR 10ug/ml anti-CD3 (for WT CD8 T cells) in sterile PBS. Coat enough wells for \~5million CD8 per well. Allow antibody to adhere to the plastic by incubating at 37 degrees for 3hr hours.
    
    2.  Sacrifice mice, dissect spleen and lymph nodes according to the dissection protocol, and isolate CD8 T cells according to T cell isolation protocol or sorting protocol.
    
    3.  Pipette off excess PBS in 6 well plate.
    
    4.  Add isolated CD8 T cells (\~5million per well in 2ml R10) to each well + 2ug/ml anti-CD28 + 50U/ml CETUS hIL-2. Add gently to not disturb anti-CD3 coated on plate.
    
    5.  Let cells activate at least 24 hours at 37 degrees.

5.  Coat plate with retronectin for next day
    
    1.  Prepare 1ml sterile PBS + 66x dilution factor retronectin (initial conc = 1000ug/ml, final conc = 15ug/ml) for each retroviral group.
    
    2.  Coat 6 well plate (**non-tissue culture treated**) with 1ml retronectin per well for each retroviral group.
    
    3.  Seal the plate with parafilm and incubate O/N in the fridge (4deg).

**Day 1**

6.  Transduce T cells
    
    1.  carefully remove retronectin from wells with pipette (tilt the plate)
    
    2.  add 4 ml of R10 to each well to block aspecific sites (first ml drop-by-drop)
    
    3.  incubate 30min @ 37°C (while this is happening, start warming up centrifuge you will be using for transduction to 37deg).
    
    4.  Take viral media off transfected plat-E cells and spin down to pellet any floating plat-E cells. These tubes can sit in the hood while you are using them to coat virus on the retronectin plate.
    
    5.  Remove R10 from retronectin plate and add 1ml of virus and incubate 30min @ 37°C
    
    6.  Remove virus and add new 1ml of virus and incubate 30min @ 37°C
    
    7.  Remove virus again.
    
    8.  Remove T cells from yesterday’s activation plate (pipette media on cells multiple times with P1000 pipette) Spin down activated cells, and resuspend in remaining viral media (about 7ml left). Add 50U/ml IL-2.
    
    9.  Put cells in viral coated plate, wrap plate in cellophane, and spin the cells at 1600 RPM at 37 degrees for 45min.
    
    10. After spin is complete, unwrap cellophane and put cells back into 37 degree incubator, culture overnight, and change media to fresh R10+IL2 in the morning.

**Day 2-10**

7.  Culturing T cells
    
    1.  As appropriate for your experiment, continue culturing your cells or adoptively transfer into mice.
    
    2.  Keep cells at 1x10<sup>6</sup>/ml by counting every day. When cells need to expand, add additional R10 + IL-2.
    
    3.  5-6 days after activation, switch cells to 25U/ml IL-2 (1:4000 dillution) to become more rested.
    
    4.  Cells should continue to proliferate until 7-10 days after activation. Cells will stop proliferating but still be viable 10-14 days after activation. For cytokine competency to be assessed post day 7, the cells will need to be reactivated.
