# CD8+ T Cell Enrichment from Spleen + Lymph Nodes

Nicole Scharping

**Background:**

This protocol is for the enrichment of CD8+ T cells collected from mouse spleen and lymph node tissues for activation and culture.

**Caution:**

  - CAUTION: Comply with all biological and chemical safety guidelines, including disinfection of all materials contacting cultures by autoclaving or by contact (15 minutes) with 70% ethanol or 10% household bleach.

  - All steps should be performed to preserve sterility in the Tissue Culture (TC) room in the biological safety cabinet using aseptic techniques

  - CD3 and CD28 Antibodies are kept in 4<sup>o</sup>C box in 4<sup>o</sup>C fridge in TC room

  - Biotin-Abs are kept in 4<sup>o</sup>C fridge in main lab area

**What you need:**

  - Biotin-Abs (see **Table 1**)

  - CD3 Abs

  - CD28 Abs

  - Phosphate buffered saline (PBS)

  - MACS buffer

  - T-Cell Media (R10)

  - Ammonium-Chloride-Potassium Lysing Buffer (ACK)

  - Miltenyi Streptavidin Microbeads

  - MACS column

  - Magnet for column

  - 15 mL Falcon tubes

**Procedure:**

1.  **3 Hrs before plating:** prepare 6-well plate with anti-CD3
    
    1.  Dilute CD3 Ab 5ug/ml in 1ml sterile PBS per well (coat 3 wells per 1 spleen+LN isolation)
    
    2.  Aliquot into a 6-well plate; tap plate to distribute liquid equally across bottom of wells
    
    3.  Incubate at 37<sup>o</sup>C for \~3 hrs

2.  Process spleen and/or lymph node tissues collected from mouse
    
    1.  Homogenize tissue(s) in R10 with filter in a dish and place solution in 15 mL tube. Wash fixture with additional R10 to collect remaining cells.
    
    2.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant
    
    3.  Resuspend pellet in 1 mL ACK and incubate at room temperature for 3 min
    
    4.  Dilute with 3 mL R10
    
    5.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant

3.  Treatment of cells with Biotin-Ab stain (see **Table 1**)
    
    1.  Resuspend pellet in 3ml Biotin-Ab stain in MACS buffer per spleen+LN
    
    2.  Incubate at 4<sup>o</sup>C (on ice) for 15 min
    
    3.  Add 10ml MACS buffer to wash
    
    4.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant
    
    5.  Resuspend pellet with 500 uL MACS Buffer, then add 100 uL streptavidin microbeads
    
    6.  Incubate at 4<sup>o</sup>C for 15 min
    
    7.  Add 5ml MACS buffer
    
    8.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant
    
    9.  Resuspend pellet in 1mL MACS buffer

4.  Column purification of streptavidin-bound cells
    
    1.  Attach MACS column to magnet
    
    2.  Pre-treat MACS column with 3 mL MACS buffer and discard flow-through
    
    3.  Run cell solution through column, **collecting flow-through**
    
    4.  Wash column two times (2X) with 3 mL MACS buffer, collecting flow-through
    
    5.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 min and discard supernatant
    
    6.  Resuspend pellet in 6ml R10
    
    7.  Count cells using Beckman Coulter Cell Counter and record
    
    8.  **Add 2ug/ml CD28, 1:2000 IL2 (50U/mL)**

5.  Plating cells in pre-treated 6-well plate (see **step 1**)
    
    1.  Pipette off PBS/CD3 from plate, getting the plate dry (discard liquid)
    
    2.  <span class="underline">Gently\!</span> plate 2mL solution per well (6mL goes into 3 wells total)
    
    3.  Incubate at 37<sup>o</sup>C for activation for 24hr

**Additional Information:**

**Table 1:** Biotin-tagged antibody mixture for CD8+ T-Cell enrichment

| **Antibody** | **Marker for** | **Final Dilution** |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------------------ |
| MHCII        | APC            | 1:200              |
| B220         | B Cells        | 1:200              |
| CD4          | CD4 T Cells    | 1:200              |
| CD19         | B Cells        | 1:200              |
| CD11b        | Monocytes      | 1:200              |
| GR-1/Ly6G    | Neutrophils    | 1:200              |
| NK1.1        | NK cells       | 1:200              |
