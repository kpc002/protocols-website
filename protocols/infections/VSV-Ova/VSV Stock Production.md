## Reagents

The original protocol suggests using DM-5. This version uses DM-10: DMEM with 10% FCS and penicillin-streptomycin.

## Preparation

Two days before infection, prepare BHK-21 cells:

- Take a confluent medium-sized T75 flask.
- Trypsinize thoroughly.
- Seed a 1/33 and a 1/66 split into a new T75 flask.
- Optionally, count the cells and plate 5 x 10^5^ cells and 1 x 10^6^ cells.

## Day of Infection

- Select a tissue-culture flask that is 90% confluent.
- Thaw the virus stock on ice.
- A T75 flask contains approximately 5 x 10^6^ total cells.
- Aim for an MOI of 0.05, with a stated range of 0.1 to 0.01.
- Dilute the virus so that 1 mL of DM-10 contains the desired amount of virus particles.
- Remove the old culture medium.
- Add the 1 mL of virus-containing medium, ensuring that it flows over all cells several times.
- Incubate at 37 degrees C for 1 hour, tilting every 15 to 20 minutes.
- Add 5 mL of warm DM-10.
- Harvest the supernatant 16 to 18 hours after infection.
- Centrifuge the virus supernatant to remove cells, aliquot 50 uL, and store at -80 degrees C.
