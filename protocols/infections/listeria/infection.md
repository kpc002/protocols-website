# Listeria production for Infection

::: callout-important
 Do everything in a BSL2 TC hood.
:::

-   Thaw a frozen stock from -80°C (I usually stick in a 37°C H~2~0 bath).
-   Dilute 1:50 in tryptic soy broth or BHI (no more than 1 month old). For 5 ml broth, use 100μl listeria stock. Grow in 50ml conical. Do not cap conical tightly as air needs to circulate. Secure loose cap with tape. Put biohazard sticker on tube, and put a sign on the shaker as well, warning about biohazard.\
-   Grow in bacterial shaker at 37°C until you reach OD600 of approx. 0.1 (this is mid log phase) then stick culture on ice to stop growth. Anything between .09-11 is OK.

    ::: callout-info
    Depending on the dilution of the frozen stock this usually takes between 1.5 and 2 hours
    :::
    
    -   Use disposable plastic 1 ml cuvettes to OD bugs. Parafilm around the top of them before carrying out of the hood.
    -   Take an aliquot of tryptic broth alone for control.\
-   Calculate concentration of listeria. An OD600 of 0.1=2x10e8 cfu/ml. Calculate appropriate ratio. Final concentration should be 5000 cfu/mouse in 200μl. That is 2.5 x 10^4^ pfu/ml.
    -   $\frac{0.1}{2*10^8} = \frac{[your OD]}{cfu}$ --> $cfu = [your OD]*2*10^9$
    -   Usually should have to perform a dilution in the range of about 1:7000. Anything a lot lower or higher is probably incorrect. Since the dilution ratio is so high, perform serial dilution. Do not attempt to dilute entire 5ml of listeria.
-   Perform serial dilutions in sterile PBS to inject.
    -   Example:
        -   If OD yields concentration of 2.52 x 10^8^ cfu/ml, do a 1:100 dilution
        -   Take 10μl in 990μl PBS concentration now is 2.52 x 10^6^ cfu/ml.
        -   Take the total dilution you have to do and divide by the dilution you already did. That is the rest of the dilution you have to do.\
        -   Take 80μl of diluted stock and add to 7920μl PBS. This is the 2nd 1:100 dilution. which should result in 2.52 x 10^4^ cfu/ml. Now you can use this to infect.
        
        ::: callout-note
        Use the interactive calculator at [lab.beetletun.de](https://lab.beetletun.de/listeria)
        :::

::: callout-important
Bleach all tips and pipets, etc in 10% bleach before disposing as biohazard. When I do FACS staining of cells from infected mice, I flick plates into a 2 L beaker with bleach in the bottom and always use a plate with a lid (until fixed of course).
:::
