# Listeria production for Infection 

{{< hint danger >}}
Do everything in a BSL2 TC hood. 
{{</ hint>}}

- Thaw a frozen stock from -80°C (I usually stick in a 37 degree H20 bath). 
- Dilute 1:50 in tryptic soy broth or BHI (no more than 1 month old). For 5 ml broth, use 100ul listeria stock. Grow in 50ml conical. Do not cap conical tightly as air needs to circulate. Secure loose cap with tape. Put biohazard sticker on tube, and put a sign on the shaker as well, warning about biohazard.  
- Grow in bacterial shaker at 37°C until you reach OD600 of approx. 0.1 (this is mid log phase) then stick culture on ice to stop growth. Anything between .09-11 is OK.  
{{< hint info >}}
dilution of the frozen stock this usually takes between 1.5 and 2 hours 
{{</ hint>}}
  - Use disposable plastic 1 ml cuvettes to OD bugs. Parafilm around the top of them before carrying out of the hood. 
  - Take an aliquot of tryptic broth alone for control.  
- Calculate concentration of listeria. An OD600 of 0.1=2x10e8 cfu/ml. Calculate appropriate ratio. Final concentration should be 5000 cfu/mouse in 200ul. That is 2.5 x 10^4 pfu/ml. 
    - 0.1/2*10⁸ = [your OD]/cfe  -->     cfe = [your OD]*2*10⁹  
  - Usually should have to perform a dilution in the range of about 1:7000. Anything a lot lower or higher is probably incorrect. Since the dilution ratio is so high, perform serial dilution. Do not attempt to dilute entire 5ml of listeria. 
- Perform serial dilutions in sterile PBS to inject.  
  - Example:
    - If OD yields concentration of 2.52 x 10^8 cfu/ml, do a 1:100 dilution 
    - Take 10ul in 990ul PBS concentration now is 2.52 x 10^6 cfu/ml. 
    - Take the total dilution you have to do and divide by the dilution you already did. That is the rest of the dilution you have to do.  
    - Take 80ul of diluted stock and add to 7920ul PBS. This is the 2nd 1:100 dilution. which should result in 2.52 x 10^4 cfu/ml. Now you can use this to infect.

Bleach all tips and pipets, etc in 10% bleach before disposing as biohazard.
