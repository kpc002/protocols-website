Vaccinia Titration Plaques

1.  > Start with confluent 6-well plates of TK<sup>-</sup> cells. (Generally two 175 cm<sup>2</sup> flasks can be plated out in eight 6 well plates for use 24 hours later)

2.  > Aspirate the media from TK<sup>-</sup> cells.

3.  > Place 250 ul of each virus dilution per well, with duplicate wells of each dilution, to ensure good counts. 10 fold dilutions are performed in 24 well plates using 1 ml total volumes. 100 ul transfer volume and then mix with a 200-1000 ul pipettor, changing tips with each dilution. Normally, 10<sup>5</sup>, 10<sup>6</sup>, 10<sup>7</sup> dilutions are plated. Occasionally 10<sup>8</sup> is needed.

4.  > Incubate for 2 hours, rocking every 15 min.

5.  > Overlay well with 3.0 ml of 1:1 mixture of 2X EMEM and 1.8% agar.
    
    2X EMEM (500 ml) with
    
    25 ml fetal calf serum
    
    10 ml 100X glutamine
    
    1 ml gentamycin

6.  > After 3 days, stain with 2 ml/well of 1.5% neutral red in 1:1 mixture of 2X EMEM and 1.8% agar.

7.  > Stain for 6 hours or overnight and count plaques. Remember to include a multiplication factor of 4 because you initially infected with 250 ul of virus.
