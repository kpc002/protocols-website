# Infection with LCMV Armstrong

{{< hint danger >}}
When working with the LCMV stock dispose used tips in 10% bleach. Work in BSL-2 safety cabinet.
{{</ hint>}}

Our current stock (2020) has a concentration of {{< katex >}}2.3 x 10^7{{</ katex >}} PFU/ml. Each mouse should get 200000 ({{< katex >}}2 x 10^5{{</ katex >}}) pfu in a total volume of 200ul.

To archive this, make a 1:23 dilation. Then inject 200ul i.p. per mouse
