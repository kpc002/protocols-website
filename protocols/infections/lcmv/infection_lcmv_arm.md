::: callout-warning
When working with the LCMV stock dispose used tips in 10% bleach. Work in BSL-2 safety cabinet.
:::

Our current stock (2020) has a concentration of $2.3 x 10^7$ PFU/ml. Each mouse should get 200000 ($2 x 10^5$) pfu in a total volume of 200μl.

To archive this, make a 1:23 dilation. Then inject 200μl i.p. per mouse
