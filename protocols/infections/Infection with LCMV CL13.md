# Infection with LCMV CL13

Use the [LCMV CL13 Titer Calculator](LCMV%20CL13%20Titer%20calculator.qmd) to calculate the AB or NM stock-virus and PBS volumes needed for infection.

## Naïve P14

**Day -1:** Transfer naïve P14 cells (500–1,000) by retro-orbital injection.

**Day 0:** Infect retro-orbitally with LCMV Clone 13 (2 × 10^6 PFU of AB or NM virus) in 200 µL. Dilute stock with PBS. Note that AB and NM have **different titers**.

## Transduced P14

Transfer 2–10 × 10^5 cells retro-orbitally.

Infect with Clone 13 on the same day as cell transfer. Infect retro-orbitally with LCMV Clone 13 (2 × 10^6 PFU of AB and NM virus) in 200 µL. Dilute stock with PBS. Note that AB and NM have **different titers**.

These are the PFU/mL values of each Clone 13 stock in the Goldrath Lab:

| Stock | AB (2015) | NM (2020) |
|---|---:|---:|
| New average PFU/mL | 5.15 × 10^7 | 8.50 × 10^7 |

Dilute stock in the hood. Dispose of all tips in 10% bleach.

<u>All takedowns must be performed in the hood regardless of timepoint, as Clone 13 is chronic.</u>
