## Protocol:

1. Requests should be made 48 hours (at least) before you need P14 moved upstairs
2. Sign up for mouse in LabTracks
3. Navigate to cage prep request link and make new item
4. Request date: when you want mice moved upstairs
    1. They will usually do this at the end of the day so if you need them in the morning request to have them upstairs one day before
5. Type of Prep – experimental cage prep
6. Put in animal IDs from LabTracks
7. Intended Experiment: Other
8. Light Cycle: 249 (ABSL2)
9. In comments I usually put when I want the mice upstairs by
10. You will receive email confirmation your request was submitted. You can check back at your request to see if it has been scheduled
    1. Once moved should be updated in Lab Tracks group: Exp-IM-01-001-2308

## AHR 249 BSL2 Procedures/Workflow:

Things to note:

1. Isofluorane must be checked out from 4<sup>th</sup> floor cabinet and cannot be left unattended at any point
2. You MUST change into scrubs before entering BSL2 and after exiting BSL2, must change into clean clothes before re-entering lab spaces (ie you take iso down to locker room with you)
3. My normal flow:
    1. Drop of cooler outside AHR 249
    2. Go downstairs and change into scrubs
    3. Go up to 4<sup>th</sup> floor drug cabinet
    4. Check out isofluorane
    5. Go to 249 AHR – put on appropriate PPE over scrubs
    6. After procedure/collecting samples: Leave cooler outside 249 near elevator (take iso with you to locker room)
    7. Change back into clothes
    8. Back up to 4<sup>th</sup> floor to check iso back in (record amount used)
    9. Back to 2<sup>nd</sup> floor to retrieve cooler
    10. Back to 220 for processing
4. ALL LCMV or INFECTIOUS experiments MUST be carried out in a BSC (even after clearance timepoint), if propagating or making stocks must also wear mask
