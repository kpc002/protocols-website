## Preparation of PCR product

- FWD primer: 2.5 µL (1:10 dilution)
- REV primer: 2.5 µL (1:10 dilution)
- Q5 high-fidelity polymerase master mix, 2×: 25 µL
- Genomic DNA: 150–250 ng (usually 200 ng)
- H2O: bring to 50 µL

## Run PCR product

- On the middle PCR thermocycler, use the Q5 program (approximately 1 hour 45 minutes).

## Run the gel

Follow the normal PCR gel-running instructions: 100 V for approximately 30 minutes.

- Load 16.67 µL PCR product plus 3.33 µL 6× loading dye.
- Use a 1 kb ladder.

## Notes

- Expected band size for Glp1r new SS guide 1: 572 bp.
- Expected band size for Glp1r new SS guide 2 (for use with only guide AB): 656 bp.
