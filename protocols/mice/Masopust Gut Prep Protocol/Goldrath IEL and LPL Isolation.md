<span class="underline">IEL Isolation:</span>

1\. Turn on hot plate and add DTE to DTE solution

2\. Bring reagents to appropriate temperature

\-CMF on ice (ice bucket)

\-DTE solution and collagenase solution (if isolating LP)to 37<sup>o</sup>C (water bath)

\-Percolls to RT (bench)

3\. Resect small intestine from mouse (we put it in RPMI+5% serum on ice until ready to isolate cells)

4\. Cut out peyers patches; Push out fecal content; Cut longitudinally to flatten intestine and then cut intestine into \~ 1cm pieces (laterally)

5\. Place pieces into 125ml Erlenmeyer flask w/30ml CMF. Let pieces settle; pour off supernatant. (discard). Repeat wash with another 30ml CMF; pour off supernatant. (discard)

6\. Add 35ml DTE solution and a stir bar; spin on hot plate for 30 min@37<sup>o</sup>C

7\. Pour off supernatant through a cell strainer into clean 50ml conical. Wash flask/intestine pieces with additional DTE solution and top up the 50ml conical. Put on ice. (These are your IEL’s)

> \*if you are isolating cells from LP start this prep now by returning all intestine pieces to the flask and adding 30ml collagenase Type I solution (see LP protocol).

8\. Spin down IEL and discard supernatant. Resuspend pellet in 5ml 44% Percoll + transfer to 15 ml round bottom tube. Underlay 2ml of 67% Percoll (we do this with Pasteur pipettes).

9\. Centrifuge @RT for 20 min @2000rpm (4 Acceleration and **0 BRAKE\!**)

10\. Remove lymphocyte interface into 15ml conical and top off tube with RPMI+5% serum.

11\. Centrifuge @4 <sup>o</sup>C for 10min @2000rpm (with brake). The resultant pellet are your <span class="underline">purified IELs</span>.

<span class="underline">LPL Isolation</span>

1\. Add Collagenase to solution

2\. Bring reagent to appropriate temperature

\-Collagenase to 37<sup>o</sup>C (water bath)

\-Percolls to RT (bench)

3\. Add 30ml Collagenase solution to intestine pieces (post-IEL prep); **spin on hot plate for 45min @37<sup>o</sup>C**

4\. Pour off supernatant through cell strainer into new 50ml conical (These are your LP cells)

5\. Centrifuge @4 <sup>o</sup>C for 5 min @1600rpm; Resuspend pellet in 5 ml 44% Percoll; transfer to 15 ml

round-bottom tube; Underlay with 2ml of 67% Percoll (with Pasteur pipette)

6\. Centrifuge @RT for 20 min @2000rpm (4 Acceleration and **0 BRAKE\!**)

7\. Remove lymphocyte interface into 15ml conical and top off tube with RPMI+5% serum.

8\. Centrifuge @4 <sup>o</sup>C for 10min @2000rpm (with brake). The resultant pellet are your <span class="underline">purified LPLs</span>.
