Tamoxifen treatment

Treat mice with Tamoxifen to activate CreERT2
Author

Maximilian Heeg

Published

April 18, 2023

Background

The Cre-LoxP system

The Cre recombinase recognizes a specific DNA fragment called loxP and mediates site specific deletion of larger DNA between two loxP sites. Concerning the mechanism of Cre-loxP system, a single Cre recombinase recognizes two directly repeated loxP site, then the Cre excises the loxP flanked (floxed) DNA. If a gene is flanked by two loxP sites, this gene (or a part of a gene) is excised leading to a functional knockout of that gene.

NoteMore usages for the loxP Cre sysyem

Of course, the Cre-LoxP system can do more than just knockouts. Depending on the orientation of the loxP sites, it can invert parts of DNA. Or is a STOP codon is flanked by loxP sites, the expression of a gene can be turned on.

More background information and illustrations can be found on the JAX website: Cre Lox Breeding for Beginners,

It is important to keep in mind, that the floxed allele is only excised when a Cre recombinase is expressed. We can hence create lineage specific knockouts by fusing fusing the Cre enzyme to the expression of a lineage specific gene. For example a CD4-Cre deletes the floxed allele in all cells that ever expressed CD4. This leads to a KO in CD4 and also CD8 T cells, since CD8 T cells express CD4 and CD8 in the DP stage in the thymic development. Another commonly used Cre is a Lck-Cre or Gzmb-Cre.

However, with these systems, it is not possible to timely control the kockout of a gene. To address that, a different version of the Cre recombinase can be used: Cre-ERT2. Here, the Cre is fused to a mutated form estrogen receptor, that does not bind to estrogen, but only tamoxifen. These Cre-ERT2 fusion proteins are sequestered in the cytoplasm and can therefor not induce DNA recombination of DNA in the nucleus. Only upon binding to tamoxifen, this fusion enzyme translocates to the nucleus and becomes functionally active. We can exploit that and treat mice with tamoxifen at certain times to activate the Cre-ERT2 and induce KO (or reporter expression) at chosen time points.

In the case of the Hic1 floxed mice, I use a Cre-ERT2 that is included in the ROSA26 locus and expressed constitutively. If we isolate P14 cells and transfer them into new recipients, we can induce the KO (iKO) specifically in P14 cells by administration of tamoxifen. The best control to use is a CreERT2- Hic1fl/fl P14, that also contains the loxP sites, but does not express the Cre fusion enzyme.

Make a Tamoxifen stock

Warning

Tamoxifen is a hazardous substance and must be handled with care!

Dissolving tamoxifen is tedious. The “old” protocol that was used in the Goldrath lab, made a stock of tamoxifen (100mg/ml) in pure ethanol first and then further diluted that fresh in sunflower oil (1:10 dilution). However, that did not work very well for me.

Instead I am following the combination of the old protocol and the protocol that can be found on the JAX website.

Procedure

  • Dissolve tamoxifen in sunflower oil at a concentration of 10 mg/ml by shaking overnight at 37°C. Tamoxifen is light sensitive, and should be made and stored in a light‐ blocking vessel (amber, or foil wrapped).
  • Tamoxifen is very hard to dissolve. It helps to vortex frequently and brake up larger clumps with a pipette or syringe.
  • I have also had good success in sonicating the Tamoxifen solution briefly.
  • At the end, the oil should appear clear and without any remaining clumps.
  • After tamoxifen is in solution, store at 4°C for the duration of injections and according the the Kaech lab aliquots can be frozen and stored at -20 C for longer time periods.

Treatment

  • Inject 100ul of the 10mg/ml tamoxifen solution i.p. into mice using a 26G needle. There is some resistance, but it should not be too hard to inject the oil with the 26G needle.
  • The resulting dose is 1mg per mouse per injection (similar to Milner et al. (2017) or Lin et al. (2022))
  • Mice are treated for 4-5 consecutive days to yield optimal recombination. Then I wait 3-5 days before checking protein deletion, this is the fastest it can happen.

References
