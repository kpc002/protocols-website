**Genotyping PMEL mice by flow cytometry**

1.  Bleed mice to type animals and coordinate with ear punch so we know which mice is what genotype in the cage

2.  Put blood into FACS tubes with 2 mls of RBC lysis buffer

3.  Wash 2x with lysis buffer

4.  Stain with master mix diluted in RBC lysis buffer for 20 minutes at 4C.
    
    1.  CD45.1- APC (1:100)
    
    2.  CD45.2- PE-Cy7 (1:100)
    
    3.  Thy1.2- PerCP (1:100)
    
    4.  CD8- Pac Orange (1:100)
    
    5.  CD4- Pac Blue (1:100)
    
    6.  VA2- FITC (1:100)
    
    7.  VB13- PE (1:100)

5.  Wash 1x with RBC lysis buffer and 1x with FACS

6.  Run flow on the samples to determine P14 status
