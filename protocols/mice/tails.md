# Processing Tails

## Procedure

- Thaw tail juice in water bath
- Add 400uL of tail juice to each tube
- Shake in 56°C thermocycler @ 500 rpm for at least 1 hour (or overnight)
- Add 150uL 5M NaCl to each tube
- Vortex and centrifuge @ 15000 rpm for 5 minutes
- Pipet 400uL of supernatant into a new eppi. Discard pellet
- Add 250uL isopropanol to each tube
- Vortex and centrifuge @ 15000 rpm for 5 minutes 
- Pour off supernatant
- Add 500uL of 70% EtOH to each tube
- Centrifuge @ 15000 rpm for 5 minutes
- Pour off supernatant and allow pellets to dry completely on drying rack
- Resuspend in 150uL DI water
- Freeze in eppies or plate


## Tail juice
Make 1.25L for every bottle (50mg) of Proteinase K!
- First, resuspend 50mg Proteinase K in 1mL MQ H2O
- Add in this order
    - 974mL MQ H2O
    - 12.5mL, 1 M TRIS, pH 7.8 (final 10 mM)
    - 12.5mL, 500 mM EDTA pH 8 (final 5mM) 
    - 125mL, 10% SDS pH 7.2 (final 1%) 
    - 125mL, 3M NaOAc (final 0.3 M)         *don’t pH (should be slightly basic)   
    - 1mL, 50 mg/ml Proteinase K                                
- TOTAL: 1.25L 1X Tail Juice
