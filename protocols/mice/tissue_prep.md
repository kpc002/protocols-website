# Lymphocyte isolation from mouse tissues

- For all Trm panels: 3 minutes prior to sacrifice, inject mice retro-orbitrally w/ 200 uL antibody mix (15 uL CD8a-APCcy7 + 185 uL PBS) 
- Sacrifice mice, remove small intestine and spleen, place in ice cold RP5 or CMF, and trim off fat 


{{< hint info >}}
Recipes for the required media can be found [here](/docs/recipes/takedown_media)  
An interactive calculator can be found on [lab.beetletun.de](https://lab.beetletun.de/media)
{{< /hint >}}


## IEL prep 

- Place paper towels on desk and wet w/ CMF 
- Wet gut w/ CMF, cut out Peyer’s patches and clean out the poop 
- Cut the small intestine in half lengthwise and then cut intestine into small (~1 cm pieces) 
- Place in flask and wash 2X w/ CMF, pour out contents carefully so no gut comes out 
- Dump out CMF carefully and replace w/ ~30-50 mL DTE, place on 37°C stir plate for 30 min 
- Pour contents into 50 mL conical with cell strainer 
 
 

## Kidney, SG, Liver, Fat

- Clean organs, remove fat and capsule 
- Cut into small pieces in 50ml falcon. 
- Add 10-20ml collagenase solution 
  - SG, Fat & Kidney: 10ml
  - Liver 20ml
- Incubate for 30 min @ 37°C 
- Mash through cell strainer. 


## Percoll 

Proceed as follows below for both IEL and other organs 

- Spin at 1600 rpm for 4 min 
- Make sure you see a pellet, then pour supernatant out and wipe top of conical 
- Resuspend each sample in 5 mL 44% percoll solution, make sure pellet is well resuspended 
  - For liver, make two percolls per sample
- Place glass pasteur pipette in conical and add 2 mL 67% percol solution to top of pipette to make a bottom layer. Don’t disturb after adding! 
- Spin at 21°C at 2000 rpm for 20 min, set acceleration to 4 and brake to 0 
- Check for an interphase between the two layers and transfer it to 15 mL conical (usually about 3 mL) 
- Fill to top with PBS then spin at 2000 rpm for 5 min
  {{< hint info >}} Make sure to mix, otherwise the interphase might remain {{</ hint>}}
- Resuspend pellet in 250 uL of RP5
