# Lymphnode processing

## Breaking the tissue and collect the cells
- Take lymph nodes from mouse & store in plate with some media (RP5)
- Prepare ~3ml of RP5 media in a small petri-dish for each mouse lymph nodes
- Grind up the lymph nodes in the media with 2 microscope slides using the rough surface
- Use some RP5 to rinse the slides and collect remaining cells into the petri-dish
## Filtering the cells

- Get a 15ml conical tube and place a nitex filter over the opening
- Transfer the cells from petri-dish over to the conical tube by passing it through the filter
- After all the cell with media has been transferred, rinse the petri-dish with some RP5 and transfer the contents through the filter to collect any remaining cells on the dish to the tube. 
- Centrifuge the tube at 1600RPM for 4 minutes. Discard the supernatant and save the pellet. 
- Resuspend pellet in ~175uL-200uL RP5/TCM/MACS for next step.
