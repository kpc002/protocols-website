# Spleen processing

## Breaking the tissue and collect the cells

1.  Take spleen from mouse & store in plate with some media (RP5)
2.  Prepare \~3ml of RP5 media in a small petri-dish for each spleen
3.  Grind up the spleen in the media with 2 microscope slides using the rough surface
4.  Use some RP5 to rinse the slides and collect remaining cells into the petri-dish

## Filtering the cells

5.  Get a 15ml conical tube and place a nitex filter over the opening
6.  Transfer the spleen cells from petri-dish over to the conical tube by passing it through the filter
7.  After all the cell with media has been transferred, rinse the petri-dish with some RP5 and transfer the contents through the filter to collect any remaining cells on the dish to the tube.

## Lyse the cells

8.  After all the cells are collected, centrifuge tube at 1600RPM for 4 minutes
9.  Discard the supernatant and save the pellet
10. Add 1mL of 1X ACK to the tube with pelleted cells and let lyse for \~5 minutes or until the cell mixture looks clear and lysed
    -   Dilute the ACK if the concentration is not 1X
11. After cells are lysed, centrifuge tube at 1600RMP for 4 minutes
12. Discard supernatant and save the pellet
    -   We should have a white pellet at this point
13. Resuspend pellet in 1mL of RP5/TCM/MACS for next step.
