**Supplementing humanized mice with cytokines**

**IL-15 complexes:** Inject twice a week.

2.5ul of hIL-15 (100ng/ul) to 2.4ul sIL-15Ra-Fc (0.5ug/ul) mix and incubate at 37C for 30 minutes.

**IL-7 complexes:** Inject twice a week.

7.5ul of hIL-7 (100ng/ul) to 1.5ul of anti-IL7 AB (clone M25, 5ug/ul) mix and incubate at 37C for 30 minutes.

**rGMCSF:** inject twice a week.

1ul of rhGMCSF (0.5ug/ul)

**human plasma:** inject 100ul of thawed, frozen human plasma twice a week.

Add all desired ingredients and add sterile PBS to a total volume of 200ul per mouse. Inject i.p. or i.v..
