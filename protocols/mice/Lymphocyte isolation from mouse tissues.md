Lymphocyte isolation from mouse tissues

**Materials**

  - RP-5 (RPMI with 5% BGS)

  - CMF medium

  - DTE solution

  - Collagenase solution

  - 44% and 67% Percoll solutions

  - PBS

  - Cell strainers

  - 15 ml and 50 ml conical tubes

  - Glass Pasteur pipettes

Can use iterative takedown media calculator here: https://protocols.heeg.io/recipes/takedown/\#interactive-calculator

**IV Labeling for T Cell Resident Memory (TRM) Panels**

1.  3 minutes prior to sacrifice, inject mice retro-orbitally with 200 μl antibody mix containing 3 μg of the labeling antibody (e.g. 15 μl CD8a-APC-Cy7 + 185 μl PBS)

2.  Sacrifice mice, remove small intestine and spleen, place in ice-cold RP-5 or CMF, and trim off fat

**Intraepithelial Lymphocyte (IEL) Preparation**

1.  Place paper towels on desk and wet with CMF

2.  Wet gut with CMF, cut out Peyer’s patches and clean out fecal matter

3.  Cut the small intestine in half lengthwise and then into small (\~1 cm) pieces. Keep the tissue wet during the entire time.

4.  Place in flask and wash 2× with CMF, pouring out contents carefully to retain tissue

5.  Discard CMF carefully and replace with \~30-50 ml prewarmed DTE

6.  Incubate on a 37°C stir plate for 30 minutes

7.  Pour contents into a 50 ml conical tube with cell strainer

**Kidney, Salivary Gland (SG), Liver, Lung, and Fat Processing**

1.  Clean organs, remove fat and capsule

2.  Cut into small pieces in a 50 ml conical tube

3.  Add prewarmed collagenase solution:
    
      - Salivary Gland, Fat & Kidney: 10 ml
    
      - Liver: 20 ml
    
      - Lung: 5 ml

4.  Incubate for 30 minutes at 37°C (1 hour for lung)

5.  Mash through cell strainer

**Percoll Gradient Separation**

Proceed as follows for both IEL and other organ preparations:

1.  Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 4 minutes to pellet single cell suspension

2.  Verify pellet formation, then carefully discard supernatant and wipe top of conical tube

3.  Resuspend each sample in 5 ml of 44% Percoll solution, ensuring the pellet is well resuspended
    
      - For liver, prepare two Percoll gradients per sample

4.  Place a glass Pasteur pipette in the conical tube and add 2 ml of 67% Percoll solution to the top of pipette to create a bottom layer. Do not disturb after adding\!

5.  Centrifuge at 21°C at 2000 rpm for 20 minutes, with acceleration set to 4 and brake to 0

6.  Locate the interphase between the two Percoll layers and transfer it to a 15 ml conical tube (approximately 3 ml)

7.  Fill the tube with PBS, mix by inverting and centrifuge at 2000 rpm for 5 minutes

> **Tip**
> 
> Ensure thorough mixing when adding PBS, otherwise the interphase might remain intact

8.  Resuspend the cell pellet in 200 μl of RP-5 media
