-   collect blood in PBS + 0.5% EDTA tubes
-   Spin down tubes at 5000rpm for 5 min
-   Remove supernatant
-   Add 1ml ACK
-   Incubate at RT for 8-10min
-   Spin down at 5000rpm for 3 min
-   Remove supernatant
-   Resuspend in FACS/PBS
