**Gut Prep Reagents**

**MRC - 2019**

<span class="underline">10X HEPES Bicarbonate STOCK (STERILE)</span>: makes 500 ml

  - WILL NEED 100 ml PER LITER OF CMF

  - 10.5g Na-bicarbonate (FISHER \#BP328-1

  - 11.9g HEPES (FISHER \#BP310-500)

  - \-500ml ddH2O (Millipore)

<!-- end list -->

  - pH to 7.2 using pH meter (do not usually need to add anything)

  - filter sterilize in hood using 0.2 um pore Nalgene \#291-3320 bottle top -\> stro @ RT in dark; do not use after 30d

<span class="underline">CMF solution</span>: makes 1L

  - WILL NEED AT LEAST 150 ml PER MOUSE

  - 100 ml 10x HBSS w/o Na-bicarb. Ca Mg (MEDIATECH \#20-021-CV)

  - 100ml 10x HEPES-bicarbonate (recipe above)

  - 20 ml Fetal Bovine Serum (HYCLONE \#SV30014.03)

  - 780 ml ddH2O (Millipore)

<!-- end list -->

  - Store @ 4C in fridge overnight

  - DOE: transfer to ice bucket

<span class="underline">DTE solution</span>: makes 500 ml

  - WILL NEED AT LEAST 60 ml per mouse

  - 460ml CMF (recipe above)

  - 40 ml Fetal Bovine Serum (HYCLONE \#SV30014.03)

<!-- end list -->

  - Keep sterile

  - DOE: add 77 mg Dithioerythritol (CALBIOCHEM \#233152)

  - DOE: warm to 37C in water bath

<span class="underline">1x PERCOLL STOCK (STERILE):</span> makes 500 ml

  - 450 ml Percoll (GE Healthcare \#17-0891-01)

  - 50 ml 10X DPBS w/o Ca Mg (HYCLONE \#SH30378.03)

<!-- end list -->

  - Keep sterile

  - Store @ 4C in fridge long-term

  - If removed 50 ml from original 500ml percoll bottle to make room for DPBS, bring this to 1X too, do not discard

<span class="underline">44% PERCOLL</span>: makes 100 ml

  - WILL NEEDAT LEAST 5 ml PER TISSUE (IEL+LP) PER MOUSE

  - 44ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

  - 56 ml RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE \#SH30027.02)

<!-- end list -->

  - Store @ 4C in fridge overnight

  - DOE: bring to RT

<span class="underline">67% PERCOLL</span>: makes 100 ml

  - WILL NEED AT LEAST 2.5 ml PER TISSUE (IEL+LP) PER MOUSE

  - 67 ml 1x Percoll (recipe above)

  - 33 ml 1X DPBS w/o Ca Mg (HYCLONE \#SH30028.03)

<!-- end list -->

  - Store @ 4C in fridge overnight

  - DOE: bring to RT

<span class="underline">100x HG STOCK SOLUTION (Sterile):</span> makes 500 ml

  - 59.8 g HEPE (FISHER \# BP310-500)

  - 14.6 g L-glutamine B-grade (CALBIOCHEM \#3521)

  - 500 ml (QS to line) RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE \#SH30027.02)

<!-- end list -->

  - Use stir bar to mix

  - pH to 7.5 (will take 10-20 ml 5N NaOH)

  - make 10 ml aliqupts

  - store @ 20C in freezer long term

<span class="underline">COLLAGENASE SOLUTION:</span> makes 200 ml

  - WILL NEED AT LEAST 35 ml PER MOUSE

  - 2 ml HG (recipe above)

  - 200 ul 1M MgCl2 (AMBION \#AM9530G)

  - 200ul 1M CaCl2 (HYCLONE \#SH30289.01)

  - 10 ml Fetal Bovine Serum (HYCLONE \#SV30014.03)

  - QS to 200 ml RPMI 1640 1x (HYCLONE \#SH30027.02)

<!-- end list -->

  - Store @4C in fridge overnight

  - DOE: add 20000 U type I Collagenase (WORTHINGTON \#4197)

  - DOE: warm to 37C in water bath
