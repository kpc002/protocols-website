Tumor Injection Protocol

1\) Remove media from B16 tumor cells

2\) Rinse once with 5 mL PBS

3\) Add 5 mL Trypsin

4\) Put in Incubator for 5 min

5\) Tap plate to dislodge most cells

6\) Add 5 mL of D10 to stop Trypsin enzymatic rxn

7\) Transfer to 15 ml Conical

8\) Take 10uL of resuspended cells (mix tube just before pipetting to make sure you get the most accurate cell count possible) and put in 1 well of V-bottom plate in order to count

9\) Spin cells down 1500 RPM for 5 minutes

10\) Count cells while spinning

11\) Resuspend in fresh d10 to get a concentration of 2.5 million cells/ml (so that 250,000 cells will be in 100 ul)

12\) also replate \~1 million B16 to a new 15cm plate to continue culturing them in vitro

13\) Put resuspended cells on ice

14\) Bring cells plus 3 syringes to mouse room (use orange capped insulin syringes for the injection, located above Amir’s bench)

15\) Grab 2 cage mice for 10 total mice (the ones in the middle rack against the wall that have “Nicole” on them) \*these are B6 mice from Jax, will have a white cage card with a Jax sticker too

16\) Expose mice to isoflurane

17\) Shave backside of mice

18\) Prepare syringe by moving plunger, then taking up media + cells, flick to make sure all air bubbles raise to the top, move plunger up and down slowly to remove all bubbles, make sure media is coming from top of syringe, do not hit plastic with syringe, point syringe away from self at all times

19\) Hold mice up by the back

20\) Insert syringe at about 30 degree angle (or less, the needle should be fairly parallel to your forfinger and thumb holding the skin) to the horizontal and then pull syringe up slightly to make sure the needle has pierced the skin, but has not gone through all layers of the skin of the mice. Make sure a nice bubble appears in the skin after the injection to make sure you’ve done the injection correctly\!

21\) turn off the oxygen and isoflurane when you’re done\!

22\) Return mice to mouse room, clean up hair in hood
