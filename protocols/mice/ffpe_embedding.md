## Prior to harvesting/setting up experiment:

1. Coordinate with LabOps for sample drop off and pick up. You will have to put samples in outside services queue in SLIMS once samples are ready.
    1. If Lab Ops is unavailable I have taken samples over myself
2. If first time making blocks: talk with histology team (Nina & Jocelin) about the Fred Hutch histology core form.
3. Drop off by 3pm for same day embedding (Mon-Thurs).
    1. Unsure if samples are ok over the weekend, but Vida says overnight (24h) in 70% EtOH is ok.

## Day 1

### Materials:

- 6 well plate, 1.5mL epi, 15 mL conical
- Pre-chilled 1x PBS
- Ice
- Cold 10% NBF ([Sigma](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/ht501128) [Aldrich](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/ht501128) [HT501128](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/ht501128))
- Minutien pins ([Fine](https://www.fishersci.com/shop/products/munitien-pins-500-pk/NC9681411) [Science](https://www.fishersci.com/shop/products/munitien-pins-500-pk/NC9681411) [Tools](https://www.fishersci.com/shop/products/munitien-pins-500-pk/NC9681411) [2600220](https://www.fishersci.com/shop/products/munitien-pins-500-pk/NC9681411))
- Scissors, forceps

Gut specific materials\*:

- Gavage, 10ml syringe
- Mouse Intestinal Slicing Tool (MIST) (<https://doi.org/10.1002/ame2.12228>, DeLucia, et al.)
- Silvalume 2.75 mm diameter, 7” length double point knitting needle (<https://a.co/d/2F3BP1p>)
- Thick blotting paper
- Clear western blot box
- Scalpel Blade size #10 Gut

\*as of 9/2/2024 we have no materials for small intestine embedding\*

### Protocol:

1. For all tissues: add cold 1x PBS to a 6-well plate on ice to preserve tissues after harvesting
2. Set shaker incubator in 220 to 4 degrees
    1. If conflicting TD may have to use cloning incubator in 520
3. Put PBS and 70% EtOH in fridge for Day 2

#### Small Intestine:

1. Harvest the small intestine and store it on ice with cold PBS.
2. The time between harvesting and submersion in NBF should not exceed 30 minutes. The faster the tissue is fixed; the more RNA will be preserved.
3. Add cold PBS to a clean beaker and have a separate beaker for waste
4. Cut region of interest of the gut
5. Flush each section of the gut with 10ml of 1x PBS using a gavage needle, discarding flow-through into the waste beaker. Repeat with 10 mL from other end.
6. Moisten knitting needle with 1x PBS for ease of loading
7. Insert the needle through the intestinal lumen
8. Place loaded needle directly onto blotting paper
9. Place MIST gently on top of the loaded needle with even force
10. Along MIST’s cutting guide, use a scalpel to cut open the intestine longitudinally
11. Carefully remove the MIST and spread intestine onto the work surface as you remove the needle (villi will be facing up)
12. Add minutien pin to each corner of the tissue and along the edges as needed (4-8 pins along the edges)
13. Add pre-chilled 10% NBF to a plastic container and submerge the pinned tissue. Close lid.
14. Incubate in 10% NBF for 24 hours at 4ºC in the dark.

#### Mesenteric lymph node (mLN)

1. Harvest mLN in cold PBS
2. Place mLN in 1.5ml epi
3. Add 1ml of 10% NBF
4. Incubate for 24 hours on rocker at 4ºC in the dark

#### Spleen, Salivary Gland, Kidney

1. Harvest spleen in cold PBS
2. Add to 15 ml conical
3. Add 10 ml of cold 10% NBF
    1. I bring 10mL of NBF in 15mL conical tubes directly to mouse room. Put spleen in PBS first then move to NBF
4. Incubate for 24 hours in shaker incubator (set to 4 degrees)

# Prostate

1. Cut rubber squares and place in 6 well plate
2. Harvest prostate in cold PBS
3. Clean up and place organs in the same orientation on the rubber
4. Use 3 pins to pin the prostate on both seminal vesicles and the bladder
5. Add 8 mL of cold 10% NBF, or enough to submerge the tissue
6. Incubate 24 hours at 4ºC in the dark

## Day 2

### Materials:

- Pre-chilled 70% ethanol
- Pre-chilled PBS

Gut specific:

- Cassettes and foam
- Empty bottle
- 2% LB agar
- Toothpick
- Ice
- Embedding mold ([Epredia™](https://www.fishersci.com/shop/products/shandon-peel-a-way-disposable-embedding-molds/2219) [2219](https://www.fishersci.com/shop/products/shandon-peel-a-way-disposable-embedding-molds/2219)) Gut

All NBF waste must be collected in hazardous waste container. Submit ticket in ServiceNow for waste pickup when bottle full. I usually collect the NBF and the 2 PBS washes as a precaution.

#### Small Intestine:

1. Discard NBF from chamber
2. Wash the gut in cold PBS 2x
3. Submerge tissue in pre-chilled 70% ethanol
4. Incubate at 4ºC for a minimum of 3 hours in the dark\*
5. Prepare a 2% agar solution and equilibrate the temperature to 45-50ºC in a water bath
6. Roll the gut into a Swiss roll with the villi facing out
7. Add 4-6 mL of 2% agar to an embedding mold on ice and submerge Swiss roll slowly to avoid bubbles.
8. Allow agar to solidify completely on ice
9. Cut away the plastic embedding mold and excess agar
10. 10.Place the Swiss roll stabilized with agar into a cassette labelled with a pencil and add to a bottle filled with chilled 70% ethanol
11. Send to Moore’s Cancer Center Histology Core for FFPE embedding _\*Can be stored short term in 70% ethanol at 4ºC_

#### mLN, Spleen, SG, Kidney, and Prostate

1. Remove tissue from NBF and wash with cold PBS twice
2. Place tissue in 15mL or 50mL conical with 70% EtOH
    1. Orientation should be specified in Fred Hutch form in the paraffin block
3. Add chilled 70% ethanol to tissue and incubate 3 hours\*
4. Tell Lab Ops when sample is ready so they can take to Fred Hutch for FFPE embedding. I usually print FH histology form and give to Lab Ops and also email a copy to the FH histology core [exphisto@fredhutch.org](mailto:exphisto@fredhutch.org)

_\*Can be stored short-term in 70% ethanol at 4ºC, up to 24h (overnight)_
