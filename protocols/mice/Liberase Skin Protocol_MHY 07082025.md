## Prepare Liberase solution

- Each vial of Liberase TL research grade (Roche 5401020001) makes 20 mL Liberase solution. Normally prepare 3–4 vials at a time.
- Add 2 mL RPMI plus supplementary complement per vial of lyophilized enzyme and place on ice to reconstitute for 20 minutes.
- Add 5 mL RPMI plus supplementary complement to each vial after reconstitution (7 mL total). Mix with a stripette before transferring to a 50 mL tube.
- Bring to 20 mL per vial with 13 mL RPMI plus supplementary complement. For example, if pooling two vials, bring the 50 mL tube to 40 mL total.
- Add 100 µL of 1 mg/mL DNase per 20 mL Liberase solution (200 µL for 40 mL). Use Sigma bovine DNase.

## Harvest

- Shave the right and left flanks, then perfuse mice with 10 mL HBSS.
- Harvest both ears, separate dorsal and ventral sides, and place in 1 mL Liberase solution.
- Harvest 3 cm flank skin (1.5 × 2 cm area) into 1.5 mL Liberase solution.

## Process flank skin

1. Incubate for 30 minutes at 37°C in a water bath in Liberase solution.
2. Peel epidermis from dermis and return it to the same Liberase tube.
3. Chop the skin.
4. Incubate for a further 80 minutes at 37°C in a water bath.
5. Transfer to 3 mL RP-10 and triturate 30 times with a transfer pipette.
6. Filter through a 70 µm nylon filter cone into a fresh tube, rinsing the filter with RP-10.
7. Centrifuge and aspirate RP-10 from the pellet. The pellet is ready to stain.

## Process ear skin

1. Incubate for 120 minutes at 37°C in a water bath in Liberase solution.
2. Chop the ears.
3. Transfer to 3 mL RP-10 and triturate 30 times with a transfer pipette.
4. Filter through a 70 µm nylon filter cone into a fresh tube, rinsing the filter with RP-10.
5. Centrifuge and aspirate RP-10 from the pellet. The pellet is ready to stain.

## Notes

- Flank skin has two incubations: 30 minutes before peeling/chopping, then 80 minutes at 37°C. Ear skin has one 120-minute incubation before chopping and filtering.
- Peel flank skin gently but thoroughly enough to see the white, stringy epidermal material removed.
- Do not over-chop the skin: chop vigorously for no more than 40–60 seconds to protect viability.
