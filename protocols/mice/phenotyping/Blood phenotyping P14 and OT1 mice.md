**PHENOTYPING**

\*\* for P14 mouse line, using blood

1.  Before starting, obtain 96-wells plate, add 100uL EDTA in PBS (same as blood collection tubes) per well needed, make sure to space the wells for easy access and prevent cross-contamination

2.  Collect mouse needed to phenotype

3.  Set up isoflourane
    
    1.  1.5 oxygen, 5% isoflourane

4.  After anesthetized the mouse, snip tail and collect blood into wells. Make sure to label for clarity
    
    1.  Only need 1 drop of blood
    
    2.  Can dip tail lightly into liquid if the blood does not drop down

5.  After harvested the blood, add 100uL ACK to each well with blood, lyse for \~5mins.

6.  Centrifuge for 2mins @2000 RPM. Discard supernatant.

7.  If pellet is still red, add 100uL ACK to each well with blood and continue lysing for another \~5mins, then wash with FACS buffer (\~180uL) and spin and discard supernatant.

8.  While lysing, prepare the antibodies for staining: CD90.2/Thy1.2, CD90.1/Thy1.1, CD8, CD45.1, CD45.2, Valpha2, Vbeta5
    
    1.  Check panel to make sure fluorophores will work on selected cytometer
    
    2.  Antibody master mix: make \~100uL/well

9.  After cells are finished lysing, centrifuge @2000 RPM for 2 mins. Discard supernatant and stain with 100uL of ab mixture per well.

10. Incubate on ice for 15mins.

11. After incubation, add FACS buffer (\~180uL) to the wells and wash @ 2000 RPM for 2 mins

12. Discard supernatant and fix with 1%PFA at RT for 15mins.

13. Wash with FACS buffer and resuspend in 200uL of facs buffer

14. Cover wells with parafilm and wrap plate in foil, to protect from evaporation and light

15. Fixed cells can be kept in the fridge (or CR) until needed (max time \~couple of days)

16. Transfer contents over to FACS/bullet tube before running

17. Running:
    
    1.  Looking for Valpha2+ in CD8 population for indications of P14+
    
    2.  Check for indication of strains
