Transduction using retro-viral sup

1.  Warm centrifuge to 37C

2.  Thaw retro-viral (rv) sup in 37C water bath and keep warm.

3.  Make polybrene mix and leave at room temp.

Recipe for 100uL

> 8uL Polybrene (1:12.5)
> 
> 10uL BME (1:10)
> 
> 82uL OPTIMEM.

4.  Combine rv sup and polybrene mix

> Use 2ul polybrene mix for every 170ml rv sup. Mix well

5.  Transfer media from cell culture to eppies, keep in water bath. Proceed with steps A-D, while step 7 is in progress.

> \*Pipet on the edge of well to avoid disturbing cells.
> 
> \*A. Spin this media (1600rpm for 4min) dead cells will pellet.
> 
> \*B. Transfer media to fresh tube.
> 
> \*C. Add 1x fresh TCM media .
> 
> \*D. Keep warm in incubator/water bath, until step 10.

6.  Add 200ul of polybrene+rv mix to each well.

> \*Add slowly dropwise to top of cell layer. Do not disturb cells.

7.  Spin 2000 rpm at 37C for 1hr

8.  Take plate out and put in incubator for 1 hr.

9.  Remove polybrene+rv mix by pipetting on the side of the well.

10. Add back media from step 4 in a manner similar to step 5.

11. Return plate to incubator.
