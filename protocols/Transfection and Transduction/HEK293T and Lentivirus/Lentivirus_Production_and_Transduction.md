  - Complete DMEM (See Nicoles Media Recipes)

  - Trypsin

  - 1X PBS

**<span class="underline">Protocol</span>**

**Day 0: Plate HEK293T Cells**

  - Plate 3.5 × 10⁶ HEK293T cells in one 10 cm dish (MRC)

  - > Dhruv plates 750K-1M 293Ts per well of a 6 well plate.

  - Use 10 mL complete DMEM per dish or 2ml per well of 6 well

  - Return the plate to the incubator.

**Day 1: Transfection**

  - Approximately 24 hours after plating, transfect the cells using
    TransIT-LT1.

  - If the medium appears exhausted or the cells need additional
    attachment time, replace the medium 3-4 hours before transfection.

  - For the medium change, add 10 mL fresh complete DMEM (2ml per well
    of a 6 well) and return the cells to the incubator.

  - Bring Opti-MEM and TransIT-LT1 to room temperature before use.

  - Prepare the transfection mixture in a 14 mL tube in the following
    order:

<!-- end list -->

  - Add plasmid DNA at an approximately 4:3:1 mass ratio:

  - 15 µg lentiviral transfer plasmid

  - 11 µg psPAX2

  - 4 µg pCMV-VSV-G

  - 30 µg total DNA

  - Add Opti-MEM to a total volume of 1.5 mL.

  - Mix by pipetting, then gently swirl the tube.

  - Add TransIT-LT1 directly into the liquid, dropwise.

  - Use 3 µL TransIT-LT1 per µg DNA as an optimization range; 90 µL for
    30 µg total DNA.

  - Gently swirl the tube. Do not pipette or vortex after adding
    TransIT-LT1.

  - Incubate the mixture at room temperature for 30 minutes.

<!-- end list -->

  - Add the DNA/Opti-MEM/TransIT-LT1 mixture to the cells dropwise in a
    circular motion.

  - Return the plate to the incubator at 5% CO₂.

**Day 2: Medium Change**

  - Approximately 16 hours after transfection, gently remove the medium.

  - Replace it with 10 mL fresh complete medium for virus collection
    (R10 for T cells)

**Days 3 and 4: Viral Supernatant Collection**

  - Collect the cellular supernatant at approximately 24 and 48 hours
    after the medium change

  - Pipette the HEK293T supernatant into a 50 mL conical tube.

  - Pool supernatant from multiple 10 cm dishes if needed.

  - Centrifuge at 1600 rpm for 5 minutes.

  - Optional: Filter the supernatant through a 0.45 µm filter.

  - Transfer the viral supernatant into 5 mL tubes or another suitable
    aliquot format.

  - Freeze in -80C

  - Prepare single-use aliquots and avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.
    
    **T cell activation: Follow Lab Protocol from Giovanni**
    
    **Transduction Option 1: MRC**

  - Coated retronectin plates O/N at 4C or for 2-3 hours at RT

  - > 90ul of RN in 5910 of PBS. Coat each well using 900-1000ul of
    > solution.

  - > Block plates with 4ml of R10 for 30 min prior to transduction

  - After 24 hours of activation, prepare Lib5 and Lib7 lentiviral
    library supernatants supplemented with: LentiBOOST (Sirion Biotech)
    at 1:200 dilution. Add both Solution A and Solution B

  - Add the activated cells to the supplemented lentiviral supernatants.
    **Dhruv has used 3ml of virus with cells, in each well of
    retronectin coasted plate**

  - Spin-infect the cells at: 800 × g for 3 hours

  - Following centrifugation, incubate the cells for an additional 4
    hours at: 37°C, 5% CO₂

  - Wash the cells to remove residual viral supernatant and transduction
    reagents.

  - Culture the washed cells for 4 days in medium supplemented with:
    Human IL-2: 20 IU/mL.

  - Depending on the goal, add mouse IL-7 (2.5ng/mL), and Mouse IL-15:
    25 ng/mL
    
    **Transduction Option 2: Arlene Sharpe (Naïve T cells)**

  - Coat non-tissue culture–treated 6-well plates with RetroNectin
    (Takara Bio, Cat. \#T100B) at approximately 15 µg/cm² overnight at
    4°C.

  - The next morning, remove RetroNectin and block each well with 2 mL
    of 2% BSA in PBS−/− for 30 minutes at room temperature.

  - Remove the blocking solution, add 2 mL lentiviral supernatant per
    well, and centrifuge at 2,000 × g for 2 hours at 32°C.

  - Isolate naive CD8⁺ T cells using the Miltenyi Naive CD8a⁺ T Cell
    Isolation Kit (Cat. \#130-096-543).

  - Plate the cells onto the virus-coated wells in complete RPMI
    containing 1 mg/mL LentiBOOST and 1 ng/mL recombinant murine IL-7.

  - Complete RPMI consisted of RPMI 1640 with L-glutamine, 10% FBS, 1%
    penicillin/streptomycin, 10 mM HEPES, 55 µM 2-mercaptoethanol, 1%
    sodium pyruvate, and 1% non-essential amino acids.

  - Culture the cells for 7 days with 1 ng/mL IL-7. Change the medium on
    days 2 and 5, and add fresh cytokine on day 4.
