**Spin Transfection**

  - Warm centrifuge to 30<sup>0</sup>C.

  - Mix 2 ml Virus Supernatant with 20 ul of DOTAP (DOTAP liposomal transfection reagent from Roche) in a conical tube. Leave on ice for 10min.

  - Pellet cells intended for infection. Remove supernatant

  - Transfer virus/DOTAP mix into tube containing cell pellet (2x10^6 cells/ 2 ml virus)

  - Transfer cells/virus/DOTAP mix into a 24 well plate

  - Spin for 90min at 30<sup>o</sup>C at 2500 rpm

  - Put in 37<sup>o</sup>C/ 6% CO2 incubator 3-4 hours or O/N

  - Remove supernatant carefully and add media gently so as to not detach the cells. I usually do this 3-4 hours after transduction and leave a film of media over the cells so not to risk aspirating them.
