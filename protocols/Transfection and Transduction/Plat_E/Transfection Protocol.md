**Transfection-96 well plate format**

**Day 1**

1.  Plate 2-4x10<sup>4</sup> Plat E cells/well in 96-well flat bottom plate (total volume 200uL/well)
    
    1.  Only use middle 60 wells, fill outer 36 wells with 100uL media
    
    2.  For six well plates- we typically use \~0.8-1.2x10<sup>6</sup> cell per well in 2mL and typically scale everything up by 10

2.  Plate cells in Plat E media <span class="underline">without</span> Pen/Strep
    
    1.  Plat E media = DMEM + L-glut, 10% FBS, 50uM BME, 10mM HEPES

**Day 2**

3.  Remove 150uL of media from Plat E cells and add back 50uL of fresh, warm Plate E media without Pen/Strep
    
    1.  At least 1 hr prior to transfection

4.  Prep for transfection
    
    1.  Warm mirus transfection agent and opti-mem to room temperature
    
    2.  **Tube 1**
        
        1.  Add 1uL of 100ng/uL of Pcl-Eco (helper plasmid) + 9uL of Opti-mem media per transfection well
            
            1.  i.e. if you are transfecting 10 wells, add 90uL of Opti + 10uL of Pcl-Eco to Tube 1
        
        2.  Add 200ng of shRNA plasmid per well (2uL of 100ng/uL) to Tube 1
            
            1.  i.e. for 10 transfection wells add 20uL
    
    3.  **Tube 2**: mirus/Opti Mix
        
        1.  Add 0.6 uL of mirus transfection reagent +9.4 uL of Opti-mem per transfection well
            
            1.  i.e. for 10 wells add 6uL mirus + 94uL of Opti-mem to Tube 2
    
    4.  Add Tube 2 to Tube 1 and mix well
        
        1.  Always add mirus/opti mix (Tube 2) last and mix well
    
    5.  Incubate for 20min rm temp

5.  After 20min incubation add shRNA mixture to Plat E cells dropwise

**Day 3**

1.  Replace 70uL of Plat E media with 70uL of warm T cell media

**Day 4**

1.  Harvest 70uL of RV supernatant and replace with 70uL of fresh T cell media

2.  We typically activate CD8 T cells here for transducing the following day
    
    1.  Typically getting 40-80% transduction with this protocol

**Day 5**

1.  Harvest all of RV supernatant and pool for transduction (final 170uL/well)
