**<span class="underline">Retroviral transduction of CD8<sup>+</sup> T cells</span>**

<span class="underline"></span>

**Have virus ready or start making virus following our protocol at day -1.**

**Day 1 (4-5pm). Anti-CD3/anti-CD28 stimulation**

Purify CD8s by negative selection – Follow our protocol

**Day 2 (3-4pm). Retroviral Transduction (24hours post stimulation; mitotic phase is 22-26 hrs post stim, which is the optimal time for transduction)**

  - \*Pre spin centrifuge at 4000rpm, 40C-- to get it up to 37C or as close to it as possible

> \*If making virus: Collect viral media and filter with a 0.45 um syringe filter to remove cell debris (alternatively centrifuge at 3000 rpm 10 min). Store it at -80 C.

\*If virus was stored at -80, pre-warm at 37C.

  - Remove \~1.8 ml of media (from the 2ml on the 6well plate). And **set it aside for after spin infection.**

  - Add 1 mL viral media (filtered) supplemented with 8ug/ml polybrene (1/1000) and BME 50 mM (1/1000) to each well. Double polybrene if double transduction (1/500).

  - Spin at 2000 rpm for 60 min at 37° (or as close as practical) to infect cells (120 min for double transduction).

  - Place them in the incubator for 2h.

  - Remove \~900 uL of viral media or aspirate, and replace with 2 mL old CD3/CD28 activation media + 2ml of fresh TCM media. Put in incubator.

**Day 3 (8-9am)** - **Add IL-2** (10 ng/mL r-human **IL-2**),

  - Pool CD8 T cells from the 6 well plates into a 50 ml conical and centrifuge 1600 rpm 5 min.

  - **Resuspend in 20 ml** of TCM media containing 10 ng/ml r-human IL-2 and transfer to T75 flask
    
    **Day 4 (5-6pm). Transfer activated T cells**

<!-- end list -->

  - > **Wash CD8 T cells from the flask in cold PBS 2x**

<!-- end list -->

  - **Resuspend in 1ml an count them in the hemocytometer**
