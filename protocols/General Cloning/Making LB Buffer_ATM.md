## Procedure

## Procedure

1. Take 25g of the LB granules and put them in one of the big LB bottles.
2. Put autoclave tape on the side
3. Grab one of the flat plastic buckets.
4. Go to the autoclave room and check if there is an open autoclave. Sign name and check in time.
5. Fill the LB bottle with 1L water. Specifically the clean water in the autoclave room.
6. Shake the LB bottle until the granules are dissolved.
7. Fill the plastic bucket with a solid amount of water, maybe like 1/4-1/3 full.
8. CRITICALUNSCREW THE CAP A BIT ON THE LB BOTTLE. Don't screw it off all the way.
9. Place the LB bottle into the bucket with the autoclave.
10. Start program 9 on the autoclave.
11. Come back in 1.5 hours.

01:30:00
