## Glycerol Prep

## Introduction

How to glycerol prep a plasmid for future use.

## Materials

- › Materials
- › 50% glycerol
- › good bacteria in LB

## Procedure

## Procedure

1. Add 3 ml of LB to the tube with the good bacteria.
2. Put the tube in the 37 degree shaker overnight.
3. The next morning: Add 500 ul glycerol 50% to 500 ul of the bacteria.
4. Put it in my box in the top shelf of the -80.
