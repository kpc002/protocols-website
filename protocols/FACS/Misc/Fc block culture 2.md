Fc blocking antibody production using 2.4G2 cell line from ATCC

Culture method: <https://www.atcc.org/Products/All/HB-197.aspx>

Media

DMEM high glucose (4500 mg/L)

10% FBS

4 mM L-glutamine

1.5 g/L sodium bicarbonate

Protocol

1.  Thaw the hybridoma.

2.  Resuspend in \~10 ml media to dilute DMSO.

3.  Spin down (125g, 5 min) and discard media.

4.  Resuspend in 1 ml media. And count cells (see Counting Cell section)

5.  Plate the cell in small ventilated flask with cell concentration at 100,000 cells/ml

6.  Put in incubator.

7.  Check the flask 1,2 day later: count cells and add media to expand the cells (keep cell concentration in the range of 100,000 to 1 million cells per ml.

8.  Grow the cell to bigger flasks (200 ml total should be enough).

\* If stock cell line is running low. Save a flask for cryopreservation.

9.  Let the cell produce anti-body till they are all dead (media should be yellow)

\*Start from 100,000 cells/ml in big flask, media should be ready to harvest after 10 days.

10. Harvest the sup and test.

\*A good batch should be able to block 1-2 mil spleenocytes using 1ul harvested media.

Counting cells with hemacytometer

1.  Resuspend cells in appropriate volume of media. If there are lot of dead cells, do a slow spin at 300g for 4 min to pellet live cells and resuspend in media.

2.  Dilute tryphan blue (1:2) with PBS

3.  Mix 2 ul cells with 18 ul diluted tryphan blue (1:10)

4.  Inject 10ul to hemacytometer and count

(10,000cells/ml) x (dilution factor/number of counted squares) x (average cells in counted squares) = number of cells per ml

\*count 4 squares is convenient
