**Antibody titration**

**Plate cells**

1.  Take aliquot of in vivo day 7 cells and in vitro stimulated P14. Take around 60e6 and then verify by cell count. Plate 2e6 cells/well.

2.  Plate 2e6 cells/well in 96 well plate

3.  Wash 1x with PBS

**Live/dead & Fc block**

1.  Resuspend in 50 ul of Fc block/well and then 50 ul of 1:500 APC-Cy7 live/dead dye

2.  Incubate for 15 min at RT in the dark

3.  Wash 1x with 100 ul of FACS

**Surface stain**

1.  Resuspend cells in 50 ul of surface stain/well with antibodies diluted accordingly in FACS buffer.

2.  CD45.2 – BV510 (1:100)

3.  CD45.2 – BV786 (1:100)

4.  Incubate for 20 min at 4C in the dark

5.  Wash 2x with 100 ul FACS

Use Intracellular staining (Foxp3 eBioscience kit if intranuclear molecules such as transcription factors, Cytofix/Cytoperm BD kit if cytoplasmic molecules such as cytokines want to be stained)

**Cell fixation & intracellular staining (Foxp3 eBioscience kit)**

1.  Prepare fixation buffer by diluting 1:4 of fix perm to diluent (1 ml of fix perm, 3 ml of diluent)

2.  Prepare 1x perm buffer from 10x by diluting buffer with H2O (1 ml perm buffer, 9 mls water)

3.  Resuspend cells in 100 ul fixation buffer

4.  Incubate for 20 min at 4C in the dark

5.  While cells are incubating, make serial diluted antibodies in a 96 well plate by adding 100 ul of perm buffer in the first well and 50 ul of perm buffer in each successive well.

6.  Then add antibody (one at a time for titering) at 1:25 to the first well, mix, and then transfer 50 ul successfully to each well afterwards all the way until you reach 1:400 (which is really 1:800 dilution).

7.  Do not add antibody to the last well, this is your blank control. \*Make each dilution twice as concentrated bc you are going to be diluting it 2x in each well bc there’s 50 ul already in each well. \* Go down the column with these dilutions
    
    1.  1:50
    
    2.  1:100
    
    3.  1:200
    
    4.  1:400
    
    5.  1:800

8.  Wash 1x with 100 ul PERM buffer (not FACS)

9.  Resuspend cells in intracellular staining antibodies diluted in perm buffer and incubate for 30 min at 4C in the dark.

10. After incubation wash 1x with perm buffer and 1x with FACS and you are ready to flow. Use QC voltages from Giovanni when you titer.

**Cell fixation & intracellular staining (Cytofix BD kit)**

1.  Use cytofix as is in the dark bottle by adding 100 ul/well after the FACS wash down prior.

2.  Incubate for 20 min at 4C in the dark

3.  Prepare 1x perm buffer from 10x by diluting buffer with H2O (1 ml perm buffer, 9 mls water)

4.  While cells are incubating, make serial diluted antibodies in a 96 well plate by adding 100 ul of perm buffer in the first well and 50 ul of perm buffer in each successive well.

5.  Then add antibody (one at a time for titering) at 1:25 to the first well, mix, and then transfer 50 ul successfully to each well afterwards all the way until you reach 1:400 (which is really 1:800 dilution).

6.  Do not add antibody to the last well, this is your blank control. \*Make each dilution twice as concentrated bc you are going to be diluting it 2x in each well bc there’s 50 ul already in each well.\* Go down the column with these dilutions
    
    1.  1:50
    
    2.  1:100
    
    3.  1:200
    
    4.  1:400
    
    5.  1:800

7.  Wash 1x with 100 ul PERM buffer (not FACS)

8.  Resuspend cells in intracellular staining antibodies diluted in cytoperm perm buffer and incubate for 30 min at 4C in the dark.

9.  After incubation wash 1x with perm buffer and 1x with FACS and you are ready to flow. Use QC voltages from Giovanni when you titer.
