Fc blocking antibody production using 2.4G2 cell line from ATCC

Culture method: <https://www.atcc.org/Products/All/HB-197.aspx>

Media (make 1.5L for splitting and saving for final FC block)

DMEM with 4 mM L-glutamine with 4.5 g/L glucose 500ml

FBS 5% 25ml

BME 1000x 500ul

PSG 5ml

Sodium Pyruvate 100x 5ml

NEAA 100x 5ml

1M HEPES 5ml

Protocol

1.  Thaw the hybridoma.

2.  Resuspend in \~10 ml media to dilute DMSO.

3.  Spin down (1600rpm, 5 min) and discard media.

4.  Resuspend in 4 ml and add to a well in a 6-well plate

5.  Split and maintain cell culture at a concentration between 1 x
    10<sup>5</sup> and 1 x 10<sup>6</sup> cells/mL.

6.  Grow the cell to bigger flasks

\* If stock cell line is running low, save a flask for cryopreservation.

7.  Let the cell produce anti-body till they are all dead (media should
    be yellow)

\*Start from 100,000 cells/ml in big flask, media should be ready to
harvest after 10 days.

8.  Harvest the sup and test blocking ability with flow cytometry.
