**Caspase 3/7 & 7AAD Staining**

*Staining prep*

1.  Transfer the cells and supernatant to flow tubes

2.  Wash wells 1x with media.

3.  Combine samples for controls.

4.  Spin down at 1200rpm for 5min.

5.  Resuspend in 200uL and transfer into a 96 well round bottom plate for staining.

6.  Spin plate down at 1200rpm for 5 min. Flick.

*Surface staining for surface panel*

  - CD4-PB (1:100)

  - CD14/19- BV510 (1:200)

  - Thy1.1- BV711 (1:100)

  - PD-1- BV786 (1:200)

  - CD8- PE (1:100)

  - FCGRIIB/STREP-APC (1:200)

  - CD44- APC-Cy7 (1:300)

  - Leave FITC and PerCP open for caspase and 7AAD

*Caspase/7AAD staining*

\*As caspase/7AAD stain does not get washed out, cells continue to stain even after the incubation. Therefore, make sure all your comps and experiment are set up on the cytometer so you can directly run your samples. If this is not possible, incubate the samples in caspase and 7AAD stain in shifts (e.g. one tissue type at a time) as staining will continue and your last samples will be more stained than the first run ones. This is a technical difference and you could mistake it for biological.

7.  Thaw caspase reagent.

8.  Resuspend wells in 50 ul of counting beads and move to FACS tubes.

9.  Make sure heat block is at correct temp. Heat block positive controls for 5 min at 95C.

10. Make 1:1000 stock solution of caspase (1 ul per 1 ml of FACS) and add 50 ul into sample (1:2000 final dilution—I promise\!) Add to all samples except FMO.

11. When positive controls/comps are done on the heat block, add 50 ul of caspase solution and incubate tubes at 37C for 20 min.

12. Make 7AAD solution (1:500 dilution) and add 100 ul of 7AAD into 100 ul of solution caspase+cells, making a final dilution of 1:1000.

13. Let incubate for 5 min in drawer in dark at RT. Then go to flow\!
