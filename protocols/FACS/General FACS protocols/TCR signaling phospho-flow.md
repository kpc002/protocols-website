**Phospho-flow of TCR signaling**

**Day -1**

*Coat Plate*

1.  Make a 5 ug/ml anti-CD3 solution and 2 ug/ml anti-CD28

2.  Add 50 ul of PBS to 96 well flat bottom plate and incubate overnight at 4C

**Day 0**

*Cell Stimulation*

1.  Transfer cells after isolation or culture from well to 50 ml conical vials and wash well 2x with media by spinning cells 1500 RPM for 5 min

2.  Dump media and resuspend wells in serum-starved media.

3.  Make conditions of interest such as protein that induces cell death, promotes proliferation, etc. Use PBS or buffer as a control.

4.  Plate 2e6 cells to uncoated wells for unstim controls and then to anti-CD3/28 coated wells (+/- protein of interest) for 5, 10, 15, 30 min stim. Ideal to have different plates if you can to not disrupt the other time frames.

5.  Incubate at 37C for 5, 10, 15, 30 minutes

6.  With each timepoint, aspirate cells, wash well 1x with 100 ul FACS and put into 96 U bottom wells

*Samples*

  - Starved media unstim controls

  - 5 ug/ml CD3, 2 ug/ml anti-CD28 in starved media

<!-- end list -->

  - Phospho-protein controls: pERK isotype, pSLP76 isotype, pZap70 isotype, pS6 isotype

  - Whole protein controls: ERK, SLP76, S6, ZAP70

*MeOH Fix/perm for phospho-flow (Byron Au-Yeung lab)*

1.  Wash 1x with FACS

2.  Aspirate all FACS and resuspend in 4% paraformaldehyde

3.  Fix cells for 10 min with 4% paraformaldehyde (150 ul) in the dark at 4C or on ice.

4.  Spin down and wash cells 1x with FACS and aspirate sup

5.  Perform surface staining

6.  Wash 1x with FACS

7.  Add 40 ul of ice cold 90% MeOH (kept in freezer, diluted with water) dropwise, vortex (repeat 3x)

8.  Incubate on ice for 30 min

9.  Wash with FACS, spin, aspirate.

10. Rehydrate with 50 ul of FACS for 15-20 min

11. Wash with FACS, spin, and aspirate.

12. Stain with intracellular master mix for 30 min at 4C.

13. Wash 2x with FACS

14. Flow
