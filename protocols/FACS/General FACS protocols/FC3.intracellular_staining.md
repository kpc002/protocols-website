**<span class="underline">Protocol for intracellular staining for flow cytometry</span>**

**NB:** To eliminate potential artifacts due to dead cell contamination, we recommend the use of a Fixable Viability Dye to allow the exclusion of dead cells from the analysis (as described in protocol CB.10). The saponin BD Perm/Wash™ buffer can be used in intracellular cytokine staining to permeabilize cells and to serve as an antibody diluent and cell wash buffer. Because saponin-mediated cell permeabilization is a reversible process, it is important to keep the cells in the presence of saponin during intracellular cytokine staining.

**Materials**

  - BD Fix/Perm solution is supplied 4X: prepare 1X with 1 part of Fix/Perm Buffer + 3 parts of Diluent Buffer

  - BD Perm/Wash buffer is supplied 5X: prepare 1X with 1 part Perm/Wash + 4 parts of distilled water

  - Sterile FACS washing buffer (1X PBS-/- supplemented with 2% FBS or 1X HBSS-/- supplemented with 2% FBS)

  - Antibodies

  - Sample

**Protocol**

  - Stain cell surface antigen(s).

  - After the last wash, aspirate the supernatant and pulse vortex the sample to completely dissociate the pellet.

**1. Fix and Permeabilize Cells**

  - Thoroughly resuspend cells in 500 μL of BD Fix/Perm solution per tube and incubate for 40 min at 4°C.

  - Wash cells in 2 mL BD Perm/Wash™ buffer which can be used as the wash buffer and as the antibody diluent.

  - Centrifuge samples at 1600 rpm at room temperature for 4 minutes, then aspirate the supernatant.

**2. Stain for Intracellular Cytokines**

  - Thoroughly resuspend fixed/permeabilized cells in 100 μL of BD Perm/Wash™ buffer 1X containing a predetermined optimal concentration of a fluorochrome-conjugated anti-cytokine antibody.

  - Incubate at 4°C for 40 minutes in the dark.

  - Wash cells with BD Perm/Wash™ buffer 1X.

  - Centrifuge samples at 1600 rpm at room temperature for 5 minutes, then aspirate the supernatant.

  - Resuspend in 200 uL FACS buffer prior to flow cytometric analysis.

  - Keep the sample at 4°C till the acquisition.
